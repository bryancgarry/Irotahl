# Talia

> [!summary] At a Glance
> [[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]]'s ex. Works at V's Lounge, willingly.

## Played
- In [[Session 31 - Welcome to Hollowmere]] she ran out of a house on the Commons and grabbed Aalyn after the fight with Vargrax: "Ally, what are you doing here? You almost died."
- In [[Session 32 - Exploring the City]] she waits tables at the Drunken Star for old man Olaf and works a second job at V's in the Fog Slab, saving for a house. Gave Aalyn a pamphlet; the lady who runs V's wants to meet him. Bog told her she was disabled, and she took it to heart.
- In [[Session 33 - Friends and Lovers]] she and Aalyn walked their old route and spent the night together. "I love you. You don't have to say it back." V's clients can go masked; powerful people come.
- In [[Session 37 - Back From The Hunt]] she danced at V's with a front-row seat for Aalyn, told him about the masked newcomer, and will introduce him to Miss V. Took an old patron's bag of gold and gave Aalyn 50 gp of it.
