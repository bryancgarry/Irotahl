# Fenmarel

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A wild lord of [[Arborea]] who never fit its courts. Araushnee's lover, cast out into [[Limbo]] when their affair was discovered. The distant, half-mythical ancestor of [[House Fenwyn]].

## Motivation
**Open.** An outcast who was never forgiven by the court that cast him out.

## Relationships
- **Araushnee / [[Lolth]].** His lover before her fall. Their plotting against Titania's court ended in her betrayal and his exile.
- **[[House Fenwyn]].** His distant descendants, so far back that the line can barely be traced.
- **The wolves of Limbo.** His hunting pack.

> [!secret] The affair and the exile
> revealed:: false
> Before her fall, Araushnee, Titania's twin, took Fenmarel as her lover. When their plotting against Titania came apart, Araushnee's fall began, and Fenmarel was cast out of Arborea into Limbo.

> [!secret] The outcasts' flank
> revealed:: false
> When the obyriths broke into Arborea during [[The Sundering of Planes]], Fenmarel came back out of Limbo with a host of outcast elves and the wolves of Limbo. The courts wouldn't let them fight under their banners, so they held a flank no one else would.
> Possibly where the outcasts saved the dragons: *"Our family saved your kind when the corruption came,"* as Alaric once told the gold dragon Aurixia.

> [!secret] The Deck
> revealed:: false
> In exile, Fenmarel made [[The Deck of Many Things]]: Limbo's chaos turned into chance, fate as a card game.

> [!info] DM Note
> Open: whether Fenmarel is still alive in Limbo, and what he'd want from a descendant who comes looking.
