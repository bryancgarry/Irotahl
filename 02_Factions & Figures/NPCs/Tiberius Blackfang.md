# Tiberius Blackfang

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A tabaxi bearing the Brand of Sacrifice, wielding a dragon-scale glaive. He intervened during the Graz'zt ascension scene in [[Oakhollow]].

## Played
- Sir Romulus Darken's bodyguard (the table rolled him a 15).
- In [[Session 10 - The Orchid]], Wayne overheard him pressing Silas Holloway about the keep attack coming through the guild's tunnels.
- In [[Session 11 - The Plot Thickens]] he lifted his eye patch to show the same brand the party carries, and was not surprised Bog is unbranded. He agreed to join the party in the sewer tunnels.
- In [[Session 12 - The Establishment is Corrupt]] he waited for the party under the Orchid for hours, then went back to the keep.
- In [[Session 14 - A Little Bit of Everything]] Silas said Tiberius works closely with a member of the Obsidian Enclave (Orthus).
- In [[Session 17 - The Vault]] he was guarding Rosalie's door. He said he doesn't work for the Enclave ("My allegiance is to the Darken family"), that Darkstone has its own Enclave adviser, and that the Enclave keeps tabs on everyone but is no threat.
- In [[Session 18 - The Stage is Set]] he brought word that Malrik was seen at the Grand Cathedral, brought Bragthar and Sheila, and gave the party a sending stone to call him. He waited outside.
- In [[Session 21 - The Godhand]] he answered the sending stone, cut into Graz'zt's domain with a golden glaive, and fought all five members of the Godhand in runic armor that unfolds from his eye patch, the same make as the lithoweave. The demons know him: "Oh Tiberius, how long are we going to play this game?" He helped break the domain from inside. He has fought them before. Wayne heard screams through him that weren't his.
- In [[Session 22 - A Long Night Ends]] he brought Ino Silvertone's mother to heal Prince Darken, told Aalyn the party is in over its head, and gave him the scouts' report of the Bloodlancer army. "This is probably the best outcome that could have happened when dealing with the Godhand."
- In [[Session 23 - Magic and Religion]] he left Oakhollow with Prince Darken on a stretcher. Bog knows of a dwarven smith who makes armor like his.
