# Tiberius Blackfang

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A tabaxi bearing the Brand of Sacrifice, wielding a dragon-scale glaive. He intervened during the Graz'zt ascension scene in [[Oakhollow]].

## Played
- Sir Romulus Darken's bodyguard (the table rolled him a 15).
- In [[Session 10 - The Orchid]], Wayne overheard him pressing Silas Holloway about the keep attack coming through the guild's tunnels.
- In [[Session 11 - The Plot Thickens]] he lifted his eye patch to show the same brand the party carries, and was not surprised Bog is unbranded. He agreed to join the party in the sewer tunnels.
