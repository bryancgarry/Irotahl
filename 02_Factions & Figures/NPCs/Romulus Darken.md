# Romulus Darken

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Sir Romulus Darken, Prince of Darkstone, who married Princess Rosalie Fern. He held Oakhollow as temporary ruler after the wedding, with [[Tiberius Blackfang]] as his bodyguard. Lost an arm the night Graz'zt rose.

## Played
- **[[Session 3 - The Bridge King|Session 3]].** His Darkstone guards in black and gray held Oakhollow's south gate. Thimble forged a royal decree in his name.
- **[[Session 10 - The Orchid|Session 10]] and [[Session 11 - The Plot Thickens|Session 11]].** Knelt by Rosalie, floating in dark-red magic. Briefed the party on her condition and the court records missing from around her birth, paid the second half of the stone trees fee (50 gp each), and asked them to search Malrik's Solarium. His closest confidants are Tiberius and Orthus.
- **[[Session 14 - A Little Bit of Everything|Session 14]].** Heard Aalyn's report. Tension between his guards and the Oakhollow guard; he thinks the looming threat is all that keeps the city from erupting. "You have my protection, but that only goes so far in the city. Use it sparingly."
- **[[Session 17 - The Vault|Session 17]].** Sent the party after Edith Valeheart's vault. The nobility resist his temporary rule. He knows who Alaric Fenwyn is, and winked.
- **[[Session 21 - The Godhand|Session 21]] and [[Session 22 - A Long Night Ends|Session 22]].** Found slumped beside Rosalie with a hole in his side. He survived, missing an arm, in the care of Ino Silvertone's mother. Rosalie died.
- **[[Session 23 - Magic and Religion|Session 23]].** Tiberius carried him out of Oakhollow on a stretcher. Aalyn forged an evacuation order in his name.
- **[[Session 37 - Back From The Hunt|Session 37]].** Orthus told the baroness he was "recovering from quite an eventful wedding night."

> [!info] DM Note
> His seal appears on a Hellhammer letter in Atul's Dark Stone vault, carried by Tiberius (see [[Session 54 - The Heart of the Void]]). Open: what Darkstone does next, and what happened to the Fern and Darken alliance.
