# Luna au Vermillion

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Imperator of the Holy Silver Chain Knights in Hollowmere, of the au Vermillion family of Vermillion Bay.

## Played
- In [[Session 28 - Emperor Hawk Tuah and The Goon Generals]] she rode up on a white mare with two riders in silver armor, on a rocky trail halfway between Westbury and Hollowmere: short blonde hair, a silver sword, early twenties. "Identify yourselves." Her first name slipped out of character after the session; the party doesn't know it in game.
- In [[Session 29 - The Holy Silver Chain Knights]] she introduced herself, scanned the party, and stood down after Thimble invoked his name. Emerald eyes, blonde hair in buns, 23, from Vermillion Bay. Came to Hollowmere with her brother one summer and stayed. Was friends with Rosalie.
- In [[Session 30 - Journey Through the Fog]] she told Thimble she lost her brother on a full-moon night, and wants to use the brand as bait for the monster that killed him. Dragged into the fog by a Wendigo; Aalyn healed her awake.
- In [[Session 31 - Welcome to Hollowmere]] she offered the party rooms at the Silver Keep and passes. She says Vargrax the Executioner was harmless and ran a tiefling orphanage.
