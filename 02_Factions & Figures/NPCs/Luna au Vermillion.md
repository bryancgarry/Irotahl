# Luna au Vermillion

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Imperator of the Holy Silver Chain Knights in Hollowmere, of the au Vermillion family of Vermillion Bay.

## Played
- In [[Session 28 - Emperor Hawk Tuah and The Goon Generals]] she rode up on a white mare with two riders in silver armor, on a rocky trail halfway between Westbury and Hollowmere: short blonde hair, a silver sword, early twenties. "Identify yourselves." Her first name slipped out of character after the session; the party doesn't know it in game.
- In [[Session 29 - The Holy Silver Chain Knights]] she introduced herself, scanned the party, and stood down after Thimble invoked his name. Emerald eyes, blonde hair in buns, 23, from Vermillion Bay. Came to Hollowmere with her brother one summer and stayed. Was friends with Rosalie.
- In [[Session 30 - Journey Through the Fog]] she told Thimble she lost her brother on a full-moon night, and wants to use the brand as bait for the monster that killed him. Dragged into the fog by a Wendigo; Aalyn healed her awake.
- In [[Session 31 - Welcome to Hollowmere]] she offered the party rooms at the Silver Keep and passes. She says Vargrax the Executioner was harmless and ran a tiefling orphanage.
- In [[Session 32 - Exploring the City]] she told Thimble the full story: the Vermillions of Vermillion Bay on Vermore keep a chalet outside Hollowmere; her brother saved her from thugs as children, joined the knights and became Imperator; a monster too fast to see killed him protecting her; his body vanished, leaving a chain link. She admitted she led the party through the fog to test the brand, which attracts monsters.
- In [[Session 33 - Friends and Lovers]] Dame said the knights fear rather than love her, and that Selara steers her. She told Thimble of the Temple of 1000 Candles and asked him to wait a week to heal. Her tailor from the chalet will come to the keep.
- In [[Session 38 - Digging For Information]] she came down when the archives' alarm went off and covered for the party after Thimble's appeal. "We might have to accelerate our plans." Then: "What's a devil?"
- In [[Session 39 - Escape From the Keep]] she has never heard of devils. She expects to lose her rank when the Chain Lords summon her, and planned to take the party down the mag lift to Durnhollow, where she has tracked an arcane signature. Next morning she was led away in handcuffs to a carriage.
