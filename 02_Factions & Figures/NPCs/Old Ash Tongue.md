# Old Ash Tongue

> [!summary] At a Glance
> An old bearded salamander from the stalactite prison of [[Vistro IV]]'s city. Claimed he was there 25 years for "unspeakable things" with the king's mother; admitted he is an alleged serial bomber and an old friend of the king. Keeps dynamite in unlikely places. Broke out with Wayne and Thimble, fought beside them in the trial by combat, and now travels with the party; he joined them for [[The Hollow Reliquary]].

## Played
- **[[Session 50.5 - The Wayne and Thimble Episode|Session 50.5]].**
  - Sang the old salamander song of the Kyn'Ruthar: stone skin, hollow heart, the green went gray where he turned, "and then he turned again."
  - The salamanders guard the world from what is in the castle, the pilgrimage site of the Kyn'Ruthar who slew their ancestors. Told Wayne to keep the title quiet.
  - Blew up his own cell with a hidden stick of dynamite, posed as a guard to walk them across the bridge, and led them to the trial by combat.
  - Bit the head off a newt warrior (natural 20) and stuck a second stick of dynamite on the king's champion. Tagged Wayne in for the kill.
