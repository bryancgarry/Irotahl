# Clausius Clapeyron

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Chief metallurgist of Durnhollow. His journals, seeded across the dungeon floors, are the main way its lore reaches the players.

## Played
- The party found his research journal in the steam layer's testing lab ([[Session 42 - Tempting Fate]]): an enhancement chamber that infuses an item with arcane steam through silver. Dated the year 412, about a thousand years ago.
