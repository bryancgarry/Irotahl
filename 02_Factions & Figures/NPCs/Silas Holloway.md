# Silas Holloway

> [!summary] At a Glance
> The owner of the Orchid in [[Oakhollow]] and the [[The Thieves Guild|Thieves Guild]] safe house beneath it. Long black hair in a knot, sharp features, rings, and a half mask over one side of his jaw. Knows Jack by legend.

## Played
- **[[Session 10 - The Orchid|Session 10]].** Gave Wayne the rat job. One of his runners did a job for an Obsidian Enclave member (something from ruins in the forest) and was never paid. Tiberius pressed him about the keep attack coming through the guild's tunnels.
- **[[Session 14 - A Little Bit of Everything|Session 14]].** Took the Rat Lord's Hat as proof, let Wayne keep it, and paid 50 gp. Named the runner, Calder. Says Tiberius works closely with Orthus. Considers the safe house compromised.
- **[[Session 16 - Action and Apologies|Session 16]].** The mask job came to Calder through him, for Edith Valeheart.
