# Fenril Fenwyn

> [!summary] At a Glance
> [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]]'s older brother. A powerful elven warrior-mage who carved his name into [[House Fenwyn]]'s history before Alaric was grown. Alive and somewhere out in the world.

## Personality and Voice
In Alaric's memories: warm, a little mysterious, a teacher who speaks in lessons.
- *"You don't step out of my shadow, Alaric. You walk in a different direction entirely. Find your own sun."*
- *"Choose growth. Even if it terrifies you. Especially if it terrifies you."*

## Relationships
- **[[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]].** Younger brother, raised in his shadow. When Alaric was thirteen, Fenril brought him before the ancient gold dragon Aurixia. As children, he told Alaric stories of the eladrin's seasonal blades and of a stone in the far west that held every color, and promised they'd go find that mountain someday.

> [!secret] Between the brothers
> revealed:: false
> Something stands between them that Alaric blames himself for (the Hall of Wants idea was "Fenril, forgiven").

> [!info] DM Note
> His story is still to be written: where he is, why he left, and when he enters the campaign. Built as a Sasuke and Itachi-style dynamic. Kept separate from the Limbo wolf thread.
