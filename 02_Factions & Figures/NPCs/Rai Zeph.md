# Rai Zeph

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A storm Goliath, exiled after failing his trial of adulthood, who blinded himself in penance. Healed in Hollowmere and turned into a werebear in the process. Guards the city's outer gate and drinks at Terry's. Calls [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]] "my Kyn'Ruthar."

## Played
- **[[Session 33 - Friends and Lovers|Session 33]].** Jud met "a nice bear with slashes over its eyes" in a fey grove, who said he was healed in the city.
- **[[Session 48 - Magic Mushroom Fun Pt. 2|Session 48]].** Found Wayne in the Feywild and knelt to him as the Kyn'Ruthar.
  - Storm Goliath accent, from the stormy south of Wayne's continent. The scars over his eyes are self-inflicted, a penance for failing his trial of adulthood; he was exiled.
  - A guard on Hollowmere's outer gate, who sweated when the party first arrived.
  - Came to Hollowmere seeking healing. A temple, **the Gilded Conservatory** by the Silver Keep, healed his eyes and changed his body: he can take bear and werebear hybrid forms at will. He stays in the city to warn people away from its healing. "They have their way with your body."
  - Travels to and from the Feywild with a satchel of diamonds that brute-force planar gates. Gave Wayne one.
  - Asked Wayne to go home and speak with the elders: the [[Oughan Gor'Drak|Gor'Drak Dominion]] is ravaging the Goliath homelands and they need their protector.
  - "Should you need an ally in the city, find me at Terry's," an ale house, in his guard uniform.
  - Warned Wayne to pull Thimble out of the nymphs' water. "I am forever in your service as you are to our people, my kin."
