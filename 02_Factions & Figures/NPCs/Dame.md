# Dame

> [!summary] At a Glance
> One of the Holy Silver Chain Knights' two Vice-Imperators, beside [[Corvin Hale]]. A Hollowmere street orphan raised by an old knight. Friendly to the party, and the only knight who drinks at the Drunken Star.

## Played
- **[[Session 29 - The Holy Silver Chain Knights|Session 29]].** Rode at Luna's side. "We seek truth before silver."
- **[[Session 30 - Journey Through the Fog|Session 30]].** Talked with Aalyn on the road: an orphan and street kid until an old knight found him. 28. Survived the wendigo attack with gashes on his chime arm.
- **[[Session 33 - Friends and Lovers|Session 33]].** Cleared the party with the city guard. Says the knights fear Luna rather than love her, and that Selara steers her. Had never heard of the Godhand. Lost a wererat at the Temple of 1000 Candles.
- **[[Session 39 - Escape From the Keep|Session 39]].** Still drinking at the Drunken Star after Luna's arrest.

> [!info] DM Note
> Open: where he stands now that Corvin commands the knights.
