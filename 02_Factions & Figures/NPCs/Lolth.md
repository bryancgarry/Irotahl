# Lolth

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Godhand of fate, webs, and the fallen Archfey. The first of the Godhand to be reborn. [[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]]'s warlock patron.

## Motivation
Her agents work through [[The Cult of the Dark Moon]]: sleeper cells, coded symbols, dead drops.

> [!secret] Her origin
> revealed:: false
> Lolth was once an Archfey of [[Arborea]], twin sister to Queen Titania. Her obyrith essence awoke in her after the Sundering had ended, at the First [[Moon Darkenings|Moon Darkening]]. Her fall draws on Araushnee: betrayal of her own court and a forbidden affair with Fenmarel. Details open.

> [!secret] Verenestra
> revealed:: false
> Lolth holds the Archfey [[Verenestra]] captive. Aalyn knows her as Sumina, his believed-dead childhood love.

> [!secret] Her relics
> revealed:: false
> The Tome of Lolth is in [[Syfaeril]]. The Spool of Lolth's Thread is held at [[The Temple of 1000 Candles]].
