# Bragthar

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A troll in a top hat who guarded the Bridge King's bridge. He speaks only Giant, and **he obeys whoever wears the Bridge King's crown**. Wayne took the crown in [[Session 3 - The Bridge King]], and Bragthar has fought for the party since. 94 HP.

## What the Party Knows
- His ex lives in [[Oakhollow]]. He asked Aalyn about dental.
- He has a hidden mushroom cove with a magic pool, tended by "housekeepers" who change the mushrooms. See [[Session 4 - Mushrooms & Trees]].
- He ate Shroomly, Aalyn's mimic mushroom.
- Takes orders literally. "Go crazy" gets him break-dancing or swinging at whatever is closest. He keeps losing his rock.
- Ate a Bloodlancer's head at the camp in [[Session 5 - Bluelock and Bloodlancers]].
