# Birdolomew Feathersworth

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A dramatic aarakocra merchant from the mountains of [[Vyrmor]], "the undisputed number one name in aven commerce." He runs the Feathersworth Emporium in [[Oakhollow]] with his wife and son, and travels the roads selling oddities.

## What the Party Knows
- Met him on the road to the Godspire ruins in [[Session 6 - The Path to Godspire Ruins]]. He sold a featherweight elixir, a treasure compass, pocket phoenixes, Wild Wager coins, a mineral moisturizer and squawking stones, and privately offered Thimble an immovable rod.
- Bog intimidated a tail feather out of him for 5 gp.
- Aalyn sent him toward the looted Bloodlancer camp ("Tell them Malrik Greene sent you"). Whether he made it out is unknown.
- His wife runs Birdolomew's Magical Emporium in Oakhollow with their young son, Falcon. In [[Session 11 - The Plot Thickens]] she sold Wayne a heated immovable rod for his egg, and said Birdolomew is overdue home.
