# Professor Bifflefapple

> [!summary] At a Glance
> An elderly, non-tenured gnome professor at Cinderward in [[Hollowmere]] who restores old magic. Sits on a booster seat. Inspired by Heimerdinger. He's restoring an airship found under the mountain ice, and needs a power source to fly it.

## Played
- **[[Session 29 - The Holy Silver Chain Knights|Session 29]].** Barnaby asked whether Thimble had studied with him.
- **[[Session 32 - Exploring the City|Session 32]].** Wayne got into Cinderward by pretending to be him. He gave Wayne *Wizardry 101.1* and coaxed out his first telekinetic pull ("Wayne's Pull"). He knows Alaric's father, and studies old archives, artifacts, enchantment and enhancement. Showed the party the airship.
- **[[Session 33 - Friends and Lovers|Session 33]].** The airship needs the heart of Nerevath, the goose alive (he'd model a compression device on its esophagus), or dwarven air compression from the ruins below the city. Selara says the Enclave invited him and he turned them down.
- **[[Session 37 - Back From The Hunt|Session 37]].** Took in Salvatore, who'll let him study his honks for a share. Wayne is on strike two for using his badge.
- **[[Session 40 - The Elevator Down|Session 40]].** Pointed the party to the old mine elevator into [[Durnhollow]]. Knows Thimble's father ("Hopefully not like his father"). Close to a breakthrough on Nerevath, and wants to see the party after the ruins. Tip: the dwarves were researching an enhancement for the mountains' silver.
- **[[Session 49 - The Rock God|Session 49]].** Alaric wants him to line up buyers for the restarted silver refinery.

> [!info] DM Note
> Open: his grudge with the Danglesworths, what he dreams about ("a conversation for next time"), and the extra rare part if the compression mechanism comes out cracked.
