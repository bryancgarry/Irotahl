# Papa Glabb

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A gag bard. His Harp and Songbook are uncommon bard items, re-flavored from the official Instruments of the Bard.

## Played
- A Bloodlancer orc scout, charmed by Aalyn, claimed Papa Glabb as his grandpappy. See [[Session 4 - Mushrooms & Trees]].
- The party found a CD of his classic hits in Sheila's glovebox ([[Session 9 - The Off-Branded Misfits]]).
- The old owner of Whispering Wares in Oakhollow was his best friend. He says Papa Glabb never made it big because he fell in love. Aalyn bought Papa Glabb's lute there ([[Session 11 - The Plot Thickens]]).
