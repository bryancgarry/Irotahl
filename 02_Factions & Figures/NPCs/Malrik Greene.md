# Malrik Greene

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Former Obsidian Enclave advisor to the court of [[Oakhollow]].

> [!secret] Graz'zt's apostle
> revealed:: false
> Malrik performed the Bridge King's ascension beneath the Grand Cathedral. Graz'zt's first act was to make him his apostle and pull him into the Abyss. The players do not know what became of him.

## Played
- **Session 6.** Found tortured in a blood-rune cage at the Bloodlancer camp in the [[The Tall Oak Forest|Tall Oak Forest]], with three levels of exhaustion. Freed and healed by the party.
  - He left the royal reception because his confidant Orthus (Obsidian Enclave, adviser to Darkstone) sent him to investigate activity at the Godspire ruins, which he called "the prophecy" to Alaric.
  - His illusionary battery (it stores prophecy from the solar refractory) was in the commander's lockbox, and the party returned it.
  - His confiscated note had a Draconic margin line: "The daughter is key."
  - He came to Oakhollow shortly after Rosalie's birth and says the Baron spent an unusual amount of time in the prayer room.
  - He walked back to Oakhollow alone with Aalyn's potion of healing. See [[Session 6 - The Path to Godspire Ruins]].
- **Session 9.** A black-robed man with a hobble pointed Muzzle's gnomes toward Thimble from a back alley.
- **Session 11.** He never returned to Oakhollow. He took over as adviser after Rosalie's birth, which is when the court's records go missing. Sir Romulus thinks he was a student or devotee of the previous adviser, **Gormar**. He invited the party to the reception but wasn't there himself. Romulus asked the party to search his quarters at the Solarium in the Mages Guild.
- **Session 14.** Orthus had the Mages Guild bar access to his Solarium. Its door had been tampered with from the inside. A glyph of warding guarded the solar refractory, and construct drones defended the room. A hidden storeroom held a chest for Alaric from his parents. See [[Session 14 - A Little Bit of Everything]].
- **Session 15.** His storeroom held a battery not in the Solarium's catalog, redacted logs (his other logs are locked away elsewhere), the Enclave's note about Wayne and Thimble, and half of a heart key.
- **Session 17.** His name is in the ledger of Edith Valeheart's vault, and his redacted logs were inside it. See [[Session 17 - The Vault]].
- **Session 18.** Spotted entering the Grand Cathedral through the south gate late at night. Rose into the chamber beneath it on a pedestal. The DM says he is really an elemental wizard, and recruited Alaric as an illusionist for a reason.
- **Session 19.** Demanded his mask back and the gemstone on Wayne's neck. Said he has seen and dreamt the future, that he is trying to prevent the world's destruction, that he has "the vessel" ready, and asked how they plan to defeat the Godhand. Woke the chamber's guardians. See [[Session 19 - The Fight Begins]].
- **Session 20.** Called Alaric "Son." When Talula took the gemstone: "No, no, no. You don't know what you've done." Bog knocked him out, and Aalyn tied him up with the chains. See [[Session 20 - The Ascension]].
- **Session 21.** Aalyn healed him in Graz'zt's domain. Seeing the Godhand: "No. Oh no." He searched for something the party had already taken. "It wasn't supposed to be like this." Graz'zt made him his apostle (blue skin, red eyes, a tusked boar-like head, blue flame) and sent him into the Abyss. See [[Session 21 - The Godhand]].
- **Session 22.** His bag of holding held three greater healing potions, rings of water walking and jumping, a rune of recall, the illusionary battery, an incomplete Abyssal grimoire, refractory schematics, and an aetherstone order to D. Bronzebottom of Glimmerstone. His charts mark another gemstone off Midland's southern coast. See [[Session 22 - A Long Night Ends]].
- **Session 23.** The repaired solar refractory showed his prophecy: a darkness invading the world, rifts in the skies, and demons flying in from above and below. His grimoire is the introductory volume to the Godhand tomes. See [[Session 23 - Magic and Religion]].
