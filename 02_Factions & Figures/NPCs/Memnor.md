# Memnor

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A son of Annam All-Father and Othea, god of pride and control in the Ordning of the giants.

> [!secret] He sold his brother
> revealed:: false
> During the Sundering, Memnor bargained with [[Malcanthet]] for power, and the price was his brother Naki-Uthai, delivered to her as a gift. He became [[The First Hollow Vessel]]. Annam struck Naki-Uthai's name from the Ordning, and Memnor's part was buried with it. The only hint at the table is a hidden second hand in the Reliquary's fourth War mural.
