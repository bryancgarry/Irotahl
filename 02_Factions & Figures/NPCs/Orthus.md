# Orthus

> [!summary] At a Glance
> The elderly Obsidian Enclave adviser to Darkstone and a confidant of [[Romulus Darken|Sir Romulus]]. Seemed to know what Wayne's pale gemstone was. Lied to Hollowmere's baroness about Oakhollow.

## Played
- **[[Session 6 - The Path to Godspire Ruins|Session 6]].** Sent [[Malrik Greene]] away from the wedding reception to investigate activity at the Godspire ruins. Never passed Malrik's message on to the party.
- **[[Session 10 - The Orchid|Session 10]] and [[Session 11 - The Plot Thickens|Session 11]].** Sent the party up to Rosalie's room. Wasn't forthcoming about the records missing from around Rosalie's birth.
- **[[Session 14 - A Little Bit of Everything|Session 14]].** Had the Mages Guild bar access to Malrik's Solarium. Silas says Tiberius works closely with him.
- **[[Session 37 - Back From The Hunt|Session 37]].** Appeared by obsidian-cube projection in Selara's wing. Played dumb about Oakhollow: the prince is "recovering from quite an eventful wedding night." Asked after Wayne. Tiberius is "screaming at the sky as he always does." He outranks Selara. To the baroness: "I'm sure you will see the princess and prince again one day."

> [!info] DM Note
> Open: what he knows about the pale gemstone, why Malrik's message never reached the party, why he barred the Solarium, and what his ominous line to the baroness means. A candidate for whoever woke the Godspire (see [[Story Planning]]).
