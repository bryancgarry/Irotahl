# Caelum au Vermillion

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Of the au Vermillion family. Not yet met at the table.

> [!secret] Luna's brother
> revealed:: false
> [[Luna au Vermillion|Luna]]'s older brother, the Imperator before her, who was "killed" by a monster too fast to see on a full-moon night, leaving only a chain link. The arcane signature Luna tracked into Durnhollow is him.

> [!secret] Vashara's experiment
> revealed:: false
> [[Vashara]] has been experimenting on him in Durnhollow's deepest room, turning him into a **pseudo-vessel**: a fake Hollow Vessel. In practice he's a hugely buffed-up wendigo, wielding a devastating huge greatsword in one hand. He's her best attempt so far, and still a failure: she can still a body, but not a mind or a soul.

> [!info] DM Note
> A natural end boss for Durnhollow. See [[Luna au Vermillion]] and [[Vashara]].
