# Malcanthet

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Godhand of desire, corruption, and flesh. An original obyrith and creator of [[The First Hollow Vessel]]. Her current host is unknown. Tender toward Wayne, which is worse than a threat.

## Motivation
She wants a new Hollow Vessel, a new champion of the Abyss. She has chosen [[Wayne Kerr]].

> [!secret] Hollowing is her art
> revealed:: false
> Perfected in [[The Soul Sanctum]], with [[The Sea of Void]] as her medium. See [[Hollowing and Petrification]].

> [!secret] How she reaches the world
> revealed:: false
> She can reach across the planes only through tethers (the Void Trial's mark, a [[The Void Heart|Void Heart]] fragment), as a voice or a vestige, never her true form. She selected Wayne through the Void Trial in [[The Rikk Borgar]] and began hollowing him in the Soul Sanctum.

> [!secret] Her instruments
> revealed:: false
> [[Vashara]] is her apostle in Hollowmere.

> [!secret] Her Tome
> revealed:: false
> The Tome of Malcanthet is carried by [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], and she hates that it exists: a room of her own house, torn out and bound in ink, watching her. Carrying it into the Reliquary's Soul trial alerts her.

> [!secret] Session 54
> revealed:: true
> She appeared at Wayne's shoulder the moment he took the [[Shell of the Void Heart]]: "Oh, how perfect you are. How glorious you are... You're even better than I could have imagined."

> **Session 55 opens on her.** Her dialogue is the kickoff for Wayne's journey toward becoming another fully realized Hollow Vessel. The Godhand scene in [[Session 54 - The Heart of the Void]] (section 3), including her fury at Thimble over the Tome, hasn't been played yet.
> Wants: an ending appearance (vestige, portal, or similar) where she can interact with Wayne, her vessel, and Thimble, who uses her Tome.
