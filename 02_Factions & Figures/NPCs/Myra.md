# Myra

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A fungal myconid companion traveling with the party. Manager of the party's silver refinery in [[Durnhollow]].

## Played
- **[[Session 48 - Magic Mushroom Fun Pt. 2|Session 48]].** Lived 60 years on a boulder by the second mushroom circle, cut off from the fungal network. Stunned Thimble with pacifying spores when he cast *Blight*. Wayne cut her free with the Sun Blade (she has sunlight sickness). Carries sleeping Bog in a fungal pod. Shields the party by ballooning wide, and heals with spores.
- **[[Session 49 - The Rock God|Session 49]].** Took the gargoyles' claws in the quarry. Pulled an amulet of the drunkard out of a cave-in for Aalyn (he owes her). **Agreed to manage the party's restarted silver refinery**: salary and royalties, a contract to come. Follows the party through the dungeon first.
- **[[Session 50 - Into the Inferno|Session 50]].** Bog came out of her pod and went back in. Rode the lava luge with him, cushioned a hot spot with spores, and gave the carts mushroom wings and a spoiler.
