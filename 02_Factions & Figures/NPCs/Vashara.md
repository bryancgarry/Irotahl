# Vashara

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Runs V's Lounge in [[Hollowmere]]. Once the drow princess who married King [[King Atul Stonemarch|Atul Stonemarch]] and became queen of [[Durnhollow]]: "the cleverest mind in Durnhollow. The foundries were hers, the silver was hers."

> [!secret] Malcanthet's apostle
> revealed:: false
> Vashara opened [[The Hollow Reliquary]]'s doors with her own blood, learned hollowing from its stone book, and found an Ember inside. It made her a succubus apostle of [[Malcanthet]]. When Atul led his army across the lava lake to stop her, she petrified them all. Her prayer alcove in the Reliquary reads *Forgive me, Atul*.

> [!secret] The lounge is a front
> revealed:: false
> V's Lounge fronts her wendigo experiments and a body-supply arrangement with [[The Temple of 1000 Candles]]. [[Bastian Dray]] is held beneath it. Her crude hollowing handles the body but not the mind or the soul, so what comes out is a wendigo. She is a dark mirror of what Malcanthet wants from Wayne.

> [!secret] Still working in Durnhollow
> revealed:: false
> She still uses Durnhollow (fresh ink, a live experimental rat, a used teleportation circle) and experiments with the vault's lithoweave.

## Played
- In [[Session 38 - Digging For Information]] she came for Bog after his scene on the pole: plum skin, red eyes, black hair. *Dominate Person*. As "Miss V" she met Aalyn in her office, gave her name, had him sign a contract unread, and gave him four names of missing patrons.
- In [[Session 39 - Escape From the Keep]] Bog woke bound in her velvet room: she had him tell her about the party, plucked his scales, and planted "the lounge misses you." Note: "Thank you for your conversation. You're welcome back anytime. V." Talia never sees her by day.
