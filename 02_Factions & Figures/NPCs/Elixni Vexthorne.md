# Elixni Vexthorne

> [!summary] At a Glance
> The stern baroness of [[Hollowmere]], who rules from Hollow Hall beside the Silver Keep. Advised by [[Selara Du'Cant]]. Has kept to her quarter lately.

## Played
- **[[Session 33 - Friends and Lovers|Session 33]].** Sanctioned a hunt for Nerevath (5,000 gp to the slayer) and welcomed the party as "a fifth group." Exploring the dwarven ruins needs her permission.
- **[[Session 34 - The Hunt Begins|Session 34]].** "If you wish to kill each other, please just do it out there." Asked for her family heirloom from the mountain lake. Credits the marriage of House Darken and House Fern for the region's peace, and says Rosalie's mother is "just on holiday."
- **[[Session 37 - Back From The Hunt|Session 37]].** Paid 1,500 gp for the heirlooms. Over tea, Aalyn told her the truth about Oakhollow. Orthus's projection told her she'd see the princess and prince again one day, and she retired shaken. Corvin Hale now works closely with her.
- **[[Session 40 - The Elevator Down|Session 40]].** Bifflefapple says she has kept to her quarter lately.

> [!info] DM Note
> Selara's one request in her truce: "Do not push the baroness. All will be revealed in time." Open: what Selara is protecting.
