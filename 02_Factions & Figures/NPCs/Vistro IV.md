# Vistro IV

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Salamander King of [[The Fire Sages of Vistro]], descendant of the war-sage Vistro who first bound [[The Hollow Reliquary]] in fire. Fought Wayne and Thimble, then made peace and gave them summoning tattoos.

## With the Party
- Asked the party to rid the material plane of the Reliquary.
- Bringing back the Shard of the Ashen Reach earns real goodwill. Wayne taking the Void Heart fragment means a tense reckoning.

> [!secret] The impossible task
> revealed:: false
> The Reliquary can't be unmade from this side. Only something like a Sundering could tear it loose.
