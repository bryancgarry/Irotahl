# Vistro IV

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Salamander King of [[The Fire Sages of Vistro]], descendant of the war-sage Vistro who first bound [[The Hollow Reliquary]] in fire. Fought Wayne and Thimble, then made peace and gave them summoning tattoos.

## With the Party
- Asked the party to rid the material plane of the Reliquary.
- Bringing back the Shard of the Ashen Reach earns real goodwill. Wayne taking the Void Heart fragment means a tense reckoning.

> [!secret] The impossible task
> revealed:: false
> The Reliquary can't be unmade from this side. Only something like a Sundering could tear it loose.

## Played
- **[[Session 50 - Into the Inferno|Session 50]].** Escorted Wayne and Thimble to his island. Set Thimble's star ruby in his chest piece. "Your family is the reason I'm here": banished to this plane because of the Danglesworths of Glimmerstone. At "Kyn'Ruthar" and the Tome of Malcanthet, he blasted them. A salamander inferno master, CR 15.
- **[[Session 51 - The Wayne and Thimble Episode|Session 51]].** Took them prisoner, then freed them after they won his trial by combat. Knew the Mind Medallion as King Atul's, torn from his stone body by his queen. His ancestors took the tome from an evil queen in the temple and gave it to knights. Asked them to return it to the temple, then help his sages' ritual (Wayne sensed he is lying about what it can do). Gave Thimble his crest tattoo and the sages gave Wayne Nerevath's.
