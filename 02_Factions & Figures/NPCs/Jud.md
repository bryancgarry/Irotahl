# Jud

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A friend of Thimble's from Oakhollow who never brought the drinks at the wedding. Branded. Now hiding in the Silver Keep as "Knight Zodius."

## Played
- **[[Session 16 - Action and Apologies|Session 16]].** Turned up alive after the Orchid blew open. Swept up in a wave of black ink at the reception, he never brought the drinks because a "baddie" was talking to him. Thimble sensed he left something out. Rolled Bog into a haystack and got Thimble out the south gate.
- **[[Session 32 - Exploring the City|Session 32]] and [[Session 33 - Friends and Lovers|Session 33]].** Found in the Silver Keep's mess hall. He fled Oakhollow in a noble's trunk, ran from dogs into a burning grove, and came out of the Feywild in the Gilded Conservatory. He met a bear with slashed eyes there ([[Rai Zeph]]). He has a brand, "down low." Luna knows him as "Knight Zodius." Selara says he's not a knight, "but that's not for me to tell."

> [!info] DM Note
> Open: what he left out about the wedding, who the "baddie" was, and where he went after Luna's arrest.
