# Graz'zt

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Godhand of ambition, deception, and the ascended. Before his ascension he was the Bridge King, a petty, cruel goblin the party met in their earliest days in [[Oakhollow]].

> [!secret] The Bridge King's ascension
> revealed:: false
> His ascension took place beneath the Grand Cathedral of Oakhollow, where [[Malrik Greene]] performed the ritual with the same Ember that had branded the party. The party was present and did not understand what they witnessed. It was the Fifth [[Moon Darkenings|Moon Darkening]], completing the Godhand.

> [!secret] A completion, not a corruption
> revealed:: false
> Graz'zt retains full memory of being the Bridge King. His first act was to make Malrik his apostle and pull him into the Abyss.

> [!secret] Tiberius was there
> revealed:: false
> [[Tiberius Blackfang]] intervened during the ascension scene.

## Played
- As the Bridge King he held his bridge outside Oakhollow with [[Bragthar]], lost his crown to Wayne, and floated away downriver. See [[Session 3 - The Bridge King]]. Thimble later skipped a stone off his skull: "I will remember that."
- In [[Session 20 - The Ascension]] the party found him flayed and bound on a pedestal beneath the Grand Cathedral. Talula had placed the gemstone in his chest, and he rose about 12 ft tall as a demon lord.
- In [[Session 21 - The Godhand]] he condensed the party's rat cannon into a time-stopped fireball, pulled them into his domain, drew an obsidian blade, disintegrated Sheila and killed Bragthar. He joined the Godhand, named himself, and made Malrik his apostle. To Thimble as the party fled: "I told you I would remember that."
