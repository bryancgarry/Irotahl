# Talula Fern

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Rosalie's mother, chained as a demon beneath the Grand Cathedral of [[Oakhollow]] since Rosalie's birth. She took the pale gemstone from the party and placed it in the Bridge King's chest. Last seen at the ascension.

## Played
- **[[Session 18 - The Stage is Set|Session 18]].** The Pendant of the Ferns opened her tomb onto a calcified tunnel. Through the wooden mask, Aalyn saw the young auburn-haired woman inside the demon. Her memories, shown to everyone: her mother, Queen Daffy, died; [[Baron Fern]] grew possessive and abusive and locked her away; she fell pregnant, learned she wouldn't survive it, and an Obsidian Enclave adviser was present; Rosalie was born healthy, Talula turned into the demon, and the chains bound her as the Baron ran off with the baby.
- **[[Session 19 - The Fight Begins|Session 19]].** Her chains strained toward the gemstone on Wayne's neck. Once it was hidden she went for anyone branded. The woman inside wept black tears, and the chains began to snap.
- **[[Session 20 - The Ascension|Session 20]].** Saying Rosalie's name stirred the woman inside. She broke free, mentioned "the artifact," and Alaric tossed her the gemstone. She fled deeper, and the party found her sitting calmly beside the flayed Bridge King with the gemstone in his chest.
- **[[Session 21 - The Godhand|Session 21]].** She sat in awe as he rose. Thimble saw the woman inside fading, settling into the demon form for good. She was pulled into Graz'zt's domain with everyone else.
- **[[Session 34 - The Hunt Begins|Session 34]].** Hollowmere's baroness loved "Rosalie's mother," who she says is "just on holiday, for some time."

> [!secret] Her part in the ritual
> revealed:: false
> The ritual was [[Malrik Greene]]'s. Talula placing the [[Embers of Sacrifice|Ember]] inside the Bridge King was its final piece. The gemstone "was delivered to who it was meant to always find its way to."

> [!info] DM Note
> Open: what she wanted, what she meant about the artifact, where she went after Session 21, and which Enclave adviser was at Rosalie's birth (Gormar?).
