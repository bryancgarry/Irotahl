# The Volkarr

> [!summary] At a Glance
> The ancient Goliath shamans of the monastery of Vol'undar in [[Drak'mor]]. They choose warriors for the Void Trial in [[The Rikk Borgar]].

> [!secret] Unwitting servants
> revealed:: false
> For ages, the trial they guard has sorted Goliath warriors for [[Malcanthet]] without the Goliaths knowing.
