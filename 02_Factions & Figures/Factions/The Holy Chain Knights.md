# The Holy Chain Knights

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A militant holy order of [[The Pale Goddess]], sworn to hunt and bind the corruption of the deep. Chains are their weapon and their vow. Led by the Chain Lords. Inspired by Berserk's Holy Iron Chain Knights.

## Branches
- **The Holy Gold Chain Knights.** Role open.
- **The Holy Silver Chain Knights.** Active in [[Hollowmere]], headquartered at the Silver Keep.
- **The Holy Bronze Chain Knights.** Role open.

## Ranks
Chain Lords, Imperator, Vice-Imperator, knights, squires.
