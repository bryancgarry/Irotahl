# The Silver Lyre

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A network of bards, similar to the Harpers.

> [!info] DM Note
> Everything else is open.

## Played
- **Ino Silvertone**, Aalyn's rival and "the biggest nepo baby of the Silver Lyre," was humiliated by Aalyn and Wayne at the Orchid in [[Session 10 - The Orchid]]. He was due to play the Valehearts' the next day.
- In [[Session 12 - The Establishment is Corrupt]], Ino's mother paid Commander Seth 200 gold to keep Ino's name out of Angelique Firestorm's death. Ino's report to the guards named Bog, Aalyn and Wayne.
- In [[Session 22 - A Long Night Ends]] Ino's mother, an elderly silver-haired healer, stabilized Prince Darken but wouldn't try for Rosalie: "That one is beyond saving, my dear. And frankly, the city is better off without her."
