# House Fenwyn

> [!summary] At a Glance
> One of the Lesser Houses of the Septarion in Calenthia, [[Syfaeril]]. An old, wealthy elven house known for brilliant mages. Home of [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]] and [[Fenril Fenwyn|Fenril]].

## What the World Believes
A respected Lesser House with a long line of great mages, among them Thalanir Fenwyn, who walked through battlefields unseen in the early elven wars and left only echoes of himself to be struck down.

## Notable Members
- **[[Alaric Fenwyn]].** Prodigy, illusionist, runaway.
- **[[Fenril Fenwyn]].** Alaric's older brother, a powerful warrior-mage.
- **Thalanir Fenwyn.** Alaric's grandfather, deceased. A great mage and a veteran of the elven wars. Alaric wears his cloak, the Cloak of Thalanir's Echo.

> [!secret] The origin myth
> revealed:: false
> The Fenwyn descend from [[Fenmarel]], the outcast lord of [[Arborea]] and Lolth's lover before her fall. It was so long ago that the line can barely be traced, so even inside the family it survives as an origin myth more than a fact.
> After [[The Sundering of Planes]], Arborea wouldn't take Fenmarel's outcasts back, and they settled in Syfaeril. That may be why the Fenwyn are a Lesser House: tolerated, never trusted.

> [!secret] The Limbo vault
> revealed:: false
> The Fenwyn have a magical vault in their home city that opens onto a pocket of [[Limbo]]. It's a storage space with no fixed size or shape, like the hidden-inventory spaces in Jujutsu Kaisen.
> **Only the Head of House is told** what the vault really is and where the family came from.
