# The Obsidian Enclave

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A secretive order of powerful mages based in Runestone. They send magical advisors to the courts of Midland to counsel local rulers. Some see them as guardians of dangerous knowledge, others as manipulators hoarding power.

**Symbol:** a black square within a black ring.

## History
At the Fourth [[Moon Darkenings|Moon Darkening]], Enclave mages helped seal [[Durnhollow]], and several died doing it.
