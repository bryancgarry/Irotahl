# The Thieves Guild

> [!summary] At a Glance
> The guild [[Jackmerius Tacktheratrix|Jack]] brought [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]] into. Connected figures include Silas Holloway and The Gilded Face.

> [!info] DM Note
> Open: its name, and its role. Also open: the Merchant Alliance, seeded in Durnhollow's Guild Hall and Gold Vault.

## Played
- A safe house under **the Orchid** in [[Oakhollow]]'s Craftsman's Row: a hidden bar, a fighting pit with betting, and Silas Holloway's office. Weapons get checked at the door. See [[Session 10 - The Orchid]].
- **Silas Holloway** knows Jack by legend. Rats have cut off the guild's runner routes in the sewers, and Tiberius blames the guild for the keep attack. Silas will name the runner who did a job for the Obsidian Enclave (something taken from forest ruins) if the party clears the rats.
- **The rat job** ([[Session 12 - The Establishment is Corrupt]] to [[Session 14 - A Little Bit of Everything]]). The party cleared the wererats and killed the Rat Lord. Silas paid 50 gp and named the runner: **Calder**, a gambler who watches the fighting pits.
- Wayne let the Western Gate guard know about the bar, and Silas now considers the safe house compromised.
- Fresh wooden tokens are bought with blood: a cut on the hand for each one.
- A bandit's challenge in the sewers: "Did the tunnels treat you kindly?"
