# The Fire Sages of Vistro

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Fire salamanders who have held a ring of living fire around [[The Hollow Reliquary]], in the lava lake beyond Durnhollow, for an age. Ruled by Salamander King [[Vistro IV]].

## History
Their ancestors laid siege to the Reliquary on the [[Elemental Plane of Fire]] under the war-sage Vistro, because the Vessel had stilled the Ashen Reach and whole salamander nations. When the Sundering broke the world, they were dragged into Durnhollow with it. They hate the Kyn'Ruthar.

## With the Party
- Wayne claimed the title Kyn'Ruthar to them, and Thimble revealed the Tome of Malcanthet. It ended in a fight, then peace.
- Vistro and the sages marked Thimble and Wayne with summoning tattoos, and enhanced Aalyn's stone battle-axe in the fire enchantment chamber.
- Vistro asked the party to rid the material plane of the Reliquary.

> [!secret] What the ring holds in
> revealed:: false
> The ring truly holds in the [[The Void Heart|Void Heart]] fragment at the Seat. If Wayne takes it, Vistro will feel it.
