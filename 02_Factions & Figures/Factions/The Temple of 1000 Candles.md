# The Temple of 1000 Candles

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A public healing temple in [[Hollowmere]] with a beloved reputation.

> [!secret] Baphomet's inner circle
> revealed:: false
> Its inner circle secretly pursues lycanthropic transformation tied to [[Baphomet]]'s rites. They are conducting a five-anchor-point, city-wide lycanthropy ritual using the Spool of Lolth's Thread, a spindle of black wood wrapped in thread visible only in moonlight, held at the Temple.

> [!secret] The body trade
> revealed:: false
> The Temple has a body-supply arrangement with [[Vashara]] through V's Lounge.

> [!secret] Its real headquarters
> revealed:: false
> Possibly Sowengrad, in [[The Golden Republic]].
