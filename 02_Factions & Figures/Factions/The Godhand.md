# The Godhand

> [!summary] At a Glance
> DM-only entry. The five obyriths of [[The Sundering of Planes]], reborn in mortal hosts. The central antagonists of Campaign 1.

> [!secret] Who they are
> revealed:: false
> Facing defeat in the Sundering, the five greatest obyriths sealed their essences into the most powerful [[Embers of Sacrifice]] as a failsafe. Each awoke in a mortal or near-mortal host whose fate matched it. Each is part obyrith essence and part the being they were before, a lesser reincarnation of what they once were (the Bridge King goblin is still inside Graz'zt). Each rebirth came with a [[Moon Darkenings|Moon Darkening]].

> [!secret] The Second Sundering
> revealed:: false
> With Graz'zt's rise at the Fifth Darkening, all five seats are filled, and the Godhand can unite the Abyss. See [[The Second Sundering]].

> [!secret] They serve Tharizdun unwittingly
> revealed:: false
> They guard [[The Heart of Entropy]] with reverence and terror and believe they are the Mandate's rightful instruments. They are Tharizdun's.
