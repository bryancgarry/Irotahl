# The Soul Armor

**Re-flavor of:** TBD
**Rarity:** Artifact (mythic endgame item)

> [!secret] What it is
> revealed:: false
> A mythic item that holds the souls, or echoes, of past Kyn'Ruthar (see [[The First Hollow Vessel]]). Its soul magic is powerful enough to break an [[Abyssal Brands|Abyssal Brand]]. It is [[Wayne Kerr]]'s endgame item and his way to break his brand.

> [!secret] Two halves, like the Kingsoul
> revealed:: false
> The Soul Armor works like the Kingsoul charm in *Hollow Knight*: it comes in two halves that must be brought together.
> - **The first half** lies at the bottom of [[The Rikk Borgar]]. [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]] found it during his Void Trial and has no memory of it. The full memory comes back in [[Session 55]].
> - **The second half** is found by walking the **Tyrn'Haka**, the Endless Climb. The climb is the Kyn'Ruthar's journey, and the second half is earned by embodying it.
> - Combined, the halves become the Soul Armor.

> [!info] DM Note
> Open: whether Wayne carried the first half out of the Rikk Borgar or left it below, and where the Tyrn'Haka ends. An earlier version placed the armor atop [[Silath Shal]]; that is no longer settled.
