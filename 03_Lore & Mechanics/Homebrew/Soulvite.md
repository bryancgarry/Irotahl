# Soulvite

Silver infused with raw elemental energy, refined in [[Durnhollow]]'s foundries. Silver aligns with order and the moon, and Durnhollow's process fused it with ley line chaos energy drawn from [[The Runelands]].

## History
Its forerunner is the eladrin silver blades of the Sundering: poured, and while the metal was still molten, the warrior sang part of themselves into it, so the blade remembered who held it. See [[Arborea]].

> [!secret] Vashara's foundry
> revealed:: false
> [[Vashara]] poured raw elemental energy into silver to make Soulvite.
