# Rod of (Res)Erection

**Re-flavor of:** Rod of Resurrection (weakened)
**Rarity:** Very Rare
**Holder:** [[Aalyn Ravaceran]]

## Flavor
Aalyn's spent [[Rod of Resurrection]], weakened, and christened by [[Papa Glabb]].

> [!info] DM Note
> - This replaces Aalyn's spent Rod of Resurrection. Start it at whatever charge count fits the story. I'd say 1 charge, since it's "spent for now."
> - The official rod requires a Cleric, Druid, or Paladin. Letting a Bard attune is the trade-off for the weaker spells, and it fits a Papa Glabb item. Aalyn's attunement slots are full (Stormsong, Winged Boots, harp), so he'd have to swap one out to use it.

## D&D Beyond Text
Paste-ready text for D&D Beyond.

A rod of polished white ash capped with a gold knob, christened by Papa Glabb in a moment nobody involved will discuss. It was once a true [magicitem]Rod of Resurrection[/magicitem]. Most of that power has been spent, and what's left is temperamental. Whenever a creature within 30 feet of it is at 0 Hit Points, the rod springs bolt upright in its holder's hand, quivering with urgency.

Spells. While holding the rod, you can expend charges to cast one of the following spells from it: [spell]Revivify[/spell] (1 charge) or [spell]Raise Dead[/spell] (3 charges).

Regaining Charges. The rod regains 1 expended charge daily at dawn. If you expend the rod's last charge, roll 1d20. On a 1, the rod goes limp. It loses all its properties until someone plays it a song it likes, at the DM's discretion, after which it regains 1 charge and stands proudly once more.
