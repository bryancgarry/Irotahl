# Shell of the Void Heart

**Re-flavor of:** Homebrew
**Rarity:** Very Rare
**Holder:** [[Wayne Kerr]]

## Flavor
The first fragment of [[The Void Heart]], taken from the summit of [[The Hollow Reliquary]] in [[Session 54 - The Heart of the Void|Session 54]]. Wayne tore it out of the Remnant's chest, which cut the stitch that held the Reliquary in the material plane.

> [!info] DM Note
> - Wayne starts at 0 Hollowing. Jack's memory kept the void from taking its first point. *(Played mid-fight in Session 54 as the mountaintop training, not the squire scene.)*
> - Wayne's attunement slots were full when he took it. Decide what it displaces.
> - DC 15 is fixed because DDB can't scale an item off proficiency bonus or Dexterity.
> - For anyone not marked by Malcanthet, the Shell is an inert, faintly warm stone with no properties and no curse.

## D&D Beyond Text
Paste-ready text for D&D Beyond.

The broken shell of a giant's pendant, grey stone worn smooth by a climber's hand, its setting cracked wide open. Inside, a bead of black void moves on its own, rising into tiny spikes whenever someone nearby thinks too hard.

Shade Step. As a Bonus Action, you can expend 1 charge to dissolve into living shadow and move up to your Speed in a straight line. During this move, you can pass through other creatures' spaces, and you don't provoke Opportunity Attacks. Each creature whose space you pass through must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 2d6 Necrotic damage. If the Shell has no charges left, you can still use Shade Step, but each use gives you 1 Hollowing.

Kin of the Void. The Sea of Void doesn't bristle at you, and hollowed creatures hesitate before striking you. The first attack each hollowed creature makes against you in a combat has Disadvantage.

Curse. Once you attune to the Shell, you can't remove it or end your attunement, and [spell]Remove Curse[/spell] has no effect on it. Only breaking Malcanthet's claim on you ends the curse.

Hollowing. Each point of Hollowing costs you a memory of the DM's choosing. Hollowing doesn't go away when you finish a rest.
- At 3 Hollowing, Malcanthet can whisper to you through the Shell at will. Her words are suggestions only. You can always refuse.
- At 5 Hollowing, your mind is hollowed. Once per long rest, Malcanthet can cast [spell]Command[/spell] on you through the Shell, and you automatically fail the saving throw.
