# Shell of the Void Heart

**Re-flavor of:** Homebrew
**Rarity:** Very Rare
**Holder:** [[Wayne Kerr]]

## Flavor
The first fragment of [[The Void Heart]], taken from the summit of [[The Hollow Reliquary]] in [[Session 54 - The Heart of the Void|Session 54]]. Wayne tore it out of the Remnant's chest, which cut the stitch that held the Reliquary in the material plane.

> [!info] DM Note
> - **Session 55:** Malcanthet offers to implant it. **If Wayne accepts**, she presses it into his chest, it uses no attunement slot, and the text below applies. This begins [[Become the Hollow Vessel]]. **If he refuses**, the Shell is completely dormant: a grey broken pendant with no properties, no curse, and no Hollowing, until he accepts.
> - Wayne starts at 0 Hollowing. Jack's memory kept the void from taking its first point.
> - **Kin of the Void** is held back for now. It unlocks as a reward in the questline (Stage 2). Its text is kept below for later.
> - For anyone not marked by Malcanthet, the Shell is an inert, faintly warm stone with no properties and no curse.

## D&D Beyond Text
Paste-ready text for D&D Beyond.

The broken shell of a giant's pendant, grey stone worn smooth by a climber's hand, now sunk into your chest. Beneath the stone of your skin, a shape of black void turns slowly, and beats like a second heart that isn't yours.

Implanted. The Shell is part of your body. It doesn't require attunement and can't be removed.

Shade Step. As a Bonus Action, you can expend 1 charge to dissolve into living shadow and teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space you can see or know is there. You pass through creatures and any amount of solid material along the way. If you have no charges left, you can still use Shade Step, but each use gives you 1 Hollowing.

Curse. Remove Curse has no effect on the Shell. Only breaking Malcanthet's claim on you ends the curse.

Hollowing. Each point of Hollowing costs you a memory of the DM's choosing. Hollowing doesn't go away when you finish a rest.
- At 3 Hollowing, Malcanthet can whisper to you through the Shell at will. Her words are suggestions only. You can always refuse.
- At 5 Hollowing, your mind is hollowed. Once per long rest, Malcanthet can cast Command on you through the Shell, and you automatically fail the saving throw.
