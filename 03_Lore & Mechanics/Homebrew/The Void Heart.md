# The Void Heart

**Re-flavor of:** Homebrew, inspired by Hollow Knight's Kingsoul and Void Heart

> [!secret] What it was
> revealed:: false
> A pendant Othea gave her son Naki-Uthai, which he wore on every climb. [[Malcanthet]] broke it open, filled it with [[The Sea of Void]], and hung it back around his neck. It kept his mind hollow and answering to her: his own mother's gift, turned into his leash. See [[The First Hollow Vessel]].

## What It Is Now
It lies in pieces, scattered. [[The Hollow Reliquary]] kept one fragment as a holy relic at the Seat, and the void leaking from it is the source of the Reliquary's corruption. The fire salamanders' ring of fire truly exists to hold it in, since breaking it would spill its void into the world.

## For a Would-Be Vessel
- Each fragment grants void powers (Shade Step, Kin of the Void) at the price of a cursed bond and a Hollowing track.
- At 3 Hollowing, Malcanthet can whisper suggestions through it. At 5, she can command its bearer.
- The complete Void Heart would turn its bearer's blades to void and let Malcanthet reach them freely.

> The first fragment is [[Shell of the Void Heart]] (Session 54). Open: where the other fragments are, and what each adds.
