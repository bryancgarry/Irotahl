# The Deck of Many Things

**Re-flavor of:** Deck of Many Things (official, Dungeon Master's Guide)
**Rarity:** Legendary
**Holder:** Unknown

## Flavor
A deck that deals fate. Each card is chance made solid, and drawing one can remake a life.

> [!secret] Made in Limbo
> revealed:: false
> [[Fenmarel]] made the Deck in his exile in [[Limbo]], turning raw chaos into chance: fate as a card game, played by the god of outcasts.

> [!secret] Alaric's deck
> revealed:: false
> The Deck is part of [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]]'s long-term story. His card magic already sets it up: the Card Sharp's Deck, the Deck of Illusions, and *Spray of Cards*. One of its cards could be the way into Limbo, or the way to call a wolf of Limbo.

> [!info] DM Note
> Open: where the Deck is now (the [[House Fenwyn]] Limbo vault?), and how Alaric learns of it.
