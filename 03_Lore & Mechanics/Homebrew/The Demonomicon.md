# The Demonomicon

**Re-flavor of:** Demonomicon of Iggwilv (the full work, once assembled)
**Rarity:** Artifact

## What the World Believes
A legendary book of demon names, said to have been split into five volumes.

## Origin
During [[The Sundering of Planes]], an ancient elven archmage of [[Arborea]] set out to fight the five obyriths. She recorded each one's true name and binding in a single great work. To keep any one power from holding all five, she split it into five volumes. When the obyriths sealed themselves into Embers, the volumes became the keys to [[The Godhand]].

## The Tomes
- **Each Tome** holds one Godhand's name and binding, and is a lesser version of the whole.
- **The full Demonomicon** forms when all five are collected.
- **Weapon and danger.** Each Tome holds a piece of its Godhand's own layer of [[The Abyss]], which keeps its contents current: it writes its demon down, hour by hour. That same piece gives it nefarious powers, and it slowly corrupts whoever carries it.
