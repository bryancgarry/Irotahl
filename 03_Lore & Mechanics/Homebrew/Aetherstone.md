# Aetherstone

A many-colored stone mined only beneath [[Glimmerstone]].

## History
The oldest eladrin blades of [[The Sundering of Planes]] were not poured at all, but cut from Aetherstone, from a mountain in the far west that wasn't really a mountain.

> [!secret] Primordial stone
> revealed:: false
> Pure Aetherstone comes from the calcified body of [[The Chaos Primordial]].
