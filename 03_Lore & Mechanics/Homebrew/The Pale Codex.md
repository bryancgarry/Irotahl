# The Pale Codex

**Re-flavor of:** Tome of Clear Thought (weakened), plus spells
**Rarity:** Rare
**Holder:** [[Alaric Fenwyn]]

## Flavor
A pre-Calamity text on the passage of souls, written in service of [[The Pale Goddess]]. Found in the restricted vault of the Celestial Archives. It holds the passage on the Abyssal Lien.

> [!info] DM Note
> - I couldn't find the original text of the Moonlit Purification spell in our past chats, so I rebuilt it on top of Remove Curse. If you have the original, send it and I'll swap it in.
> - It has no attunement because Alaric's three slots are full.
> - The Intelligence bump is a modifier DDB applies the moment the item is equipped, so leave it off until the 30 days are done, or have Trevor add the +1 by hand.

## D&D Beyond Text
Paste-ready text for D&D Beyond.

[FOCUS] A slim book bound in pale grey vellum that is always faintly cool to the touch. Its pages look blank by daylight. By moonlight or silver candlelight, script surfaces on them in silver ink, slowly, like something rising from deep water. It is a pre-Calamity text on the passage of souls, written by a scholar in the service of the Pale Goddess, and parts of it were clearly written in a hurry.

Spellcasting Focus. A Wizard can use the codex as a spellcasting focus.

Spells. While holding the codex, you can expend charges to cast one of the following spells from it (save DC 15): [spell]Protection from Evil and Good[/spell] (1 charge), [spell]Moonbeam[/spell] (2 charges), or [spell]Remove Curse[/spell] (3 charges). When cast from the codex, Remove Curse is called Moonlit Purification: it can be cast only under moonlight or silver candlelight, and it can suppress, but not end, an Abyssal Brand's effects for 1 hour.

Regaining Charges. The codex regains 1d3 expended charges daily at dawn. If it spends the entire night under a full moon, it regains all of them instead.

Study. If you spend 30 days, at least 4 hours a day, reading the codex by moonlight, your Intelligence score increases by 1, to a maximum of 21. The codex can grant this benefit to a creature only once.

The Abyssal Lien. One passage is marked in the margin with a burned symbol identical to the brands of those offered to the Abyss. It reads: "A soul that bears the Abyssal Lien does not die as others die. The Pale Goddess cannot reach it. Her lantern finds no purchase."
