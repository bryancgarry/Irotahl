# Calendar of Irotahl

A year has **13 months** and **390 days** (30 days per month).

Twelve months are named from a **seasonal prefix** and a **cycling suffix**, and the thirteenth stands alone.

| Season | -mark | -tide | -break |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Frost | Frostmark | Frosttide | Frostbreak |
| Thorn | Thornmark | Thorntide | Thornbreak |
| Ember | Embermark | Embertide | Emberbreak |
| Gale | Galemark | Galetide | Galebreak |

**Darkfall** is the standalone thirteenth month.

> [!info] DM Note
> Check the month order and season mapping (Thorn as spring, Ember as summer, and so on) against your master doc. I inferred the table layout from the naming scheme.
