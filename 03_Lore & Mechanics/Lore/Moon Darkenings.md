# Moon Darkenings

## What the World Believes
Eclipses in which [[The Moon]]'s light fails. There have been five in recorded history, each remembered as an age of disaster. The Fifth was recent.

## What Happens During One
Order weakens, and the barrier between [[The Abyss]], the material plane, and the Astral Sea thins. [[The Godhand]] can briefly manifest, apostles grow stronger, and the Abyss's influence surges.

> [!secret] Each Darkening is a Godhand rebirth
> revealed:: false
> Darkenings happen only at a Godhand's rebirth. No lesser ascension can open such a gap between the planes.
