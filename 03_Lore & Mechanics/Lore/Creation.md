# Creation

## What the World Believes
Two great powers made the world and died making it. The myth survives in many forms, and none of them agree on details. Some dragon-revering peoples tell of a third, older power that watched it happen, but few take them seriously.

## What Actually Happened
There are three primordials: **Chaos**, **Order**, and **Time**.

Before the world, [[The Time Primordial|Time]] already was. Order and Chaos were two forces pressed against each other, and without Time their meeting would have been a single frozen instant. Time is what turned that instant into a sequence, one thing after another. That sequence is causality (see [[Laws of Causality]]).

[[The Order Primordial]] and [[The Chaos Primordial]] clashed. Raw chaos, shaped by causality, ignited the world of Irotahl into being, and the planes crystallized outward from it. In the clash, each primordial was struck by the other's power:
- **Chaos, struck by order, calcified.** Its stilled soul hangs in the sky as [[The Moon]]. A fragment of its body broke off and fell to the land, and the gnomes of [[Glimmerstone]] later carved their city into it. Body and soul are the same being in two places.
- **Order, struck by chaos, suffered the mirror fate.** Where Chaos froze, Order came apart. What became of it is unknown.

Chaos and Order are gone. Time is not.

> [!secret] What became of Order
> revealed:: false
> Order was shattered and scattered. Where its pieces went, and whether the lawful planes wove [[The Lattice of Heaven]] partly to gather it back together, is Campaign 2 material.

> [!secret] The children of Chaos
> revealed:: false
> The raw chaos spilled in the clash gave rise to beings of pure chaos. The greatest of them were the **obyriths**, the Five who would become [[The Godhand]]. [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], shaped from the Glimmerstone fragment, is made of the same stuff.

> [!info] DM Note
> Retired: the Eternal Void as a separate entity older than creation. Its role as the end of all things now belongs to [[The Eternal Void|the Devourer]], tied to Time.
