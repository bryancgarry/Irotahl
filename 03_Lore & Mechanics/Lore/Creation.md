# Creation

## What the World Believes
Two great powers made the world and died making it. The myth survives in many forms, and none of them agree on details.

## What Actually Happened
Before the world existed there was only the Aether, infinite and still. Within it dwelt [[The Eternal Void]], whose only purpose was to keep the Aether unformed.

Then [[The Order Primordial]] and [[The Chaos Primordial]] collided. Raw chaos, shaped by causality, ignited the world of Irotahl into being, and the planes crystallized outward from it.

The Eternal Void stirred. To it, reality was an error to be corrected. The two Primordials, already dying from the effort of creation, sealed the Void back into the deep Aether. Both were extinguished.

> [!secret] Their bodies remain
> revealed:: false
> The Chaos Primordial lies calcified beneath [[Glimmerstone]]. The Order Primordial's body is [[The Moon]].
