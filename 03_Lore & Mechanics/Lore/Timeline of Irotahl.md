# Timeline of Irotahl

1. **[[Creation]].** The Order and Chaos Primordials collide and make Irotahl. Together they seal [[The Eternal Void]] away and die in the effort.
2. **[[The Lattice of Heaven]].** The lawful planes, led by Mount Celestia and the Nine Hells, weave the Lattice, binding the material world into order. Its nodes in the world are the [[Lithoweaves]].
3. **The Shard.** A sliver of the Void slips into the world and corrupts [[The Heart of Entropy|Tharizdun]] into chaos incarnate. [[The Abyss]] grows around him.
4. **[[The Shattering of Spheres]].** Tharizdun shatters the Lattice. Heaven and Hell chain him at the bottom of the Abyss. Then every lawful outer plane cuts itself away, leaving only the inner planes and the chaotic outer planes bound to the world.
5. **[[The Sundering of Planes]].** With the jailers gone, the obyriths invade [[Ysgard]], [[Arborea]], the Beastlands, and the material plane. The giants of Ysgard are the heart of the war. [[Malcanthet]]'s [[The First Hollow Vessel|First Hollow Vessel]] is turned against his own kin, then turns on his makers. The war ends in a costly near-stalemate at the site of [[Glimmerstone]], and the obyriths seal themselves into [[Embers of Sacrifice|Embers]].
6. **The rebirths.** One by one, the obyriths awaken in mortal hosts. Each rebirth brings a [[Moon Darkenings|Moon Darkening]]. [[Lolth]] is the first. [[Durnhollow]] falls at the Fourth, about 1,000 years ago. [[Graz'zt]], at the Fifth, is the last.
7. **The present age.** All five of [[The Godhand]] have been reborn. [[The Second Sundering]] is beginning.

> [!info] DM Note
> Steps 2 through 4 and the cause of each Darkening are hidden from players. In-world, scholars know only of a "Shattering" that broke the heavens, a "Sundering" war against the demons of the deep, and five Darkenings remembered as ages of disaster.
