# Timeline of Irotahl

1. **[[Creation]].** [[The Time Primordial|Time]] already exists. The Order and Chaos Primordials clash and make Irotahl, and Time turns their meeting into causality. Chaos calcifies: its soul becomes [[The Moon]], and a fragment of its body falls to the land as [[Glimmerstone]]. Order is shattered and scattered. Beings of pure chaos rise from the spilled chaos, the greatest of them the five obyriths.
2. **[[The Lattice of Heaven]].** The lawful planes, led by Mount Celestia and the Nine Hells, weave the Lattice, binding the material world into order. Its nodes in the world are the [[Lithoweaves]].
3. **The Shard.** A fragment, perhaps from the end of Time itself ([[The Eternal Void|the Devourer]]), falls into the world and corrupts [[The Heart of Entropy|Tharizdun]]. [[The Abyss]] grows around him.
4. **[[The Shattering of Spheres]].** Tharizdun shatters the Lattice. Heaven and Hell chain him at the bottom of the Abyss. Then every lawful outer plane cuts itself away, leaving only the inner planes and the chaotic outer planes bound to the world.
5. **[[The Sundering of Planes]].** With the jailers gone, the obyriths invade [[Ysgard]], [[Arborea]], the Beastlands, the material plane, and the [[Elemental Plane of Fire]]. The giants of Ysgard are the heart of the defense. [[Malcanthet]]'s [[The First Hollow Vessel|First Hollow Vessel]] is turned against his own kin, then turns on his makers. The war ends at the site of Glimmerstone, the pathways between the planes are torn apart, and the obyriths seal their souls into the five great [[Embers of Sacrifice|Embers]]. It ends **about 5,000 years ago**.
6. **The rebirths.** Roughly every 1,000 years an obyrith soul takes flesh again, and the moon darkens. See [[Moon Darkenings]].
   - **First, about 4,000 years ago:** [[Lolth]], about 1,000 years after the Sundering ended.
   - **Second, about 3,000 years ago:** [[Baphomet]].
   - **Third, about 2,000 years ago:** [[Malcanthet]].
   - **Fourth, about 1,000 years ago:** [[Orcus]]. [[Durnhollow]] falls not long after, and the Gor'Drak Dominion begins to rise.
   - **Fifth, recent:** [[Graz'zt]].
7. **The present age.** All five of [[The Godhand]] have been reborn. [[The Second Sundering]] is beginning.

> [!info] DM Note
> Steps 1 through 5 and the cause of each Darkening are hidden from players. In-world, scholars know only of a "Shattering" that broke the heavens, a "Sundering" war against the demons of the deep, and five Darkenings remembered as ages of disaster. The roughly 1,000-year spacing means no elf lives through two Darkenings, so each is living memory for one generation and legend after.
> **Open:** how exactly the pathways between the planes were sundered at the end of the war. It's more complicated than one side doing it. To be worked out in its own discussion.
