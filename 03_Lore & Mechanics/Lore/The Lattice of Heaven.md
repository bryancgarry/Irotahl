# The Lattice of Heaven

## What the World Believes
Nothing. The Lattice is entirely forgotten.

> [!secret] The binding of the world
> revealed:: false
> After [[Creation]], the lawful outer planes, led by Mount Celestia and the Nine Hells (see [[The Lost Heavens]]), wove the Lattice of Heaven: a vast structure of order that bound the material world into their design. Through it, celestials, devils, and the other children of order walked the world. They were rare and powerful, but real.
>
> The Lattice reached the world through nodes, the machines now called [[Lithoweaves]]. Each node drew chaos out of the world and turned it into stillness, sending it along the Lattice's strands to the order planes.

## Connections
- Shattered in [[The Shattering of Spheres]].
- Its remnants chain [[The Heart of Entropy|Tharizdun]].
- Reconnecting its strands is part of Campaign 1's finale. See [[The Second Sundering]].
