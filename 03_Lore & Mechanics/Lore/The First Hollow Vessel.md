# The First Hollow Vessel

## What the World Believes
**Kyn'Ruthar**, Goliath for "void walker," is the name that survived in stories: a destroyer from the age of the Sundering, named by his victims. The fire salamanders hate him. Separately, the Goliaths honor **Naki-Uthai, the Brave Climber**, as a god of bravery and the Endless Climb.

Among the Goliaths, Kyn'Ruthar is also the title earned by those who survive the Void Trial in [[The Rikk Borgar]].

> [!info] DM Note
> "Hollow Vessel" is used only by [[Malcanthet]] and those with direct ties to the Abyss and the Godhand.

> [!secret] Naki-Uthai was real
> revealed:: false
> The Vessel was **Naki-Uthai**, son of Annam All-Father and Othea, the youngest and brightest of the giant princes of [[Ysgard]], called the Brave Climber. During [[The Sundering of Planes]], his brother Memnor, god of pride and control, bargained with Malcanthet for power, and the price was Naki-Uthai, delivered to her as a gift. After what he became, Annam struck his name from the Ordning in grief and shame, and Memnor's part was buried with it. The Goliaths kept only the name, with no idea he was ever real, ever a prince of giants, or ever the Vessel.

> [!secret] How he was hollowed
> revealed:: false
> Malcanthet hollowed him in [[The Soul Sanctum]]: his body stilled into stone, his mind pierced hollow with needles of void, his soul made dark in [[The Sea of Void]]. She broke open the pendant his mother Othea had given him, filled it with void, and hung it back around his neck to keep his mind hollow and answering to her. See [[The Void Heart]].

> [!secret] The champion
> revealed:: false
> Filled with order stolen from [[The Lattice of Heaven]], he became the Abyss's champion: a living weapon that drew the chaos out of Ysgard and turned its fields grey. On the Shield-Halls, giants knelt to the prince they remembered, and he drank them as they knelt. He also stilled the Ashen Reach and whole salamander nations. He was enthroned between battles in [[The Hollow Reliquary]].

> [!secret] The turn
> revealed:: false
> The giant souls inside him remembered his name, the one Malcanthet had taken. Enough of his own people in him, and he came back to himself. He turned on his makers and drained the obyriths in turn. His betrayal was central to the obyriths' collapse.

> [!secret] His true name at the table
> revealed:: false
> Reveal the name Naki-Uthai only if Wayne rolls a natural 20 on a very specific check or does something incredible. Otherwise, save it for later.

## The Ideal
A true Kyn'Ruthar at full strength is a warrior refined in body, mind, and soul. See the two paths in [[Hollowing and Petrification]].
