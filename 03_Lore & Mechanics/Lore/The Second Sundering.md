# The Second Sundering

## What the World Believes
Nothing yet. To the world it will look like war, revolution, and disaster.

> [!secret] The invasion resumes
> revealed:: false
> With all five of [[The Godhand]] reborn, [[The Abyss]] can unite. Its demons are returning to the material plane to continue the invasion [[The Sundering of Planes|the first Sundering]] never finished.

> [!secret] It begins in Glimmerstone
> revealed:: false
> [[Glimmerstone]], built into [[The Chaos Primordial]]'s body above the first Sundering's final battlefield, is where it starts. The city's politics provide the cover: a revolution between arcane wielders and those without magic, the World Games, and an event involving the Luminarch. Details are deferred to that arc.

> [!secret] The twist
> revealed:: false
> The Godhand were never the top of the hierarchy. Every lithoweave they woke, and every step of their invasion, fed and loosened [[The Heart of Entropy|Tharizdun]]. Reveal near the end of the campaign.

## The Finale (Campaign 1)
- The Godhand fall, or at least some of them.
- The party reconnects [[The Lattice of Heaven]]'s strands, so drained chaos flows outward to the order planes instead of down into Tharizdun. The prison holds, and the way opens for the lawful planes to return, setting up Campaign 2.
- A final confrontation with Tharizdun.
