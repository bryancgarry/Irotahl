# Laws of Causality

## What the World Believes
Causality is the principle of cause and effect upon which all reality in Irotahl operates. It is morally neutral, cosmic, and impersonal. It is not destiny, which implies the predetermined, and not karma, which implies moral accounting.

Scholars and cultists speak of the Mandate: causality bent into predetermined grooves, flowing from [[The Heart of Entropy]].

## What Actually Happened
[[The Godhand]] believe the Mandate is impersonal and that they are its instruments by right. Their foresight comes from proximity to it.

> [!secret] The Mandate is Tharizdun's will
> revealed:: false
> From his chains, [[The Heart of Entropy|Tharizdun]] presses on causality and steers events in the world toward his release. The Godhand are, without knowing it, his instruments.

## Connections
- Causality carries each of the [[Embers of Sacrifice]] to a bearer whose fate fits it.
