# The Sundering of Planes

## What the World Believes
The great war against the demons of the deep. Arborea and its eladrin, with giants, dwarves, gnomes, and others, held the line at terrible cost. The last battle was fought in the west of Midland. The war ended in a near stalemate, and the world was cut off from the wider planes.

## What Actually Happened
With the lawful planes gone and [[The Heart of Entropy|Tharizdun]] chained, the obyriths saw their opening. They invaded their chaotic neighbors, [[Ysgard]], [[Arborea]], and the Beastlands, along with the material plane, and drove a wedge into the [[Elemental Plane of Fire]].

**Ysgard and its giants were the heart of the war.** Its giants held the line against the Five. Beside them fought Arborea and its eladrin, the Beastlands, and the mortal peoples of the world.

**The end.** The final battle was fought at the site that is now [[Glimmerstone]]. The giants, the eladrin, and the mortal peoples prevailed in defending the material plane, at terrible cost.

**The aftermath.** The Sundering tore the chaotic planes and the material plane from their places in the cosmos, cutting them off from one another and from the Astral Sea. Fragments of the Abyss raised in other places were flung through space and time into the world, among them [[The Hollow Reliquary]] and [[The Soul Sanctum]].

> [!secret] The First Hollow Vessel
> revealed:: false
> [[Malcanthet]] built [[The First Hollow Vessel]] to break the giants' line: a hollowed prince of giants sent against his own kin. He later turned on his makers, and his betrayal was central to the obyriths' collapse.

> [!secret] The obyriths became the Godhand
> revealed:: false
> Facing defeat, the five greatest obyriths sealed their essences into the most powerful [[Embers of Sacrifice]] as a failsafe. They have since been reborn as [[The Godhand]].

> [!secret] The Demonomicon was written here
> revealed:: false
> An ancient elven archmage of Arborea recorded each obyrith's true name and binding, then split the work into five volumes. See [[The Demonomicon]].
