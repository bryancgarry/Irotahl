# Lithoweaves

## What the World Believes
Ancient machines of unknown origin that turn living things to stone. "Lithoweave" refers only to the machines. There is no natural network.

## Known Lithoweaves
- **The Godspire** (Tall Oak Forest, outside [[Oakhollow]]). Someone woke it, and it began turning the forest to stone. When the party stopped it, it collapsed like a black hole and vanished. The party learned very little about it.
- **[[Durnhollow]].** A lithoweave rests in the final vault room, in the chamber after the Soulvite Colossus fight on the top floor. It is not running. [[Vashara]] uses it to experiment on the hollowing process.

> [!secret] Nodes of the Lattice
> revealed:: false
> Lithoweaves are the surviving nodes of [[The Lattice of Heaven]], ancient technology of the order planes. They drew chaos out of the world and sent it outward to the order planes. Everything around an active one is drained of chaos and petrifies. They predate [[The First Hollow Vessel]].

> [!secret] Stakes for Tharizdun's chains
> revealed:: false
> The surviving lithoweaves are the stakes that hold [[The Heart of Entropy|Tharizdun]]'s chains taut from the material world. With the lawful planes gone, the chaos a running lithoweave drains has only one path: down the chains and into Tharizdun. Every lithoweave the Godhand or their agents wake for their own purposes feeds him. They do not know this.
