# Hollowing and Petrification

## What the World Believes
Stories tell of people turned to stone. Few know there is a difference between stone that seals from without and stone grown from within.

## Petrification
What order does to chaos: draining it until only stillness and stone remain. [[Lithoweaves]] petrify. [[The First Hollow Vessel]], filled with stolen Lattice power, petrified at his peak: he drew the chaos out of a battlefield and turned green fields grey.

## Hollowing
[[Malcanthet]]'s art, perfected by her hollowers in [[The Soul Sanctum]]. It is more complex than petrification and works on all three parts of a being:

| Part | What hollowing does | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body | Stilled into a shell of stone | So the vessel can't be broken |
| Mind | Pierced inward with needles of void until every word and thought is cut away | So the vessel doesn't question (the Soulknife in reverse: blades going in instead of out) |
| Soul | Drawn out, lowered into [[The Sea of Void]] until it turns dark, and set back | So the vessel is hers |

> [!secret] Vashara's wendigos
> revealed:: false
> [[Vashara]]'s crude imitation, learned from the [[The Hollow Reliquary|Reliquary]]'s stone book, handles the body but not the mind or the soul, so what comes out is pure hunger: a wendigo.

## The Two Paths
Malcanthet wants a new Hollow Vessel. Anyone she shapes toward that end could instead become a true Kyn'Ruthar.

| | Hollow Vessel | Kyn'Ruthar |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Body | Stilled into stone, an unbreakable shell | Refined, strong and alive |
| Mind | Pierced hollow, so the vessel obeys | Honed to a blade |
| Soul | Made dark, bound to the Abyss | Free, and carrying others |
