# The Shattering of Spheres

## What the World Believes
The oldest catastrophe in memory. Scholars agree something broke the structure of the heavens. They do not agree on what, or why.

> [!info] DM Note
> Background lore. The war's details are Campaign 2 material. It explains why the world is full of chaos powers and empty of order's children.

> [!secret] Tharizdun broke the Lattice
> revealed:: false
> [[The Heart of Entropy|Tharizdun]], corrupted by the Shard of Evil, shattered [[The Lattice of Heaven]] and cracked the cosmological structure of the world. Heaven and Hell, at great cost, chained him at the bottom of [[The Abyss]]. Chaos incarnate cannot be destroyed, only stilled, so they bound him with the Lattice's remnants.

> [!secret] The cosmos split along order and chaos
> revealed:: false
> With the Lattice in pieces, every lawful outer plane cut its own strands and withdrew. What stayed bound to the world were the inner planes (the elements) and the chaotic outer planes: [[Ysgard]], [[Arborea]], the Beastlands, Limbo, the Abyss, and their neighbors. Mortals had known celestials and devils before this. Within a generation the severed planes passed into myth, and within several more into silence.
