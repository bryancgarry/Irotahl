# Embers of Sacrifice

## What the World Believes
Pale stones like pearls, spoken of in whispers. Legends say they grant a wish at a terrible price.

## How They Work
Embers do not appear at random. Causality carries each one to a bearer whose fate fits it. An Ember can be invoked more than once. When a bearer invokes one, they must offer something of great worth, and the Ember weighs the bearer by causality:

| Outcome | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Too little causal weight | The bearer becomes a demon. |
| Real weight, but the wrong fate | The bearer becomes an apostle. The Ember keeps its power and moves on. |
| The matching fate (essence-bearing Embers only) | The sealed obyrith essence awakens in the bearer, who becomes one of [[The Godhand]]. The Ember is spent. |
| Those offered up | They are branded. See [[Abyssal Brands]]. |

A Godhand rebirth is the only ascension great enough to darken the moon. Demons and apostles are lesser ascensions and cause no [[Moon Darkenings|Moon Darkening]].

An Ember is not a temptation. It is a conclusion.

> [!secret] Splinters of the Shard
> revealed:: false
> Embers are splinters of the Shard of Evil (a fragment of [[The Eternal Void]]), scattered across the world when [[The Lattice of Heaven]] shattered. Abyssal scripture calls them the "seeds" the Deep weeps into the world, so that "any who would might make an offering." That is the closest any in-world source comes to their true origin.

> [!secret] The five greatest hold the obyriths
> revealed:: false
> The five greatest Embers held the sealed essences of the five obyriths from [[The Sundering of Planes]]. All five have now found their matches.

## Known Embers
- **The Oakhollow Ember.** Invoked by Baron Fern to save his daughter, which branded the party (all but Bogtrotter). Used days later in the Bridge King's ritual beneath the Grand Cathedral of [[Oakhollow]], where it found its match. See [[Graz'zt]].
- **The Reliquary Ember.** Found by [[Vashara]] inside [[The Hollow Reliquary]], and it corrupted her.
