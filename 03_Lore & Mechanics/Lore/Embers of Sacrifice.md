# Embers of Sacrifice

## What the World Believes
Pale stones like pearls, spoken of in whispers. Legends say they grant a wish at a terrible price.

## How They Work
Embers do not appear at random. Causality carries each one to a bearer whose fate fits it. An Ember can be invoked more than once. When a bearer invokes one, they must offer something of great worth, and the Ember weighs the bearer by causality:

| Outcome | Result |
| --- | --- |
| Too little causal weight | The bearer becomes a demon. |
| Real weight, but the wrong fate | The bearer becomes an apostle. The Ember keeps its power and moves on. |
| The matching fate (the five great Embers only) | The sealed obyrith soul takes flesh in the bearer, who becomes one of [[The Godhand]]. The Ember is spent. |
| Those offered up | They are branded. See [[Abyssal Brands]]. |

A Godhand rebirth is the only ascension great enough to darken the moon. Demons and apostles are lesser ascensions and cause no [[Moon Darkenings|Moon Darkening]].

An Ember is not a temptation. It is a conclusion.

> [!secret] Embers are souls
> revealed:: false
> Embers are the calcified souls of chaos-beings. The obyriths and lesser beings of chaos are children of [[The Chaos Primordial]], and when they die their souls calcify the way their parent's did, into pale stones that look like small moons. [[The Moon]] itself is the Chaos Primordial's soul.
> Invoking an Ember is a soul looking for a body. The offering, and the bearer's fate, decide what it can take: too little weight makes a demon, the wrong fate makes an apostle, and a matching fate lets an obyrith soul take flesh again. When an Ember cracks open, black void erupts and pulls those in the ritual into a fragment of [[The Abyss]].
> Abyssal scripture calls them the "seeds" the Deep weeps into the world, so that "any who would might make an offering." That is the closest any in-world source comes to their true origin.

> [!secret] The five greatest are the obyriths
> revealed:: false
> The five greatest Embers were the souls of the five obyriths, sealed at the end of [[The Sundering of Planes]]. All five have now found their matches. The lesser Embers (the Oakhollow Ember, the Reliquary Ember, the one on Malrik's charts) are souls of lesser chaos-beings killed in the war.

## Known Embers
- **The Oakhollow Ember.** Invoked by Baron Fern to save his daughter, which branded the party (all but Bogtrotter). The party clawed it out of the Baron's monstrous form, and Wayne wore it until Session 20, when Talula Fern placed it in the Bridge King's chest to complete Malrik's ritual beneath the Grand Cathedral of [[Oakhollow]], where it found its match. See [[Graz'zt]].
- **The Reliquary Ember.** Found by [[Vashara]] inside [[The Hollow Reliquary]], and it corrupted her.
