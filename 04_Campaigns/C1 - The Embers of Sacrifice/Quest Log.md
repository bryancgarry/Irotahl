# Quest Log

> [!info] How to use this note
> Quest lines the party knows about, written from **their** side: what they know, not what's true. Update it after each session recap. Secrets stay in the linked notes. The full play-by-play is in [[The Story So Far]], and longer-term planning lives in [[Story Planning]].
> Status: **Active** (being pursued), **Open** (known but not pursued yet), **Personal** (one character's story), **Resolved**.

*As of the end of Session 54. Session 55 picks up mid-collapse at the summit of the Reliquary.*

---

## Active

### Escape the Hollow Reliquary
**Status:** Active · **Where:** [[The Hollow Reliquary]]
- Wayne tore the Shell of the Void Heart out of the Remnant's chest, and the Reliquary began to come apart.
- A woman's voice spoke at Wayne's shoulder: *"Oh, how perfect you are."*
- King Atul is still on the Soul floor. As a mind with no soul, he couldn't pass the Soul trial.
- **Next:** get out, and get Atul out.

### Vistro's Request
**Status:** Active · **Given by:** [[Vistro IV]] ([[Session 50.5 - The Wayne and Thimble Episode|Session 50.5]])
- After the trial by combat and the peace, the Salamander King asked the party to rid the world of the temple in the lava lake.
- He knew Thimble's medallion as King Atul's Mind Medallion, and told them to bring it to the temple.
- He gave Wayne and Thimble summoning tattoos. [[Old Ash Tongue]] now travels with the party.
- **Next:** report back across the lava lake.

### King Atul Stonemarch
**Status:** Active · [[King Atul Stonemarch]]
- The last king of Durnhollow. His stone army stands frozen mid-charge outside the Reliquary. The Mind Medallion woke him, and he followed the party inside after his queen.
- Durnhollow's plaques say he married a drow princess, and four elementals came as the marriage gift.
- **Next:** see him out of the Reliquary, and learn what happened to him.

### The Vault of Four Doors
**Status:** Active · **Where:** [[Durnhollow]]
- Durnhollow's Confluence has four silver cradles before a sealed vault. Each elemental Warden drops a Soulvite shard. The party holds **air** (the Tempest Warden) and **water** (the Tide Warden). Fire and earth remain.
- The Omnishroom showed Wayne a vault with **bronze, silver, and gold doors, and a fourth**. The party has a **bronze key** (Session 41) and a **silver key** (Session 44). The gold key is "above the lava pits. Take the third track."
- Alaric's treasure map marks three sites in the ruins (Session 36).
- The central elevator stays dead until power comes back somewhere on the lava floor. Bog spotted a power station (Session 50).
- Bifflefapple's tip: the dwarves were researching "an enhancement for the silver of these mountains."
- **Next:** the lava floor.

### The Airship
**Status:** Active · **Contact:** [[Professor Bifflefapple]]
- Bifflefapple has an old airship found under the mountain ice. To fly, it needs power: the heart of Nerevath, the goose herself, or dwarven air compression from the ruins below the city.
- Nerevath turned out to be Salvatore Pearcetti, the pear merchant, and Pebbles' mother. He's at the workshop, and the party owes him funding for a pear order.
- Bifflefapple is close to a breakthrough on Nerevath and wants to see the party after the ruins.
- **Next:** find a dwarven air compression device in Durnhollow.

### Lolth's Spool
**Status:** Active · **Given by:** [[Lolth]], to Aalyn ([[Session 30 - Journey Through the Fog|Session 30]])
- In the fog, Lolth set Aalyn a challenge: find her Spool, hidden in Hollowmere, before "the others" do.
- Students on campus have been wearing strange new black threads (Session 40).
- **Next:** follow the thread.

### V's Lounge and the Missing
**Status:** Active · **Where:** V's Lounge, [[Hollowmere]]
- Miss V, who gave her name as **Vashara**, runs the lounge. She gave Aalyn four missing patrons: **Garret Wulloch, Oswin Tetch, [[Bastian Dray]], and Pitor Senn**. Talia brought the details.
- Aalyn signed a contract to perform there without reading it.
- Talia never sees her boss by day, and asked the party not to kill her.
- The name Vashara also turns up in Durnhollow's history, as Atul's queen. The party partly suspects they're the same woman.
- **Next:** find the missing, and find out who V really is.

### Bog's Compulsion
**Status:** Active · [[Bogtrotter]]
- Vashara took Bog under *Dominate Person*. He told her everything about the party, and she plucked scales from his back.
- If he hears **"the lounge misses you,"** he must resist or go back to V's. He knows.
- He wants to kill "that purple lady."

### Luna's Arrest
**Status:** Open · [[Luna au Vermillion]]
- After the party got into the archives' restricted section, Luna was led away in handcuffs to the Chain Lords ([[Session 39 - Escape From the Keep|Session 39]]).
- Talia heard through Selara that "the powers that be wanted her out."
- Luna had planned to take the party down to Durnhollow, where she'd tracked an arcane signature.
- Her brother, the Imperator before her, was killed by a monster too fast to see on a full-moon night. His body vanished, leaving a chain link. She wanted to use the party's brands as bait for it.

### Wanted by the Silver Knights
**Status:** Open · [[Corvin Hale]], [[The Holy Chain Knights]]
- With Luna gone, Corvin Hale took command: "Let's go grab the hooligans." The party slipped out of the keep with the Pale Codex and Papa Glabb's harp.
- **[[Dame]]** is friendly, and the only knight who drinks at the Drunken Star.

### Selara and the Baroness
**Status:** Open · [[Selara Du'Cant]], [[Elixni Vexthorne|Baroness Elixni Vexthorne]]
- Selara shook on a truce: **"Do not push the baroness. All will be revealed in time."**
- The baroness heard the truth about Oakhollow and was shaken. Orthus, on a projection, played dumb and told her, "I'm sure you will see the princess and prince again one day."
- Corvin Hale now works closely with the baroness.

### The Temple of 1000 Candles
**Status:** Open · **Where:** the Terrace, [[Hollowmere]]
- Known for healing miracles. Luna asked Thimble to wait a week before going there to heal.
- Dame lost a wererat there.
- Candles keep turning up: a patch on a bandit at the fake guard post, and on the robes of the dog party's leader at the summit.

### The Gilded Conservatory
**Status:** Open · **Where:** the summit by the Silver Keep
- [[Rai Zeph]] stays in the city to warn people away from its healing.
- Jud came out of the Feywild there, after a burning grove took him in.

## Personal

### Wayne: The Stone, the Kyn'Ruthar, and Jack
**Status:** Personal · [[Wayne Kerr]]
- Turned to stone in ancient ruins in the Drak'mor mountains, under a doorway carved with a point-down triangle and a pupil-less eye. Jack taught him to move the stone and fight. Wayne wants to cure the curse and continue Jack's journey.
- The Godhand's winged woman called him "my hollow vessel." He carries the Shell of the Void Heart, and his stone came back ebony.
- [[Rai Zeph]] knelt to him as the Kyn'Ruthar and asked him to go home: the Gor'Drak Dominion is ravaging the Goliath homelands, and the elders need their protector.
- In the Celestial Archives he found a myth of a dwarf named Jack with soulvite daggers.
- His sprout has "the power to absorb." He's practicing telekinesis from *Wizardry 101.1*.

### Aalyn: Sumina and the Thread
**Status:** Personal · [[Aalyn Ravaceran]]
- He promised to bring Sumina back. In the Soul trial he saw her soul, until Lolth dragged her back ("Insolent child").
- Lolth told him Kalisar's mother had the same sickness that took his love.
- His hair keeps getting darker.
- He holds two of Papa Glabb's instruments (the harp and the lute).

### Alaric: The Egg and the Brother
**Status:** Personal · [[Alaric Fenwyn]]
- He took an egg from the silver dragon's nest. It likes cold, humid air.
- His brother, Fenril, is out in the world. A flashback brought back the day Fenril took him to the gold dragon Aurixia.
- His parents left him his grandfather's cloak and a letter in Malrik's Solarium.
- He carries the Pale Codex.

### Thimble: The Tome and Home
**Status:** Personal · [[Thimblewick Danglesworth]]
- He carries the Tome of Malcanthet. The Godhand's winged woman called him "a chaos born," and his wild magic has turned more malicious.
- Home is calling: the world tournament in Glimmerstone, his two brothers, and the voice beneath Glimmerstone that said *"Little spark, come find me."* Bifflefapple knows his father, and has a grudge with the Danglesworths.
- He holds two rainbow eggs from the summit.

### Bog: Kin of the Silver
**Status:** Personal · [[Bogtrotter]]
- The silver dragon called him kin and woke a silver heritage in him: cold breath at will.
- The Godhand's horned one wanted to know who made a dragonborn.
- The mushroom pool supercharged him with chaos. He can wild magic surge now.
- He knows of Doran, a dwarven smith who makes armor like Tiberius's.

### The Brands
**Status:** Personal · Everyone but Bog
- Branded at the wedding in Oakhollow, from the pale stone Baron Fern used. Tiberius and Jud carry brands too.
- The brands burned at midnight on the road west. Wayne's and Thimble's darkened in the Soul trial.
- **Next:** find out what they are, and how to be rid of them.

## Loose Threads
Known to the party, but not being chased right now.
- **[[The Godhand]].** Five demon lords. Graz'zt, once the Bridge King, made Malrik his apostle. To Thimble: "I told you I would remember that."
- **[[Talula Fern]].** Rosalie's mother, chained as a demon beneath the Grand Cathedral. Last seen at the ascension. The baroness thinks she's "just on holiday."
- **[[Romulus Darken|Prince Romulus Darken]].** Lost an arm the night Rosalie died, and left Oakhollow with Tiberius.
- **[[Tiberius Blackfang]].** Branded, and he has fought the Godhand before. Wayne has a sending stone to him.
- **[[Orthus]].** The Enclave's adviser to Darkstone. Lied about Oakhollow, and seemed to know what Wayne's gemstone was.
- **Malrik's bag.** An aetherstone order to a D. Bronzebottom in Glimmerstone, charts marking another gemstone in the sea between Midland and Syfaeril, and his prophecy of rifts in the sky and demons from above and below.
- **The Bloodlancers.** The Gor'Drak Dominion's army was at Oakhollow's gates when the party left.
- **[[Jud]].** Alive, branded, and posing as "Knight Zodius" at the Silver Keep.
- **The burning grove.** It kept turning up beside the party on the road west.
- **Mr. Strong Legs' signet.** The Strong Legs family in Daggerford will pay for answers.
- **The silver refinery.** Restarted in Durnhollow with Myra as manager. Alaric wants Bifflefapple to line up buyers.
- **Devils.** Nobody in the world knows the word.
- **Purple lilacs.** Seen on Sumina's grave, in the room beneath the Grand Cathedral, and around the Reliquary.
