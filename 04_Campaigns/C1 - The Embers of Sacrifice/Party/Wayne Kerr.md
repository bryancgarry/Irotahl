# Wayne Kerr

**Player:** Kevin
**Race / Class:** Goliath Rogue (Soulknife)

> [!summary] Quick Reference
> - **Theme:** Body, Mind, and Soul.
> - **Body:** Fully stone. Malcanthet's hollowing has begun.
> - **Mind:** Sharp. Soulknife, honed by Jack. This is what keeps him himself.
> - **Soul:** Branded by the Abyss at Oakhollow, and contested.
> - **Core conflict:** Malcanthet wants him as her next Hollow Vessel. He can become that, or a true Kyn'Ruthar.
> - **Endgame item:** [[The Soul Armor]].

## Backstory (as the player knows it)
- A Goliath of [[Drak'mor]].
- Chosen for the Void Trial. He descended into [[The Rikk Borgar]], emerged, and completed it, earning the title Kyn'Ruthar. He has no memory of what happened below.
- Some time later, exploring old ruins in the Drak'mor mountains, he was turned to stone. He remembers a mark over a doorway there: a triangle, point down, with a pupil-less eye at its center.
- [[Jackmerius Tacktheratrix|Jack]], a dwarf, found him, taught him Soulknife, and brought him into [[The Thieves Guild|the thieves guild]].
- He seeks to continue Jack's journey and to cure his stone curse.
- He claimed the title Kyn'Ruthar to the fire salamanders.

## The Truth
> [!secret] Marked twice at the Rikk Borgar
> revealed:: false
> [[Malcanthet]] selected him as a future Hollow Vessel through the trial, and its mark is her tether to him. The remnant of the Kyn'Ruthar's last stand also left a spark in him. That is why he can go either way.

> [!secret] The ruins are the Soul Sanctum
> revealed:: false
> The ruins are [[The Soul Sanctum]], where [[The First Hollow Vessel]] was made. Malcanthet reached through his mark with a projection and began hollowing him there. His body was stilled into stone. The piercing of his mind never finished.

> [!secret] Why he still moves and thinks
> revealed:: false
> The Kyn'Ruthar spark refused to be stilled, and Jack taught him to drive the stone with his mind. Soulknife is literally how Wayne moves: his psychic blades are his mind pushing outward, the opposite of the needles that hollow a mind from the outside in.

> [!secret] His god was the Vessel
> revealed:: false
> Naki-Uthai, the Brave Climber, god of the Endless Climb, was the First Hollow Vessel.

> [!secret] Jack
> revealed:: false
> Jack is a very powerful figure on a quest to defeat the Godhand.

## The Stone Body
The stone isn't evil. What matters is whose will holds it. Three ways it can resolve:
1. **Defeat Malcanthet** and undo her hollowing. His flesh is restored.
2. **Complete the Kyn'Ruthar path.** His flesh is restored.
3. **Keep the stone by choice.** It grows into a strength over time, with resistances and similar benefits, opening a stone Kyn'Ruthar. Benefits are to be designed, preferably re-flavored from official features.

## Abyssal Brand
Branded at [[Oakhollow]] with the rest of the party. His way to break it is [[The Soul Armor]], which would also free him from the Void Heart's curse.
