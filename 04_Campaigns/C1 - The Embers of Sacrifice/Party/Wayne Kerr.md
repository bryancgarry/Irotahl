> [!summary] Quick Reference
> - **Theme:** Body, Mind, and Soul.
> - **Body:** Fully stone, now a deeper **ebony** since he broke out of the Remnant's Stilling (Session 54). Malcanthet's hollowing has begun.

> [!quote]- Backstory (written by Kevin)
> **Early Life: The Goliath of the Peaks**
>
> Wayne Kerr was not born into a life of subtlety or finesse. Standing at a towering 8 feet tall, with skin the color of weathered stone and muscles like the ridges of the mountains he roamed, Wayne's early years were defined by survival and strength. Born among the hardy Goliaths in the mountain peaks of ???, Wayne was a prodigy in the art of physical combat, training in the traditional ways of his people. His hands were as powerful as anvils, and his endurance matched the fury of the storms that ravaged the cliffs he called home. But as he grew, Wayne began to feel a strange unease in his soul—a sensation that his path was meant to be more than just brute strength. There was something else calling to him, a whisper in the wind, a flicker of something within his heart that could not be explained by the simple, straightforward philosophy of his clan. This inner restlessness led him to leave the mountains in search of purpose.
>
> **The Stone Curse**
>
> Wayne’s journey led him to many places: ancient ruins, forgotten temples, and long-lost forests. He encountered strange creatures and ancient forces, but none of them gave him the answers he sought. One fateful day, as he ventured deep into an ancient labyrinth, Wayne encountered a creature of terrible power—a Medusa, cursed with the ability to turn any living thing to stone with a mere glance. Though Wayne was strong and quick, the Medusa’s gaze caught him off guard, and in a flash of stony magic, his entire body turned to stone.
>
> But something was different about Wayne’s petrification. Unlike most who were turned to statues, frozen in time, Wayne’s mind remained clear. He could still think, still feel, still dream—he was simply trapped in a form that no longer bore any resemblance to his once- living body. His stony shell was unyielding and impervious to harm, but it did not diminish his consciousness or his will.
>
> For years, Wayne remained in this petrified state, an immobile statue trapped in the dark labyrinth, his thoughts wandering but his body still and lifeless. He could hear nothing, see nothing—until one day, the curse was broken.
>
> **The Meeting of Master and Apprentice**
>
> Jackmerius, a Dwarf of great renown and a master of the Soulknife arts, was the one who came across Wayne’s petrified form. Jackmerius had spent much of his life traveling and found himself transported to this new world through his journeys. He had discovered the soulknife techniques long ago, learning to harness his psychic abilities into deadly blades of pure thought and energy. Though his expertise was vast, Jackmerius had always searched for a successor—a worthy apprentice to pass on his knowledge and teachings. When Jackmerius encountered Wayne, he was initially struck by the Goliath’s stone form. It was an unusual sight, even for the wandering Dwarf, but Jackmerius sensed something within the stony figure—something alive, something untapped. He began researching the curse that had turned Wayne to stone, learning through his magical and intellectual prowess that Wayne's mind remained untouched, though his body had been transformed into an unyielding material.
>
> Jackmerius, having known many forms of psionic magic and mental disciplines, realized that Wayne’s mind was far from broken. In fact, it was potentially even more powerful than before, the stone exterior now protecting and focusing his inner strength. He also recognized that Wayne had an innate, latent psychic power that had been locked away, perhaps by the curse itself. Jackmerius, intrigued, began the delicate process of breaking the petrification, using his knowledge of psionic magic and stone to carefully reverse the curse.
>
> When Wayne awoke from his stone prison, he found himself fully restored to his original form, though with one key difference: his body was now composed of stone, a stony exterior that still bore the full measure of his Goliath strength. His appearance was no longer that of a living Goliath, but of a creature whose form was an unbreakable, gray stone—a living statue.
>
> While Wayne was disoriented at first, he quickly adapted to his new form, realizing that his mind remained sharp, his body strong, and his spirit unbroken. Jackmerius, recognizing Wayne’s potential, took him on as an apprentice, teaching him the ways of the Soulknife. Jackmerius had been a Soulknife for many years, wielding his psychic blades with precision and wisdom. The Soulknife arts were not about raw strength alone, but about the mastery of one’s mind—learning to focus one’s thoughts to conjure weapons of pure psychic energy. While Wayne had the physical strength of a Goliath, he was now learning to harness his mind as a weapon, shaping his thoughts into blades of mental energy. The discipline was difficult, but Jackmerius was patient, guiding Wayne through the necessary steps to unlock his inner power.
>
> One fine evening, after a particularly grueling training session in a small village, Jackmerius turned to Wayne with an unusually serious expression.
>
> "Wayne, I’ve been thinking," Jackmerius began, his beard twitching as he spoke. "I’m getting the itch. This beard, its grown too long. That means it’s about time I try to get out of here and find out how the hell I even got here to begin with."
>
> Wayne, still wiping stone dust from his hands, blinked at his Dwarven mentor. "You’re leaving?" Wayne asked, his stone face remaining as unreadable as ever. "Well, yeah," Jackmerius shrugged. "I’ve trained you as much as I can. I think it’s time you took your rock-solid brain and started doing some soul-stabbing on your own." Wayne, slightly puzzled by the sudden announcement, stood still for a long moment. "But... we’re a good team," he said, still getting used to the idea of having a partner who didn’t share his stony physique. "We’ve defeated goblins, bandits, and that rogue octopus in the tavern. I even learned how to keep my stone hand steady when throwing knives." Jackmerius gave a wry grin. "Aye, but you’re ready. You’ve got the psychic knives, the rock- solid confidence, and, well, I think I’ve exhausted all my personal knowledge of stone jokes. Plus, this world somehow sucks more than my own and I need to get back eventually I guess."
>
> Wayne sighed dramatically, though it wasn’t entirely clear whether he was sighing because of his emotional attachment to the Dwarf or because his stone lungs were tired. Either way, he knew Jackmerius was right. It was time for them to part ways.
>
> "Well, if you’re sure," Wayne rumbled.
>
> "I’m sure," Jackmerius replied, adjusting his belt with a proud grunt. "Don’t worry, Wayne. I’ll send you a postcard—probably just a drawing of my beard in different shapes." With that, the two shared an awkward but heartfelt farewell, and Jackmerius went off in search of his next grand adventure, leaving Wayne with his newfound abilities, his stone form, and a small bag of rocks that Jackmerius swore was a “practical gift for emergencies.”
>
> **The Wild Magic Sorcerer: Thimblewick Danglesworth**
>
> Journeying without his master, Wayne found himself in the city of ... where he had a rather fateful encounter with a gnome. Thimblewick was a wild magic sorcerer, known for causing unpredictable and sometimes disastrous magical surges with little to no warning. His magical talents, while potent, were completely beyond his control. But what Thimblewick lacked in precision, he made up for with an abundance of chaotic energy and irrepressible curiosity.
>
> Despite his small size, Thimblewick was a noble in his homeland, though a rather unconventional one. His family’s wealth was of no interest to him and Thimblewick’s true calling lays in exploring the chaotic mysteries of magic. He had no interest in the rigid constraints of nobility or diplomacy; instead, he reveled in the unpredictability of his magical experiments.
>
> When Wayne first met Thimblewick, the gnome had accidentally triggered a wild surge that turned a nearby tavern’s entire stock of ale into an army of dancing chairs. Wayne had to quickly disarm a rampaging table before it crushed anyone. In that moment, the two formed an unlikely friendship, united by the chaos they both thrived in, albeit for different reasons. While Wayne had learned to control the chaos of his own mind, Thimblewick embraced the unpredictability of his magic.
>
> The gnome's chaos was the perfect complement to Wayne’s disciplined precision. While Thimblewick’s magic could often get them into trouble, it also served as an excellent distraction—or weapon—when things got dicey. Thimblewick, in turn, came to rely on Wayne’s raw power and steady guidance. The two made an unusual but effective duo.
>
> **The Future of Wayne Kerr**
>
> Wayne Kerr, now a Soulknife Rogue with a stone body and a sharp mind, continues his journey with Thimblewick by his side. Jackmerius may have parted ways, but his teachings remain with Wayne, shaping him into a formidable force. As for Wayne's future? Who knows—maybe he’ll find a cure for his stony exterior. Or maybe he’ll embrace it fully and become the most terrifying statue in the land. One thing is for certain: with Thimblewick’s chaotic magic and Wayne’s rock-solid resolve, their adventures are bound to be just as unpredictable as ever.

## Backstory (as the player knows it)
- A Goliath of [[Drak'mor]], born in the peaks. Probably of the Kerr'Athrai, the cloud Goliaths who live closest to [[The Rikk Borgar]] (his Cloud Giant ancestry fits). A prodigy in combat who felt a restlessness his clan's ways couldn't explain.
- Chosen by [[The Volkarr]] for the Void Trial. He descended into the Rikk Borgar, emerged, and completed it, earning the title **Kyn'Ruthar**. He has no memory of what happened below.
- He left the mountains in search of purpose. Deep in an ancient labyrinth of old ruins, a creature with a petrifying gaze, a "Medusa," turned him to stone. He remembers a mark over a doorway there: a triangle, point down, with a pupil-less eye at its center. His mind stayed clear for years inside the stone.
- [[Jackmerius Tacktheratrix|Jack]], a dwarf from another world, found him, sensed the mind alive inside, and taught him to move the stone and fight as a Soulknife. He brought Wayne into [[The Thieves Guild|the thieves guild]]. Then Jack left to find out how he'd come to this world and how to get home, leaving Wayne a bag of rocks "for emergencies."
- He met [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]] when a wild magic surge turned a tavern's ale into an army of dancing chairs, and they've been a duo since.
- He seeks to continue Jack's journey and to cure his stone curse.
- He claimed the title Kyn'Ruthar to the fire salamanders. [[Vistro IV]] tasked the party with ridding the material plane of the [[The Hollow Reliquary|Hollow Reliquary]].
- Aalyn calls him "the Stone God."

## The Truth
> [!secret] The Void Trial
> revealed:: false
> The Void Trial is the Goliaths' most sacred rite: a brutal descent into the Rikk Borgar through fire and the void's temptation, survived by very few. At the bottom are two things at once:
> - **Malcanthet's harvest.** Long ago, [[Malcanthet]] corrupted the bottom of the Rikk Borgar. For ages the trial has sorted Goliath warriors for her without the Goliaths knowing. The rare stone a trial-taker brings back marks those she finds worthy.
> - **The Kyn'Ruthar's last stand.** A remnant of the turned Vessel's last stand still fights back down there, and it judges the trial-takers too.
>
> Wayne was marked by both. Malcanthet selected him as a future Hollow Vessel, and the remnant left a spark in him. That is why he can go either way.

> [!secret] Malcanthet's mark
> revealed:: false
> The mark from the trial is Malcanthet's tether: a door she can reach through, even while the Abyss is hard to cross. She can reach Wayne through it again.

> [!secret] The labyrinth was the Soul Sanctum, and the Medusa was Malcanthet
> revealed:: false
> The "ancient labyrinth" is [[The Soul Sanctum]], Malcanthet's house of making, torn loose in the Sundering and half buried in the Drak'mor mountains. It is where Naki-Uthai was hollowed into the First Vessel. Its sigil is the triangle with the pupil-less eye.
> The "Medusa" is how Wayne remembers what met him there: Malcanthet, reaching through his mark as a projection, not her true form. Its petrifying gaze was the first step of hollowing. **His body was stilled into stone. The second step, piercing his mind hollow with needles of void, never finished.**

> [!secret] Why he still moves and thinks
> revealed:: false
> The Kyn'Ruthar spark refused to be stilled, and Jack taught him to drive the stone with his mind. Soulknife is literally how Wayne moves: his psychic blades are his mind pushing outward, the opposite of the needles that hollow a mind from the outside in. His mind is the one pillar Malcanthet never reached.

> [!secret] His god was the Vessel
> revealed:: false
> The Hollow Vessel was **Naki-Uthai**, a son of Annam All-Father, given to Malcanthet by his brother [[Memnor]]. Annam struck his name from the Ordning. The Goliaths still honor him as the Brave Climber, god of the Endless Climb, with no idea he was real. Hollowed in the Soul Sanctum with his mother Othea's corrupted pendant as a leash, he was sent against [[Ysgard]] and drank his own kin's souls, until the souls inside him remembered his name and he turned on his makers.
> **Kyn'Ruthar**, "void walker," is what his victims called him, and the name that survived in Goliath stories. **The Hollow Vessel** is the name only the Abyss uses. Wayne's own people's god of the Endless Climb is the Hollow Vessel.

> [!secret] Jack
> revealed:: false
> Jack is from another world. He's a very powerful figure on a quest to defeat [[The Godhand]], and he is looking for a way home. Wayne knows Jack came from somewhere else, but not the rest.
> **Open:** how Jack crossed into a world cut off from the planes, and where he is now.

> [!secret] What the brand really does
> revealed:: false
> At death, a branded soul bypasses [[The Pale Goddess]] and is delivered intact and aware to the Abyss. Wayne doesn't know this yet.

> [!secret] He said yes
> revealed:: false
> In the Mind trial, the needle **waited for his consent**, and he let it in willingly. In the Soul trial he chose to become the blade. Both are steps toward the Vessel, taken freely.

## The Two Paths
| | Hollow Vessel | Kyn'Ruthar | Where Wayne stands |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Body | Stilled into stone | Refined, strong and alive | Stone. Leaning Vessel. |
| Mind | Pierced hollow, so the vessel obeys | Honed to a blade | Sharp. Leaning Kyn'Ruthar. The Void Heart aims here. |
| Soul | Made dark, bound to the Abyss | Free, and carrying others | Branded and darkened. The deciding fight. |

**The Hollow Vessel path:** Malcanthet finishes what the stone started, pierces his mind hollow, and collects on the brand.
**The Kyn'Ruthar path:** The Soul Armor breaks the brand and his body returns to life. His mind holds the line in between.

## The Void Heart
[[The Void Heart]] is Othea's pendant, broken open and filled with the Sea of Void: the leash that kept the First Vessel's mind hollow. It lies in fragments. The first, the **[[Shell of the Void Heart]]**, is his: in Session 54 he drove his hand into the Remnant's chest and took it. Malcanthet appeared at his shoulder the moment he held it.
- **The temptation.** Real void power: **Shade Step** (dashing through enemies as living shadow) and **Kin of the Void** (hollowed things hesitate against him).
- **The leash.** Once attuned, he can't remove it. Every use beyond his free ones costs **Hollowing**, and each point costs a memory.
  - **3 Hollowing:** Malcanthet can whisper suggestions at will. He can always refuse.
  - **5 Hollowing:** his mind is hollowed, and she can *command* him once per long rest with no save.
- **The full Void Heart** would turn his psychic blades into blades of void dealing necrotic damage, make hollowed creatures stop treating him as an enemy, and let Malcanthet reach him without any Hollowing at all.

## The Soul Armor: Two Halves
> [!secret] The Kingsoul
> revealed:: false
> [[The Soul Armor]] works like the Kingsoul charm in *Hollow Knight*: it comes in two halves.
> - **The first half** is at the bottom of [[The Rikk Borgar]]. Wayne found it during his Void Trial and has no memory of it. That memory comes back in full in Session 55.
> - **The second half** is found by walking the **Tyrn'Haka**, the Endless Climb. The climb itself is the Kyn'Ruthar's journey: the second half is earned by embodying it.
> - When the two halves come together, they make the Soul Armor.

## The Stone Body
The stone isn't evil. What matters is whose will holds it. Three ways it can resolve:
1. **Defeat Malcanthet** and undo her hollowing. His flesh is restored.
2. **Complete the Kyn'Ruthar path.** His flesh is restored.
3. **Keep the stone by choice.** It grows into a strength over time, opening a stone Kyn'Ruthar.

## Abyssal Brand
Branded at [[Oakhollow]] along with everyone in the party except [[Bogtrotter]]. **Darkened** in the Soul trial. His way to break it is [[The Soul Armor]], which would also free him from the Void Heart's curse.
