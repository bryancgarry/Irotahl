## Campaign Log
- **Session 3, The Bridge King.** Leveled up, with Savage Attacker on unarmed strikes. Guessed "hands," then "fire." Lightning breath and a Flurry of "purple nurples." Found a copper piece in his belly button. Took the Bridge King's Scepter.
- **Session 4, Mushrooms & Trees.** Declined the mushroom pool. Helped finish off Geralt.
- **Session 5, Bluelock and Bloodlancers.** Got heroic inspiration from Aalyn's song. Vaulted the camp wall with Step of the Wind, taunted ("store brand," "spaghetti"), and killed the last Bloodlancer by twisting his fingers in its ears. Ate the goblin's breakfast.
- **Session 6, The Path to Godspire Ruins.**
  - Ate the orc front-line stew with Bragthar (+5 ft movement for a day). Natural 20 Insight on Malrik.
  - Threw "The daughter is key" a quarter mile into the woods, because dragons aren't real. Intimidated a tail feather out of Birdolomew.
  - Punched the obsidian obelisk, saw a bronze dragon in it, and shattered it with lightning.
- **Session 7, The Goose is Loose.** Helped hold the rings. Tickled the white ring still so Wayne and Alaric could reach the core. Floated down on the aarakocra feather.
- **Session 8, Gnomes and Fireballs.** The poop catapult (natural 20). Climbed into the trap wagon and hooked up with Fizzle underneath it.
- **Session 9, The Off-Branded Misfits.** Killed Fizzle under the wagon with unarmed strikes, carried her body, and went into the bushes with Dipple. Met the Fog Master in the Orchid's steam room.
- **Session 10, The Orchid.** Breathing lessons gave his breath weapon +1 damage. Seduced the fourth friend of Heladur's group, then strangled her with rope and tried to make it look self-inflicted. Told Wayne he'd heard "dragon" and gotten angry.
- **Session 11, The Plot Thickens.** *(Gabe absent.)* Went shopping alone and was arrested for the murder. Last seen breathing lightning from a building by the west gate.
- **Session 12, The Establishment is Corrupt.**
  - Told the party he choked Angelique Firestorm because "she hurt me first." Interviewed by Commander Seth, who found his rope.
  - Freed by the party's bribe. Left a fermented milk bomb with his cellmate Dragon.
  - Fried a rat swarm for 21 with his breath and kept five for dinner.
- **Session 13, The Rat Lord.** Hasted. Killed a bandit and three wererats, and ate a fried rat in front of the Rat Lord.
- **Session 14, A Little Bit of Everything.**
  - Ate the Rat Lord's brain and carried off his torso.
  - Armed two children with daggers, and a boy was slashed across the eye.
  - Made the Rat Lord's skull into a codpiece.
- **Session 15, Here We Go Again.** Bound to his Rat Lord's Skullcup. Romanced Ellie the waitress, then had Thimble put her to sleep thinking she'd spent the night with the Rat Lord's corpse. Made Thimble his first friend.
- **Session 16, Action and Apologies.** *(Gabe absent.)* Blasted onto the roof by Aalyn's swing and knocked out. Thimble and Jud rolled him into a haystack.
- **Session 17, The Vault.**
  - Dreamed of being a hungry gecko who planted trees, and woke in the hay with 4 temporary hit points.
  - Revealed his wings, which he flies on every morning. Flew Wayne's rope to the estate's chimney.
- **Session 18, The Stage is Set.** Pulled Buro back with the Rat Lord's Skullcup, claimed to be Malrik's inspector, and lectured him about dust.
- **Session 19, The Fight Begins.** Destroyed three flying swords with his fists, the first on back-to-back natural 20s.
- **Session 20, The Ascension.**
  - Used the Bridge King's Scepter for the first time. Its tentacles restrained the bone naga and later Malrik.
  - Destroyed the naga and drank what came out of it. Effect unknown.
  - Knocked Malrik out without killing him.
- **Session 53, What Is Left of You.** Punched the needle out of Thimble's head, guided Aalyn through the mindscape, threw a pebble at Alaric (the start of the beef), and ate the watermelon. Found he could stand on the void. Held on through the Soul trial.
- **Session 54, The Heart of the Void.** *(Gabe absent. The DM ran Bog.)*
  - Jumped onto the central platform with his Ring of Jumping and breathed lightning. His surge (92) added 18 lightning damage.
  - Killed two Void Shades. Then jumped down and flanked with Nerevath, shattering the Remnant's knee for about 43 damage in a turn.
  - Levels to 8 with the party.
