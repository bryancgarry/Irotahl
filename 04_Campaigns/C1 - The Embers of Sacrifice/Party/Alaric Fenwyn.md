# Alaric Fenwyn

**Player:** Trevor
**Race / Class:** Elf (High Elf lineage) Wizard (Illusionist) 7
**Background:** Noble. Age 22, 5'10".
**Sheet:** [D&D Beyond](https://www.dndbeyond.com/characters/136285112)

> [!summary] Quick Reference
> - **Core drive:** To be truly tested. A prodigy who left a gilded life to find his own identity and "his own sun."
> - **Family:** [[House Fenwyn]] of [[Syfaeril]]. Older brother [[Fenril Fenwyn|Fenril]]. Grandfather **Thalanir**, a war hero.

> - **Brand:** Held in the Soul trial. Not darkened.

> [!quote]- Backstory (written by Trevor)
> Born into the prestigious House Fenwyn, Alaric was raised in a world of decadence and comfort. His family, known for their wealth and influence across the realm, spared no expense in ensuring he received the finest education available. Even as a child, Alaric’s intellect was remarkable—he mastered mathematics, history, and philosophy with ease, and his tutors marveled at how quickly he absorbed even the most complex of subjects. By the time he was a teenager, Alaric was not just a prodigy; he was a legend within the noble circles, spoken of in admiring whispers and praised endlessly by those who envied his effortless brilliance.
>
> But to Alaric, life felt hollow. He quickly grew bored with the intellectual exercises his tutors assigned and found no thrill in the high society games that entertained his peers. He saw through the thin veils of courtly politeness and the petty rivalries around him, and soon, even his family’s ambitions felt shallow and predictable. He yearned for something… more—a challenge worthy of his mind and spirit.
>
> One evening, while roaming his family’s vast library, Alaric discovered a dusty tome on illusion magic hidden within a forgotten alcove. Captivated by the potential of illusions—the art of tricking the mind, of bending perception and manipulating reality—he felt an excitement he hadn’t felt in years. He realized that illusion magic was more than just spells; it was a dance of intellect, a test of wit and imagination. The idea that he could craft entire worlds, ensnare minds, and orchestrate events without needing wealth or title was intoxicating. Magic was a puzzle, a riddle, and a stage—all in one.
>
> Alaric began studying in secret, teaching himself the arcane arts from whatever scraps he could find in the library and from quiet meetings with obscure magic tutors who would come under the cover of night. As his skill grew, so did his desire to use it beyond the confines of his family’s estate. He wanted to see how far his mastery of illusions could take him—and where it would lead him.
>
> One night, he packed his belongings and left his family’s estate, leaving only a brief note for his parents. He was setting off to test his craft in the real world, to find worthy opponents, and to live a life where he could use his gifts without the constraints of nobility. Now, traveling from town to town under various disguises, Alaric uses his illusions to both challenge himself and experience life in its raw, untamed form. He thrives on the thrill of new situations, weaving his way through the unknown with cunning, intellect, and magic, always searching for something that can truly challenge his mind.
>
> For the first time, Alaric is free. And with each passing day, he edges closer to discovering the potential he has always known he possessed—potential that, when fully realized, will make him not just a noble or a mage, but a force to be reckoned with.
>
> Yearning for complete freedom and tired of hiding behind the masks and guises his illusions provided, Alaric decided to leave Syfaeril altogether. The noble courts and high towers of his homeland felt suffocating, their expectations and schemes binding him in invisible chains. The lure of Midland, with its sprawling diversity, endless opportunities, and storied history of conflict and conquest, became too strong to resist. If there was a place where Alaric could lose himself and find himself all at once, it was Midland.
>
> He set his sights on the Free Kingdoms of the east, drawn to their fragmented and untamed nature. Unlike the rigid hierarchy of Syfaeril or the structured Golden Republic of western Midland, the Free Kingdoms promised chaos, unpredictability, and opportunity—a fertile ground for someone of his talents. But what truly caught Alaric’s attention were the rumors of an orcish splinter cell invading the region. While he had no love for war, he was intrigued by the possibilities such conflict presented: alliances to forge, power to uncover, and perhaps secrets to exploit. Conflict bred opportunity, and Alaric was nothing if not opportunistic.
>
> His journey led him to the City of Oakhollow, a bustling hub nestled within the Free Kingdoms. The city’s vibrant tavern scene, filled with exotic foods, drink, and music, offered a stark contrast to the opulence of Syfaeril’s courtly feasts. Alaric quickly found himself enamored with the raw authenticity of it all.
>
> Alaric’s immediate goals are flexible, depending on the opportunities that present themselves. He might take up work with a faction looking to resist the Gor’Drak Dominion’s orcish splinter cell, hoping to pit his cunning against the raw brutality of his foes. Or he may delve into the mysteries of Oakhollow or Midland itself, searching for whispers of forgotten magic that could elevate his craft.
>
> Ultimately, Alaric remains a man in search of his own identity, unbound by titles or expectations. In Oakhollow, he seeks to carve out a new chapter in his life, one where he can live freely as himself.

## Backstory (as the player knows it)
- A member of House Fenwyn, one of the Lesser Houses of the Septarion in Calenthia, [[Syfaeril]]. A celebrated prodigy who found the noble life hollow.
- Found a hidden tome of illusion magic in the family library and taught himself in secret, with tutors who came at night.
- Left the estate with only a note, traveled in disguise, then left Syfaeril altogether for the Free Kingdoms of Midland, drawn by rumors of the [[Oughan Gor'Drak|Gor'Drak Dominion]]'s orc splinter cell. Ended up in [[Oakhollow]].
- **[[Fenril Fenwyn|Fenril]]**, his older brother, already carved his name into House Fenwyn's history. Alaric grew up in his shadow. When Alaric was thirteen, Fenril brought him before the ancient gold dragon **Aurixia** on the Sky Terrace, and she asked him what *he* had ever done. Fenril's lesson: *"You don't step out of my shadow. You walk in a different direction entirely. Find your own sun."* And: *"Choose growth. Even if it terrifies you."*
- As children, Fenril told him stories of the eladrin and their seasonal blades (green, gold, blue, silver), and of a stone in the far west that held every color, and promised that someday they'd go find that mountain.
- **Thalanir Fenwyn**, his grandfather, is one of House Fenwyn's great mages, now deceased. He fought in the elven wars and was said to walk through battlefields unseen, leaving only echoes of himself to be struck down. Alaric wears his cloak.
- He took an egg from [[Vaelindra]]'s nest.
