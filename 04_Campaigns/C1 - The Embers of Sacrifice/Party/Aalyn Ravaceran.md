# Aalyn Ravaceran

**Player:** Spano
**Race / Class:** Half-Elf Bard / Warlock

## Backstory (as the player knows it)
- His warlock patron is [[Lolth]].
- His childhood love, Sumina, is believed dead. Purple lilacs grow on her grave.
- His ex, [[Talia]], works at V's Lounge in Hollowmere.

## The Truth
> [!secret] Sumina is alive
> revealed:: false
> Sumina is the Archfey [[Verenestra]], held captive by Lolth. The lilacs on her grave are Abyssal flowers, the same ones from the Graz'zt ascension room and the Reliquary's Lilac Hollow. Aalyn hasn't made the connection.

> [!secret] Already promised
> revealed:: false
> In the Reliquary's Soul trial, the void asks him first: *"You are already promised. To whom?"* Malcanthet calls him "Lolth's little songbird."

## Abyssal Brand
Branded at [[Oakhollow]] along with everyone in the party except [[Bogtrotter]]. His way to break it is open.

## Personal Quest Hooks
- Sumina's fate.
- What his pact with Lolth really costs.

## Notable Items
- **Silverwind.** A +2 silvered longsword with wind runes he crafted. Rare, 2 charges (Warding Wind and Silver Spark), plus a Gale Slash ranged attack.
- A stone battle-axe, enhanced in the salamanders' fire enchantment chamber.
