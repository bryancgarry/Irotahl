> *Written by Aalyn Ravaceran*

> **Note From the Author**
>
> First of all, my real name is Aalyn Waeslamin. As one of the most famous bards in all of the kingdoms you’d think I’d be better at writing about myself. But here I am writing down my life story and nobody will ever read it, well unless you are, and if that’s the case I guess I either made someone proud or you found this on my dead body, and that wouldn’t be very fitting for an incredible story like mine to be told. None the less I hope this finds the right audience… enjoy the story my life.
>
> **Chapter One: The Musical Prodigy**
>
> My earliest memory of music was listening to my mother sing to me before I could even crawl. She would sing to me at all hours of the day, and I remember the smiles on her and my father’s faces. Sometimes it wasn’t even words it could have just been melodies and I remember just feeling so calm and loved. As I got older I began to hum those same melodies before I could even talk, and once again I watched the smiles on my parents faces. They were proud of me. When I learned to speak my mother taught me the words to her songs, and now with the context I have I know they weren’t hers but she had me convinced. She taught me the techniques behind singing and this was something that made our bond stronger than anything.
>
> My father owned our farmland and it was a beautiful place to grow up. He would teach me how to care for the animals and the plants, and I really did have the best life ever. When I learned to walk I would follow him around all day, we would walk all around the land and I’d be so tired by the time we went back inside for supper. And then to end the day me and mother would sit by the fireplace and sing until I fell asleep. And I got to do this over and over again every day.
>
> Around five or six years old I started to learn to play different instruments. Once again I started with the melodies that my mother sang to me as a baby. It was then when I realized how much I really loved music. Even at this young of an age I had the feeling that music could change lives for everyone just like it did for me and my family. During the day me and my mother began to make the 30 minute walk into town to visit her friends and I would always spend time playing with their kids. I would play my instruments for them and we would all make up fun songs and sings them for our parents before we would all leave to go home for dinner.
>
> It was during these trips into town when I met a girl that would completely change my view of the world. Her name was Sumina. She was the daughter of one of my mother’s closest friends and we spent more time together than any of our other friends. Our families were so close that when her parents were busy working she would spend days with me and my family on our farm. She loved listening to my music and likewise I loved playing it for her. Something about this friendship was special and not only do I think we both knew it, but I think our parent knew it too.
>
> **Chapter Two: Childhood Love**
>
> “Childhood love” it’s so weird writing those words out. As Sumina and myself got older and started our schooling we began to see each other more and more often. Between our normal schooling and the beginner magic classes our parents had put us in we were spending almost all day every day together. It was around the age of ten that I began to see the true beauty that she possessed. She had long silver hair that her mother would always tie into some kind of braid so that it wouldn’t get dirty when we would explore around town. She had the most beautiful lavender eyes in the entire village, and don’t even get me started on her smile. I really was the luckiest boy in that entire village and I was too naive to know anything about love at that age.
>
> As the years advanced we grew even closer and by twelve years old we were inseparable. Almost everyone in the village expected us to be together at any given time of the day. We really were connected at the hip… well except for the fact that Sumina was a super genius in every sense of the word, and I was really only genius when it came to music. But once again she always found ways to make me better. It’s thanks to Sumina that I learned bardic magic. Our village really only produced average skilled mages, but she was already one of the best at this point.
>
> As Sumina focused on the more well know forms of magic, she still made time to help me with my own. After school we would spend the afternoons in the woods behind my family’s farm and I would use my songs to cast magical spells and effects. I found ways to use my instruments to cast spells I never would have been able to without her help. Looking back at these times now it’s easy for me to say I was madly in love with her, but in that moment I could only think of how special the rest of our lives were going to be together.
>
> **Chapter Three: Plague**
>
> We were sixteen when the plague reached our town. My family was one of the main volunteers to help the sick. My father supplied the town with free food, and my mother and I used our magical abilities to help ease the pain of the sick. Sumina and her family were right alongside us the whole time, and she became one of the most helpful people in the entire town. Even during this horrible time for all the people of our town Sumina and I were still able to spend time together. It was around this time that I began writing my own music for the first time and she was the first person to hear every song.
>
> The plague ravaged our town for months thankfully my family remained healthy this whole time, but Sumina’s wasn’t as lucky. About a month or so after the town got sick her mother passed away.
>
> Following this Sumina still kept a smile on her face every day. She helped every single person she could, and I vowed to stay right there by her side. I used my music to keep smiles on people’s faces even in the face of certain demise. I knew I loved her now. A few weeks after her mother’s passing her father took his own life… In their home… and she found him.
>
> This was the first time I had ever seen her cry. The hope that had always been inside her had disappeared and something was missing. Soon after this she moved in with my family. We continued to help as many people as we could until it looked like we had finally weathered the storm. I remember this like it was yesterday… We were at our favorite spot in the woods talking about the future… Our future. This was the first time I told her I loved her. I used a silly spell to form the clouds into the words “I love you” and we kissed. Two weeks later she was gone too.
>
> It all happened so fast. One day she’s perfectly healthy kissing me in the woods, and two days later she’s stuck in bed unable to move. I would sit by her side every hour of every day and sing her favorite songs hoping that I would create some magical way to cure her. She would hold my hand a say she would beat it, and we could grow old together and change the world. She really was the love of my life. On the day she passed I sat by her bed all day… Tears rolled down my face endlessly. Right before she left me she grabbed my hand and said two things, “You never got to write a song about us did you?” which made me chuckle. But then she said, “Aalyn, you know I’ve always loved you… And I wouldn’t change a single thing about how we made it here. But I’m not making it past this. But I know you will, and I know you’re going to make this world a better place…” I lost control. I laid down next to her and whispered her favorite song into her ear until I felt her pulse stop.
>
> **Chapter Four: “Make This World A Better Place”**
>
> To this day I replay that moment over and over in my head. “Make this a better place,” I know she would have done it if she were still here. Which in theory means I would have been right by her side.
>
> After she passed I spent months locked away in my home. I turned to simple farmwork with my father, helped him tend the fields, but I refused to leave my home. My love for music almost died with her. My instruments laid on the floor for weeks to the point where they were covered in dust… they had never looked like that before. I stopped singing. My mother would walk around the house singing our songs and never once did I have the urge to sing along with her. Every night I had the same nightmare, just sitting there watching her fade away and not being able to do anything about it. Sometimes she would reach for me and repeating the things she said in that real moment. Other times she would grab me violently and beg for me to save to, to do anything, but I would just breakdown into tears and freeze. A failure… I had never felt that feeling before, but now every night I get that same feeling.
>
> A few months after her passing I was summoned into town by our town elder. Sumina’s childhood home needed to be cleared out and prepared to be resold. It took hours of convincing, my mother and I promised that we would collect her families belongings. I took us three days to move everything out of her house. My parents spent majority of their time cleaning out her parents belongings, and I spent my time laying on the floor of her room. Eventually I got to cleaning, but very quickly stopped when I found some old love letters she had written about me… Almost every single one of them ended with the words, “I can’t wait to change the world with him.”
>
> Those letters where the kick in the ass I needed to chase the dreams she had for us. That day when we went back home I cleaned off all my instruments and got back to writing songs. Even when she was gone she finally found a way to inspire me… I spent the next few days walking around town spending time with people I hadn’t seen since Sumina’s passing. I spent the nights in our local tavern playing my songs for the people of our town who were all still healing from this plague in their own right. I still didn’t know if I was going to be able to change the world, but I could start by changing our town.
>
> **Chapter Five: Early Fame**
>
> Around a year after Sumina passed I finally felt like my music was making a difference. The town was thriving for what felt like the first time in my entire life. My father was having the most bountiful harvests our farm has ever had, my mother began running an orphanage in town for children whose parents passed during the plague, and I was generating the town unbelievable amounts of money from playing my music. I was making so much money at the tavern that eventually we had to construct a stage area outside to fit more people.
>
> At this point in time I was only keeping some of the money that I was generating and giving the rest right back to the town to keep improving. We had hundreds of people of all different walks of life who were traveling from all of the nearby towns and cities to come listen to my music. To the music that Sumina inspired me to keep creating… But it wasn’t always my music I was using to inspire people. With all the music I was writing I was also perfecting my magic abilities. Sumina always told me that my abilities were tied to my confidence, at this moment in time I’ve recognized this as being true. I felt stronger than ever, I felt as if I could help everyone in this town all at the same time. So I kept expanding… I kept asking to grow my concert space to the point where we had an amphitheater built just for me shows.
>
> By my nineteenth birthday I was one of the most famous bards in our region. My town had now grown into a small city, and my family’s farm was becoming a larger and larger business. My father had reached the point where he could hire people to help him tend the farms. My mothers orphanage was running so well that now she was taking in children from all of the surrounding towns near us. And I was to thank for it all. I was honored to be looked at like a hero, but I’m not sure at this moment I knew what this attention was doing to me.
>
> **Chapter Six: Kicked Out**
>
> I don’t want to remember this… It was the winter. I had just finished one of my afternoon concerts, and I was taking a solo walk over the cemetery where we laid Sumina to rest. I did this almost once a day. We buried her next to her parents in a beautiful corner of the cemetery. When I reached her grave I sat in the thin layer of snow on the ground a talked to her for a while. After about thirty minutes I saw my mother making her way through the cemetery towards me.
>
> Once she reached me I could tell that something was bothering her, which quickly led to me asking what was wrong. She then informed me that our village elder was looking for me and that I should make my way there right away. The elder asking for me wasn’t that uncommon, I mean I was basically funding the town by myself at this point, so I was used to having to go and approve plans. But this time felt different… I said my goodbyes to Sumina and my mother and started making my way back into the center of town.
>
> I arrived at town hall and was quickly ushered into the grand hall where our elder was waiting for me. I remember these words all too well, “Aalyn, I want you to know that this town appreciates everything that you’ve done for us…” And then came that stupid fucking “But.” My heart dropped… Anger. Hatred. Rage. “We’re going to have to stop your shows. We find that it’s in the towns best interest to stop your performances immediately.”
>
> I blacked out after those words. How could he even say that. This nowhere town wouldn’t have even been on the map if it wasn’t for me! I demanded all of the money that I had made over the past few days and stormed out of the building. As I walked through the streets people were swarming me, asking me to sing to them, asking about my show that was supposed to be happening in a few hours. I ignored it for the first few minutes, but more and more people started gathering and I couldn’t think of anything to say. Me of all people not having words for this certain situation, what a moron. I released a magical scream saying, “Fucking back away! There are no more fucking shows…” The crowd around me gasped. I heard children starting to cry and run away. Parents exchanging whispers and asking questions. “If you wants to ask any more questions go ask the stupid fucks in town hall. Now fucking leave me alone.” Then I let out one last scream and began the familiar thirty minute walk to my home.
>
> My parents were waiting for me when I arrived home. I think my mother attempted to say something, but I walked straight past her and into the house. I found every instrument in that house and destroyed every single one. They watched on in horror. My parents, the two people who always were proud of me now looked on in horror as I destroyed everything that mattered to me. This is when my mother told me she knew why I was sent to the elder… She said that the town has grown too large for what it is now. Too popular. I thought that’s what we wanted, I thought I was helping, making this boring old town a better place. Just like she would have wanted… I broke down into tears on the floor.
>
> The last thing I ever heard my mother say was, “Please calm down my love…” and she began humming our song. I’d never felt that much rage before and I grabbed her by the throat and threw her to the ground. My father screamed and ran to her aid. I looked over through teary eyes and saw my mother gasping for air and trying to speak, but I heard no sounds. I looked on in horror, this was where I remembered first humming my mother and I’s song and seeing the smile or my parents faces. And now they’re looking at me like I’m a monster.
>
> I sprinted to my room and grabbed everything I thought I would need to survive on my own. Some clothes, my stash of money, a dagger for protection, and my song journal. I then broke open the window in my room and ran through the farmland and into the woods where Sumina and I used to spend afternoons. As I hit the tree line I heard the sound of guards in the road making their way towards my former home. I sat in our clearing for hours until sunset. I remember sitting under a tree as the snow began to fall, and thinking maybe I should just stay here and freeze. That’s when I heard her voice, I’m not sure if it was in my head or not, but I heard her say, “Aalyn you need to make the world a better place! And once you do that I can come back and we can be together again. Forever.” I made a promise that night to bring her back no matter the cost. I sat in silence a little longer until I finally decided to pull my sorry ass off the ground, and walk away from everything I’ve ever known. My last memory of my home was listening to the sound of metal clanging of town guards as I watched torch lights move towards my old home.
>
> **Chapter Seven: Traveling Singer**
>
> After my town exiled me I found myself traveling as far away from my home as possible. At this point I had found plenty of ways to distract myself from the events that happened a couple of months prior. Playing music in almost every tavern I see makes it very easy to find women to make the time pass quickly. But something always feels off no matter where I go. Like I’m being watched, or hunted, or followed by someone who knows my past.
>
> I spent a lot of time reflecting, not only on the things I’ve done, but also in the ways that Sumina would have wanted me to improve the world. I wandered for months and months seeing all regions of this beautiful world and I really began to believe in this dream we once shared. Every city I’d find a new reason to keep going. I regained my joy that I once had for performing, the coin didn’t hurt either… But through all of that I found love again. Love for people, and music, and life. But mostly myself. Even after all these years Sumina was still helping me, whether she knows it or not.
>
> **Chapter Eight: Optimistic Obscurity**
>
> Well I think its been about a year since I’ve opened this thing… Life has been good to me recently, which is weird to say because nothing of interest has really happened. I’m still playing my music, and recently I’ve actually started writing original songs again. I’m currently in a town named Silverwind, a lot of people around here haven’t heard of me before which is honestly a very nice feeling. There’s no expectations anymore, I don’t have to be this superstar that everyone knows. I can allow myself to feel things that the Ally from a couple years ago never would have let himself feel.
>
> I met a girl recently! Well more than met, she told me she loved me… And TRUST ME I’ve been with a lot of women since I left home, but hearing those words from her terrified me more than anything I’ve ever heard. Her name is Rosalie, she’s actually the Princess of Oakhollow. Yeeaaahhhh, I think I messed up big time there. She actually gave me a family heirloom the night she said she loved me, it’s a pair of magical boots that she had customized to match my outfit. Just really sucks that after that night I ran away without her knowing, and haven’t looked back since.
>
> I’m honestly not sure how I feel about her. She really is incredible, but she reminds me of Sumina in every way possible. I’m not even sure why I’m writing this whole thing down in here anyway. Whoever ends up reading this isn’t going to care about my love life. I don’t know… maybe I need to keep my distance from women all together, I still have that goal of making the world a better place, and something like a girlfriend would make that a bit more challenging…
>
> **Chapter Nine: The Wedding Night**
>
> Well I found myself back in Oakhollow sharing a bed with Rosalie the night before her wedding… I know, not my smartest move. But she invited me to the wedding and who was I to turn down an invitation to a party? Just wasn’t expected to be ambushed by a women who said she loved me in the cheapest inn in town. FOR THE RECORD SHE CAME ONTO ME!
>
> Now that that’s out of the way I can get to the weird shit that happened the rest of the day. So as I was coming down the stairs from my room there was a was a group of guys standing around a table. Two of them looked like wizard types, but then there was a rock dude, a gnome who looked like he was stoned out of his mind, and a fucking dragon born? And for some reason the older looking wizard guy called me over and handed me an invitation to Rosalie’s wedding, little did he know I was already invited…
>
> So later after the wedding ceremony I somehow stumbled back into the rest of those guys that I was invited to the wedding with, so we just decided to head over to the keep together. I learned their names on the way there too. Alaric was a younger looking half elf, basically just a kid, he was one of the wizards. Then Wayne was the big rock guy, and Thimblewick was the gnome that was with him. And the dragon borns name was Bogtrotter, because of fucking course it was…
>
> The party was incredible, I immediately went to the dancefloor and found a beautiful girl to show my dance moves. Well Rosalie spotted this and baited me into performing in front of all of those very important people… and honestly what kind of bard would I be if I said no? I gave them the best performance they’ve ever seen! But, right as I’m finishing my song all hell broke loose.
>
> All I heard was glass breaking and next thing I knew the room was filled with smoke. All I could think about was where Rosalie was, and trying to keep her safe but I couldn’t seem to find her through the smoke. Thankfully the rest of the guys were able to take down everyone who was attacking, but by the time I found Rosalie it was too late. She had been stabbed in the chest and was bleeding out.
>
> Before I could do anything to help her, the Baron-her father, let out some monstrous scream and the next time I opened my eyes we were in some kind of dark mirror dimension. The only thing we could see if the Baron turn into a crazy monster, and I giant symbol way off in the distance. We assumed our only option was to kill this monster in front of us, but during the fight the symbol exploded hitting all of us and leaving some kind of brand on us. That didn’t stop us from killing the old man and notice the walls around us begin to crack and we’re back in the great hall of the keep. Before we can even regain our sense of space, we’re greeted by Roaslie’s new husband, Prince Darkin of Darkstone…

While all this was happening the castle clerics and mages immediately brought Rosalie upstairs to try and save her life. Honestly she was all I could think about in this moment. Darkin allowed us to stay in the Keep that night, but I honestly didn’t get much sleep. Which is very unfortunate because it was the most comfortable bed I have probably ever slept in. But once again I had to deal with the idea that someone I cared about could potentially be on their death bed, and I feel so powerless once again.

## Backstory (as the player knows it)
- Born **Aalyn Waeslamin** on his father's farm, a 30-minute walk from a small village. He dropped his family name and took Sumina's: **Ravaceran**. He calls himself Ally or Allie. His mother sang to him from birth. Her songs, he later realized, weren't really hers.
- **Sumina** was the daughter of his mother's close friend: silver hair, lavender eyes, a magical prodigy who taught him bardic magic. When plague hit at sixteen, her mother died of it and her father took his own life. She moved in with Aalyn's family. He told her he loved her, two weeks later she fell sick, and she died with him whispering her favorite song in her ear. Her last words: *"I know you're going to make this world a better place."*
- Her old love letters pulled him out of his grief. His music grew the village into a small city, and by nineteen he was one of the most famous bards in the region.
- **Exile.** The elder shut down his shows because the town had grown too big. In a rage he smashed every instrument in the house and choked his mother when she tried to calm him with their song. He fled as the guards came, and he doesn't know what became of her.
- **The night in the woods.** Under the tree he and Sumina used to sit beneath, in falling snow, he thought about staying there to freeze. Then he heard her voice: make the world better, and she could come back to him, forever. He promised to bring her back no matter the cost. That's all he remembers.
- He wandered and played for years, always feeling watched or followed. In the town of **Silverwind** he met **Rosalie**, Princess of [[Oakhollow]] and daughter of [[Baron Fern]], who gave him her heirloom winged boots. He ran away from her.
- In Oakhollow he crossed paths with **Ino Silvertone**, a rival bard he has beef with.
- He came back for her wedding to **Prince Darkin of Darkstone**, met the party there, and got branded when Fern turned. Rosalie was stabbed during the attack and did not survive.
- His ex, [[Talia]], works at V's Lounge in Hollowmere.
- In the void beneath the Reliquary, he heard **Sumina's song** and saw her soul before Lolth erupted from a nest of webs, hissed "*Insolent child*," and dragged her back.

## The Truth
> [!secret] The thread
> revealed:: false
> Aalyn didn't just sit under the winter tree. He hanged himself from it. At the moment of death, Lolth freed him from the rope and wound her own thread around his neck instead, binding him to her service in exchange for power. He has no memory of it. **When he lashes out against Lolth, his neck tightens in pain.** He doesn't know why.
> The winter tree is the setting for his scenes with Lolth. It is where the contract was made.
> **Open:** whose voice he heard that night: Sumina's, Lolth wearing it, or both.

> [!secret] Sumina is Verenestra
> revealed:: partial
> Sumina was the Archfey [[Verenestra]], walking among mortals after giving up her godhood. Lolth holds her captive. The lilacs on her grave are Abyssal flowers, the same ones from the Graz'zt ascension room and the Reliquary's Lilac Hollow.
> **Revealed so far:** her soul exists, and Lolth holds it. **Still hidden:** who she really is, and what the lilacs mean.
> **Open:** how Sumina's death and Lolth's involvement fit together.

> [!secret] A family feud
> revealed:: false
> Verenestra is Lolth's niece, from before Lolth's corruption, when Lolth was still Araushnee of the fey pantheon. Taking Sumina is Lolth's revenge on the fey for slighting her. The feud is far older and bigger than Aalyn. He just happens to be standing in the middle of it.

> [!secret] Lolth's Bowstring
> revealed:: false
> The "golden bowstring" that blazed in the void is *Lolth's Bowstring*, his Hexblade pact weapon: a gold-plated rapier with a white hilt, empowered by his contract with Lolth, with more abilities that wake as the bond deepens. When he defied her, it was her own weapon that burned brightest.
> **Open:** whether that was Lolth's power answering him, Sumina reaching through the pact, or the thread straining.

> [!secret] Lolth's reaction
> revealed:: false
> Lolth knows Aalyn reached for Sumina, and she took it as defiance. She needs to answer soon. The thread is the obvious lever.

> [!secret] Already promised
> revealed:: false
> Not yet delivered. Still available to Malcanthet when she speaks after the Remnant falls: *"Lolth's little songbird. She lets you out so far from the web. How trusting of her."* "Already promised" has a literal answer now: the promise in the snow.

> [!secret] His mother
> revealed:: false
> Alive. Her voice is damaged from what he did. Saved for a future arc.
