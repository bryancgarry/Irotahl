# Become the Hollow Vessel

> [!summary] At a Glance
> [[Wayne Kerr]]'s questline from [[Malcanthet]], offered in [[Session 55]]. If he accepts the [[Shell of the Void Heart]] into his body, she begins finishing what she started: making him the next Hollow Vessel, her weapon in [[The Second Sundering]]. Its counterweight is the Kyn'Ruthar road ([[The Soul Armor]], the Tyrn'Haka).

> [!info] DM Note
> - **Status:** offered in Session 55. Update when Wayne answers.
> - If he refuses, the Shell goes **completely dormant** (no powers, no curse, no Hollowing). He can carry it, the offer stays open, and the questline waits until he accepts.
> - Malcanthet reveals it **a stage at a time**. In Session 55 she gives only the shape ("a body, a mind, a soul; the body you have given me") and the first step (the shining mountain in the west).
> - The deeds point Wayne at goals that serve the Godhand, not against the party directly. But the first big objective is helping start the war in Thimble's home city. Wayne can play along all the way to the wound and choose there, like the first Vessel did.

## The Shape
Hollowing works on the body, the mind, and the soul (see [[Hollowing and Petrification]]). Each stage has a **rite** (something done to him), a **deed** (something done for her), a **reward**, and a **price** (Hollowing, which costs memories; she aims for [[Jackmerius Tacktheratrix|Jack]]).

### Stage 1: The Body *(Session 55)*
- **Rite:** He survived the Remnant's Stilling and came back harder. Now he takes the Shell into his chest, the same place he tore it out of the Remnant.
- **Reward:** **Shade Step** (see [[Shell of the Void Heart]]). The Shell no longer uses an attunement slot.
- **Price:** the Shell is part of him now. The curse and the Hollowing track begin.
- **Her first instruction:** "Go to the west. To the mountain that shines and is not a mountain... When the mountain opens, I need *you* standing in the wound."

### Stage 2: The Stitch *(the Glimmerstone arc)*
The core of the questline, and a large general point of the Glimmerstone arc (to be planned in its own thread).
- **The idea:** the Shell was [[The Hollow Reliquary]]'s anchor, the stitch that held an Abyssal house in the material plane. When it left, the house went home. The reverse is what Malcanthet wants: planted in the right wound, the Shell stitches the Abyss to the world and holds a gateway open. **With the Shell inside him, Wayne is the door.**
- **Objectives (working):**
  - Get to [[Glimmerstone]]. The airship points there.
  - Get into the mountain's heart. One option: enter the World Games as a champion of the Goliaths, since champions are honored at the Luminarch's event. Goliath delegations (the Volkarr included) bring his homeland to him.
  - Find the wound. The Shell pulls toward the place where the first Sundering ended, deep in the Chaos Primordial's body, like a compass.
  - When the mountain opens, stand in the wound. The Shell anchors the gateway, and [[The Second Sundering]] begins. **Or he turns.**
- **Rewards along the way (working):** Kin of the Void, then his Soulknife blades turn to void (necrotic) damage.

### Stage 3: The Mind *(loose)*
- **Rite:** [[The Soul Sanctum]] in Drak'mor, where she first held him. Her hollowers finish piercing the mind she left unfinished.

### Stage 4: The Soul *(loose)*
- **Rite:** his soul is lowered into [[The Sea of Void]] and darkened.
- **The brand:** the party's brands are [[Graz'zt]]'s claim (the Oakhollow Ember became him). The Godhand are unified, so Graz'zt hands Wayne's claim over, and Malcanthet burns the brand off herself, as promised. What she leaves out: a vessel needs no brand. It is already hers.

### Stage 5: The Blade *(loose)*
- Her champion in the war that follows, and eventually the rest of [[The Void Heart]].

## The Other Road
In the Session 55 memory, young Wayne reached the bottom of [[The Rikk Borgar]], the battlefield where [[Ysgard]] tore away and the first Vessel turned. Malcanthet's stone and the turned Vessel's spark both went into him. He came out carrying both, which is what a Kyn'Ruthar is: a vessel that chose. Every stage of this questline can be walked toward her or turned against her.
