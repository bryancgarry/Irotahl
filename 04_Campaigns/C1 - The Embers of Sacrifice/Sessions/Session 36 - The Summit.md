# Session 36: The Summit

**Party:** Level 6, leveling to 7 at the end ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 35 - Bugs and Dragons]], [[Fenril Fenwyn]], [[House Fenwyn]], [[Hollowmere]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "The Summit" (Episode 36)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J3WmkoQibXo). Opens on a flashback: young Alaric meets the gold dragon Aurixia, brought by his brother Fenril. Back at the summit, the party fought beside the silver dragon against the hooded figures, who turn out to be drow. Bog's double natural 20 on Father Celery's remains yielded a tome of understanding, a belt of stone giant strength and a rod of resurrection. Wayne and Thimble broke the last chains, Nerevath flew the party out, the dragon touched Bog and woke a silver heritage in him, and Alaric secretly took an egg from her nest. They grabbed two rainbow eggs, Alaric turned Nerevath back into Salvatore for the walk into the city, and the party leveled to 7.

## What Happened at the Table

**Flashback: Aurixia**
- The sky terrace of the House Fenwyn estate at sunset, over fields of flowers and rivers. **Fenril** ("baby brother, I have something to show you"), perfectly formal, hands behind his back.
- **A gold dragon** landed: golden scales, flames for eyes, wings 100 feet wide. Fenril bowed: **"Lady Aurixia"** (said at the table as "Orexia/Orixia"). He asked her to give Alaric a lesson.
- She said the child wasn't worth her time; his storybooks don't make him special. Fenril: that was the lesson he wanted. Brilliance without purpose is just potential, and potential not pointed anywhere is nothing. Greatness is earned through action and the willingness to step into the unknown. She didn't rest on her ancestors.
- Aurixia: Alaric stands in the shadow of a brother who carved his name into House Fenwyn's legend, with the same gifts, but untested. **"A dragon is powerful because it has lived through enough to understand the weight of its choices."** **"Do not waste your gifts on a safe life. The comfort of nobles does not need one more."**
- Fenril: "Your time will come, when you find a sun all on your own."

**Back at the summit**
- The DM introduced a monster he found, the goon balloon. Not in play.
- **Wayne** pulled Pebbles out and asked the dragon if she's a mother. She thought he'd abducted her child until she saw Pebbles is stone like him. She'll leave him the goose if he gets it out of her nest. Asked for a lift (natural 1s with advantage), she stomped him (3) and pushed him back. He hid as a boulder (stealth with advantage).
- **Aalyn** *Message*d the dog party's leader that the dragon would murder everyone. He replied they were trying to heal the dragon but would turn back. Later Aalyn talked two of the three into retreating (Persuasion 24).
- **Alaric** told Wayne to bring the goose to him once the hunters were dealt with; he has a plan to get it into the city. Drank a potion and held a *Scorching Ray* to strike alongside the dragon.
- **Thimble** *Message*d the hooded group to get off the goose or die by dragon. The reply came in an aggressive language he didn't know.
- **The dragon's breath of cold** on the hooded group, with Alaric's *Scorching Ray* (20 fire) on their leader.
- A hooded mage cast something that made the dragon drowsy (a 4th-level spell). **Alaric's *Dispel Magic*** (16) ended it.
- **Wayne's jade daggers broke a chain**; it discharged arcane energy into its holder.
- **Nerevath's honk blast** went east and set off an avalanche.
- **Bog** desecrated Father Celery's remains: Investigation with disadvantage, **two natural 20s**. In his remains, and in what the dragon spat up:
  - **a tome of understanding**,
  - **a belt of stone giant strength**,
  - **a rod of resurrection** (Father Celery was a renowned priest),
  - and 6 cp. The Fortunate Five were broke.
- A hooded warrior cast *Darkness*. **Alaric counterspelled it** (natural 1 save) and kept his slot.
- A hooded mage hit the dragon and misty-stepped behind a tree. **The dog party's "dog"** bolted in front of the dragon, who snapped it up and spat it out: "Disgusting abomination." It's **a decrepit, spectral-looking fox**, bigger than a fox, dribbling.
- **Thimble's *Fireball*** (34) with Tides of Chaos: it came out fire and cold together, and he surged: **resistance to all damage for one minute**, glowing gold like Aurixia. The heat-then-cold shattered **the last chain**. Nerevath was free.
- The dragon's **paralyzing breath** caught the hooded leader and a mage. Wayne's daggers tore into the leader (16, 8). Wayne stood next to the dragon. "We're just two dudes."
- **Aalyn's *Eldritch Blast* blew the leader's hood off: a drow man**, very dark purple skin. Unsettling Words on a hooded warrior.
- **Alaric's *Moat of Hell*** caught the hooded priest. Its psychic damage (on a failed WIS save) finished one of three in there.
- Wayne told Nerevath to pick up Thimble. Thimble apologized to him: "I love you so much." Nerevath: "Does this guy think he's my dad? I have one father in this life."
- **Bog handed Alaric the tome and the rod** for safekeeping.
- The hooded warrior shot twice at Aalyn and missed, then dug in. She's a woman.
- **Thimble climbed onto Nerevath** with his boots. "Let's go look at some ruins."
- The dragon offered Wayne a proper lift in her claw. Then she called Bog "my kin," touched two claws between his eyes, and (Arcana, advantage) **his bronze scales rippled silver**: Bog can now **switch his draconic heritage to silver at will**, breath of cold and resistance to cold instead of lightning.
- Combat ended. The dog party's leader tipped his cap to Aalyn as they left. **Aalyn (Perception 14) saw a candle symbol on the back of his robes.**
- Aalyn cast *Comprehend Languages* and checked on the downed drow woman. She hissed: **"Leave us. We do not require the presence of our champion here."**
- Nerevath took off with all five. Wayne renewed the psionic link.

**The nest**
- **Alaric (natural 20 Perception)** saw a hole in the mountainside with something silver inside. They looped around; Aalyn flew him down.
- **Inside: a silver dragon egg.** Alaric put it straight into his bag of holding. Aalyn grabbed **a parchment** and **120 gp**.
- They got back on in a midair catch. Alaric told no one about the egg; asked what was in the hole: "Nothing. Bunch of gold." His voice cracked.

**The rainbow eggs**
- Thimble told the others about the eggs. They swung by the remorhaz lake: **the big remorhaz floats dead with its belly blown open. Four of the six eggs are eaten.** Aalyn swooped down and **took the last two**, about ostrich-egg size, then handed them off. **Thimble holds them** (DM, after the fact).
- Thimble: eat one and it's permanently +1 to a stat, or your insides explode.
- Alaric minor-illusioned a chicken egg ("third egg") to cover himself.

**The parchment and the loot**
- Alaric ritual-cast *Identify*. **The parchment is a treasure map: three marked locations in the dwarven ruins beneath the city.**
- The tome and the rod impressed him: the rod "could save our lives." Bog doesn't want them. "I don't know how to read."

**Salvatore**
- Alaric cast *Nystul's Magic Aura* to make Nerevath count as a beast for 24 hours. They landed about an hour's walk out (around 6 p.m.), and Alaric used a beast-to-humanoid transmutation to turn him into **Salvatore Pearcetti** for an hour: olive-skinned, big belly, his work apron from the pear stall. Alaric guessed and made him bald; he had baby blonde locks.
- Salvatore was overjoyed to see his hands, then upset it's temporary. Alaric says people in the city may be able to make it permanent.
- Wayne explained he had three geese and only one was a man. Alaric pieced together that Wayne had been beating the man back into his bag.
- Aalyn took a large goose feather as proof.

**The split**
- **Aalyn and Thimble** went to the baroness with the feather and the snorkel gear.
- **Alaric, Wayne and Bog** took Salvatore toward the waterfall to Bifflefapple's workshop. Wayne's link is up for eight hours.

**Level 7**
- The party leveled to 7. Hit points rolled.
  - Aalyn: a level of bard, and **Metamagic Adept** (Twinned and Subtle Spell).
  - Alaric: 4th-level spells. Plans *Elemental Bane* to combo with Wayne's sneak attack.
  - Wayne: **Reliable Talent**. He also has Fey Touched (*Identify*, *Misty Step*), which he doesn't realize are spells.
  - Bog: the belt makes his unarmed strikes +10 to hit, 1d8+7.
