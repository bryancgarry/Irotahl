# Session 41: A Man and His Goose

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 40 - The Elevator Down]], [[Durnhollow]], [[Rec]], [[The Demonomicon]]

> [!important] Played: "A Man and His Goose" (Episode 41)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-_iGUcun5Nw). The party split up across Durnhollow's steam layer. Alaric scouted with *Arcane Eye*; Thimble and Bog walked among the worker constructs, found a worker's stash (237 sp and a pipe of smoke monsters), and Rec swallowed the chest. Wayne spent his free natural 20 walking through a sealed stone door forehead and knee first, found the boiler room, and with Pebbles' help pickpocketed the keys to start the boilers. Aalyn led the detention drone away and shut it down from its back panel. Wayne accidentally threw Pebbles into a 1,000-foot chasm, dove after her, heard her first word, "Dad," and launched himself back up: the Feat of the Kyn'Ruthar. Alaric found the Steam Nexus (missing its key) and a key ring in the foreman's office. Thimble used the Tome of Malcanthet as a focus for a max-damage *Thunderwave* that destroyed six steam mephits.

## What Happened at the Table

**Before the session**
- Thimble and Wayne rolled on the goon balloons at camp: **119 copper.** Wayne kept 60, Thimble 59.

**The grand highway**
- The DM recapped Session 40. The lift docked at a highway leading to the vault, buildings on both sides, cave-ins above, **large dwarven statues at a crossroads, and a detention drone on patrol.** No organic life; the constructs still keep their routines.
- **Sealed doors** have no handles: stone slabs on a steam-pressure lock. "She has a pulse. She just needs to be turned on."
- Thimble (Perception 20): the drone is a peacekeeper still on its route, part construct and part magic, with orbs and something storing a spell.

**The warehouse**
- A half-open roll-up door. Rec slipped in and backed out: it's full of **worker constructs.**
- **Alaric cast *Arcane Eye*.** The constructs all have **blindsight** and tracked the eye, but didn't react. Natural 20 Investigation: a shipping warehouse, no controls, and **a faint arcane signature behind a door at the back.**
- Thimble and Bog walked in. Thimble offered a spider a ration; it scanned it and carried it through a hatch to **the commissary**, where a sphere in a hairnet runs the line (**the lunch lady**). Bog gave a sphere a dirty water dog; same result. The hatches scan for each construct's arcane signature, like inventory.
- **Thimble handed the centurion a box** (Deception 15 with advantage); it bowed to him as a dwarf-sized worker and carried it off.
- Bog asked out loud, "Are we not opening this door?" A sphere adjusted its wrist and **opened the storage closet** for him.
- Thimble (Arcana 16, with Rec) found the signature under a box: **a locked wooden hatch. Bog chopped through it (12).** Inside, **a worker's hidden stash: 237 sp and a *pipe of smoke monsters*** (bone, carved like a winged lizard's head). Thimble kept the pipe and gave Bog 150 sp. **Rec swallowed the empty chest.**

**The detention drone**
- Aalyn waved at the drone: "Anomaly detected." **It fired an orb that expands into a sealed bubble** (CON DC 15). He saved.
- Wayne psionically linked Alaric, Aalyn and Thimble for four hours.
- **Aalyn ran off the bridge, dropped 50 feet and flew under it on his boots.** The drone followed: **air sigils on its boots and back let it fly.** He broke line of sight among the arches and statues and hid behind a statue with *Misty Step*.
- Later the drone rose back up carrying a special spider trapped in its own bubble. **Alaric threw a card from his deck: a knight illusion** made to look like the drone, and cast *Blink*.
- **Bog walked up, got hit with an orb and sealed in a bubble.** Behind the drone, Aalyn (Perception 21) found **a panel on its back: "emergency override controls."** He licked his fingers and popped it open (Sleight of Hand 22): **shut down.** The drone fell 120 feet, the bubble burst, and Bog was free. "Guys, I'm so sick of these robots."

**Wayne's door**
- Wayne spent the session staring at the sealed door, then remembered **"Rob Dyrdek"**: walk through a door forehead and knee at the same time. **He spent his free natural 20 on it. The stone door fractured and crumbled to sand around him.** "There was never even a door there to begin with."
- **The boiler room:** dormant boilers, spheres maintaining them, and a control panel with levers, switches and **ignition keys.**
- **Pebbles pickpocketed a key from a sphere** (after a cog and a spare blade) and waddled it over. Wayne took a second key (Sleight of Hand 35) and 3 sp. Pebbles tried to fly and flopped.
- With two of three keys they guessed right: **the boilers fired up**, steam ran through the pipes, and **doors around the floor opened.**

**The Steam Nexus**
- Alaric's opened door led to **the Steam Nexus** (labeled in Dwarvish): a large sky-blue glass vessel with pumps and pipes, meant to direct the boilers' steam through the facility. **It needs an activation key.**
- A special spider sprang from the crates. Its orb flew at Alaric; he **knocked it back** and the spider sealed itself in its own bubble. He mage-handed it off the edge.

**The maintenance chasm**
- Thimble and Wayne went toward **the maintenance room**: a collapsed area of metal catwalks bolted to the cavern walls over a pit at least 300 feet deep.
- Wayne (Strength 10) caught the first catwalk with his gut. Then he tried to teach Pebbles to fly: the girder sank, he stumbled, and **threw her 75 degrees straight down into the steam.**
- **Wayne dove after her.** Thimble tried to throw him his ring of feather falling. Three Acrobatics checks (17, 21, 21) closed the gap. **Pebbles, falling, spoke into his mind for the first time: "Dad."** He caught her toe 30 feet from the ground.
- **The DM awarded Wayne the Feat of the Kyn'Ruthar.** He flipped and telekinetically pushed himself back up so hard the floor below caved in, and shot out of the clouds past Thimble holding Pebbles. They fell nearly **1,000 feet.** Pebbles is shaken but safe and hugged him with her wings. "She called me dad and then I went super saiyan."
- **Thimble's Session 40 surge: his hair is six inches longer.** A mullet.
- Thimble crossed the catwalks with his boots and feather fall. A steam vent burst behind him.

**The crossroads and the vault**
- The center of the crossroads is **a retractable grated platform over a stone slab**, with **four braziers** piped for gas, dim until the Steam Nexus is on.
- **The vault door:** a silver slab, no mechanism. **Four empty capsules on stone pedestals** beside it, and **a statue with a panel of colored wires and numbers.**
- Aalyn tried Rob Dyrdek on the vault: 1 bludgeoning damage.
- Another door down the side walkway: **the pipe chamber**, triggered by pipes running beneath the bridge.

**The foreman's office**
- Alaric stacked boxes as cover, cast *Knock* on **the foreman's office** and hid himself behind *Minor Illusion*.
- **A key ring: an arcane key (like a key fob) and a bronze key.** Neither is for the Steam Nexus.

**The mephits**
- Thimble's dwarven treasure map has **an X in the maintenance area.**
- Past the catwalks, **steam mephits** coalesced out of the steam: **the Runelands' ley lines run through here and have chaotically charged the steam.** They sensed something like themselves in Thimble.
- Thimble taunted all six together (Performance 15) and opened the tome. **Using the Tome of Malcanthet as a spellcasting focus, he cast a 4th-level *Thunderwave*; against fiends it deals maximum damage: 40 to each.** All six failed and were pushed 10 feet, and their death bursts went off harmlessly. Two sonic booms in one session.
- Thimble asked if he heard a voice. "Maybe not yet."
- Aalyn flew to a missed side passage and found **a hatch**. Alaric is flickering in and out of the ethereal plane (*Blink*). Session ends.

**After the session**
- The DM: Aalyn's missing-patron names are coming. **Papa Glabb's songbook isn't magical.** The Pale Codex works under the right conditions. The dungeon has been built since mid-February.
- "Wayne did not open any door."
