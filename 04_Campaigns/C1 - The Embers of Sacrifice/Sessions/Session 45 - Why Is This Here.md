# Session 45: Why Is This Here

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 44 - And I Click 5 Again]], [[Durnhollow]], [[King Atul Stonemarch]], [[Vashara]], [[Malcanthet]], [[The Demonomicon]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "Why Is This Here" (Episode 45)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kEm0c2JF_3I). Alaric showed the party his silver dragon egg. Thimble solved the second pump; Wayne and Alaric reversed the turbine. Wayne went off alone (with Bog) and started the third pump under ceiling ooze, finding a second enhancement chamber. The others fell through a broken pipe into a flooded archive: plaques to King Atul Stonemarch and a diplomatic pact for his marriage to a drow princess and the gift of four elementals for silver research. Behind a door with a new ward, Thimble's *Shatter* opened a hidden study: potions, an electrified rat, a teleportation circle in heavy use, a black statue of Malcanthet, and a moss pool whose mirrored drow star-map records, in Abyssal, a lunar eclipse from just days ago. Thimble spoke Malcanthet's true name from his tome and the statue gave him a medallion with a face. Then each of them looked into the pool, failed, and lived a vision before falling back into the turbine, rested. The fourth pump opened the conduit room.

## What Happened at the Table

**Recap**
- The DM recapped Session 44. The air soulvite shard is in a bag of holding. Each ale barrel weighs 70 lb, and a bag of holding holds 500.

**The egg**
- In the cold, wet air, **Alaric took out the silver dragon egg** in front of everyone. Thimble, from a noble magical family, knows a dragon egg's aura on sight: pristine silver scales, icicles floating off it. Alaric "just found it." He asked Wayne for dad advice.
- **Pebbles is shivering.** Offered Alaric's old cloak or Thimble's pants, she picked **Thimble's pants** and wore them backwards. Thimble is pantsless for the rest of the session.
- Aalyn loaded **three barrels of dwarven ale** (70 lb each) into his bag of holding.

**The second pump**
- Thimble started the second pump's pipe puzzle; the table solved it. **Two of the four lights** on the sealed south door off the turbine turned green.
- Wayne and Alaric pulled the turbine levers together: **the turbine reversed direction.** The party rode the pipes in new directions.

**Wayne's pump**
- Wayne (with Bog trailing him) came out of a pipe into **a grimy, water-damaged maintenance room** behind a cave-in, with **the third pump**.
- He solved its puzzle with 30 seconds to spare, then caught a wrong piece with a DC 25 Sleight of Hand. **Pockets of gray ooze on the ceiling**, full of corroded tools, jiggled but didn't fall.
- The pump started; a light came on; blast doors opened on **another enhancement chamber**, its water churning but idle. **Wayne started the fourth pump as well.**
- Bright lights came on and blinded him through his night goggles.

**The flooded archive**
- The others took a leak in a pipe and fell 30 feet into **a dark flooded room**: collapsed dwarven stonework, iron shelving below the water, steps out on one side.
- Alaric lit the water with *Control Flame* and guided Aalyn with currents (*Control Water*). Thimble pulled a brazier to his hand by magic and lit it.
- **Underwater plaques** (Aalyn cleaned them, 20): **"By order of King Atul Stonemarch, third king of Durnhollow." "This facility is meant to last a thousand years." "May the deep stone hold."** Dated about **the year 400.** Carvings show the king with a chiseled jaw and a scruffy beard.
- **A sealed iron case.** Alaric heated and froze it; Aalyn smashed it open with his warhammer (proficient). Some pages were lost. In Dwarvish and Elvish, **a diplomatic pact**:
  - **a marriage compact between the king and a princess of the drow**, and
  - **a research agreement for enhanced silver, with the gift of four elemental beings to aid it.**
- "I really hope there's not three more of those down here."
- Wayne's link reached them: he's off by himself (Bog with him). Alaric told him to keep on the main quest.

**The warded door**
- A stone door with **a ward newer than the door itself**; the door is old and feeble.
- Alaric (Detect Magic, Arcana 17) also sensed **something that scared him, to the north, roughly where Wayne had been: an aberration, not elemental. Something that could end the party, and something very powerful.** Wayne: "Maybe it was that jelly on the roof."
- Alaric's Fire Bolt at the door lit the ward's fire sigils and burned him (8); he healed with the *Cure Wounds* in his ring. His Mage Hand rotted to dust when it touched the ward.
- **Thimble cast *Shatter* on a point behind the ward.** The door fell in depowered pieces. Then **his surge (83): he glowed like the sun and blinded everyone for a minute.**

**The study**
- Potions: Thimble a **potion of healing and fire resistance**; Alaric **greater healing and heroism**; Aalyn **healing and a basic poison**; later Bog a healing potion (drank it) and Wayne a greater healing (drank it).
- **A glass vessel holding a rat in a solution, crackling with electricity.**
- **A statue of dark stone**, unlike the dwarven stone: Thimble (religion) knows it as **Malcanthet**. It has a closed drawer.
- **A stone circle: a teleportation circle, scorched from frequent use.**
- **A moss grotto with a still pool**, streams running in, **purple flowers** in the rocks, and a ceiling carved with **constellations in drow script, mirrored.** In the pool's reflection (Arcana), notes hidden in **Abyssal**: lunar movements. Thimble read it with the tome (17): **a lunar eclipse, dated just a few days ago.** From Oakhollow, Thimble remembers rumors that **an eclipse came out of nowhere while the party was underground.** Either someone was here recently, or it was written in fate.
- Aalyn picked some of the purple flowers. Alaric didn't recognize them.

**The medallion**
- Wayne and Bog played cards ("castle"); Bog ate a dirty-water-dog card sandwich (8 damage from paper cuts) and gave Wayne **35 gp** for losing. Then they rode a pipe and fell into the archive pool too.
- **Thimble spoke Malcanthet's true name from his tome at the statue.** Its eyes glowed crimson and the drawer slid out: **a medallion on a necklace, radial, with a face in the center.** Like his ring of mind shielding, **it contains something.** Nothing happened when he took it.

**The pool**
- **Aalyn**, meditating with his harp, saw **a figure reflected upside down in the water**: his patron. He asked if the thread is here. A wide grin: it isn't. Wisdom save failed (12): he was standing in the pool and his friends were gone. Asked what one thing he would say that could echo through the planes for anyone to hear: **"Stay."** He fell through bubbles into the central turbine, long-rested.
- **Alaric** stepped in looking for him: **his brother's reflection reached down to take his hand** and he sank. Asked what truth about himself he'd want: **when he'll be seen as an adult, like his brother.** The answer feels further away than he'd like.
- **Wayne and Thimble** jumped in holding hands. A shared bad trip: **Wayne watched Thimble crack and explode in arcane energy; Thimble watched Wayne try to calm him and explode too, scattered like stardust.** They woke holding each other in tears. "Everything we've been through."
- **Bog**: a tiny reptile child in a tree, chased by his boogeyman, **a huge, dripping, non-alcoholic slug.**
- All five fell into the central turbine and **took a long rest.** Nobody talked for a while.

**The conduit room**
- Alaric's tingle is stronger, still to the north.
- With all four pumps running (Wayne started the last two), the south door off the turbine opened: a small chamber with **four pumps feeding a central conduit.**
- Thimble went back to the study: **the teleportation circle is in frequent use.** Alaric is reading the books through Rec.
- **Wayne and Bog walked onto the conduit room's center.** Nothing yet. Session ends.

**After the session**
- Alaric's player wants to keep exploring the study. The party plans to destroy the teleportation circle so nobody else uses it.
