# Session 8: Gnomes and Fireballs

**Party:** Level 5 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 7 - The Goose is Loose]], [[Bragthar]], [[Glimmerstone]], [[The Tall Oak Forest]]

> [!important] Played: "Gnomes and Fireballs" (Episode 8)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=48sV4q_S2-w). Bragthar fell back out of the sky, Wayne found a goose egg, the party found a hidden shrine to "the Architect," and on the road home they met a wagon of Glimmerstone gnomes. Aalyn's rap battle for the wagon ended in a tie, and one of the gnomes, Muzzle, swung a bat at Thimble for killing his sister. Ends at initiative.

## What Happened at the Table

**Aftermath at the summit**
- Night, rain. The calcification has stopped, but everything already turned stays stone. Where the illusory ancient tree stood there is now just a hole.
- Bragthar reappeared in the sky right where he had vanished and started to fall. Alaric cast Feather Fall on him, his first time using it.
- Wayne checked his satchel (16): **a magical goose egg**, laid as reality was being rewritten, and perhaps changed by it the way the goose was. Wayne cracked his first smile.

**The hidden shrine**
- Thimble cast Detect Magic with sorcery points and sensed something in ruins on the far side of the hill, hidden in tree cover.
- Older and more decrepit than the other ruins: a collapsed multi-level building whose staircase is **visually inverted**, climbing up while leading down. It looked like the space where they fought the Baron in his monstrous form.
- Alaric's natural 20 Arcana: a voice through the ether said **"Trust in the design,"** and he sensed very faint **planar magic**, as if this was a conduit or access point to another space.
- Thimble (Investigation 21, then natural 20) found a mossy shrine with crumbled figures and, in a hidden compartment beneath it, **a blue sapphire**.
- Thimble's natural 20 Religion (Glimmerstone noble schooling): five figures should be there. One is intact and labeled in Common: **"The Architect."**
- Alaric's follow-up Arcana was a 2. He sensed something that might help make illusory fields and couldn't make sense of it.

**The bean**
- Thimble planted his first bean in the hole where the lithoweave had been (d100: 53). Steam, then four bright pink toads. He touched two, and they became **owlbears**, indifferent to him. After a minute they vanished in pink dust. **Thimble has 8 beans left.**

**The night**
- Alaric took first watch, Aalyn second. The rain eased.
- **New DM mechanic:** reflection prompts during rests (a d100 roll). Aalyn's (81): what his name means to him. For most of his life it was just a name he was born with. As his talent grew and his fame took off, it became a badge of honor, and when he left home he shed his old life and became someone new.
- At dawn Aalyn gave heroic inspiration to Alaric and Wayne. Privately he had a small crisis of confidence: his magic has always been music, and this is beyond him. He buried it and played something fun as the others woke.
- Alaric's owl hasn't come back from Oakhollow yet.

**Morning**
- Aalyn asked if they were ever going to talk about everything (the brands, the ring machine). Alaric checked his book of hero stories: it all checks out, and he's surprised no one has died yet.
- Wayne found his pebbles had grown back in, and figured out that Alaric had offered them to the core.
- Bog's morning constitutional: a 10-foot run-up, a spin and a somersault (natural 20 Performance, Acrobatics 23), launched into the back of Aalyn's head. Aalyn cleaned up with Prestidigitation without turning around. Wayne wrote the technique down.
- Alaric cast **Fireball** for the first time, growing it from the flame of Wayne's tinderbox with Control Flames and throwing it down the path over Aalyn's head. Thimble answered with a Distant Spell Fireball 300 feet out. Aalyn's eyes flashed orange at Thimble.

**The trap wagon**
- A self-propelled wooden wagon patched with scrap metal and tin, no horse, blasting bass-heavy trap music. Five gnomes from Glimmerstone in flat caps:
  - **Chunks:** big for a gnome (about 4 feet), in scrappy plate.
  - **Dipple:** the beatboxer, Chunks's partner.
  - **Fizzle:** a twitchy woman.
  - **Muzzle** and **Greece:** up front.
- Aalyn told them that wasn't real music. The exchange got personal, and his jabs led Chunks to call him a homophobe. Aalyn decided he wanted the wagon and challenged Chunks to a **rap battle** for it.
- Bog, Aalyn, Wayne, Alaric and Bragthar all climbed in. Alaric's Mending restored the wagon, kicking on underglow lights and a bouncier suspension.
- Greece sold Thimble three blunts of Glimmerstone "Hello Glint" for 27 gp (9 each), then quietly asked him to hang back at the front.
- The battle: round one a wash (15 each). Round two to Aalyn (21 to 18). In round three Chunks rolled a natural 20 with a verse of pity for Aalyn; Aalyn's final verse (24) was punctuated by a Pocket Phoenix. **Tied.** It goes to sudden death next session.
- Meanwhile Bog told Fizzle that Greece had been going through her bag, and the two of them hooked up under the wagon.

**Muzzle**
- At the front, Muzzle recognized **Thimblewick Danglesworth** by his House Danglesworth robe. He grew up downwind of the uplifts in Glimmerstone. His grandmother kept a portrait of the Danglesworths over the sink, called them "the jewels of Glimmerstone," and said Thimble's father was the smartest man ever to step into the council hall.
- On the night of the **Luminox Convergence** (once a year, when the sky turns violet; said to be the ley lines reflecting in the geode city), House Danglesworth threw a gala. Muzzle's older sister **Zenina** was working it, her first job, to buy him a school uniform. She was carrying a tray of sugared figs when Thimble's wild magic surged out of the ballroom: chandeliers to dust, fireworks and lightning, the ice sculptures melted. She was killed, and they swept her up with a dustpan. The Danglesworths sent only an apology.
- "This is for my sister." Muzzle swung a bat at Thimble.
- **Initiative:** Wayne 16, Aalyn 15, Thimble 12, Alaric 10, Bog 9, Bragthar 8.

**Thimble's chaos die**
- It keeps stepping down with each leveled spell: d10 at Detect Magic, then d8 at his Fireball, and it is a d6 next.
