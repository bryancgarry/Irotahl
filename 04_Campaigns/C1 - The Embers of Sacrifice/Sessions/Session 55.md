## Recap

> [!readaloud]
> At the summit of the Hollow Reliquary, a heart of crystal and void was beating, and out of it rose the remnant of the Hollow Vessel. It dragged you into its domain: a sphere of black obsidian under an eclipse, held together by three stone faces drinking the souls of every pilgrim who ever climbed this spire. Body. Mind. Soul.
>
> You broke them one by one. Wayne's dagger split the face of the Body and brought light into the dark. Aalyn's blasts shattered the Soul, and when the void lashed back, a cloak of gold turned it to sunlight. Bog leapt from the heights and broke the giant's knee. Thimble burned it with sickening light and fell for it, and Aalyn's song pulled him back. And Alaric, with Rec at his shoulder, put a ray of fire through the face of the Mind.
>
> With nothing left to hold it, the Remnant made its last stand. Grey dust breathed out across the room, and the Stilling came for all of you. Only one of you couldn't shake it.
>
> Wayne, you were turning to stone again, and this time no one was coming to wake you. But you remembered a mountaintop, an old dwarf with a cane, and a pot of goat stew. *One of these days, you will fly.* And the stone broke off you, darker than before, and you rose out of the shadow and put your hand through the Remnant's heart.
>
> When it was gone, you were holding a shell of black stone with the void moving inside it. The Reliquary groaned. The stone began to crack.
>
> And someone spoke, very close, just behind your shoulder.
>
> *"Oh, how perfect you are."*

## Intro Notes

> [!info] DM Note: the shape of the opening
> Malcanthet first, then the memory, then the Collapse. Planning beyond this is still to do.

**1. Malcanthet speaks to Wayne**
- Her dialogue sets him on the road to becoming another **fully realized Hollow Vessel**, now that he holds the Shell. She's tender, delighted, and certain.
- The other Godhand beats from Session 54's section 3 can fold in here or be cut. Decide which.

**2. The bottom of the Void Trial**
- The Shell, or her presence, opens the door to the memory he's never had: the **full memory of the bottom of the Rikk Borgar**.
- There he found the **first half of [[The Soul Armor]]**, and from it he learns he is the **Kyn'Ruthar**.
- The Soul Armor works like the Kingsoul: the second half is found by walking the **Tyrn'Haka**, the Endless Climb. The climb itself is the Kyn'Ruthar's journey.
- The same moment that makes Malcanthet think she's won gives Wayne his other road.

**3. Then the Collapse**
- Carried over from [[Session 54 - The Heart of the Void]] (section 5), adjusted for Atul on the Soul floor and the conversation running while the house falls.
