# Session 42: Tempting Fate

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 41 - A Man and His Goose]], [[Durnhollow]], [[Clausius Clapeyron]], [[Calendar of Irotahl]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "Tempting Fate" (Episode 42)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sDB2hi2XRac). Aalyn found pipes of haunting behind a hatch. Thimble got blasted by a steam pipe and felt the steam was alive. Bog solved a prime-number code (43), Alaric set the pipe valves, Thimble found the square Steam Nexus key in the maintenance room, and the whole table solved Wayne's color-flow puzzle to bring the power back. Aalyn's arcane key opened a testing lab with Clausius Clapeyron's research journal: an enhancement chamber that infuses an item with arcane steam through silver. It turned Aalyn's +1 longsword into a +2 silvered blade with wind runes, cooled in Bog's cold room. Thimble then fed it his winged boots and 256 gp of gold instead of silver: it exploded, and he went down, failed two death saves, then made three. The boots are gone and he has lightning scars. Alaric found a ring of warmth. The pipe chamber opened and a column of steam lifted the party to the Tempest Warden.

## What Happened at the Table

**Recap**
- The DM recapped Session 41: the warehouse, Thimble's pipe, the key ring, Wayne's door and dive, Aalyn shutting down the drone, and Thimble's mephits.

**Aalyn's hatch**
- Aalyn opened the ceiling hatch (Sleight of Hand 16, Rec helping). A crawl space led to a dislodged chest: **pipes of haunting**, bone pipes with bird skulls on the ends.
- Aalyn and Thimble compared pipes. Thimble puffed his and a little dragon floated out.

**The live steam**
- Thimble touched a crack in the wall; a pebble fell out and **steam blasted him** (6 fire). It's a pipe lodged in the wall.
- **Natural 20 Investigation:** from Glimmerstone's vents he knows steam, and **this steam feels alive, tickling the arcane in him. If he hadn't already resisted the fog outside Hollowmere, it might have given him hallucinations.**
- Wayne asked Thimble if he'd ever seen a glory hole.

**The codes and valves**
- Bog and Alaric came out of the foreman's office under *Minor Illusion*. Bog wanted the big door open; Alaric: the power isn't on yet.
- **The four capsules at the vault** sit on stone pedestals with metal fittings and glass, each piped to the door, made to hold something and channel its energy.
- The pipe chamber door has four pipes feeding it.
- **The Steam Nexus** has four panels around **a square keyhole**: an admin key that lets it run. Wayne's passive Investigation (20) says it isn't in the crates.
- Through a mage-handed door, Alaric and Bog found a room with two levers and a console with **a code missing its last digit: 1, 3, 5, 11, 21.** Bog, who can't read, punched in **43.** It worked. "Magic boy."
- Both levers opened two rooms:
  - **The cold room:** cooling coils and frost. **Bog found a greasy note from the foreman:** "C holds two, same as always. Pipe B always runs double pipe C. A runs two below D. Get at least 20 across all four. Get it wrong, seal's going to blow."
  - **The valve room:** four dials, A to D, eight positions each. **Alaric set A 6, B 4, C 2, D 8.** No steam yet.

**The maintenance room**
- Thimble and Aalyn crossed the catwalks.
- **The arcane door** to the lab takes a key swipe; Rec ferried Alaric's arcane key to Aalyn.
- **Thimble (Investigation 22) found the square key** in a chest in the maintenance room, with **an old opened can of dwarven beer**, **a set of thieves' tools** for Aalyn, and **spare construct parts.** Rec carried the key to Wayne.

**The Steam Nexus**
- Wayne slotted the key in. The panels lit up with colored dots: **a color-flow puzzle** in four quadrants. The whole table worked it, Wayne's player through the colors.
- Done: the dwarven lights danced, the pipes filled, and **the gauge read 20 exactly.** **The power is on.** A once-locked door opened somewhere.

**The testing lab**
- **Aalyn's arcane key opened the lab:** a researcher's desk with notes, buttons and dials, and behind blast glass **an apparatus with dwarven clamps like a metal tree around an empty center**, arcane glyphs on the floor, steam pipes and fans, scorch marks from old experiments.
- **The research journal of Clausius Clapeyron**, a dwarven metallurgist (posted on Discord). Its calendar uses **13 months of 30 days** (Frostmark is a winter month). **The year in the journal is 412, roughly a thousand years ago.**
- When the power came on, the chamber started up on its own and went red. Alaric, through Rec, read the notes and talked Thimble and Aalyn through venting the pressure (Arcana with inspiration). The machine finished its cycle and unlocked.
- **It's an enhancement chamber**: it infuses an item (up to about two feet across) with arcane steam, channeled through silver.
- Aalyn found **five silver ingots and 60 sp.**
- **Aalyn put in his +1 longsword with four ingots.** At 75% a siren went off; both slammed green buttons and a stabilizer kicked in. Lightning in the cloud, then **a +2 silvered longsword etched with runes.** It requires attunement.
- The blade was too hot to hold steady and the runes started flaking. **Aalyn flew it to Bog's cold room** (thermal gloves, CON with advantage, then Antarctic gear) and **the runes set.**
- **Alaric's *Identify*: Auran runes, wind elemental sigils. Once attuned, a limited number of times per day, it can throw ranged air slashes.** The DM will set the details.

**Thimble and the gold**
- Thimble wanted to try **gold instead of silver.** The DM: the same effect would take about 64 gp per bar, **256 gp.** Rec rammed him. Alaric: they used silver for a reason. Thimble's Arcana (15): magic going awry is nothing new to him.
- Aalyn left: "Why have two of us die when only one of us dies?" Alaric recalled Rec. Wayne, by link: "Take life by the balls."
- **Thimble put his winged boots in with the gold** and pressed go. Warnings at 25%. He tried to rev it like a manual gearbox (Sleight of Hand 18): wrong sequence. **The coins floated, lightning chained between them, the boots burst into flame, and it exploded.** Gold shot through the blast glass like bullets (3), the lightning caught him (18), the blast threw him into the wall (7).
- **Thimble dropped. Death saves: two failures, then three successes.** Aalyn flew back and **healed him to full with the rod of resurrection** (Alaric had said, "Worst comes to worst, just use your wand").
- Lying there, Thimble alone felt that **if he had let go at the brink, the release might have killed his friends too.**
- **The boots are gone.** He got **3 gp** back of the 256. He keeps both eyes; his monocle is cracked, and he has **lightning scars on his neck and face, coins melted into his skin.** The machine is wrecked.
- Thimble: "This world must atone for its sins." He keeps blowing things up. Wayne: "Now no one gets to use it ever again except us." Thimble: "Fuck this world and everybody in it except for us." He wants a new tome.
- Aalyn carried him out like a baby.

**Alaric's offices**
- Alaric cast *Knock* on **a logistics office.** He found **logistics for the floors below** (the DM will reveal them when they get there) and, under the desk, a hatch (upcast *Knock*): **a huge chest with a single ring of warmth.**
- The bronze key still has no door.

**The pipe chamber**
- With the power on, **the pipe chamber door opened.** Inside: the inside of a steam pipe, flanked by heads. Steam built beneath them and **lifted the whole party** up through a grate.
- Above: steam swirling, **a metallic silver shard floating at the center**, and **a huge creature of steam** looming over them as a wave came down. **The Tempest Warden.** Session ends.

**After the session**
- Two automatic fails would have ruined the chamber: anything but silver, or a true magic item. The +1 sword counted as merely enhanced.
- Birthdays were picked on the 13-month calendar (month 6 day 15, month 2 day 22).
