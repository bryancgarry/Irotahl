# Session 49: The Rock God

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]]); [[Bogtrotter]] absent (asleep)
**Ref:** [[Session 48 - Magic Mushroom Fun Pt. 2]], [[Durnhollow]], [[Myra]], [[Rec]], [[Soulvite]]

> [!important] Played: "The Rock God" (Episode 49)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=747zsYydKJs). The quarry fight ended inside a round: Wayne grew huge, Aalyn disguised himself as stone and named Wayne their god, and Alaric's illusion gave the Rock God his voice. The stone commander crumbled and Wayne took his axe. Hasted and with Thimble's *Shatter*, Wayne headbutted straight through a wall into a dead dwarven silver refinery. Thimble found a star ruby, Myra pulled an amulet of the drunkard out of a cave-in for Aalyn, and Wayne and Thimble got a keycard off a droid caught on the toilet. Beyond it: a channeling chamber around a great engraved block of silver. The party restarted the refinery and made Myra its manager. Wayne then pushed Aalyn down a minecart track, and he came out 300 feet above a lake of lava.

## What Happened at the Table

**Recap**
- The DM recapped Sessions 47 and 48. Thimble's flesh construct is gone. Bog is still asleep in Myra's pod; his player is still on his honeymoon.
- **Thimble's bath in the Feywild counts as a short rest** (Wisdom save 20, with advantage).
- Initiative: Wayne 22, Myra 16, Alaric 13, Aalyn 5, Thimble 5.

**The quarry**
- The **stone berserker commander** grew a chunk of rock into stone javelins and threw three: two at Myra, one across the map at Thimble. All missed.
- Rocky enemies make Wayne's Stealth and Deception rolls with advantage. **Wayne got big** (Large, about 12 feet), vaulted the wall, hid as a moving piece of wall (29), and threw a dagger through a gargoyle's neck (19). Pebbles peeked out at "big mama."
- Gargoyles clawed at Thimble (missed) and Myra (14 total). Myra hit back with mushroom-hammer hands; the poison does nothing to stone.
- A berserker ran up on Wayne with a stone axe. Wayne: "Friendly fire, man." It stood down.
- **Alaric took Wayne's hand, cast *Haste* on him**, and wrapped himself in a *Minor Illusion* of stone on Wayne's chest, hidden with Rec's help.
- Aalyn's light blinded the gargoyle next to him. **He cast *Disguise Self* to look like stone too** and shouted at the commander: **"Can you not see your stone god?"** Deception 23. The commander saw a bright stone figure and Wayne, an enormous stone mass, and called off the attack on them. Every eye turned to Myra and Thimble.
- **Aalyn: Myra and Thimble are "our prisoners."**
- **Alaric gave the Rock God a voice** with *Minor Illusion* and *Shape Sand*: **"Behold, I am the Rock God, Wayne Kerr. My subjects, I implore you to cease fire. If not, you shall face my wrath."** Thimble played the disgruntled prisoner.
- Group performance (DC 10): **Alaric rolled a natural 20.** The voice shook stalactites loose. **Combat ended.** The gargoyles went back to their perches; a berserker carrying another blasting charge walked it back around the corner.
- Aalyn cast *Hold Person* on Thimble to sell it (he saved, and stormed off). Wayne: "Return to your slumber, friends. We mean you no harm." Aalyn: Thimble is "ours to deal with." Persuasion 27.
- **The commander jammed his axe into the stone, sat down and crumbled into pebbles.** Wayne pulled out **his stone greataxe** (Athletics 15) and put it in his bag of holding.
- The gargoyles fall to **gargoyle powder**. Alaric knows it is ground dragon eggs encased in volcanic ash, used for petrification magic, and isn't interested.

**The wall**
- The doors don't open. Wayne, still hasted, climbed up for a run-up; Thimble cast *Shatter* on the door (19) as he came.
- **Wayne charged** (280 feet of movement) and **Rob Deered in midair**, knee and forehead: Athletics with "super advantage," DC 12. He drilled clean through.
- He'd gone **through the wall between two of the doors**, not a door. ("We don't need a door.") The doors on the far side were open the whole time. Haste ran out and Wayne lay on the floor, the most tired he's ever been, for about six seconds.
- Thimble's surge (16): nothing. He is a d10 next time.

**The refinery**
- **A dead dwarven ore factory**: minecart rails from the quarry, grinding mills, purifying basins and pools, cranes, a gantry. Nothing runs, but the spheres and spiders still carry empty boxes from place to place.
- **Thimble searched the hole Wayne made** and spent his heroic inspiration (22): **a star ruby**, red with a white star at its center. Arcana: not magical; an offering to a dwarven lord or deity. Wayne asked if they can implant it in him.
- Aalyn checked his treasure map: **a marked spot just left of the rubble.**

**The power room**
- Rec opened a deadbolted door: **a power generator** for the filtering pools. Alaric (Investigation 1) couldn't make sense of it and called for help.

**Wayne and Thimble**
- A break room with a closed-off elevator shaft. Wayne found **the next door trapped** (Investigation 23): a wire to an alarm and defenses. **He disarmed it** (24).
- A storeroom. Behind the next door, **a sphere droid sitting on a toilet, passing oil and stone bits.** It covered itself with its sword arms and rolled off.
- The next door was **keycard-locked.** Three identical droids. Wayne's Insight spotted the confused one; Thimble's *Prestidigitation* keycard and toilet charade (Performance 17) made the other droid point at the guilty one, who ran. Thimble tripped over a bench. **Wayne caught it** (27), carried it back to the toilet to finish, and it handed over **the keycard.**
- Beyond: a caved-in room of bookshelves and research gear, then a barred blast door, then **a round chamber**: a runic floor like the steam and water layers' enhancement rooms but more magical, **eight pedestals**, and **a huge engraved block** in the middle. Dwarvish runes: enhance, strengthen, channel, containment.
- Thimble (Arcana 18): **a channeling rock**, part of a ritual for drawing energy in from around it and pouring it into something.

**Aalyn and Myra**
- At the map's spot, Myra threaded her fungal tendrils through the rubble and found **a crate.** She pulled out what was in it in a bloom of mushroom: **an amulet of the drunkard.** Aalyn kept it and promised to pay her later, "when we get out of here."

**Restarting the refinery**
- Alaric and Rec found the minecart tunnel and loading crane; ore in the pools is **silver ore**, ground and polished but crude.
- At the generator, Wayne talked to it and reached into its works (Sleight of Hand, then Performance 12); Thimble plugged a missing gap with two of his clockwork devices. Both rolled over 5 on a flat d20.
- **The refinery came back to life**: mills grinding, carts running, basins flowing, the droids working again. **It refines silver ore.**

**Myra's business deal**
- The party offered Myra the job of **general manager of their refinery.** She doesn't like the sun and doesn't mind staying. The party owns it; she gets a salary and royalties.
- Alaric's pitch: tell Professor Bifflefapple discreetly and have him line up buyers; there's always a market for silver. No laws cover it; it was buried for a thousand years.
- Group Persuasion (13) passed. Myra asked quiet Wayne his role: **"I'm the face of the company."** Thimble started sketching an advertisement.
- Myra shook all four hands at once and held Thimble's a little longer. **She'll follow the party through the dungeon, then stay to run the place.** Alaric wants a proper contract drafted.
- Thimble took **three chunks of silver ore.**

**The channeling chamber**
- Everyone went to the round chamber. Alaric (Investigation 26, History) and Aalyn (24):
  - **The engraved block is a huge piece of silver.**
  - This room is **the top of a much larger chamber below**, a maintenance room. The pedestals take fuel: put something of an element on one and its property is channeled through the silver into whatever lies below.
  - The way down is likely the last door, to the south. **Don't blow up this room.**
- Thimble touched the silver: **his surge die reset to a d20.**
- Everyone agreed: try it if they understand it, otherwise leave it alone. Thimble wants the Danglesworth name in history.
- Thimble left a note on the door: **"Elemental infusion chamber. Do not explode."**
- Talk turned to Luna, who they still mean to save. It is still the first day in Durnhollow, about four hours in.

**The minecarts**
- With the refinery running, **four minecart lines opened** by a barred iron door to the south. The air is getting hotter.
- Aalyn clicked his boots and got in a cart. **Wayne pushed him.**
- Alaric: the next step in refining is heating the ore. Wayne may have pushed Aalyn into the furnace. Alaric sent Rec after him at full speed and watched through Rec's eyes. Aalyn is out of the psionic link.
- **Aalyn** rattled through dark tunnels and burst out **300 feet above a lake of multicolored lava**, the rails winding down around it like a roller coaster, ballistas along the path, and **a chest.** Session ends.

**After the session**
- Wayne's **ring of water walking** works on lava (heat still burns).
- Aalyn may give the amulet of the drunkard to Bog. The table wants a downtime session to trade magic items.
- The campaign so far: about five days in the first city, a week and a half on the road, and a few days in Hollowmere.
