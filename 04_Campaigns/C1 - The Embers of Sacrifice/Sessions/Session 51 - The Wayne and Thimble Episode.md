# Session 51: The Wayne and Thimble Episode

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]]); [[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]] and [[Bogtrotter]] not in this session
**Ref:** [[Session 50 - Into the Inferno]], [[Vistro IV]], [[The Fire Sages of Vistro]], [[The Demonomicon]], [[The Mind Medallion]], [[King Atul Stonemarch]], [[Durnhollow]]

> [!important] Played: "The Wayne and Thimble Episode"
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CfeMRJDNu3Y). Just Wayne and Thimble. Vistro's trident hit Wayne once, and they surrendered the tome, which shrugged off his trident. They were towed to a salamander city hidden in a great lava pillar and locked in a stalactite prison beside Old Ash Tongue, who sang them a song about the Kyn'Ruthar. They broke out between the bars' pulses, threw a guard into the lava, and walked out in his cloak, straight into a trial by combat. They won it to a roaring crowd. Vistro recognized Thimble's medallion as King Atul's, gave back the tome, and asked them to return it to the temple. In return he would lower the barrier, and he was lying about what his sages' ritual can do. The fire sages tattooed Vistro's crest on Thimble's palm and Nerevath on Wayne's.

## What Happened at the Table

**Surrender**
- Initiative. **Vistro's trident** hit Wayne at 30 to hit: 17 piercing (8 after Uncanny Dodge), then 11 fire as it burst from his side.
- **Wayne dropped his daggers. Thimble threw the tome on the ground.** Vistro's held trident struck the tome, and **a maroon barrier over the book threw the flame up into the sky.** "It must be destroyed."
- Wayne (Persuasion with advantage, 13): let the magic people look at it. Vistro: "Perhaps we have the fire sages look at this tome." He took the tome in a cage of flame and had them bound in fire manacles. "We sail for the citadel."
- Thimble tried to look at his medallion; he was too shaken to find it.

**The salamander city**
- Lashed to their own mast and towed behind two guards, they reached **a lava pillar about a kilometer around** holding up the cavern. The lava falls parted on **a hidden salamander city** carved inside: homes and markets on many levels. Little salamanders hissed and threw fireballs at the "heretics."
- Thimble heard the king tell a guard, **Essi**, to bind them in their cells. Blindfolded, they were walked up and across **a magical retracting bridge**.
- **The prison is a huge stalactite** hanging from the ceiling, 120 feet over lava: five levels of three triangular cells, **bars of black glass with runes that pulse on a steady rhythm** (Wayne, Investigation 22). The runes are Primordial (Ignan). Their link still works.

**Old Ash Tongue**
- In the third cell: **Old Ash Tongue**, an old salamander with a beard. "Quit your yelling, I'm trying to sleep." He'd heard the guards talk about **the Kyn'Ruthar, the one made of stone**; the whole city has.
- He says he's in for "things he did with the king's mother," and has been here about 25 years.
- **His song about the Kyn'Ruthar**, from his grandfather's grandfather, played to Thimble's gong and Wayne's bagpipes:
  > *Stone was his skin and hollow his heart. He walked where the fires burned. He drank them down, the bright and the old, and the green went gray where he turned. And then he turned again. He turned on the ones who made him. He drank the dark as he drank the light, and no one living could name him. The fields that he walked went silent, a hundred years and a hundred more. So never speak the walker's name unless you mean to speak of war.*
- Thimble: like the gray stone tree. Wayne is 25; it can't be about him.
- **The salamanders are tasked with protecting the world from what is inside that castle.** The fire sages keep it contained under the dome. It is **a pilgrimage site of the very Kyn'Ruthar who slew so many of their ancestors.** His advice: keep the title under wraps here.

**The breakout**
- Thimble's *Ray of Frost* bounced off the bars while the runes were lit. Timed to the pulse (Wayne helped spot it), it froze a bar into a thousand cracks, and **Thimble squeezed out.** Patrols every five minutes.
- He broke Wayne's bars and Ash Tongue's (Persuasion 15: "you've lived your whole life for this moment").
- The only way off is the retracting bridge. Wayne had **dust of disappearance** as a plan. Ash Tongue pulled **a stick of dynamite** from where he'd hidden it for 25 years and blew his own cell as a diversion.
- On the way down, a guard came for Thimble's laugh. **Wayne and Ash Tongue pushed; Thimble used Tides of Chaos and rolled a natural 20 Athletics**, shoulder-charging the guard 100 feet out into the lava below. They took **his cloak and bridge key**.
- Ash Tongue, in the cloak, told the bridge guards in Primordial he was moving the prisoners for safety. They walked out.
- A dark tunnel led to **a staging room with racks of basic weapons and armor.** Insight (Thimble): **Ash Tongue isn't who he said.** "I'm actually a terrorist. A serial bomber," all alleged, not 25 years, and an old friend of the king. He'd led them to **a trial by combat**; win it and the king might see reason.
- Thimble gave Wayne the medallion to look at: **the Mind Medallion is surging, trying to pulse out.** Thimble's Arcana failed again.

**Trial by combat**
- A volcanic arena, salamanders roaring, Vistro presiding. **Win against the king's champion and the crimes are resolved.** "You are not just a Goliath. You are an arbiter of destruction and chaos."
- Opponents: two newt warriors and **the king's champion, the Black Blade, arbiter of truth**, who backflipped in with two scimitars.
- **Thimble opened with a 3rd-level *Shatter*.** Surge (81): **he shone with bright light; anything ending its turn within 5 feet of him is blinded.** He worked the crowd (Performance 17) and drank a potion of fire resistance.
- **Old Ash Tongue's natural 20 bite** tore the head off one newt.
- **Wayne ran up the wall and bicycle-kicked a psionic blade** into the other (25), killing it, then hit the champion twice on the way down. His war gong hype (Performance) frazzled the champion: half his actions next turn.
- The champion missed Wayne and gave Thimble a haircut.
- **Thimble's 3rd-level *Thunderwave*** (22) blew the blinded champion back. **Ash Tongue stuck his second stick of dynamite on its back** (31).
- **"Tag me in."** Ash Tongue's tag added fire to Wayne's blades; Wayne backflipped into a swanton with his daggers and **killed the Black Blade.** The crowd went wild.

**Vistro**
- "Who am I to deny elemental justice? Clearly you are not guilty." All three are free; Ash Tongue went with them.
- Vistro saw **Thimble's medallion: "That medallion belonged to an old friend of mine. The king of the dwarven ruins you sailed from. The last time I saw it, it was being ripped off the king's stone body by his very queen."**
- He asked if they were friends of the queen. Wayne (History) knows she was the drow princess, and she's not one to defend. Thimble covered with Deception.
- He would not take them into the temple. Instead, **the fire sages**: on an island, five hooded salamanders chanting in Ignan, trying and failing to burn through **the tome's barrier.**
- **Vistro: his ancestors took this tome off an evil, pernicious queen in that very temple, and entrusted it to brave knights long ago.** Thimble admitted they took it from the knights' restricted section.
- The fires have judged them pure in intent. **His proposition:** his sages will **lower the barrier**; they take **the tome back to its rightful place in the castle**, then return and help **channel the temple out of this plane into the Abyss.** He wants to move on from this stain on their history.
- **Wayne's Insight: he is lying about what the sages' ritual can do.** Insight 17: he won't go in himself, out of trauma or old damage to his people, not inability.
- To Thimble: "You and I are not so different. Born from the primordial soup, we wield chaos to our advantage." **He gave the tome back.** "Take the book and either destroy it or rid these lava lakes of this evil, and you will have an ally in me for eternity."
- On the medallion: **"Keep it. Bring it to the temple. You will know what to do with it."**

**The tattoos**
- The sages do magical needlepoint. **Thimble: the crest of the Vistro lineage on his right palm**, a fire salamander coiled in a circle, glowing red. **Once a day he can summon a vestige of Vistro** to fight at reduced strength.
- **Wayne: Nerevath on his right palm**, wings and bill like the Skyrim seal, burned in red and turning bright silver. **It summons a weakened Nerevath**, like a draconic spirit.
- **They dapped up**: a salamander and Nerevath coiled together in the air. **The others will hear that dap a mile away**, with no context. A combined form is in the works.
- A royal escort of salamanders in golden chariots sailed them back (Performance 20).

**After the session**
- **Wayne and Thimble each get heroic inspiration** for playing out the session as a pair.
- Thimble's other plan had been to stuff his blasting charge into the open tome and throw it.
- The DM confirmed this is an optional side area of the dungeon.
