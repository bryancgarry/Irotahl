# Session 37: Back From The Hunt

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 36 - The Summit]], [[Hollowmere]], [[Selara Du'Cant]], [[Corvin Hale]], [[Talia|Talia Shay]], [[The Holy Chain Knights]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "Back From The Hunt" (Episode 37)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SH55jHzgE4E). Alaric, Wayne and Bog delivered Salvatore to Bifflefapple's workshop and learned he is Pebbles' mother. Aalyn and Thimble humiliated Corvin Hale, were paid 1,500 gp for the baroness's heirlooms, and told her the truth about Oakhollow. Under Aalyn's *Zone of Truth*, the Enclave's Orthus said all was well in Oakhollow. Selara later shook on a truce. That night Alaric, Wayne and Thimble went down to the Celestial Archives, while Aalyn met Talia at V's, where Bog crashed the stage with Gorgonzola. Ends with Aalyn and Talia in a back room.

## What Happened at the Table

**Recap and level ups**
- Everyone finished their level-7 changes.

**Cinderward**
- Alaric, Wayne and Bog ran through the city with Salvatore on a timer.
- At the gate, the old lady (natural 1) didn't remember them. Wayne: "Who's Wayne? I'm Professor Bifflefapple. I forgot my ID card again." She took back last time's temporary badge and gave him another. **This is strike two on "his" record; strike three is a 500 gp fine.** Salvatore: "What up, GMA?"
- Bifflefapple wasn't in his office. At the workshop he was humming on the back of the ship. They cleared a 15-by-15 space.
- **Salvatore turned back into Nerevath** as the spell ended. "Being a goose sucks." He's hungry and wants no more rations (Wayne loves them). The professor looked inside his mouth and got stuck.
- The professor will keep him here and make him comfortable. He's a tinkerer, not one for this kind of magic, and nobody at the institute farms or does animals, as far as he knows.
- **Dinner** on the upper deck: dining-hall food, some divine mac and cheese, some slop. Alaric turned Salvatore human again for the talk.
- **Salvatore Pearcetti:** "best pear salesman in Oakhollow." He knows Wayne ruined his life, and recognized Thimble as the one who turned him. In goose form he thinks like a goose: "There's not a lot of Sal up there, there's a lot of Nerevath."
  - **He's a female goose, and he laid Pebbles' egg. Pebbles is his child.** He was trying to take her back when he went for her. Pebbles honked at him and he honked back.
  - Wayne and Salvatore argued custody and child support.
  - He lost track of things while a goose. Alaric told him the Gordra Dominion may have razed Oakhollow. He'd spent the last of his money on a pear shipment.
- **The deal:**
  - He'll let the professor study his honks to build a compression device for the airship, if he gets a share. The ship isn't for sale, but **they'll use it to search for someone who can make him human permanently** (Alaric suggests his elven homeland).
  - **The party will fund a bulk pear order and sell Pearcetti pears on their travels**, maybe permanently. He keeps "fairy dust" away from him.
  - His dream is to **sponsor the Youth Goliath Skyball League**. Wayne: "They're not that good." Alaric pitched Pebbles as a mascot.
  - Goose-form neck scratches help.
- Alaric will try to turn him human whenever he's in the area. The professor will accommodate him.
- **Wayne pulled a knife off the table into his hand** (Arcana) and flipped it like a drumstick.
- Leaving, Wayne realized he still had the badge and ran back to return it.

**Aalyn and Thimble at Hollow Hall**
- Aalyn told Thimble about **the magical thread somewhere in the city**, to keep within the group. Thimble's in: libraries, ruins, loot.
- Outside Hollow Hall, **Corvin Hale** was leaving the baroness and Selara, kissing the baroness's hand: "I look forward to working with you." To them: "Survived the hunt, I see."
- Aalyn told him his shoe was untied (Deception 20). He was wearing armored boots, and checked anyway. Corvin: **"Thin ice you're walking on, gentlemen."** He tried to shoulder through them. **Aalyn subtle-cast *Hex*** on his Strength; he rolled a 3 and bounced off Thimble. Aalyn stuck a foot out; Corvin crashed, hit his head, starfished in his armor, walked off the wrong way and had to walk back past them.
- Selara called him **"the vice captain."** The Silver Knights aren't city-run.
- **The hunt:** Aalyn showed the goose feather. The beast won't trouble the city again. Selara doesn't trust their word.
- **The heirlooms:** the snorkel gear is **Grandpy's**; he goes scuba diving alone in the nude up there. **1,000 gp.** The cloak of the manta ray isn't his (he writes his name in his clothes), but she bought it anyway: **500 gp**, and Thimble keeps the cloak.
- **The hunt reward was never paid.**
- The baroness invited them to tea. Her favorite meal is pork shipped from Dramore; elven jasmine tea, elven cheeses and jams, bread from the Golden Republic.
- She asked after Rosalie's honeymoon and the handsome **Prince Darken**.
- **Aalyn told her the truth:** the Gordra Dominion attacked the reception; the Baron turned into a nightmare and was killed; the demon chained beneath the city escaped; the goblin transformed; the city exploded and left a giant hole; **Prince Darken lost an arm; Princess Rosalie did not survive** (Persuasion with disadvantage, 22).
- Selara told the baroness they tell stories.
- **Thimble blurted the rest:** Malrik is a devil. The Godhand took them to a pocket dimension and killed them over and over. He showed his brand. Malrik had been harnessing the demon under the town, Rosalie's mother, and it turned the goblin into a member of the Godhand, and Malrik into a general. Aalyn (Insight 27) saw a vein flex in Selara.
- Selara: she **heard from Malrik two days ago**, and he is well.
- Aalyn proposed calling **Orthus**, the old Enclave adviser to Darkstone from the wedding (Persuasion with advantage, 29). The baroness agreed. She has an uneasy feeling.

**The call**
- In Selara's Enclave wing (apparatus, a chalkboard of notes, stacked books), **an obsidian cube** rose and unfolded into a projection of Orthus.
- **Aalyn subtle-cast *Zone of Truth***. **Selara and the baroness failed the save** (Selara doesn't know it was cast). Aalyn failed on purpose. It doesn't reach Orthus.
- **Orthus:** the prince is "recovering from quite an eventful wedding night." Oakhollow "has seen better days," given the absence of the new prince and princess. He played dumb about the hole and Rosalie's death. He asked after Wayne. Tiberius is "screaming at the sky as he always does."
- **Inside the zone, Selara said again she corresponded with Malrik two days ago**, and the details are Enclave business.
- **The Enclave is led by the Grandmaster**, who has a name. Orthus outranks Selara by age and wisdom.
- Orthus to the baroness: **"I'm sure you will see the princess and prince again one day."** Aalyn, out loud: "How ominous."
- The baroness, shaken, retired for the night.

**Selara**
- Thimble told her they're lying to the baroness. Selara: is that what serves the baroness with an invasion coming? Aalyn: he wants to save the people of this city. Selara: Malrik's faults are his own, "but you all had a part to play as well."
- She doesn't know how to remove the brand.
- **On the Godhand:** a force that has piqued various Enclave members' curiosity, ominous and shrouded. The interested ones just seek answers. Malrik pursued that beyond his means.
- She only wanted to know what happened to her friend; a city blew up and a member vanished, so she took precautions.
- Aalyn: no need to sneak around; a face like hers shouldn't hide in invisibility. **They shook on a truce** (Persuasion 19s). Her one request: **"Do not push the baroness. All will be revealed in time."**
- Dinner at a 20-foot royal table, the two of them at either end. **5 temporary HP each**, lasting through tomorrow.
- "We never got our reward for the goose."

**The groups regroup**
- Around 8:30. Aalyn has a date at V's with Talia, and it's a lead on the thread. Thimble headed for the keep. Rec found Thimble and carried him like a skateboard to meet Alaric's group.
- Alaric caught Thimble up on Salvatore; Thimble "might know a guy" for selling pears. Alaric realized V's is the Fog Slab place Luna said to avoid. **Bog left for V's** on his own.
- Thimble gave Pebbles his cookies from the baroness's table.

**The Celestial Archives**
- Down the stairs from the keep's atrium: low light, parchment and ink, a flicker of magic. **Archivist Willamir**, a very pale half-elf (Snape-like): "Please do not destroy anything."
- **The restricted section** is in the center, where moonlight comes down from the spire. Entering means he alerts Imperator Luna. Access comes only from **the Chain Lords**, above even the Imperator. Wayne asked where it was so he could avoid it (Persuasion, nearly a natural 20).
- **Wings:** theology; history and politics; arcana and the cosmos; **the bestiary (wing four)**; the military and history of the order.
- Alaric asked how dragons raise their young and whether humans have ever raised a dragon. Willamir led him toward it. Scene cut.

**V's**
- Down in the Fog Slab: homeless people, river and vents, a canal district. **The Ebony Courtyard**, with V's a two-story building at its center, wine crates out front.
- A hostess. Clients can wear porcelain masks. Aalyn instead cast *Disguise Self*: warrior-cut black hair, a face tattoo down the right side, a black tank top, gold boots without wings. Talia reserved him **a front-row seat**.
- Inside it's far classier than expected: dim candlelight, pipe and cigar smoke, olives and wine, booths and seats, **men and women dancing**, hushed and refined.
- **Talia** dances, dressed as a maid, with special attention for him. She recognizes his disguise.
- Aalyn (Perception 13) spotted **a masked man three booths back**: a black-and-white mask over half his face, jet-black hair, less polished than the rest but carrying gravitas. Through *Message*, Talia says he's **new, coming in the last day or two, and friendly with the patrons.**
- **Talia will introduce Aalyn to Miss V after the night.**
- **Bog arrived**, asked the hostess for **"Gorgonzola"** (deception, natural 20), and stood front and center at her pole.
  - Gorgonzola, mid-dance: "Bog, what are you doing here? I can't lose this job. **Not again.** Miss V is not going to approve." She pulled him on stage.
  - Bog stripped. Old patrons got out their monocles: "Miss V knows where to find talent."
  - He froze the pole with his new cold breath, stuck himself to it, wound around it (CON 18), had a worker sent over by Talia, and released himself with a natural 20 Acrobatics. **His spring-loaded release knocked the worker out cold.** He made **100 gp** in tips.
- Aalyn tossed Talia 10 gp. **An old patron** demanded her attention; Aalyn told him to pay more, and he took her to a back room with a bag of gold. Aalyn cast *Hex* on him as he walked away. He came out 10 minutes later, frustrated, and left.
- Talia gave Aalyn **50 gp** from the bag ("I could fight my own battles") and took him to a back room. Session ends.

**After the session**
- The table jokes Corvin is in love with Luna.
