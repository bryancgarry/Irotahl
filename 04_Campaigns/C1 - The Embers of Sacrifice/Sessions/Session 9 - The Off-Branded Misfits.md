# Session 9: The Off-Branded Misfits

**Party:** Level 5 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 8 - Gnomes and Fireballs]], [[Bragthar]], [[Oakhollow]], [[Papa Glabb]]

> [!important] Played: "The Off-Branded Misfits" (Episode 9)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GfSxLSXS6PU). The fight with the gnomes, winning the trap wagon Sheila, the drive back to Oakhollow, Bragthar turned away at the gate, the party naming itself **the Off-Branded Misfits**, and a first night at the Orchid.

## What Happened at the Table

**Table trackers**
- The DM's player tracker now counts Wayne's geese: he started with three and has lost two.
- Wayne's pebbles: Wayne put all of his collected pebbles into the lithoweave's core along with his life force, so the count is reset to zero. The DM may revisit Wayne's life-force cost because of it. From now on, every hit point Wayne loses drops a pebble. Collect enough (around 500) and they can be thrown like a bag of ball bearings.

**The fight with the gnomes**
- Muzzle swung his enchanted bat at Thimble: one miss, one hit (3 bludgeoning plus 5 force).
- Wayne leapt out of the wagon and sneak-attacked Muzzle's knees with psionic daggers (21, then 5).
- Aalyn finished the rap battle at rapier point. His final verse, about losing someone he loved, was a natural 20. It cut Chunks for 1 psychic damage and left him speechless; Chunks forfeited. Aalyn gave Bog bardic inspiration.
- Grease swung at Thimble twice and missed both times. Under the wagon, Fizzle grappled Bog. Dipple threw a firebomb onto the wagon (Bragthar and Alaric took 4 fire each). The wagon itself was unharmed.
- Thimble sent Grease a Message, then cast a 2nd-level **Suggestion** on him: "Chill with the fighting. Let's sell some pot together. We won't kill you guys, but calm Muzzle down or we will." Grease was charmed. Chaos die: d6, then a d4 next.
- Alaric cast **Haste** on Bragthar.
- Bog's unarmed strikes and Flurry of Blows killed Fizzle under the wagon.
- Hasted Bragthar tried to belly-flop off the wagon onto Muzzle, clipped the edge, and knocked Muzzle and Thimble prone (8 damage each). He then mauled Muzzle for 37.
- Muzzle pressed a wooden remote. **The wagon unfolded into a construct, Sheila.** Bragthar tore Muzzle apart. Sheila gored the prone Thimble (12 piercing plus 5 fire) and dropped him. That broke Thimble's concentration, so Grease was no longer charmed.
- Wayne took the remote from Muzzle's hand and pressed the big red button, and Sheila folded back into a wagon. He told Chunks, "You don't want these hands." Chunks, shaken by Aalyn's verse and his friend's death, put his bat away.
- Aalyn flew up and called for everyone to stop (Persuasion 25). The fight ended, and his Healing Word brought Thimble back (12).

**Aftermath**
- Aalyn apologized to Chunks. Chunks said Muzzle never talked much about his sister. The gnomes had been in Oakhollow tracking Thimble, and **a man in black robes, walking with a hobble, told Muzzle in a back alley where to find him.** They only saw his back.
- The party took the wagon, as the bet promised. Bog carried Fizzle's body and went into the bushes with Dipple. Thimble told Grease to look him up if he ever wants to sell product.
- Thimble read the remote's buttons, which are in Gnomish: construct on and off (the big red one), drive, suspension bounce, music, underglow, volume, a convertible top, and a voice-command button.
- Once Sheila is started, Wayne can drive her telepathically through his psionic link. Aalyn offered Wayne a 75/25 split of future performance earnings (75% to Wayne) in exchange for the remote and ownership of the wagon. Wayne agreed and still drives. Aalyn named her **Sheila the Trap Wagon**.
- Wayne added rope reins, and Alaric's Minor Illusion extended them.

**The drive home**
- They short rested in the wagon. Thimble Jr. II came back with a letter from **Sir Romulus Darken**: thanks for dealing with the Bloodlancer camp; the city is preparing for a broader assault; scouts are watching the roads to Azure Port; and he wishes them well with the stone trees, which have hurt the lumbermen's guild.
- Aalyn snapped Chunks's mixtape (volume 3) twice, and it went into the woods. Thimble found **Papa Glabb's classic hits** in the glovebox (Papa Glabb, a bard from Drak'mor), and they played it all the way home. Someone in the Mages Guild could likely make a blank recording.
- Wayne warmed the goose egg in his hands (Animal Handling 13) and felt the faint start of a heartbeat.
- **Reflection prompt** (Thimble, d100: 35): what he admires about the person to his left. He told Bog that under all the weird stuff, Bog just wants what's best for the group. Bog told Wayne he never left his side, and they'll get through this "one pebble at a time."
- Thimble smoked a blunt with the top up.

**Back at Oakhollow** (around 5 to 6 pm)
- The city was busy preparing: lumber carts, food shipments, and guards inspecting every cart.
- They tested Sheila outside the gate. She isn't hostile and doesn't speak. Her kit includes metal gore arms, heat that radiates in combat, bright underglow light, and a Sparking Charge (piercing damage that can grapple and restrain). She answers commands through the voice button, and she did the Macarena. The DM will send her stat sheet.
- At the gate, the guard Bob (badge number unrememberable) let the five of them in on Sir Romulus's word, but refused Bragthar. Wayne threatened him, Thimble's Illusory Script forgery failed (Deception), and Aalyn hit Bob with Unsettling Words. **Bragthar and Sheila are parked at the stables outside the south gate.** Wayne told Bragthar to defend himself if the guards bother him, and Thimble told Sheila to guard Bragthar.
- Crowds recognized them as the ones who found the Bloodlancer camp.

**The Off-Branded Misfits**
- After a long debate in the town square (rejected names included "Bog and the Branded"), the party named itself **the Off-Branded Misfits**.
- Aalyn performed "The Bridge King's Fall (tavern version)" on a barrel with his Unseen Servant on viola. Alaric's Major Image played out the battle with the Bridge King, and Thimble added a beanstalk and a thrown lightning bolt. Aalyn's Performance was a 29.
- Wayne pickpocketed the back of the crowd (Sleight of Hand 16) for 37 gp, 127 sp and 2 cp. The crowd tossed 27 cp into Aalyn's jacket. Wayne Cloud's Jaunted onstage with the crown, and Thimble Messaged Bob an insult.
- At the keep the drawbridge was raised. A guard said Sir Romulus was meeting with the nobility and asked them to come back in the morning. **Rosalie is stable but still hasn't woken.** Bob just had a kid and lives in the Old Town district. A scout reported a large creature flying off to the west.
- The branded felt their brands tingle again as they passed through the city center.

**The Orchid** (Craftsman's Row)
- An inn and tavern with a front bar, rooms (small at 5 sp, large at 8 sp), a steam room and a back entertainment room. The bartender, **Brena**, knows Aalyn, who is a regular. Aalyn paid 18 sp for three rooms. Brena says security work is in demand and "those magic folks" in the outskirts are up to something.
- **Wayne** (History 13) realized his map marks the Orchid as a **Thieves Guild safe house.** He followed two people who didn't look like patrons toward the back.
- **Bog** met a halfling in the steam room, **the Fog Master**, who offered breathing lessons that might improve his breath weapon. Alaric steamed up his own tub so he couldn't see.
- **Aalyn** walked into the back room as slam poetry night ended. A young woman read "Hurt," about a lost love who called her his "mossy queen" and danced with her "beneath the fungal bloom." The table suspects she is Bragthar's ex.
