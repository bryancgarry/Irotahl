# Session 22: A Long Night Ends

**Party:** Level 6 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 21 - The Godhand]], [[Tiberius Blackfang]], [[The Silver Lyre]], [[Malrik Greene]], [[Glimmerstone]], [[Aetherstone]], [[Rec]], [[Syfaeril]], [[Oakhollow]]

> [!important] Played: "A Long Night Ends" (Episode 22)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FgJBYfUsckA). Wayne's egg hatched into Pebbles, a stone goose dragon. Rosalie is dead, and Prince Darken survived, crippled. Aalyn said goodbye to her and sang the city through the night. In the morning the party went through Malrik's bag: a Glimmerstone order for aetherstone, and charts marking another gemstone off Midland's southern coast. Alaric's augury warned against staying to fight the Bloodlancers. Ends with the mask stuck to Thimble's face.

## What Happened at the Table

**Pebbles**
- The fear Wayne felt underground was likely the egg's psionic emotions.
- In Rosalie's room the egg cracked open (Animal Handling 17). Out came **a baby stone goose dragon, female** (rolled), with stone goose wings. "Thimble, I'm a father."
- Thimble reached for her too fast (Animal Handling 7) and she curled up against Wayne.
- Wayne cleaned her with his lotion. She shook herself off and climbed into his pouch to ride with her claws hanging out.

**Rosalie and the prince**
- Alaric (Medicine 8) assumed Prince Darken was dead and turned away. "Of course."
- Aalyn (Medicine 14) found **Rosalie has no pulse.** The energy is gone and she's lifeless.
- Maids passing the open door noticed (Wayne, Perception).
- Aalyn used *Prestidigitation* to scream for a healer (Performance 26). It carried out past the keep through the hole in the wall.
- He shook Darken, who coughed up blood: alive. Aalyn has no healing left.
- Alaric left, telling Tiberius on the way, "You better hurry. It's a grim sight up there."
- **Ino Silvertone's mother** came with Tiberius: an elderly, silver-haired lady with the Silvertone crest.
  - Her healing, which sounds like a lyre, stabilized Darken. Abyssal runes around the wound resisted it, so the missing arm and side won't regrow.
  - Without going near Rosalie, she told Tiberius, "She is dead."
  - Aalyn: "You didn't even try to save her." She: "That one is beyond saving, my dear. And frankly, the city is better off without her." Under his breath: "I'm glad I put that dagger through your son's hand."

**Looting in the keep**
- Leadership is in disarray and servants were fleeing with coins and goods (Alaric, natural 20 Perception).
- Alaric cast his new spells: *Summon Beast* (a shadowy illusory wolf) and *Summon Fey* (a sad-faced mirthful fey). Intimidation 15.
  - Most dropped what they carried. The fey's blade killed a maid who slipped past, pinning her to the hall door.
- Bog's lightning breath warning shot chained across the candelabras (natural 20 Intimidation). Four more froze and dropped their loot.
- Alaric: "Just leave." He sat by the door petting his wolf.
- Thimble's *Detect Magic* found **a ring of feather falling** in a coin pile, part of a set with Aalyn's winged boots (Investigation 18). His wild magic made him **a potted plant again** (third time). Alaric's fey carried him over, and Pebbles nibbled a leaf to change him back (1 damage).
- Alaric used *Tenser's Floating Disc* to return the loot to the keep's vault and told Tiberius where it was.
- **Bog** carried the dead maid off to a spare bedroom and talked to her through the night.

**Aalyn and Tiberius**
- They carried Darken to a bed. "What happened?"
- Aalyn told him: they found Rosalie's mother, an evil older than anyone knew. He made a bad call trying to save a life, damned Rosalie's, and half the city's with it. Malrik is an apostle of the Godhand now.
- Tiberius: "You are in way over your head right now."
- Aalyn: he never thought he'd be a hero who fights gods; he just wanted to play music and make people smile. But he owes a debt to everyone lost in the city.
- Tiberius gave him **a note from Darken's body**: an Oakhollow scouts' report that **the Bloodlancer main force is nearly at the city's doorstep.** "We're going to have company very soon."
- "Thanks for the help." Tiberius: "Believe it or not, this is probably the best outcome that could have happened when dealing with the Godhand."
- **Aalyn's goodbye to Rosalie.** He held her hands. She reminded him of an old friend, and inspired him more than she'll ever know. He's sorry he couldn't keep his promise. He laid her hands on her chest. "I did love you." He walked out.

**The long night**
- Wayne and Thimble named the goose dragon **Pebbles** (Thimble's idea; Boulder for short). She perked up at her name.
- Wayne and Thimble took bunk beds in the keep. Wayne: "No one else is using it."
- **Aalyn's song.** At the triage stations in the market he played softly until a crowd gathered.
  - His speech: he and his friends were tasked with stopping the threat and failed. Sorry can't bring anyone back, but don't give up, rebuild, and carry on the legacies of the lost.
  - Then he sang a song he wrote, about broken streets, hope surviving, and building and being reborn together (Performance 15).
  - A heckler: "So you're the one who caused all this?" The young ginger guard from outside the Orchid hushed him.
  - An old commander stood. "Get off your asses. We're the great city of Oakhollow." The fisherman's rebellion left a bigger hole than this. Morale lifted.
  - Aalyn helped until about midnight.
- **Alaric** fought fires and moved the injured on his disc until 4 a.m.
  - He caught **Mibo**, the top-hatted, monocled man, rifling a dropped bag in an alley. A *Mage Hand* tap and a *Minor Illusion* shout sent him running, and Alaric took the bag to the lost and found.
  - He knows his own limits now: "I'll do what I can, but I can't do everything."
- **Aalyn on the spire.** He sat atop a keep tower over the city and the lake. "Compassion may be a weapon, but it's the only thing that keeps me going, and it won't stop me now." He heard the faintest laugh, from the city or deep in his mind.
- Long rest.

**Morning**
- Alaric slept at the Orchid. He opened **Malrik's bag of holding** (from the Investigation in Session 21):
  - Three potions of greater healing.
  - A ring of water walking and a ring of jumping.
  - **A rune of recall**: crush it to teleport back, Malrik's escape plan. Alaric keeps it.
  - The illusionary battery the party returned to Malrik in Session 6.
  - **An incomplete grimoire written in Abyssal.**
  - Papers (Investigation 29): **solar refractory schematics**, and **a sealed order form to "D. Bronzebottom"** of Glimmerstone. It likely explains why Muzzle's gnomes were in Oakhollow hunting the party.
- Alaric had breakfast at the Orchid by the roped-off hole, and sent a letter to his parents through the Mages Guild.
- Aalyn woke on the spire and almost fell off. He's writing in his journal for the first time since the stone trees.
- Pebbles slept beside Thimble. They plan a pet store visit.
- Aalyn walked in on Bog and the maid and shut the door fast. Alaric still hasn't seen any of Bog's worst.

**The debrief**
- Pebbles spat a pebble at Alaric, and he pocketed it.
- Alaric shared the haul.
  - He may be able to get the solar refractory running again.
  - **The Bronzebottoms** (Thimble): Glimmerstone's chief arcane etherists, running much of the mining and the crystal exports, and on the city council with Thimble's father. Thimble's family has political and economic standing: his father sits on the city leader's advisory council, and they own many of the high-rises.
  - The order includes **[[Aetherstone]]**: pure arcane stone found deep in Glimmerstone's geode, newly discovered when Thimble left and not something they'd export lightly. Malrik never used it in front of Alaric. It looks like the next evolution of the refractory, aiming to make illusions real.
  - Gifts: the ring of water walking to Wayne and the ring of jumping to Bog (tabled). The greater healing potions were split around the group.
- **Aalyn's update.** The prince is alive under the Silvertone mother's care. Rosalie is gone. An army is gathering outside the walls, the same Bloodlancers who attacked during the wedding. Wayne pulled out **General Veraxous's letter.**
  - Aalyn: the Godhand is out of their weight class, but mortal threats aren't. "We lost, but no good story ends at the first loss."
  - "We're the plane, not the terrorist."
  - He told Thimble he cracked the pocket dimension and is more powerful than any of them imagined. Thimble, privately: he's the danger.
  - Aalyn wants to find someone who knows what the brands mean. Tiberius may know more than they thought.
- The DM confirmed that **the screams Wayne heard weren't Tiberius's.**
- **Malrik's navigational charts** (Wayne, Investigation 15, without tools). They mark forgotten coastal ruins or a wreck in the sea between Midland and Syfaeril, to the south. They also mark **another gemstone like the pale one**, drawn as an egg-like shape with an eye and a mouth. Aalyn: if they're going to deal with the Godhand, that's the next stop.
- Alaric cast *Find Familiar* as a ritual: **a living demiplane**, a flat shadowy rectangle he named **Rec**. Aalyn gave heroic inspiration to the other three with a song after the rest.
- **Alaric's augury.** He asked how talking to Tiberius about the brands and the coming battle would go.
  - Blood rose from his feet to drown him (the Bloodlancers). Before it covered his eyes he saw sunlight and a rainbow, leading away from here.
  - His read: fighting the Bloodlancers ends badly. Trust Tiberius with the city and go somewhere else. Aalyn wants the city evacuated if it can't fight.
- Alaric looked at the grimoire. It's in Abyssal, so he asked Thimble for the mask. **It's stuck on Thimble's face.** He's had it on all along. Session ends.

**After the session**
- Aalyn's player is sending his updated autobiography, which adds context to his words to Rosalie, and Alaric's player is sending the letter to his parents.
