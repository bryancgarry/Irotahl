# Session 43: The Tempest Warden

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 42 - Tempting Fate]], [[Durnhollow]], [[Soulvite]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "The Tempest Warden" (Episode 43)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HuXr_VGAC48). The party fought the Tempest Warden, a storm of steam around a glowing silver shard, in a pipe arena where gale-force wind pins everyone to the floor. Killing its steam mephits charged floor vents; stepping on pressure plates fired them. Wayne summoned an air elemental from his gem. Aalyn's frustrated rock hit the calm air spirit in the middle and freed him from the wind. Wayne rode a vent up and stunned the Warden with his psionic dagger, bringing it to the floor. Aalyn spun up a vent and crit it for 38. It reformed, lifted the party 120 feet, then smashed down through the floor; in free fall the party hit it with everything and it burst. Its wind set them down in a rocky canyon below with a soulvite shard and a rain of coins. Aalyn named his sword Storm Song.

## What Happened at the Table

**Recap**
- The DM recapped Session 42. **Thimble got heroic inspiration for committing to the gold-and-boots bit.**
- Alaric found **logistics for the floor below** in the supply office.

**The arena**
- The top of a giant pipe: auxiliary pipes around the sides, a metal grate in the center, and above them **a storm with a glowing purple-runed metallic shard at its core.** Hurricane-force wind presses everyone into the floor: **horizontal movement only.**
- **The Tempest Warden** floats 65 feet up. Three **steam mephits** split off it, and later more, two at a time (legendary actions).
- **A small calm air spirit** split off the shard and floated in the center, unaffected by the wind.

**Round 1**
- **The Warden's wind lances** hit Wayne and Thimble for 14 each.
- **Wayne smashed his air elemental gem**: an air elemental shaped like a big Thimble. It slammed a mephit apart.
- **When a mephit dies, its death burst gets sucked into the nearest floor vent, which lights up.** Bog drank a beer (free action), punched two mephits out and **stepped on a pressure plate: a lit vent fired a blast of air into the sky.**
- **Alaric summoned a spectral wolf** (*Summon Beast*, free cast) and hid behind Aalyn. Rec helps the wolf.
- Thimble drank a potion, glared up at the Warden (Intimidation, which got its attention) and stepped on a plate. Chaos Bolt at max damage killed a mephit; mephits count as fiends.
- **Aalyn hexed the Warden** (Wisdom saves) and blasted the last mephit.

**Round 2**
- The Warden lanced Wayne and his elemental, then hovered over Thimble.
- Wayne killed a mephit and went prone to hide; **Alaric made him look like a floor tile with *Minor Illusion*.** The elemental killed another.
- **Bog** flew up on his wings (Strength save, second try) and breathed lightning at the Warden: half damage, 6. "We should launch a rat at this dude."
- Alaric's Fire Bolt missed.
- **The Warden's lightning strike hit Thimble for 16.**
- **Thimble stood on Wayne's back and cast *Dissonant Whispers*: it fled 90 feet up**, to 150 feet. He used his sorcery points to regain a spell slot.
- **Aalyn shouted, "Where you going, you big old pussy?"** and gave Alaric Bardic Inspiration ("You're being a really good boy over there").

**Round 3**
- **The Warden dove back down and knocked Bog out of the air: natural 1 on the Strength save. Slow Fall** took all the damage.
- **Wayne and Bog fired the rat cannon: 15 damage** (it saved).
- **Bog's Deflect Attacks** stopped a 13-damage lance completely.
- **Alaric used his wand of magic missile at 3rd level: 17.** Lightning hit him for 6.
- **Thimble readied *Sickening Radiance*** for the moment the Warden was held.
- **Aalyn's Eldritch Blast on a natural 1 bent back into the elemental. He threw a rock at the air spirit in frustration; it passed through and the spirit fluffed apart into white wind that settled on him: the condensed gale buff.** He no longer feels the wind pinning him down. The goldfish likes him.

**Grounded**
- **Wayne used Weightless Movement**, stepped on a plate, and the vent shot him 30 feet up. He threw his psionic jade daggers at the Warden: **32 damage, a failed INT save: stunned.** **It fell to the floor.**
- Thimble's readied *Sickening Radiance* went off; it saved. It caught Alaric's wolf, which got a level of exhaustion.
- The elemental's thunder slams passed through it: **immune to thunder.** Bog, with advantage on a stunned target, rolled double natural 1s on his first punch, then landed 33 force.
- Alaric's 3rd-level wand of magic missile again: 20. The wolf added 9.
- Thimble's Chaos Bolt (with his inspiration) came up poison and thunder: nothing. "Fuck this guy."

**Phase two**
- **Aalyn ran onto a pressure plate, *Misty Step*ped into the vent, shot up spinning like Rec and fired Eldritch Blast: natural 20, crit table 84, 38 damage.**
- Its form melted, then reformed. **Its eyes burned bright purple.** The wind reversed and lifted everyone 120 feet up in a spiral. Aalyn's air buff left him.
- **It smashed down through the floor, and the party fell with it down a huge pipe.** A damage check in free fall, everyone with advantage:
  - Aalyn: Eldritch Blast, 25.
  - Thimble: Chaos Bolt with Tides of Chaos (the steam), lightning, 11 after resistance.
  - Alaric: *Fireball*, 24.
  - Bog: flurry of blows, 52.
  - Wayne: Weightless Movement down to it, psionic daggers, 22.
- **It blew apart.** The steam spread and rolled up the cavern walls.

**After the fight**
- **The silver shard floated down with them and its wind set them gently on the ground** in a rocky open canyon. Debris sealed the hole above.
- **A soulvite shard.** Alaric took it.
- **Coins knocked loose in the pipes rained down:** Aalyn 17 gp and 57 sp, Alaric 19 gp and 38 sp, Bog 15 gp and 65 sp (he gave his to Thimble), Thimble 7 gp and 81 sp, Wayne 18 gp and 37 sp.
- **Aalyn named his sword Storm Song.**
- Thimble gave Bog the old dwarven beer.
- Alaric will cast *Leomund's Tiny Hut* for a long rest.

**After the session**
- The intended solution was to interact with the air spirit early. The party brute-forced it.
- One plan was to mage-hand a rainbow egg down its throat. Alaric picked up *Rime's Binding Ice* when he got the dragon egg; its range was too short.
