# Session 18: The Stage is Set

**Party:** Level 5 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 17 - The Vault]], [[Tiberius Blackfang]], [[Malrik Greene]], [[Baron Fern]], [[Bragthar]], [[House Fenwyn]], [[The Volkarr]], [[Oakhollow]]

> [!important] Played: "The Stage is Set" (Episode 18)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oD7LdtZtcQM). Bog bluffed Buro out of the vault room while Wayne finished the job. Back at the keep the mask split open around Thimble's wild magic. Malrik was spotted at the Grand Cathedral, and the party went down into the Fern catacombs with Bragthar and Sheila. Beneath them they found a chained demon, and through the mask Aalyn saw who she was: Tula Fern, Rosalie's mother. Ends with Malrik rising into the chamber.

## What Happened at the Table

**Opening**
- Alaric gave the recap. Thimble and Alaric got heroic inspiration for their players' birthdays.

**The vault, continued**
- Wayne's Investigation 24 turned up more:
  - Another 500 gp. *(The DM wasn't sure the 400 gp had already been given. Wayne now has 600 gp.)*
  - A six-pack of potions. Alaric later identified them (Arcana 21): three potions of greater healing, and potions of fire, cold and lightning resistance.
  - Old navigational charts.
  - Two jade daggers in an ornate box.
- Alaric, watching through the guppy (natural 20 Perception), saw fine dust settling that would show invisible movement, and shadow "enforcers" seeping out of five vents.
- Bog tried to topple a bolted shelf as a distraction (natural 1, 1 damage). Then he called out, "Would you like to see my penis?" (Persuasion 15), and Buro came back.
- **Bog and Buro.**
  - Flying, Bog put the Rat Lord's Skullcup in Buro's mouth (Acrobatics 25). Alaric dropped *Invisibility* on Bog only.
  - Bog claimed to be Malrik's inspector testing the response time (Deception 16 with advantage). His Intimidation and Persuasion (14) didn't quite land.
  - He pushed Buro 20 ft back with it. Buro sent the shadows back into the vents and showed him the triggered plate. Bog blamed the dust and lectured him about cleaning (Performance 12).
  - Bog told Buro he'd go easy in his report. Buro: "We failed our audit last year."
- Wayne retrieved the key (Sleight of Hand 26) and left the vault door open. Alaric re-cast *Invisibility* on Bog through his familiar, and they walked out the front door.
- Aalyn made 80 gp performing in the square.

**After the heist**
- Wayne gave Aalyn a potion of greater healing to pay him back, and Aalyn drank it.
- Alaric felt the mask is the most powerful magic he has ever sensed (Arcana 12) and stowed it in his bag of holding. Thimble's wild magic surged when he reached for it.
- **The jade daggers** (Wayne's Investigation 22). Jade is psychic; Jack taught him this. They feel like his psionic blades. Teleporting to one didn't work, but the tips turn psionic when stabbed into flesh.
- Aalyn silently asked his patron, "Can you hear me?" No answer, though the DM said his thinking isn't wrong (Perception 22).

**The keep**
- Aalyn cast *Message* on Rosalie. She answered, "Yes... Pain. It hurts." Her condition calms while the party is near.
- Bog held Thimble near the mask (Arcana 23). The sparks grounded through Bog.
- Alaric cast *Identify* as a ritual with candles, a pearl and water, while Wayne made a natural 20 Investigation. The mask is wood from the World Abyss on Drak'mor. It has a seam and holds something that needs a catalyst. Wayne flung it to Thimble.
- **The mask split** and the wild magic coalesced into **a playing card.** The mask grants **truesight to 120 ft** and lets the wearer speak **Abyssal and Infernal.** The cards are made of pure wild magic, and [[House Fenwyn]] keeps a few locked in its vault.
- The words Aalyn first heard in Session 1 finally made sense through the mask: **"Come save me. I am beneath the cathedral."** He didn't share the exact words with the party.
- **Tiberius burst in.** "He was spotted at the cathedral." A young guard saw Malrik enter the Grand Cathedral through the south gate late at night, and he hasn't come out. Word was slow because the guard factions don't get along.
- Aalyn messaged Rosalie: "It's Ally. I don't know how I'm going to save you, but I will." She replied, "I know you will."

**The Grand Cathedral**
- Tiberius gave the party **a sending stone** to call him if things go bad, and stayed outside. Wayne took it.
- He brought **Bragthar and Sheila**. Sheila's core had been taken and swapped for a lesser one, so both are weaker than before. Aalyn plugged his aux into Sheila.
- Bragthar called Wayne "My king." Aalyn told him he'd met his ex, **Heladur**. Bragthar said she wasn't very good at slam poetry.
- A group Persuasion got them past the clergy. Sheila took a chunk out of the door frame.

**The catacombs**
- Fern family tombs line the catacombs, each with a socket. Wayne's egg is close to hatching (Animal Handling 12).
- **The Pendant of the Ferns** fit the empty socket on the tomb of **Tula Fern**, Rosalie's mother. It opened a dark, calcified tunnel like the stone trees. Their brands tingled, and a roar came through the stone.
- **The first trap.** A mage-hand door over a pit. Alaric froze water from the pit into a bridge and jammed the gears with ice (Arcana 23). A voice said, "It's rude to open the door without asking," so they knocked.
- **The anti-magic pillars.** A room of pillars over inky goop. People kept falling in and making Con saves. Aalyn and Thimble pulled two levers together to shut the field off. Alaric found a maintenance lever (Investigation 27) that extends a walkway, so Sheila and Bragthar could cross. The ink washes off at the next door.
- Their brands bled for 1 damage. Beyond lies a huge chamber over a ley line.

**The chained demon**
- An abyssal demon is chained in the center of the chamber.
- The party finally read Malrik's logs. They mention the mother's pregnancy and the Baron's preparations.
- Aalyn put on the mask (natural 20 Arcana). With truesight he saw a young auburn-haired woman inside the demon: **Tula**. He told her about Rosalie and how good a person she is.
- **Tula's memories, shown to everyone.**
  - Her mother, Queen **Daffy**, died. The Baron grew possessive and abusive toward Tula, who resembled her mother, and locked her away.
  - Tula fell pregnant and learned she wouldn't survive it. An Obsidian Enclave adviser was present.
  - The Baron came home from the cathedral wearing a new **pale gemstone**.
  - The birth took place on an altar beneath the cathedral and went wrong. The Baron used the gemstone and summoned demons. The sigil branded the handmaidens and the old Enclave adviser **Gormar**, and the demons devoured them.
  - Rosalie was born healthy. Tula turned into the demon, and the chains bound her as the Baron ran off with the baby.
- **Malrik Greene** rose from below on a pedestal at the back of the chamber. Only Aalyn, with truesight, could see him.

**After the session**
- The DM said Malrik is really an elemental wizard, and recruited Alaric as an illusionist for a reason.
- The DM confirmed the artifact Aalyn must let be lost is the pale gemstone Wayne carries.
