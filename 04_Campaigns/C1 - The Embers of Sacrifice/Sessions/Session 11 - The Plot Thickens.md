# Session 11: The Plot Thickens

**Party:** Level 5 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 10 - The Orchid]], [[Tiberius Blackfang]], [[Malrik Greene]], [[The Obsidian Enclave]], [[Birdolomew Feathersworth]], [[Papa Glabb]]

> [!important] Played: "The Plot Thickens" (Episode 11)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=HgAw02v16Dw). *(Gabe absent; Bog went off shopping alone.)* Sir Romulus and Tiberius briefed the party on Rosalie and the missing records, and Tiberius showed his own brand. The party shopped, got their group patches, and then watched Bog get taken away for murder. Ends with the rest of them in Fern custody and Bog breathing lightning from a building by the west gate.

## What Happened at the Table

**Rosalie**
- She floats above her bed in spirals of maroon, sludgy energy. Her skin is stone cold, but she has a pulse: dead and alive at once. Thimble's Arcana (11) tied it to the strange magic in the city.
- Sir Romulus: mages, priests and the city's magisters can't explain it, and **the magic is growing.** It has darkened from red toward maroon since the party left.

**The briefing**
- The party told Romulus about the reception: the goblins, the smoke bombs, the dark pocket dimension, and the Baron turning into a monster. Aalyn showed his brand.
- **Tiberius lifted his eye patch: the same brand is on his eye.** (Alaric's Insight 21: Tiberius wasn't surprised that Bog is unbranded.)
- Tiberius is investigating the tunnels and Romulus is collecting eyewitness testimony.
- **The missing records.** Records from around the time of Rosalie's birth are missing. Those records are kept by the city's magical adviser, the Enclave member posted to each Free Kingdoms court. Orthus wasn't forthcoming.
  - **The previous adviser was named Gormar.** They think Malrik was a student or devotee of Gormar's work.
  - Malrik took over after Rosalie's birth, which lines up with when the records go missing.
- **Malrik never came back to Oakhollow.** The party told them about finding him in the Bloodlancer camp, and that Malrik invited them all to the reception (the invitations carry an Enclave logo and a table number) but wasn't there himself. They no longer have his margin note; Bog threw it into the woods.
- No one has found the weapon beneath the city. The Bloodlancer map marks tunnels but no weapon. Wayne's pale gemstone stayed still.
- Romulus asked the party to **search Malrik's quarters at the Solarium in the Mages Guild**, in the outskirts, where Alaric started the campaign. His closest confidants are Tiberius and Orthus, and he met with the city's nobility yesterday. Aalyn warned him to tell the nobles nothing, and Tiberius agreed.
- **Romulus paid the second half of the stone trees fee: 50 gp each.** Aalyn shook his hand sincerely this time.
- Aalyn stayed behind and promised Rosalie, "I'm going to save you." As he closed the door, a fleck of dust shimmered like a snowflake and faded into the sunlight.
- Wayne asked Tiberius to join them in the tunnels. **Tiberius agreed**, "to see what's so special about all of you," and will meet them at the sewer entrance under the Orchid.

**Attractiveness rolls** (table fun): Romulus 11, Thimble 20, Wayne 2 (as stone), Alaric 5, Aalyn 12. Tiberius was a 15 earlier. Bog's is still to roll.

**Shopping**
- **Alaric, Twilight Threads (Upper Commons).** A tailor sewing patches with glowing magical thread made **five patches of the Off-Branded Misfits** (30 gp total). They tie on as bands. Alaric handed them out at the fountain, and each member chose how to wear one. The patch has an effect tied to how many members wear it. The logo design is still up for group discussion (one idea is an O and a B forming a music note).
- **Aalyn, Whispering Wares (Noble's Rise).** From an old shopkeeper:
  - His late wife's **music relay kit**: a receiver with an antenna, a microphone with a battery pack, and empty recording canisters, so Aalyn can play live music through Sheila. His wife died 25 years ago in a wagon accident.
  - **[[Papa Glabb]]'s lute**, with "PG" carved underneath. The shopkeeper says Papa Glabb was his best friend, who never made it big because he fell in love.
  - Paid 50 gp, his own broken lute (initials AR, now on display in the shop), and some handwritten "tickets" to his first concert.
- **Aalyn** also bought a black leather coin pouch with an orange-gold ribbon (2 sp).
- **Wayne, Iron Branch Armory (Craftsman's Row).** A dwarven smith sold him **studded leather armor** (45 gp) and **20 +1 darts** sized for his bagpipes. He paid 55 gp and traded in his old leather armor.
- **Thimble, Root and Rune.** He smoked with the clerk, who walks on the ceiling, and bought **slippers of spider climbing** for 60 gp.
- **Wayne's egg supplies.**
  - Feather and Fang (Ordo, a ranger): a humidifier orb (3 gp) and a one-use pop-up nursery box (15 gp).
  - **Birdolomew's Magical Emporium**, run by Birdolomew's wife with their young son, Falcon. She says Ordo hates birds and his orb would kill the egg. She held the egg and said it will be a strong girl. She sold Wayne a **heated immovable rod** to keep it warm (50 gp; Wayne paid 38 by fumbling the count). Birdolomew is overdue home. Wayne told her he's staying at the Orchid if she needs help looking for him.

**Bog is arrested**
- The party met at the city-center fountain. Thimble rode on Wayne's back in his spider shoes and fell off when Wayne teleported (1 damage).
- They played a group set (Aalyn's natural 20 "Pink Pony Club") and made 23 gp 12 sp.
- Mid-song they saw **Bog being led north by guards.** Wayne chased him through the crowds but lost the trail near Noble's Rise and the west gate.
- Fern guards in green were questioning Brena and the three friends outside the Orchid. **Bog has been detained for murder.** The guards wanted the rest of the party for questioning as accomplices.
- Aalyn offered to answer under his own Zone of Truth, and they refused. His Charm Person failed. Alaric sent Thimble Jr. II to Sir Romulus. Thimble's Suggestion worked on the first guard (his chaos die was back to a d20), but a larger guard overruled her.
- Aalyn Misty Stepped onto the Orchid's roof. The commander: "As you can see by our green attire, we answer to nobody but the Ferns." Thimble surrendered. Alaric and Wayne were bound, and Aalyn was grabbed when he jumped down. Six more guards with hand crossbows arrived. The crowd whispered, "They're friends with the murderer."
- As they were walked toward the west gate, a lightning bolt shot into the sky from a building at the end of the street, and **Bog's head poked out the door.**
