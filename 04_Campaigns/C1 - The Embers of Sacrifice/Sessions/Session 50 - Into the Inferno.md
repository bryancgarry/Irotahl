# Session 50: Into the Inferno

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 49 - The Rock God]], [[Durnhollow]], [[Myra]], [[Rec]], [[Vistro IV]], [[The Fire Sages of Vistro]], [[The Demonomicon]], [[Soulvite]]

> [!important] Played: "Into the Inferno" (Episode 50)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Dyq-9abwoGg). Bog woke inside Myra's pod and went back in. Aalyn rode a cart through the lava cavern, fell off and found a rapier mold. The rest rode two carts down the lava roller coaster, with Wayne luging on top, then dangling from a rope, then flying off the rails over a lava plume. Below lay an old dwarven market on a lava river. Bog ate amber salamander and walked through lava into the fire enhancement chamber, where he, Alaric and Aalyn ran its ring puzzle and turned Aalyn's stone greataxe into a magic weapon. Wayne found a padlock key that fit a lava skiff, and he and Thimble sailed off toward an obsidian castle. Fire salamanders escorted them to King Vistro IV. Thimble gave him the star ruby and he warmed to them, until Wayne called himself the Kyn'Ruthar and Thimble pulled out the Tome of Malcanthet.

## What Happened at the Table

**Recap**
- Bog's player is back. **Bog and Alaric both get heroic inspiration** for birthdays during the break; they share a birthday.
- The DM recapped Sessions 47 to 49 for Bog. The party has not signed a contract with Myra yet.

**Bog wakes**
- Bog came to in the dark, wet warmth of **Myra's pod**, and pushed out. He rolled Myra a 9 on a d10, asked "Mind if I go back in?" and went back in. Myra: it felt nice to be so full.
- He has been dipped in the mushroom pool; he feels fine.

**Aalyn on the rails**
- Rec, flying behind, spun wildly to warn Aalyn about **a lava waterfall** over the track. Aalyn flew up off the cart; **dwarven runes on the cart raised a shield** and it passed through unharmed. He got back in.
- Tucked down with Rec covering him like a lid, he slipped past the ballistas (Stealth 20).
- Over a lava hot spot (Dex save 23) he was fine, but **a natural 1 on Acrobatics at the next bend flung him 50 feet off the cart.** His boots caught him.
- He landed by **the chest**: lockpicked (14), it held **a casting mold for a rapier.** A ballista hit him as he flew on (11 force). He followed the carts through the hole.

**The lava luge**
- Alaric chose the second track. **Alaric, Thimble and Wayne** took one cart, with Wayne lying on top as a rock. **Bog and Myra** took another; Bog's Persuasion (16) talked her into coming down, and she grew him a cushioned racing seat.
- Wayne pushed off like a luge (17). Alaric relayed everything through Rec's eyes.
- **A lava hot spot** erupted at a crossing. **Bog breathed cold on it** (9) and Myra cushioned it with spores. The carts locked together; Myra grew **mushroom wings and a spoiler**.
- At the corkscrew, the cart's wheels lifted. **Bog spent his heroic inspiration.** Wayne lost his grip; **Alaric threw a rope** and Wayne caught it (21), and Bog and Myra held the other end. Wayne dangled and swung around the turns.
- Next turn: **the cart came off the rails** and flew. Survival checks steadied it in the air (Thimble 21); Wayne swung back on.
- The last stretch: a ballista mistook dangling Wayne for lost rocks. **The ramp** at the end launched them over a lava plume past lava waterfalls; **Bog's natural 20** and a flap of his wings carried them, and Wayne barrel-rolled back into the cart.

**The market**
- Through rubber flaps they reached **a multi-level stone dock on a river of lava**: decrepit market stalls, spheres still tending empty crates, **lava skiffs of treated wood**, stone aqueduct walkways, a blazing foundry on the right with its door barricaded. In the ceiling, **the bottom of a stone cylinder**: what looks like the earth elemental's chamber.
- **Alaric (Investigation 27):** Durnhollow's own underground market. Silver crafts everywhere: jewelry, weapons, broken bottles of silver brews, ingots, armor and weapon molds. **Some tapestries carry an early merchants' alliance symbol: a gold star with a crescent above and below, like a coin.**
- **Bog** found **amber salamander skewers** in a stone cooler and **a tankard of dwarven fire whiskey**: 5 temporary hit points and **resistance to fire for 24 hours**.
- Aalyn: decayed musicians' stalls; maybe more of that deeper in.
- Everyone found about **10 gp.** **Wayne found a padlock key**, the kind for a bike lock.

**Wayne and Thimble set sail**
- The key fit **the wheel lock on a lava skiff** on the far pier.
- Thimble (History 21) learned to sail on his family's yacht off the Glimmerlands. Wayne climbed the mast (Perception 17) and saw, far off on an island in the lava lake, **a dark obsidian castle inside an energy barrier.** They sailed for it. Alaric: "Don't die."
- **Fire salamander snakes** swam alongside like dolphins, then **salamander warriors with spears of solid flame** escorted them.
- **Thimble's natural 20 Perception:** on an outcropping a quarter mile from the castle, coiled around a stone throne with a flaming trident, **the Salamander King.**

**The keycard and the door**
- Alaric and Aalyn climbed a spiral staircase on the market's left to **a big reinforced door**: sealed magically, to keep something *in*, and opened by the same kind of keycard as above. Asking nicely and knocking didn't work; a half-hearted Rob Deer cost 1 bludgeoning.
- Aalyn flew out to the skiff, got **the keycard** from Wayne (who pulled it from somewhere unseen; it's dirt, not what it looks like), cleaned it with *Prestidigitation*, and flew back.

**The fire enhancement chamber**
- Bog, with fire resistance, **walked through a lava waterfall** and found he's immune to the lava for now. Beyond: **a factory over the lava, and a platform suspended on chains over a lava chasm**, with concentric rune rings stepping down to a central pedestal, three altars with books, and braziers.
- He reached Wayne by psionic link at the edge of its range. Aalyn came back; Alaric and Aalyn came down. **Bog parted the lava waterfall with his body** (Acrobatics 23) so they could walk through.
- Alaric read the Dwarvish: **a later, better-built enhancement chamber, for fire.** Three people at three altars, three stages, each rune carried in a straight line to the center by rotating the rings, before the fire burns out.
- **Aalyn put his stone greataxe** (from the quarry commander) and **three silver ingots** on the pedestal. Bog spoke the incantation in his accent. They solved all three stages.
- **The greataxe is now a magic weapon**: carved and inlaid with silver runes, it can **cast a spell like a staff** as well as strike, and **its wielder has resistance to fire damage.**
- A golden sphere in the ceiling unfolded into **a maintenance construct** with brooms, cleaning up. Aalyn named it **Charles.** They decided to save the rest of their silver for something worth upgrading.
- Bog wandered off toward what looks like **a power station.**

**The black rocks**
- Alaric and Aalyn found a collapsed path blocked by **dark rocks.** Fire Bolt and Eldritch Blast (31) only scorched them. They need real explosives; Thimble has the blasting charge.

**King Vistro IV**
- The guards escorted the skiff to the king's island: a lava oasis, beaded and robed, a goblet in hand, flanked by guards and salamander courtiers. Horns and drums.
- Wisdom saves: **Wayne was nearly frightened by his presence**; Thimble, used to nobility, was fine.
- **Thimble offered the star ruby.** The king set it into the centerpiece of his chest piece. "A generous gift." Then: "You need to leave now."
- Thimble pushed back. **"I am King Vistro IV, guardian of these crimson wastes."**
- **At the name Danglesworth of Glimmerstone: "Your family is the reason I'm here. I should kill you where you stand." He was banished to this plane.** Thimble: so was he; they're the same. Persuasion (14, 17) kept the peace.
- He offered them his goblet: 5 hit points each.
- **Wayne said they call him the Kyn'Ruthar.** The king sat back: **"You wish to access the temple,"** the pilgrimage site of the demon **Malcanthet.**
- **Thimble pulled out the Tome of Malcanthet.** "Throw that book in these wastes in the next three seconds, or you will die." Persuasion (DC 20) failed.
- **He blasted them with his trident** (DC 18 Dex): Wayne took nothing (Evasion), Thimble took 18 fire. He called his guards forward. Session ends.

**After the session**
- Vistro is a **salamander inferno master, CR 15.**
- The skiff trip was off the map; this is an optional side area they found "because you put the keys in the boat."
- Thimble's last-resort plan if it goes south: plead to Malcanthet through the tome.
