# Session 28: Emperor Hawk Tuah and The Goon Generals

**Party:** Level 6 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 27 - The Pyramid]], [[Rec]], [[Lolth]], [[Hollowmere]]

> [!important] Played: "Emperor Hawk Tuah and The Goon Generals" (Episode 28)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=x-JT9PWFz4Q). The mummy, the Great Pharaoh Sokatoa, took Thimble for "the bean god" and demanded a lullaby to put him back to sleep. It failed, and the party fought him and his four flaming "goon generals." Thimble fireballed him to ash and he reformed; Bog finally ripped him apart and took his bandages as wraps of unarmed power. Wayne looted a Sun Blade and an air elemental gem. On the road, a burning grove of trees kept turning up beside them, and they ran. Ends as three armored riders, led by a young woman on a white mare, block the trail: "Identify yourselves."

## What Happened at the Table

**The mummy wakes**
- "You have awoken me from my slumber." The mummy fixed Thimble, standing in front, with its glare. "Are you the bean god?" Thimble allowed that he was.
- He is **the Great Pharaoh Sokatoa**. The skeletons are **his generals** (the party heard "goon generals," and laughed).
- Wayne called him "Mr. Hawk Tuah" and vouched that Thimble is the bean god. Thimble rattled his bag of beans. "I'm your father."
- His soul is here, "but also in the bean." He won't let them leave until they **put him back to rest.**
- Thimble saw **a lot of gold** in the sarcophagus.

**The lullaby**
- Sokatoa asked for **a lullaby** for him and his generals. He claims Thimble as his.
- Alaric's *Minor Illusion* tucked a **blanket** around him, and it whispered along. **Aalyn sang**: *"Hush now, oh kings of dust and bone..."* Wayne played bagpipes, out of tune. Bog refused to sing. With Bog out, everyone rolled at disadvantage.
- He gave them one more try. Thimble joined on the war gong. Sokatoa: "No. I don't like it. You will die now." Initiative. The party went first.

**The fight**
- **Alaric** cast ***Rageful Nimbus*** on Thimble (a little white cloud over his head), then a *Fire Bolt* at Sokatoa (10). His Deception made Sokatoa think **Thimble had thrown it**: "The bean god dares use the holy flames against me."
- **Wayne** put the Rat Lord's Hat on Bog and loaded the **rat cannon** from Bog's Skullcup: a compact boomrat burst in fire and then force between Sokatoa and the **red-flamed general** (9 fire, 13 force). The fire only emboldened the red one. Both held the recoil (Strength saves).
- Bog's flurry missed the **blue-flamed general**, who gives off cold. Wayne charged his psychic blades.
- **Aalyn** stepped in front of Alaric, put *Hex* on the blue general, and broke off a rib and a hand with two *Eldritch Blasts* (13, 16). "It's going to be kind of hard to goon with that, General." "Lucky for you, I goon with my left hand."
- **Thimble** went cold and quiet: "You call me the bean god. I'm the one that summoned you, and you want to challenge me? That was a mistake." Intimidation 19 left Sokatoa **hesitating**, unsure whether he was defying his god.
- Thimble's *Fireball* (Empowered Spell twice) **burned Sokatoa to nothing.** The gemstone on the sarcophagus pulsed, the bandages blew back together, and **he reformed.** "You need to do more than that, bean god."
- **Wild magic:** Thimble threw up **four frogs.** He tossed one to Wayne (Sleight of Hand 20), and **Pebbles caught it in her mouth.** "Go, my children."
- Sokatoa's rotting fist hit Thimble and **cursed him**: no regaining hit points until a long rest, and **13 off his maximum** if he doesn't take one within 24 hours. The **nimbus** struck back with lightning (11) and burned off the arm, so the second blow did nothing.
- Sokatoa turned his **dreadful glare** on Alaric. Alaric saved and is immune for 24 hours.
- The generals threw their flames: red at Wayne (16 fire), purple lightning at Aalyn (11), green poison at Thimble (4). The blue general and Bog "shadowboxed," both missing everything.
- **Wayne** stabbed Sokatoa in the back of the head and detonated the psionic charge. He was **stunned**, but the blast went off inside **the blue general**, who exploded (Bog took 5 cold). **Each general is tied to Sokatoa.** Clicks sounded from the side rooms.
- **Alaric** cast *Summon Beast* as a shadowy wolf, which bit Sokatoa. His *Minor Illusion* of the bean god's voice failed: "I know the voice of the bean god, and you are not him." Rec helped Thimble.
- **Bog**'s flurry shattered **the purple general.** He wiped his mouth with a strip of bandage and found it "powerful."
- **Aalyn** moved *Hex* to Sokatoa and offered him peace: go back to bed and leave the treasure (Persuasion 28). "I just want to die! You'd better deal the finishing blow." Two *Eldritch Blasts*.
- **Thimble** walked past the stunned mummy to look in the sarcophagus: gold, a bottle or two. He thought about stepping into Rec's space, learned he can only leave it **by breaking it, which would destroy Rec**, and didn't. *Chaos Bolt* hit the **green general** (5); wild magic **teleported him back to the entrance.**
- The skeletons in the side rooms started moving.
- **Wayne** hit Sokatoa twice more and **hid by lying flat on the floor.** His second hit blew up **the green general.**
- **Alaric** cast ***Rope Trick*** and hid in it with Thimble. "Guys, there's a bunch of skeletons coming."
- **Bog** rolled a natural 20 and a **55 on the crit table**: a crippling blow, triple max damage (**33**). He ripped the bandages off Sokatoa's groin, and the bandages coiled around his forearms: **+1 wraps of unarmed power.**
- With **Skullcup Strike** in his flurry, Bog killed **the red general** and spun Sokatoa by a bandage into his fist, then leapt and slapped his head off. Sokatoa: "I've had enough, just do it." **The mummy fell.** The skeletons collapsed. Bog dealt about **61 damage** that turn.

**The loot**
- Aalyn: **100 gp** and **a wand of conducting**, from a skeleton with crumbled sheet music.
- Alaric: **a wand of magic missiles** and **150 gp**.
- Bog: **50 gp** (and the wraps).
- Thimble: **a potion of fire breath**, **a potion of greater healing**, and **100 gp**.
- **Wayne** (natural 20): **350 gp**, **a periapt of health**, **a +1 wand of the war mage**, **dust of disappearance**, the sarcophagus gemstone (**an air elemental gem**, a blue sapphire), and **a Sun Blade.**
- **Thimble's sapphire.** Thimble held his blue sapphire from [[Session 8 - Gnomes and Fireballs|Session 8]] up to Wayne's gem (Arcana 19). Both have winds swirling inside. Alaric (Arcana): **both are air elemental gems**, one use each; break one to summon an air elemental that fights for you. Thimble gave Wayne his back.
- During the comparison, someone at the table brought up "a necklace from way back in the day, from a certain enemy that's now a demon lord." *(DM, after the fact:)* That's the Ember of Sacrifice used for Graz'zt.
- Wayne tried to cast *Fireball* with the war mage wand. Nothing. Alaric's fake *Fire Bolt* illusion didn't fool him. Alaric handed him the wand of magic missiles, and that one fired. Wayne: "Maybe I should take magic." Alaric was thrilled to teach him.
- Aalyn healed Wayne (*Cure Wounds*, 29).
- A stray magic missile hit the wall. The pyramid shook, the doors opened, and they ran out as it **sank back into the ground.** They were inside about two hours; it's around 10 or 11 in the morning.
- Thimble has the mummy's handprint on him. Aalyn told him to take it easy.
- Wayne lit a joint with his Sun Blade.

**The plains**
- Hollowmere is **five or six days** away. Aalyn led (Survival 11) off the road into open plains.
- These plains sit near the magical center of the continent. **Wandering firestorms** pass through now and then and burn small groves of trees. They are harmless and part of the land's cycle.
- **Day one:** a burning grove of trees in the distance. Alaric spent the day teaching Wayne magic; once in a hundred waves a magic missile came out.
- **Night one:** Aalyn ritual-cast *Alarm*. Thimble collapsed in the grass, and Aalyn dug his bedroll out of Wayne's bag to tuck him in. In the bag, **Pebbles was choking on a frog.** She got it down and gave **her first honk.** Wayne honked back (Animal Handling 13), and she whispered one more as she settled in.
- **Thimble's curse faded** with the long rest.
- **Day two:** another burning grove. **Aalyn** (natural 1 Perception) noticed **the flames give off no smoke**, and said nothing. His player played it as the words being taken from him.
- **Night two:** On first watch Alaric saw a glow on the horizon, not the sun. On his watch Aalyn (natural 20) recognized **the same two forked oak trees.** It's the same grove, and he has seen it three times. He kept quiet, then asked Alaric for a map. Alaric has none.
- **Day three:** Wayne saw the grove **burning 60 feet behind them.** Alaric: "Aalyn, I've seen the thing four times." **The party ran.**
- By noon they reached hillier ground with rock formations. They're about halfway to Hollowmere.

**The riders**
- On a trail through the rocks, **three riders in shining silver armor** approached. Leading them on a white mare was **a young woman** in her early twenties, short blonde hair, a silver sword at her hip.
- "Identify yourselves." Session ends.

**After the session**
- The party rolled on her looks. Thimble (19) is smitten. Aalyn and Wayne thought she was fine. Wayne prefers the horse.
- Out of character, the DM's slip gave her first name, **Luna**. The party doesn't know it in game.
- Pebbles' first honk. Aalyn is writing a song about it.
