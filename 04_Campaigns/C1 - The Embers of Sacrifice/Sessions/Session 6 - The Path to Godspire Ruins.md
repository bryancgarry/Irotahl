# Session 6: The Path to Godspire Ruins

**Party:** Level 4 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 5 - Bluelock and Bloodlancers]], [[Malrik Greene]], [[Bragthar]], [[Oakhollow]], [[Lithoweaves]], [[Oughan Gor'Drak]]

> [!important] Played: "The Path to Godspire Ruins" (Episode 6)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rVjPM6XxPjA). The party looted the Bloodlancer camp and freed Malrik Greene, met a traveling merchant, solved the race puzzle at the Godspire ruins, and pierced the illusion at the summit to find the lithoweave itself. Ends on a cliffhanger.

## What Happened at the Table

**Recap additions from the DM**
- Wayne's Session 5 vision was him winning his third championship in the **YGSL (Youth Goliath Skyball League)**. It cut off when the Volkarr mentioned the Void Trial and the title Kyn'Ruthar. Wayne came out holding the pale gemstone again.
- Aalyn's dawn choice was the light over the dark, and it blessed him with extra healing.

**Malrik Greene in the cage**
- One of the camp's cages held [[Malrik Greene]]. Alaric's natural 20 Perception: unlike the goblin cages, this one was built to hold a spellcaster, laced with iron and smeared with old and fresh blood in patterns.
- Alaric wiped and burned away the blood runes (Arcana 18, then 10), leaving only a keyless blood lock. Bragthar rolled a natural 20 and tore the top off the cage. Blood magic respects strength, so its red energy faded into Bragthar.
- Aalyn's natural 20 Medicine: Malrik had been beaten and tortured for 12 to 24 hours and had three levels of exhaustion. Aalyn's Healing Word brought him around (12, with his golden string). Malrik noticed Aalyn's brand.

**The goblin prisoners**
- Wayne (who speaks Goblin) talked to the caged goblins (Persuasion 21). Their big family was traveling when the orcs took some of them. One escaped with a big sack of their loot and is posted up by a stream about a day back toward Oakhollow. The table connected this to the Bridge King.
- A goblin in the far cage spoke a strange dialect, maybe from Drak'mor. He had been impaled by a blood lance in the fight and was dead.
- Bragthar ripped open one more cage. Thimble found the cage key in the dirt (Investigation 20), pocketed it, then freed the last goblin. The goblins ran off.

**Bog's breakfast**
- Bog (Arcana 11) found the orc stew magical but harmless: stuffed intestines capped with eyeballs, a Drak'mor front-line ration. Bog and Bragthar ate it: **+5 feet of movement for 24 hours.**

**The commander's body (Wayne, Investigation 22)**
- Ruined heavy armor (Bragthar impaled him through it), a plain mace, 15 gp, 69 sp, a bean, and an open condom wrapper.
- **General Veraxous's letter** (in Orc): march on Oakhollow within the next moon's turn. The Free Kingdoms will shatter once their central pillar falls, and Darkstone and the rest will crumble. The Bloodlancers are the first wave: burn the crops, put fear in their hearts, glory to the dominion.
- **Malrik's note**, taken from him and crumpled as if he tried to destroy it: ordinary Enclave check-ins and Oakhollow's status, signed Malrik, with notes in the margins in another language.

**The commander's tent (Aalyn)**
- A rough map of Oakhollow marked with siege points on the outer wall, a back way into the keep (the day-one attack route), a big circle on the **Grand Cathedral**, and a circle by the fishermen's quarter outside the walls: the smugglers' cove on the Bluelock shore.
- A small lockbox under the cot. Aalyn failed to pick it and failed to smash it, found 3 cp, stuffed it in his pack, and denied having it. Thimble's Detect Magic lit it up. Aalyn opened it with Wayne's thieves' tools (18) and flew up so no one could see.
- Inside: an **illusionary battery** (a 4 to 6 inch glass vial with metal caps, raw ether and sunlight swirling inside) and a letter. Alaric recognized the battery: it stores prophecy from the solar refractory and only plays back when plugged into it. It went back to Malrik.
- The letter (Comprehend Languages), the commander to General Veraxous: the weapon beneath Oakhollow is not just a legend. Scouts intercepted a missive from the Baron's court. Something vile and powerful is hidden deep in the city, and if the walls fall it will be theirs. Signed "Bloodbound, fire unyielding, victory eternal, glory to Oughan."

**Thimble's surge**
- Detect Magic triggered a wild surge (d100: 83). Light poured out of Thimble and blinded everyone for about an hour. They short rested. Aalyn's song ("I can see clearly now") gave heroic inspiration to himself and Alaric.

**Talking with Malrik**
- Bog's natural 20 Insight: Malrik tells the truth but leaves gaps.
- He left the royal reception early because his confidant **Orthus** sent him on urgent business. Orthus is the other Obsidian Enclave member, adviser to Darkstone and part of Sir Romulus's guard, and the old man who seemed to know what Wayne's pale gemstone was. Orthus never passed Malrik's message to the party.
- He did not know the Baron had turned. He asked after the princess; Aalyn told him the Baron is dead and Rosalie is alive.
- Persuasion 19: he was sent to explore the Godspire ruins. He called it "the prophecy" to Alaric, then realized his battery was missing. Orthus had reported activity at the ruins and thought the magic's origin was there. Malrik had visited the ruins before and found only old murals.
- He came to Oakhollow shortly after Rosalie's birth. The Baron spent an unusual amount of time in the prayer room. Malrik did not know the previous Enclave adviser to the king.
- The party agreed: warn Oakhollow about the invasion, and investigate the weapon themselves.

**Messages home**
- Alaric sent his owl, Thimble Jr. II, back to Oakhollow with the map ("Invasion incoming," signed by Alaric and Aalyn) and General Veraxous's letter, for the prince.
- Wayne quietly gave Malrik's margin notes to Aalyn. His third Comprehend Languages read one line in Draconic: **"The daughter is key."** Aalyn assumes Rosalie.
- Bog, who insists dragons are not real, snatched the note, wrapped it around a rock and threw it about a quarter mile into the woods (Strength 14).
- Aalyn gave Malrik his potion of healing. Malrik hobbled back to Oakhollow. Aalyn warned him to keep it quiet, since they don't know who fed the orcs their information.
- Bragthar threw the bodies into the woods. The party skipped the three patrols. Malrik gave directions to the ruins, and Aalyn started teaching Bragthar Common (first word: "give").

**[[Birdolomew Feathersworth]]**
- On the road, an aarakocra merchant, the self-proclaimed number one name in aven commerce, from the mountains of [[Vyrmor]]. He runs the Feathersworth Emporium in Oakhollow with his wife and daughter. His wares:
  - Featherweight Elixir (50 gp): a tenth of your weight for an hour.
  - Treasure compass (25 gp): its box says "Always points somewhere. Isn't that the real adventure?"
  - Pocket Phoenixes (25 gp): wooden birds that burst into harmless fireworks and reform in your pocket in 24 hours.
  - Feathersworth Wild Wager coins (50 gp): two-headed coins that always land opposite the call.
  - Marvelous Mineral Moisturizer (10 gp) and Squawking Stones (1 gp each, screech when thrown).
  - Privately, to Thimble: an immovable rod. He passed.
- Alaric called heads, lost, and covered it with Minor Illusion. Birdolomew's roll saw through it, and he respected the craft.
- Purchases:
  - Alaric: a Wild Wager coin and a Squawking Stone (51 gp).
  - Aalyn: a Wild Wager coin and a Pocket Phoenix for 50 gp total, in exchange for the location of the Bloodlancer camp ("Tell them Malrik Greene sent you").
  - Thimble: the compass (25 gp). Alaric illusioned it to point at the ruins so he wouldn't be crushed.
  - Bog: intimidated a tail feather out of him for 5 gp.
  - Wayne: the moisturizer and 10 Squawking Stones. The black lotion made his stone glitter in sunlight and gives him **one extra free use of Cloud's Jaunt** until used.
- The DM hasn't built these items in D&D Beyond yet.

**The Godspire ruins**
- Wayne led (Survival natural 20). They reached the ruins at evening as a storm rolled in.
- **The race shrine.** A faceless statue the size of Thimble on a cylindrical pedestal split into four quadrants: elves, dwarves, giants, gnomes. Each wall carving had four stone slabs beneath it.
  - **Elven** (Alaric): a figure with a lantern guiding others through darkness. "Seek the light of knowledge, but bear the burden of eternity, for wisdom is the balance between." Slabs: crescent moon, quill, owl, mirror. **Owl.**
  - **Gnomish** (Thimble): a gnome on a cliff braving a storm. "Not the swiftest nor the strongest, but the last to fall." Slabs: torch, clock, bridge, nautilus shell. **Clock.**
  - **Giant** (Wayne): a titan holding up a crumbling landmass. "Only the mighty stand unbroken. The world bends to those who do not." Slabs: battle axe, fist, tornado, campfire. **Fist.**
  - **Dwarven** (Bog, helped by Aalyn): a smith at a lava forge; legible only "blade" and "lest the forge be steady." Slabs: hammer, shield, mountain, crown. Bog picked the shield; Alaric's 24 spotted it didn't fit (the answer meant craftsmanship). **Hammer.**
  - The pedestal rose to reveal a faceless stone bust with spikes meant to hold a **mask**. It was gone. Carved into it with a dagger tip: a **triangle with an X through it**. Wayne knows from Jack that this means the place is already looted. Alaric (Arcana 22): residual magic, gone for some time.
- **Wall fragments** (Alaric, Investigation 27), one in each language:
  - Elven: an endless weave and a hand that shapes the loom. "Beware the folly of gods who play at faith."
  - Dwarven: "To forge is to shape through fire, and to sacrifice is to temper through suffering. The embers still burn, waiting for those who can withstand the heat."
  - Giant: "The river does not question its course, nor does the mountain refuse the weight it bears. Causality is the hand that binds all."
  - Gnomish: "Fire leaps where it pleases and storms twist where they will. If causality is the hand, then chaos is the wind. To bind the world in rules is to choke it. Life must be wild, untamed, and ever changing."
- **The dragon temple.** A pristine obsidian obelisk, as if it had recently shed a stone shell, faintly humming, with dragon murals. Bog punched it (Con save 19) and it resonated with him: in its reflection only he saw a **large bronze dragon** staring back. He breathed lightning through it (9). Chaos-like energy spiraled out, and the obelisk shattered.
- Alaric (Investigation 10) found a rear mural: an elegant male dragon in the center, two entities on either side, ringed by obsidian pieces like the obelisk, with writing he couldn't read. He copied it, roughly.
- **The summit.** A large tree in a circle of stone. Everyone passed a Con save. Alaric's Investigation 27 (DC 24) pierced the illusion: the forest around them is calcified, and before them is a machine like the solar refractory but powered by dark magic, metal rings carved with runes whirling. **The lithoweave, a world engine, rewriting reality and calcifying everything around it, including the party.**
