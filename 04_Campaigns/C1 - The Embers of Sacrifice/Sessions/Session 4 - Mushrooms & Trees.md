# Session 4: Mushrooms & Trees

**Party:** Level 4 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 3 - The Bridge King]], [[The Tall Oak Forest]], [[Bragthar]], [[Papa Glabb]], [[Lithoweaves]]

> [!important] Played: "Mushrooms & Trees" (Episode 4)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Iahoky1Gzr8). Bragthar's mushroom cove, two Bloodlancer scouts on the road, and the first stone tree, which woke into a Tree Blight.

## What Happened at the Table

**Table rules**
- *Identify* needs a 100 gp pearl.
- Bragthar: troll, 94 HP, wears a top hat. He obeys whoever wears the crown.
- No new combat starts after 10 pm.
- Crits use a d100 crit table from now on.

**Parting shot**
- Thimble skipped a stone off the river into the Bridge King's skull for 7 damage. The Bridge King: "I will remember that."

**Bragthar's mushroom cove**
- A hidden cove with a magic pool, a spiral stone floor, and a ring of mushrooms. Bragthar mentioned "housekeepers" who change the mushrooms.
- Alaric and Aalyn bottled flasks of the pool water. This "mushroom water" dissolves oil.
- Thimble ate a red-and-white mushroom (temporary HP, and his wild magic flared) and pocketed four more.
- Stepping into the pool spun a roulette:
  - **Wayne:** a purple mushroom granted one question to the fungal network. He asked, "Do you know where the stone trees are?" The answer: "Yes."
  - **Thimble:** green specks settled into his core. No visible effect yet.
  - **Aalyn:** a mimic mushroom, Shroomly, that repeats the last word said. He tossed it to Bragthar, who ate it.
  - **Alaric:** shrank to 12 inches tall and rode his owl.
  - **Bog:** declined.

**The road and the Bloodlancers**
- Wayne found wagon grooves and drops of black ichor along the road.
- Two Bloodlancer orc scouts. Wayne speaks Orc and overheard them:
  - Their commander is furious that outsiders foiled Durak's attack on the keep. Durak was one scout's brother.
  - They are waiting on reinforcements from Azure Port.
  - They captured a man in black robes carrying a note the general will want.
  - General Veraxous has threatened to send them back to the mines in Gormazer if they fail again.
- The "pink pony" ambush: an invisible Bragthar and Wayne killed one scout. Aalyn charmed the other, who called himself "Geralt of Rivia" and told them:
  - Four patrol pairs are out.
  - The camp is half an hour up the right fork, by a dry riverbed: the commander, two in tents, and two lookouts.
  - His grandpappy was [[Papa Glabb]].
- Aalyn then stabbed Geralt, and Bog and Thimble finished him. Loot: 5 gp and two chain shirts, stored in Alaric's Bag of Holding. Bragthar tore up the bodies.
- One missing patrol pair was found in the woods watching goblins have sex.

**The logging clearing**
- One huge stone tree, veined with black ichor. A dead lumberman lay in the pond. Alaric had Bragthar bury him.
- The Godspire ruins were visible from here, about six hours away.
- Wayne's pale gemstone necklace pulled toward the tree. The tree is the same stone Wayne is made of.
- Thimble pressed the necklace to the tree. The tree absorbed it and woke as a **Tree Blight**, with twig and needle blights. Every branded PC's brand tingled.
- Thimble's wild surges: 48 (invisibility) and 61 (flammable things he touches catch fire).
- The Blight grappled Bragthar again and again. He took over 100 damage, and Aalyn healed him.
- Wayne's natural 20 killed the Tree Blight. A prism-like core, spiked with black ichor, came down out of the tree.
