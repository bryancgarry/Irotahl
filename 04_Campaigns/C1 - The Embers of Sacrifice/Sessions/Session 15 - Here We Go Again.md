# Session 15: Here We Go Again

**Party:** Level 5 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 14 - A Little Bit of Everything]], [[Malrik Greene]], [[The Obsidian Enclave]], [[House Fenwyn]], [[The Thieves Guild]]

> [!important] Played: "Here We Go Again" (Episode 15)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uf7AWhACZno). *(First session in about three weeks.)* The party finished searching Malrik's Solarium, and Wayne woke the Rat Lord's Hat. Back at the Orchid, Bog and Thimble put a waitress to sleep, while Aalyn won a pit fight and then attacked Calder. Ends with Aalyn cornered by the crowd and Fern guards storming the Orchid.

## What Happened at the Table

**Before play**
- The DM said this arc was meant to run about ten sessions and end at level 5. Leveling is coming soon, but not yet.
- **Bog's Rat Lord's Skullcup** became a real item. He's attuned to it with no attunement time, since he crafted it.

**The Solarium, continued**
- Alaric read his parents' letter privately and put on the cloak: noble blue with a gold or white trim and the House Fenwyn crest on the back. He's giddy.
- Alaric's History 26: Malrik kept healing potions in a barrel. He found **two potions of healing and one greater healing**, and gave Aalyn one.
- Wayne and Thimble searched the storeroom together (natural 20, with Thimble on Wayne's shoulders). They found:
  - **An illusionary battery**, charged, with a different-colored core from Malrik's.
  - **Malrik's log books**, mostly redacted. One entry says he locked his other logs away somewhere else.
  - **A note about Wayne and Thimble**, tucked in the pages. *(The Obsidian Enclave note.)*
  - **A key with half a heart on the end** (the top half).
- Aalyn scraped 30 cp out of the barrels.
- **Wayne woke the Rat Lord's Hat.**
  - At first he could only pull out tufts of rat hair and bone. Once he rubbed on the Rat Lord's blood, he pulled out a whole rat skeleton.
  - His Arcana 12: more blood wouldn't help, and he needed something more drastic. **He licked the blood off his arm**, and felt the strongest connection yet.
  - He pulled out a live rat, then a **BoomRat** with red sigils. It wouldn't go back in the hat, so he set it on the floor and they ran. It went off behind them.
- Alaric returned the key to Magister Pelen, who didn't notice anything.

**Back at the Orchid** (about 9 at night)
- On the way, they checked on Sheila and Bragthar at the stables. Alaric asked Thimble about the crest.
  - Thimble, as a Danglesworth of Glimmerstone, would know House Fenwyn as one of the closer elven houses.
  - Alaric is still part of House Fenwyn. **His parents are fine with him adventuring and want them all to visit.** His parents knew Malrik and sent the chest through him.
- **The battery.** Alaric's Arcana: it isn't in the Solarium's catalog and he's never seen its kind. Only a working solar refractory can play it. The refractory is built on the same technology as the Godspire relic. Alaric can't build one from scratch, but he might repair it with parts. He's keeping the battery in his bag of holding.
- Alaric attuned to the cloak and felt flickers of illusion settle around him.
- The party took a short rest in the tubs.

**Bog and Ellie**
- At the bar Bog met a waitress from the restaurant next door on her break (attractiveness 10). Her name is **Jessa Thorne**, but she goes by **Ellie**, because her father gave her the name and used to beat her.
- Bog bought her food and two rounds of Mud Puddles. She out-drank him. She has a two-year-old at home and gets off at midnight. **She'd stay the night for 100 sp.**
- She had heard that a woman went into this room with a mystery man last night and didn't come out alive.
- Wayne watched from the bar. **The bartender asked him whether Bog killed that girl last night. He said yes.**
- Bog recruited Thimble ("you'll be my first friend," plus a promised smoke). The plan: put Ellie to sleep and make her believe she spent the night with the Rat Lord's headless body.
- Thimble cast *Suggestion* with sorcery points (Persuasion: Thimble 27, Bog 9 with heroic inspiration). **Ellie fell asleep.** Thimble has to hold concentration until 6 in the morning.
- Thimble's wild magic:
  - From the spell (93): he temporarily can't read.
  - Then Bog spat in a half-finished Mud Puddle and gave it to him (27): his nose tripled in size, giving advantage on smell for an hour.

**The fighting pits**
- Wayne, Alaric and Aalyn went down. **It now costs three wooden tokens per person.** Weapons were left in the locker room upstairs.
- Two big men grabbed Alaric to fight. They got his cloak's illusory double instead. Aalyn volunteered: "Take me."
- Wayne spotted **Calder**: the skinny man on a high stool above the pit, taking bets and signing up fighters. He calls himself the pit master. Wayne bet 5 gp on Aalyn. The odds were 1:2 for Kagra.
- **Aalyn vs. Kagra the Butcher**, a huge blue tiefling barbarian. The pit uses stamina points (Con + level + the higher of Acrobatics or Athletics + 5); Aalyn had 18.
  - Aalyn shirtless, his patch tied as a headband. He dodged, swept Kagra's leg and stomped him, spat blood in his eyes, and slipped his grab.
  - He snuck a rapier slash to the back of Kagra's knees, but everyone saw (Sleight of Hand 10).
  - Kagra pulled two daggers, rolled a natural 1, and **impaled two spectators.** Aalyn hit him with Vexing Verse, branding a music note on his chest. Kagra hit Aalyn with a thrown-in folding chair.
  - **Wayne threw a real dagger into the back of Kagra's head** (20 damage), hidden by Alaric's *Minor Illusion*. Aalyn kicked off the wall with his boots and torpedoed straight through Kagra (natural 20). **Kagra died with a hole through his middle.**
  - Wayne collected 10 gp.
- **Calder in private:** "It wasn't a fair fight." He wouldn't talk about the ruins job: "None of my business, and none of yours."
- Alaric offered a bet: information if they win, or 100 gp or his cloak if they lose. Aalyn talked big about their fighters and connections.
- The terms became two fighters picked by each side. The party picked one of the bouncers who grabbed Alaric. Calder picked **Mave**, a half-elf fighter with braided black hair and bruised knuckles.
- Before the bell, **Aalyn cast *Pacify Person* on Mave**, in plain sight. The room went silent. Calder: "Break that spell and you're getting back in the ring. Nobody messes with my pit."
- Aalyn turned and stabbed at Calder. **Wayne dove in the way** (Acrobatics vs. DC 22) and took the 8 damage himself.
- The crowd backed Aalyn into a corner and beat him. Wayne ran to Silas's office (empty), then tried to talk Calder down (Persuasion 11): "I don't think he learned his lesson yet."
- **Aalyn's eyes went pure black.** The familiar voice in his head said these people are thieves, and the world is better without them. He cut a man's hand off at the wrist.
- Upstairs, Thimble (Perception 20) heard Aalyn's screams through the floor. **Fern guards in green burst through the Orchid's doors, heading downstairs.**
