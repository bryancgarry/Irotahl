# Session 35: Bugs and Dragons

**Party:** Level 6 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 34 - The Hunt Begins]], [[Hollowmere]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "Bugs and Dragons" (Episode 35)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4f1o98Ld66I). Most of the party ran straight past the frozen lake's remorhazes while Thimble picked the island chest (a cloak of the manta ray and the baroness's family snorkel gear), used his free 20 to avoid being swallowed, and planted a magic bean that grew a nest of rainbow eggs. At the summit, Nerevath was fighting an adult silver dragon over her nest while the hunting groups closed in. Bog's natural 1 earned the party a breath of cold that dropped Aalyn; Wayne spoke to Nerevath through his link and learned he's the pear merchant; Alaric's free 20 turned the dragon to their side, and Bog's free 20 turned her on the Fortunate Five, who were wiped out.

## What Happened at the Table

**Recap**
- Bog gave the recap (last one in).

**The remorhazes** *(the DM called them "Remarz")*
- Wayne got a free head start before initiative and kept sprinting across the ice.
- The creatures: water-going centipedes with layered, metal-like armor plates venting steam, hot underneath. Standing next to one burns (fire each turn). **Immune to fire.** Their bite grapples and the big one can swallow.
- **Aalyn** cursed the small one (Hexblade's Curse) and hit it with two *Eldritch Blasts*. His pants split on the ice and never recovered until a heal. Later his *Orchestral Roar* shattered the ice under the big one and dropped it into the water.
- **Alaric** built an ice ramp and slide, propelled himself with *Shape Water*, and slid clean off the map. "Magic carpet guy." Whisper of the Fortunate Five watched him from a sled.
- **Wayne** cloud-jaunted past the big one, threw daggers mid-run and slid on his back like a turtle. Kept running.
- **Thimble** cast *Mage Armor* (the wild magic die is now rolled each time), misty-stepped to the island, and picked the triangular three-hole lock with thieves' tools after the +1 longsword failed as a crowbar.
  - **The chest:** a **cloak of the manta ray**, and plain **snorkeling gear bearing the baroness's family sigil** (the heirloom she asked for).
  - The mama remorhaz bit him and tried to swallow him (Strength save DC 19). **He used his free 20**, burst free in a blast of wild magic (d10 damage) and pushed it back 10 feet.
  - Drank a potion of greater healing (17). His *Fireball* did nothing (immune to fire) but melted the ice into steam. His *Lightning Bolt* (35) through the water seared the back half off one.
  - **Planted a magic bean on the island** (d100 roll).
- **Bog** breathed lightning into the water, which **stunned the remorhazes** in it, chain after chain. Punched one (12, 8) and finished the hexed young one by breathing lightning into its mouth until it burst. Aalyn healed 7 from the curse.
- Aalyn gave Thimble a Bardic Inspiration and left. Everyone ran the rest of the lake.

**The rainbow eggs**
- Looking back (Perception 15 with the inspiration), Thimble saw what the bean made: **a nest of six rainbow eggs**.
- Thimble (History) knows them: **eating one either permanently raises an ability score or blows up your insides with force damage**, depending on your Constitution.
- Thimble's bean count per the DM's notes: **three left**.

**To the summit**
- Alaric (natural 20 Perception) saw the path to a crown-like peak with two spires, half an hour to 45 minutes away, and heard **pain honks**: Nerevath was fighting.
- Pebbles rode on Wayne's head, wings out, listening to the honks she knows.

**The summit**
- A frozen lake at the peak, its ice solid. **Nerevath** came tumbling out of a snow cloud, locked in combat with **an adult silver dragon**, who slammed him into the ice. She landed before the party: **"Leave my nest."** Nerevath had been squatting in her nest.
- Also there: **the Fortunate Five** in the middle, **the hooded figures** circling in from another side, and **the adventuring party with the dog** coming in from the forest path. **The Silver Knights were nowhere.**
- She asked the party to rid her nest of the beast. **Bog told her all these people were here to take her eggs (natural 1).** She took it as a threat, and a stray arrow from the dog party grazed her eye. Initiative.
- Aalyn used Uncanny Metabolism and Magical Cunning.
- Thimble tried to persuade her to let them ride her (DC 27 after Bog's natural 1): "I will be a slave to humanity no longer."
- **Her breath of cold (60-foot cone, DC 20 CON):** Aalyn took 47 and **dropped**. Alaric halved it with *Absorb Elements*; Thimble, behind a tree on a natural 20, took a quarter.

**Nerevath**
- **Wayne** opened a psionic link to Nerevath and used *Speak with Animals* through it. The honks became words: **it's Salvatore Pearcetti**, the pear merchant of Oakhollow, and Wayne is "the guy that turned me into a goose from my pear stall." "I'm not even a goose, man." He'd been looking for a place to sleep when the dragon started hounding him. They agreed to work together.
- **The hooded figures threw chains over Nerevath** (four chains), and the dog party moved on him too.
- Wayne talked the Fortunate Five into freeing the goose (DC 5): "Fortunate Five, you heard the man. Free the goose."
- **Alaric used his free 20 on Persuasion** with the dragon: everyone here was sent for the goose, not for her, and they'll help rid her home of it. She agreed. Any touch of her eggs or entry to her home means death. He also gushed about her wings and skincare. He healed Aalyn with the 2nd-level *Healing Word* from his ring (21).
- **The Fortunate Five's team move:** Priscilla blessed Whisper, Father Celery's book lit two kunai with holy radiance, and Brimsley and Groank launched Whisper, who **broke two of the four chains**.
- **Bog used his free 20 on Persuasion:** that group was sent to help the goose and really they're after you. "Spoken like a true warrior. A master of the battlefield."
- **The dragon wiped out the Fortunate Five:** a tail sweep launched Groank, Brimsley and Lady Priscilla into the mountainside and a tree; she clawed the tree Whisper hung on and batted him over the mountain; and **she bit Father Celery**, who held out his celery to the last, into a mist of ice and blood. Bog yelled "Fuck you, Father Celery." Session ends.

**After the session**
- All three of Thimble's, Alaric's and Bog's free 20s are spent. Aalyn and Wayne still have theirs.
- Thimble's wild magic die is a d4.
- Alaric can't stop drooling over the dragon.
