# Session 10: The Orchid

**Party:** Level 5 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 9 - The Off-Branded Misfits]], [[The Thieves Guild]], [[Tiberius Blackfang]], [[The Silver Lyre]], [[Oakhollow]]

> [!important] Played: "The Orchid" (Episode 10)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=30hzWdrB-iM). One night at the Orchid. Wayne found the Thieves Guild under the inn and got a job from Silas Holloway. Aalyn met Heladur and her friends, sang, and humiliated his rival Ino Silvertone. Bog took lessons from the Fog Master and later killed a woman. In the morning the party went to the keep and found Rosalie floating in red magic. The tenth session, about five months after Session 0.

## What Happened at the Table

**Aalyn in the back room**
- He introduced himself to the slam poet **Heladur** and her friends **Talia** and **Kayla**, college students who grew up in Old Town. He sent them red wine with his Unseen Servant. A fourth friend came back from the bathroom and sat between him and Heladur. (She is unnamed at the table.)
- He sang a country song about turning to stone, using Disguise Self to spread stone up his skin as he went. Partway through (Intelligence save 14) he saw **a half-glowing, half-dark woman sitting between his friends at the back, watching him. She smiled, then faded.** Heladur cried. His jacket collected 27 sp.
- His rival **Ino Silvertone**, a silver-haired half-elf and the biggest nepo baby of [[The Silver Lyre]], cut him off with insults. Aalyn's comeback (Persuasion 29) put Ino off his game, but Ino's Constitution save beat Aalyn's Deafness, and he still performed well.
- Ino came to their table: "It must be so exhausting being second place your whole life."
  - Aalyn drove his dagger through Ino's hand into the table (6).
  - Wayne slammed a psionic dagger into Ino's other hand (16) and picked his pocket (Sleight of Hand 24, **69 gp**).
  - Aalyn's Unseen Servant pulled Ino's pants down.
- Ino fled, muttering that his mother would hear of this and he couldn't play at the Veil Hearts tomorrow. The waitress asked the party to leave the entertainment room.
- That night Talia, Kayla and Heladur came to Aalyn's room to talk songwriting. His Persuasion (13) didn't land, and they went back to their dorms. He gave Heladur his vial of mushroom water, claiming it summons a protective spirit if thrown. Then he **smashed his lute** in a rage.

**Thimble at the bar**
- Drinking set off his wild magic.
  - **d100: 74.** He teleported onto an old couple's table.
  - **Next roll.** His drink left the old woman poisoned.
  - **d100: 37.** He turned into a potted plant again, as in Session 1.
- A gentleman with a top hat, cane and monocle named **Mibo** had shared a beer with him and talked about weed. Mibo tried to make off with the plant, his clothes and the Bag of Beans. Alaric stopped him with a Minor Illusion decoy and Mage Hand, and Mibo fled out the door behind a smoke bomb. Thimble turned back, naked, and got everything back.

**Alaric in the steam room**
- In the steam he saw a vision of a hot spring oasis in his homeland, with cherry-blossom-like trees. He decided he had been in the steam too long.

**Bog and the Fog Master**
- Breathing lessons from the halfling. **Bog's breath weapon now deals +1 damage** (honed breath weapon).

**Wayne and the Thieves Guild**
- At the back of the Orchid, a carved mark (a dot inside circles) and a doorman. Wayne checked his short bow, shortsword and two daggers in a locker and went downstairs.
- A gear mechanism with a coin slot opened to a hidden underground bar. The bartender (slicked-back brown hair, goatee) brokers information and squeezed him some apple juice from a nearly rotten apple. Past it is a sand **fighting pit** with betting.
- Outside the owner's office, Wayne (Perception 19) overheard **[[Tiberius Blackfang|Tiberius]]**, Sir Romulus's tabaxi bodyguard: "They went in through the tunnels. That's your territory." Silas answered that he has no ill will against the Ferns, but they've had trouble in the tunnels.
- **Silas Holloway**, the owner: long black hair in a knot, sharp features, rings, a half mask over one side of his jaw.
  - He knows Jack only by legend: "one of those few that you hear but never see."
  - Tiberius has told him about the party.
  - One of Silas's runners did a job for an **Obsidian Enclave member: retrieve something from ruins in the forest.** The Enclave never paid, and never got the goods.
  - His price for the runner's name is **clearing the rats out of the sewer tunnels**, which have cut off the guild's runner routes. Tiberius blames the guild for the attack on the keep. The sewer entrance is past the fighting pit.
- Wayne teleported back upstairs to the psionic dagger he had left with Thimble.

**The rest of the night**
- Bog seduced the fourth friend (Persuasion 30) and rented a small room for 4 sp. After she passed out, **he strangled her with rope and tried to make it look self-inflicted, then locked the room and left.** None of the others know.
- Bog told Wayne she had called him "a bit of a drag" and he heard "dragon." Wayne told him it's fine to stay who he is. Bog: "You're not just a rock, you're also a gem."

**Morning**
- Bog woke in a steam room tub and told Aalyn he'd been working through some identity issues. Alaric partly mended Aalyn's lute; it will take a lot more Mending.
- Wayne bought 10 rations (5 sp each). Alaric covered breakfast and another night's rooms with 1 gp.
- The party chose the keep first and the tunnels after.
- At the keep, more Darkstone guards and fewer of the Ferns' green ones. **Orthus**, the elderly Enclave adviser, sent them up to Rosalie's room.
- **Rosalie floats above her bed, wrapped in spiraling dark-red magic.** Sir Romulus knelt beside her holding her hand. "We need to talk."
