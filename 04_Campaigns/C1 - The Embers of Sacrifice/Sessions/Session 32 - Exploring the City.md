# Session 32: Exploring the City

**Party:** Level 6 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 31 - Welcome to Hollowmere]], [[Hollowmere]], [[Talia|Talia Shay]], [[Luna au Vermillion]], [[Selara Du'Cant]], [[Corvin Hale]], [[The Holy Chain Knights]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "Exploring the City" (Episode 32)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Uogd_SJDNDM). The campaign's one-year session. Talia took the party into the Drunken Star for a free meal and slipped Aalyn a pamphlet for V's, a gentleman's lounge in the Fog Slab. Aalyn stayed behind; Alaric, Wayne and Bog talked their way into Cinderward, where Professor Bifflefapple coaxed Wayne's first telekinetic pull and showed them an airship found under the mountain ice. Thimble rode up with Luna, who told him how her brother vanished and admitted she led them through the fog on purpose. Selara Du'Cant of the Obsidian Enclave appeared out of invisibility asking about Malrik. Ends in the mess hall, where the officer across from Thimble lifts his head: Jud.

## What Happened at the Table

**One year**
- For the one-year mark the DM gave every player **a free automatic 20**, good for a year. Declared before rolling, on any d20 roll outside a boss-type combat.

**The Drunken Star**
- Talia ushered them into **the Drunken Star**, a hole-in-the-wall tavern on the street where Vargrax died. The meal is on the house for "the heroes of the street."
- The cook and owner is **old man Olaf**, a grumpy dwarf whose spiky gray hair barely clears the counter. Aalyn knows him from his last time here. Olaf says Aalyn brings in the arty crowd, not the rough-and-tumble workers he wants.
- Orders: lamb for Alaric, veggie soup for Wayne ("a seedless establishment"), and two whole watermelons for Bog.
- Aalyn *Message*d Talia not to mention Bog being a dragon. "She doesn't see race."

**Aalyn and Talia**
- In the back alley fetching the watermelons, Talia admitted she hoped he'd come back after months of waiting.
- The tips at the tavern are chump change. She wants to buy a house, so she picked up a second job at **V's** (the letter V), a refined **gentleman's lounge** in the Fog Slab right off the river: steam, refreshments, ladies who take care of their patrons. She's not associated with Oinkers.
- She gave him a pamphlet. **The lady who runs V's** heard about their night together, was very interested, and wants Talia to send him her way.
- He told her about Oakhollow and the Gordra Dominion attack. She'd heard rumblings and thought them rumor; he joked it off.

**Bog and Talia**
- Talia brought Bog two normal watermelons. "I didn't ask for your melons." Then he told her he hadn't realized she was disabled and she should tell customers about her shortcomings up front. She took it to heart and now greets tables with "I have a disability, but I will do my best to serve you."
- She brought two more watermelons, then a warm glass of milk ("warmed it up myself"). Bog bagged two watermelons in his bag of holding.
- Before he left she tucked **a V's voucher with her referral code** into his pocket and offered a "private lesson." **There was already a letter there.**
- Alaric read it: *Sorry I had to dupe you with that old dwarf in the time share back in Oakhollow. If you're still interested, I'm down to meet up again sometime. Signed, your secret admirer.*
- The party took a short rest over the meal.

**The candle and Tiffany**
- Alaric showed the group the **candle patch** from the guard-post bandit. He has now seen the candle twice (it is inside Vargrax's coat too). Aalyn and Wayne don't remember the patch.
- He showed Aalyn the postcard photo of **Tiffany**. Aalyn: "Is she going to try to kill me too?" The postcard is addressed to the orphanage in slang for the middle slab.
- Aalyn thinks something is nefarious at the orphanage. He'll ask Talia what she knows.

**Cinderward**
- Aalyn stayed to wait for Talia. Rec stayed with him as a place mat. Alaric, Wayne and Bog left for **Cinderward** (the table says "Cinderwarts"). Wayne's psionic link had about 45 minutes left.
- Three frat-bro students mocked them at the gate. Alaric assumed they meant someone else.
- The gate is a scan-badge door with **an old lady in a booth reading a magical tablet**. Alaric asked for guest passes and was told he needs a chaperone. **Wayne used his free 20 on Deception**, claimed to be a professor who left his badge in his office, and named **Professor Bifflefapple** (dropped earlier by Barnaby). She noted he'd grown and liked the new haircut, and gave him a **temporary faculty badge**.
- The real Bifflefapple, an elderly gnome with an armful of books, walked in right behind them. They followed him.

**Professor Bifflefapple**
- A tiny, non-tenured office; he sits on a booster seat. Inspired by Heimerdinger (DM).
- He gave Wayne ***Wizardry 101.1, the revised edition***, with exercises in the back.
- He knows Alaric's father: "What is a Fenwyn's son doing all the way here?"
- He looked at Wayne's sprout: part of his brain now, harmless, and someone at the institute could remove it. Not his field; he researches old magical archives, artifacts, enchantment and enhancement.
- **Wayne's telekinesis:**
  - Pull Alaric toward him: nothing, a nosebleed, and a toot.
  - Push Alaric: 1 psychic damage and a bulging vein.
  - Bog put half a watermelon on Wayne's head as a helmet. With it (advantage), Wayne **pulled a psionic dagger out of Alaric's hand**. It buzzed over the professor's head and Wayne caught it. Wayne named it **"Wayne's Pull."**
  - A gentle push: the dagger lifted slightly and dropped. His sprout spun like a propeller and blended the watermelon.
  - The professor: maybe 20 or 30 years of study and he'll be a novice.
- The DM offered Wayne a path to an extra feat by practicing in play: **Magic Initiate (wizard, with Magic Missile)** through the textbook, or **Telekinetic** through these exercises. Undecided.
- Alaric said they're headed to Glimmerstone, since a party member descends from a royal family there. The professor's advice: **stay away from the Danglesworths.** They didn't find his work worthy at Glimmerstone University, which is why he's on sabbatical here.

**The airship**
- He led them down a hatch and through a tunnel under the river to **a workshop carved into the mountain**, with rail tracks, chained loading doors, and a tunnel down to the waterfall runoff.
- Inside: **an airship**, found under the ice on the mountaintop and brought down recently. He's rebuilt most of the hull and sails over the last few months.
- What's missing is the magical flight mechanism: the arcane engines and the air compressor need raw **ether** energy strong enough to lift her.
- Alaric: "The Ether Stone?" Professor: "No, I'm talking about **the Heart of Never...**" Session ends mid-word.

**Thimble and Luna**
- Thimble rode the gondola up with Luna under a full moon.
- **Her story:**
  - The Vermillions are a family of renown from **Vermillion Bay on Vermore**. They keep **a chalet outside Hollowmere** for the summers.
  - As children on holiday here, local thugs cornered her and her older brother protected her. That caught the Silver Knights' eye. He joined, and became **Imperator**, the post she holds now.
  - He was stationed here a long time, fighting "vermin crawling in the city."
  - One moonlit night, walking with her, **a monster too fast to see** attacked. He traded his life for hers.
  - When she went back, **his body was gone**. No one, gods or knights, knew where. **Just a chain link on the ground.**
  - She joined the knights to be like him, and to find out what happened and get justice. Full moons remind her of him; she hates them.
- Thimble promised he and his friends would help.
- **The Silver Keep:** the moon-dial courtyard with trees; **the Hall of Links**, where silver chain links for every fallen knight hang from the ceiling; the mess hall; knights' quarters (where the party will sleep); the armory; a training yard; and the spire of **the Celestial Archives**, which Luna asked someone to get ready for visitors. The officers' wing is on the second floor.
- Thimble (Perception 19) glimpsed **an officer in the mess hall he recognized** but couldn't place.
- **Luna's confession:** they didn't need to go through the fog. She has read of the symbol on Aalyn's brand. It signifies destruction, and above all it **attracts monsters**. She needed to be sure, so she led them through the fog knowing what was in it, and it got knights killed. "They are worth my mission." Thimble: he has no issue with necessary sacrifices for what you want. She was relieved she hadn't scared him off.

**Selara Du'Cant**
- A young woman, mid-to-late 20s, white hair, black obsidian robes, a wand, came out of invisibility behind Thimble in Luna's office. **Selara Du'Cant** of the Obsidian Enclave (said at the table as "Solara"). She has heard of Thimblewick.
- She backed him into the desk, leg up on it: "Thimblewick, what happened to **Malrik**?" **Her scrying on him no longer works.** She knows he was in Malrik's company.
- Thimble (Deception 19): Malrik gave them some orders and left. She thinks she overestimated his abilities. He told her to check herself. She left, saying they'd be seeing more of each other.
- Luna: Selara is **the city's advisor and works with the baroness**. They're acquaintances; Luna keeps half her guard up around powerful mages. Selara has an ever-watchful eye in the city and may know all of Luna's history now. Luna joked she'll keep Thimble as her shield.

**The mess hall**
- The lunch lady gave Thimble two scoops. By the time he turned, **Corvin Hale** had taken the seat Luna saved for him, and flipped him off.
- Thimble sat across from the lone officer at the end of a long table. "I know you." He lifted his head: **Jud**.

**After the session**
- Aalyn's player was given a list of eight exes to draw on; Talia was the most open-ended.
- The party is low on gold. Aalyn has 6 gp. Alaric spent most of his on the ring of spell storing and on helping Aalyn buy his armor from Barnaby.
- Wayne can cast *Identify* and *Misty Step* from Fey Touched.
