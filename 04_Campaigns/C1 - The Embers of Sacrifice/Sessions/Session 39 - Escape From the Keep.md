# Session 39: Escape From the Keep

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 38 - Digging For Information]], [[Hollowmere]], [[Vashara]], [[Luna au Vermillion|Luna]], [[Corvin Hale]], [[Talia|Talia Shay]], [[The Holy Chain Knights]], [[The Pale Codex]], [[Papa Glabb]], [[Durnhollow]], [[The Demonomicon]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "Escape From the Keep" (Episode 39)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XeGD-L3wcAQ). Bog woke bound and blindfolded in V's, aware that he had told a voice everything about the party, that scales were plucked from his back, and that the words "the lounge misses you" will call him back. Devils turned out to be unknown in the world. Luna expects to lose her rank, and asked the party to take the mag lift down to Durnhollow with her in the morning. Thimble read his Demonomicon tome and gained a legendary boon. At breakfast Alaric and Aalyn watched Luna taken away in a carriage, and Corvin Hale sent the knights after the party. Three went out the windows invisible; Alaric and Aalyn, disguised as knights, walked out of the restricted section with the Pale Codex and Papa Glabb's harp and songbook. They regrouped at the Drunken Star, where Talia gave Aalyn the missing patrons' details, and chose the dwarven ruins.

## What Happened at the Table

**Bog in the dark**
- Bog woke (History, Perception with disadvantage 3) blindfolded and tied to a velvet chair, by candle heat, in a smell like perfume over dung. Fragments: a hallway, a door closing, a sweet voice: **"You're more interesting than you look."** He's sober, and upset about it.
- A woman walked circles around him chanting and waving incense: a ritual spell. **Natural 20 on the WIS save: he knows exactly what was done to him**, though it still took hold.
  - **He had told her who he is, who he travels with, their history, what they're doing in the city and whom they've met.** Bog doesn't know names, so she got "a little dude," "someone who stands next to me, usually behind me," and "someone who likes to sing and wears flowy clothing."
  - **His body was inspected and a few scales plucked from his back.** It was touched.
  - **A hidden compulsion: when he hears "the lounge misses you," he must make a WIS save or be compelled to return to V's Lounge.**
- Hours later he was untied in an empty round velvet room. A note: **"Thank you for your conversation. You're welcome back anytime. V."** He relieved himself on the stones in a crude smiley face and left by the only door. Behind him it was a brick wall, with no door: he was outside the back of V's, in the morning.

**What's a devil?**
- Luna, a holy knight, has never heard of a devil. Not in children's stories about selling your soul, not in "The Devil Went Down to Georgia" (Aalyn sang it). "Do you mean demons?"
- Alaric owns no book that mentions devils (natural roll). He has read every book in the Fenwyn library twice and recalls none. Thimble and Wayne don't know the word either. Luna has studied the lower planes in the archives: nothing on "devilish creatures."
- **Devils as the players know them aren't known in the world.** Alaric senses a gap, like a missing book on a shelf: he has cast *Moat of Hell*. Something like it exists, "just not in the way you might think."
- Luna knows the Abyss and demons, "and that's all I care about."

**Luna's plan**
- **She expects the archivist to report that she let people into the keep and the restricted section, that the Chain Lords will notice what's missing, and that she'll be stripped of her rank for a time, maybe permanently.** Their emissaries will come within a fortnight.
- That leaves just enough time. **Tomorrow morning: up to the upper plateau and "take the mag down to Durnhollow."** In her rituals and surveillance she has **tracked an arcane signature down there.**
- "If you guys are the answer that I think you are, I won't need the Silver Chain Knights anymore." Thimble will give her the day as an apology.

**Strong Legs and the sword**
- Thimble showed Luna **Mr. Strong Legs' signet ring**. "He was one of the best of us." **She suggests returning it to Daggerford; the Strong Legs family will pay well for answers.**
- Thimble gave Wayne **the +1 longsword** he'd hauled since then. Wayne nicked Thimble for 2 swinging it. **Alaric is proficient and took it.**

**Aalyn's hair and the contract**
- Aalyn told the room about V's: Talia, Bog's performance and private room, the naked man, Miss V (pale plum skin, glowing eyes). Thinking out loud he settled on tiefling, though she has no horns.
- He signed a contract to perform there. He didn't read it. Alaric and Wayne want him to go back and read it.
- **Alaric: "Your hair has gotten a lot darker since we met you."** Aalyn recast *Disguise Self* as himself to check, and Alaric drew a before-and-after stick figure. Aalyn thinks it started when he "went crazy." Alaric showed him *Illusory Script*: a contract doesn't have to look magical. Aalyn lay down facing the wall, scared.

**The night**
- **Aalyn put a 2nd-level *Cure Wounds* into Alaric's ring**, and Alaric stored a spell of his own in it.
- Wayne's Animal Handling: **Pebbles' rocky coat is matted. She needs more care.**
- Thimble read his tome for four hours. Luna's tailor (for Thimble) hasn't come.

**Morning, rain**
- **Bog returned soaked.** He was "put under some sort of spell, that was kind of cool," and they'll probably have to kill "that purple lady." She touched him. **"I also told her all about you guys."** Aalyn works for her now, so he'd rather she lived. Bog will kill her "at some point."
- **Alaric showed Aalyn the rod of resurrection** and a full page on it in his book. "Just be careful."
- **Thimble read the tome.** (The DM confirmed volume zero came from Malrik's vault.) The pages are blank until you pass an INT save (16 with advantage). Alaric felt the malice coming off it. Words began to flutter: **Thimble gained a new legendary boon** and sat studying, eyes rolled back.
- Aalyn gave heroic inspiration to Alaric, Wayne and Thimble.
- Wayne pulled at Pebbles across the room (she held firm), then **flicked Alaric's hat off with his mind** (Arcana 18 with advantage).
- **Alaric soothed the silver egg with *Shape Water*: cold, humid air, not ice. It likes that.** He'll keep doing it.

**Luna arrested**
- At breakfast the mess hall was nearly empty. Through Rec, Alaric saw **knights and priests in the courtyard, and Luna in handcuffs being put into a carriage.**
- **Corvin Hale** turned to the knights: **"Let's go grab the hooligans."**
- **Aalyn cast *Disguise Self* as one of the knights who died on the wagons; Alaric cast it from his ring as a living knight from the night shift.** Knights hailed Alaric as **"Sir James"** and pulled him along: "Things are changing around here. We've got to be on the right side of this."
- Alaric threw Thimble's voice down a cross hall with *Minor Illusion* to draw them off. Rec played a *Minor Illusion* of Luna's arrest for the room. Aalyn came in, pulled Thimble out of his reading; Wayne psionically woke him. Thimble only wanted 10 more minutes.
- Thimble had an idea from the tome: cast one of its spells at Corvin. He didn't.
- **Alaric cast *Invisibility* (upcast) on Thimble, Wayne and Bog**, who went out the room's windows past a lone guard kicking rocks in the back yard. Thimble left a greasy smudge; Bog slipped; "must have been the wind." Wayne linked minds to set the meeting point: **the Drunken Star.**
- Thimble ran up the walls to the top of the archives spire: **the carriage left with Luna, and Corvin's knights spread out to hunt them.** Through the dome he saw Alaric and Aalyn inside, and **drew a penis in his breath on the glass.** He floated down on his ring of feather falling onto Bog's head.

**The restricted section**
- Alaric and Aalyn, still disguised, joined the search and ended up in the opened restricted section, where **Willamir oversaw the scene** and cast analysis on the patched-up chest.
- **The books here are written in ink visible only by moonlight** (like Malrik's solar refractory, but moonlight). Items may sit on weighted pedestals.
- **Alaric (Investigation 22): *The Pale Codex***, an ebony-white book he'd sensed with *Detect Magic*. With Rec's hand and *Mage Hand* he swapped it for a book of his own he'd memorized.
- **Aalyn (natural 19): a small songbook and a harp of dark wood, like Papa Glabb's**, on its own pedestal. **He Indiana Jones'd it with a watermelon** (Sleight of Hand 21 with inspiration). No alarm.
- Aalyn asked about burning a few unimportant books for a distraction. Alaric: burning books is basically killing people.
- **A rookie knight noticed the watermelon.** Aalyn took his hand and sent him to tell Willamir. The pair walked out the front door (Deception 21 and 16).
- They found three bushes rustling below the keep. Alaric dropped the invisibility.

**The Drunken Star**
- Quiet morning. Talia served cereal and a pup cup for Pebbles. Dame is the only knight who drinks here.
- Talia: the city's in an uproar over the Imperator's arrest. **From her talks with Selara, "the powers that be wanted her out."** Luna was doing her own thing.
- **Talia brought the details on the four names** (sent on Discord). She doesn't know where Miss V lives, never sees her by day, and thinks maybe half-elf, maybe drow. "I'd like it if you didn't kill my boss."
- Options: hide with Professor Bifflefapple (Wayne would get him fired), the Tiberius sending stone (still in Wayne's inventory), walk to Glimmerstone, or kill the knights. **They chose the dwarven ruins**: the knights won't go there, the missing patrons might be there, and Bifflefapple's airship needs something from them.
- Thimble snapped out at the word "kill": "This thing's nuts." His wild magic crackles a little more malicious now.
- Short rest. Aalyn refilled heroic inspiration for himself, Alaric and Bog.

**After the session**
- The DM: the Demonomicon is an official item reworked for the campaign. He'll write up the Pale Codex and Papa Glabb's harp and songbook. Thimble's cloak may come by mail now that Luna is gone.
- Alaric's player blew his 4th-level slot on the upcast *Invisibility* and had planned *Arcane Eye* for the ruins.
- The Abyss is different from the void.
