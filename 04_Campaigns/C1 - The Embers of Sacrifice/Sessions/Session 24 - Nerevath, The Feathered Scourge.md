# Session 24: Nerevath, The Feathered Scourge

**Party:** Level 6 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 23 - Magic and Religion]], [[Session 7 - The Goose is Loose]], [[Oakhollow]], [[Hollowmere]], [[The Free Kingdoms]]

> [!important] Played: "Nerevath, The Feathered Scourge" (Episode 24)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GewqjskdXmQ). Commander Seth's magic bean became a beer geyser, and the party stole it. They resupplied, put a collar on Pebbles, and made the guard Bob soil himself on the way out of Oakhollow. On the road west to Hollowmere their brands burned in the night. Next day the giant goose from the lithoweave, Nerevath, swooped down and swallowed Pebbles. Wayne traded rations for her back with a natural 20, and Nerevath flew off toward Hollowmere.

## What Happened at the Table

**Opening**
- Wayne got heroic inspiration for his player's birthday.

**The beer geyser**
- Bog, wandering in search of a drink, found **Commander Seth** screaming "My bean!" at a planter in front of his house. The magic bean Thimble gave him had grown into a 30 ft geyser of beer. The rest of the party arrived the same way.
- Bog caught beer on his tongue (Acrobatics 18, 3 temporary HP).
- Seth had picked up Aalyn's forged note and wanted help. Bog spat a cheekful of beer in his face and asked his name again. "You will pay for this." Seth ran inside for a rag.
- **The party stole the planter** and ran through the city trailing beer like a jet ski. Alaric water-bent beer into Bog's mouth on the way, and a kid got sprayed.

**Leaving Oakhollow**
- Destination: **Hollowmere**, on the way to Glimmerstone, about eight days west on foot.
- Pebbles got hungry, and Wayne fed her rations.
- **The general store.**
  - Alaric bought a potion of healing for 50 gp (prices are inflated) and got 60 ft of rope thrown in, which he gave to Bog.
  - Wayne bought 20 rations for 20 gp. He tried to pay 22, and Alaric started teaching him to count.
  - Thimble bought a pack of **Oak Greens** (5 gp) with gold from Wayne. Bog tried to short the shopkeeper, and Thimble's Deception 28 scrambled the count.
  - Alaric sold two of his three bloodstones for 150 gp each and kept the third.
  - Aalyn bought a worn leather strap and a dented bell for 1 gp and fixed them into a collar (Sleight of Hand 16). Alaric wrote **"Pebbles"** on the bell in magic lettering that lights up when it rings. It's big enough for her to grow into.
- The magic shop near the cathedral is gone. The west and south stables (Rec scouted the south one) are empty, stripped for war prep.
- **Bob.** At the west gate, the guard Bob mocked them for running.
  - Aalyn warned him bigger problems are coming and to talk to Commander Seth.
  - Wayne set a ball bearing down and Pebbles spat it at Bob. The others ducked into alleys and left Thimble in the street.
  - Thimble grew a fireball in his hand from what's left of the ley line energy (natural 20 Intimidation with Tides of Chaos). **Bob wet and soiled himself.** Pebbles hit him again, and the townsfolk cheered.
  - Alaric: "The tale of Bob the pants-soiler." Aalyn strummed a happy song as they marched out.
- Alaric rode a ritual *Phantom Steed*. Wayne carried Thimble on his shoulder, with Pebbles on Thimble's.

**The road west**
- Open plains through the Free Kingdoms, with soot still in the air.
- Alaric led with Rec scouting (Survival 7) and took them off the road into woods by nightfall.
- Alaric's ramen, made with *Shape Water* and *Control Flames*, came out overcooked and full of dirt. He insisted it tastes normal. Aalyn's came out perfect. Wayne sprinkled ramen on Pebbles' rations, and she liked it.
- Alaric ritual cast *Leomund's Tiny Hut.*
- **Around midnight everyone's brand seared with pain** except Bog's. It faded by morning.
- **Morning.**
  - Aalyn gave a ramen cooking lesson while Alaric's *Unseen Servant* copied him.
  - **Pebbles pooped out a gold coin**, the copper one from before.
  - Aalyn checked for tracks (Perception 12) and found nothing.
- Alaric and Aalyn examined Wayne: crystalline stone eyes, stone teeth and tongue. Nobody could settle whether he needs to eat.

**Nerevath**
- Back on course (Survival 20 with Rec). Around noon in open fields a shadow passed overhead twice (everyone but Wayne noticed).
- Pebbles climbed out and ran ahead honking.
- **Nerevath, the Feathered Scourge**, crashed down behind her: the giant goose that burst out of Wayne's bag in [[Session 7 - The Goose is Loose]]. Initiative.
  - Nerevath's honk blast hit Alaric for 16 force and knocked Aalyn and Wayne back 10 ft (5 each).
  - **Nerevath scooped Pebbles into his mouth.**
  - Aalyn put **Hexblade's Curse** on him (a black-and-orange half note appeared on his neck, and his honk dropped an octave) and missed with two *Eldritch Blasts*.
  - Wayne cloud jaunted up, held out rations and said, "You came back to me, my goose." **Natural 20 Animal Handling.**
  - Nerevath coiled his neck around Wayne, opened his mouth, and **traded Pebbles for five rations.** The rations held pear slices, which resonate with his origin.
- **Nerevath flew off in the direction of Hollowmere.** The combat ended.

**After the session**
- The DM had planned a Pebbles kidnapping arc, which the natural 20 skipped. The party hasn't seen the last of Nerevath.
- Nerevath has a full stat block.
