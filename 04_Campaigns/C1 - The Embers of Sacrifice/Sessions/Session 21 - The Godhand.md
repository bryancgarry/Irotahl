# Session 21: The Godhand

**Party:** Level 6 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 20 - The Ascension]], [[Graz'zt]], [[The Godhand]], [[Malcanthet]], [[Baphomet]], [[Orcus]], [[Malrik Greene]], [[Tiberius Blackfang]], [[Bragthar]], [[Oakhollow]]

> [!important] Played: "The Godhand" (Episode 21)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Hmtz-W_7RME). The Bridge King finished his ascension, turned the party's rat cannon back on them, and pulled everyone into an abyssal domain. He destroyed Sheila, killed Bragthar, and joined four more demon lords: the Godhand. He named himself Graz'zt and made Malrik his apostle. Tiberius cut his way in and held all five off while the party fled. Thimble shattered the domain with the ley lines' power, which tore a chasm through the center of Oakhollow. Ends in Rosalie's room, with the wall blown out and Prince Darken gravely wounded.

## What Happened at the Table

**Opening**
- The party is now **level 6.**
- **DM reframing of Session 20.** Same events, softer intent. Talula broke free and hurt Alaric. Aalyn saw that giving up the relic she was after might soothe her and save his friend. Losing the gemstone was an honest attempt to fix a situation going sour, not a betrayal.
- Alaric (Investigation 27) and Aalyn (19) grabbed things off Malrik before following. The DM held back what they were. Nothing in it could stop what was happening.

**The ascension**
- Alaric ran in first while Aalyn tied up Malrik. Talula sat idle beside the flayed Bridge King. The gemstone "was delivered to who it was meant to always find its way to."
- Bog (Perception 18) spotted a husk at the back of the chamber: skeletal, with papery skin and its chest torn open. Dark energy like Rosalie's poured out of it into the Bridge King's back, where the gemstone was.
- **Wayne used the sending stone.** "Things are getting bad, man." Tiberius understood the urgency.
- Talula sat in awe. Thimble saw the woman inside her fading, not from wounds but in spirit, as if she was settling into the demon form for good.
- Alaric, terrified, told the others they had no chance and should run. Aalyn arrived and grabbed him by the shoulders to pull him out.
- Aalyn recognized the flowers in the room but can't place where he saw them (History 10).
- Wayne felt waves of fear and terror in his head, from a source he couldn't place.

**The rat cannon**
- Wayne swapped to the Rat Lord's Hat, and Bog launched a **cannonball rat** like a shot put at the husk: 10 fire and 18 force.
- The flames pulled back. The Bridge King held up a hand and condensed the heat into a ball, in a familiar but changing voice.
- **Time stop.** The party froze, aware but unable to act. The ball grew with each round of stopped time, then burst on Aalyn and the group for 28. **Aalyn, Alaric and Thimble dropped.** Alaric was one point from dying outright.

**The domain**
- The flames shifted them, like the Baron's domain at the start of the campaign: an endless hell of rock, soot and monochrome light.
- The Bridge King floated 30 ft up in a ball of black light ringed in white and finished becoming a demon lord.
- "So weak. How am I supposed to have fun with this?" He healed the fallen for 10.
- He pulled a jagged obsidian blade from the ground, purple flecks running along it. "I'm going to have fun with this." A gravity slash dropped the same three again. Wayne halved it. Bog deflected it entirely. He healed them again.
- Sheila, Bragthar, the chained Malrik and Talula had all been pulled in too (Bog and Wayne, Perception 10 and 12). Wayne felt a familiar presence.
- **Bog asked for a minute.** He pointed out the unconscious wizard he'd been planning to deal with. The Bridge King looked at Malrik with disgust and vengeance, then saw his own scepter in Bog's hand. "You dare talk to me wielding my belongings?" He flung Bog 60 ft (21 force). Bog got back up, which earned a sliver of respect, and was pulled back in.
- "You're annoying me." He **disintegrated Sheila**, countering Alaric's counterspell.
- To Bragthar: "You abandoned me for them." A whispered **power word kill. Bragthar is dead.**

**The Godhand**
- Aalyn also felt a familiar presence. Thimble, still charged from the ley lines, saw four figures floating 100 ft back. They revealed themselves, and the Bridge King rose to join them, "his rightful place among the Godhand."
  - Aalyn recognized one: **his patron.**
  - Wayne recognized one: **the woman who turned him to stone.**
  - Behind them, two larger and more menacing.
- **Malrik.** Aalyn healed him (11 HP) and demanded, "What have we done?" Malrik saw the figures: "No. Oh no." He searched his body for something that wasn't there.
  - The Bridge King lifted him up. "For all that pain, for all my suffering, you didn't do a bad job. And for that, I'll show mercy."
  - Malrik: "It wasn't supposed to be like this. This was not my intention." His neck snapped and healed.
  - "My mercy: you will serve me as an apostle." Malrik was remade with blue skin, red eyes, a tusked boar-like head and blue flame in his hands.
  - **"You never asked my name, but you will know it well as you will forever serve your demonic overlord, Graz'zt."** Malrik was slammed into the ground and sent into the Abyss as a puddle.
- **Aalyn's patron:** "You did so well. It's crazy how effective compassion can be as a tool."
  - Bog (natural 20) and Thimble saw her true form: a woman from the waist up on an eight-legged spider body. A spectral black chain runs up forever and wraps around Aalyn's neck.
- **The woman who turned Wayne to stone**, with antennae and sinewy wings, floated into his face.
  - "Wow, you turned out to be so beautiful. My hollow vessel. You were growing so strong."
  - "And your friends. I did not realize you brought me a chaos born." To the group: "I see you all are branded except for one. I'm curious."
- **The beast lord.** A huge minotaur-like demon with a mass of jagged horns and antlers, a massive upper body and hooves landed in front of Bog.
  - Bog is dragonborn. The demon has tried and failed to make one. "Who is your creator?" Bog: "Do you not know how sex works?"
  - Obsidian statues of beast-folk hang from his belt: tabaxi, lizardfolk, tortles, aarakocra. He reached for Bog.

**Tiberius**
- A golden glaive slashed through the domain. **Tiberius** leapt in screaming "Baphomet!" and clashed with him. The shock wave threw the party back 15 ft.
- Light grew from his eye patch and folded out into **ancient runic armor**, the same make and glyphs as the lithoweave machine in the forest (Arcana 25).
- He flickered between all four, then caught Graz'zt's sword. The demons laughed: "Oh Tiberius, how long are we going to play this game?" Baphomet's club slammed him into a corner. He got up and charged again.
- His cut left a rift behind the party. He nodded to them.

**The escape**
- Alaric ran. Thimble shouted, "What do you want from us?" The demons only laughed.
- Bog asked Aalyn if he was going to help the monster lady. Aalyn told him to run, and was calling to Wayne when **a blood blast from Orcus**, the huge amalgam at the back, slashed his face and knocked him out.
- Wayne's link had dropped. He reached Tiberius instead ("Don't stay too long. Get out.") and heard only screams of agony. He threw Aalyn through the rift like a paper airplane.
- Everyone dodged Orcus's blood bursts. Tiberius fought five at once.
- Thimble went through last. Graz'zt boomed after him: **"I told you I would remember that."**

**Breaking out**
- The party came out in the caved-in chamber, sealed inside a black sphere like the Baron's domain, with Tiberius's slit in it. Wayne started digging.
- Thimble felt the ley lines and remembered the Baron's domain shattered (History 13). He cast a 3rd-level *Shatter* at the barrier, doubled by the energy, for 40 thunder. The recoil pushed him back.
  - Alaric guided the energy with illusion channels along his arms (Arcana 15).
  - Aalyn put his hands on Thimble's shoulders: "You are chaos, but you're the strength we need right now, buddy." He gave him Bardic Inspiration.
  - Wayne and Bog braced him. Thimble bent luck for Alaric and used Tides of Chaos on his own roll.
- The barrier fractured like glass. From inside, Tiberius, who had heard everything through Wayne's link, struck the final blow. The explosion threw them down.
- They lay at the bottom of a chasm 100 to 200 ft deep, with moonlight above. Tiberius knelt on his glaive as the armor folded back into his eye.

**The city**
- Aalyn flew up. **The Grand Cathedral is gone.** A chasm sits in the center of Oakhollow, and ashen streaks burned into the city form **the same sigil the party is branded with.** Fires spread to the forest edge, with screams, choking and burning bodies.
- Alone above the city, Aalyn broke down and cried. He let himself fall back in, then thought of a promise and his friends and slowed. "Just got a little bit harder. I hope you're still watching over me."
- The DM: the disaster wasn't the party's doing and wouldn't have happened if Malrik hadn't meddled, but they played a part in it.
- Thimble (Perception 10) felt **the ley line is sundered, burnt to ash.** He enjoyed the power he channeled. The DM said he may be tied to the world's magic more than he thought, and all of them have grown stronger.
- Aalyn healed Alaric, Thimble ("Thank you for everything you just did"), Tiberius and Wayne. Alaric to Tiberius: "I owe you my life."
- **Tiberius.** "Why didn't you call me sooner?" They didn't know what was happening. "Did you know?" He didn't know Malrik's intentions. "But you fought them before." "I have." "This is no place for that. The city needs us now more than ever."
- They climbed out with ropes, and Aalyn flew around as a handhold.
- **Helping.**
  - Alaric, who has seen this before, fought the fires with *Shape Water*. Aalyn pulled trapped people out.
  - Bog freed a man pinned under a beam while his wife and their daughter, whose arm was broken, looked on (Athletics 9 with the wife's help). Then he ate the meat that rolled out of the burning shop.
  - Tiberius was pulling guards out of the rubble around the cathedral. He said the prince had been with the princess at the keep.
  - Wayne watched Thimble enjoying it (Insight). Thimble: "We're going to need a lot more power than this... I like that power. We should look for some more." Wayne sensed something new in him, not necessarily bad.
- Guards in black and green worked side by side. Thimble overheard a passerby: "That eclipse came out of nowhere."
- Aalyn told Tiberius they were going to the keep. He'll come once things settle.

**The keep**
- The gate was unguarded, with smoke rising from the back of the keep.
- Wayne's egg began shaking and cracking.
- In Rosalie's room the back wall had been blown out into the lake. **Rosalie lay on the bed**, no longer floating. **Prince Darken** was slumped in the corner with a huge hole in his side. Session ends.

**After the session**
- Players' motto: "We weren't the terrorists, but we were the plane."
- Disintegrated creatures' magic items drop. Aalyn's aux machine was on Sheila.
- Thimble rolled a wild magic surge (d100 24) that the DM is holding until it happens.
