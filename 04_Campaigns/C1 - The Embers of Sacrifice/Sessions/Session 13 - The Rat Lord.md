# Session 13: The Rat Lord

**Party:** Level 5 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 12 - The Establishment is Corrupt]], [[The Thieves Guild]]

> [!important] Played: "The Rat Lord" (Episode 13)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lK0SPvo-uaY). *(Aalyn's player lost power and played from his phone.)* The party rescued Aalyn and wiped out the wererats' hideout. The Rat Lord escaped down a drain. Ends with him blowing up the maintenance room he led them into.

## What Happened at the Table

**The rescue**
- The bandits tied Aalyn and hoisted him on a rope over the central pool, then lowered him so rat swarms could leap at his feet (1 piercing). He screamed "Lazy Susan."
- From the pipe, Alaric cast *Haste* on Bog. Wayne threw a real dagger and cut the rope (23). **Aalyn Misty Stepped onto a table** as he fell. "Intruders! My rats, go!"

**The fight**
- Bog (natural 20 initiative): a line of lightning breath through a bandit and two wererats, then punched the bandit dead.
- **Alaric's Fireball.** The Rat Lord used a swarm of rats as a living shield and took nothing. The wererats were cooked and a bandit turned to ash.
- **Thimble's Fireball** with Innate Sorcery (DC 17): 19 damage, and the Rat Lord took it.
- Wayne teleported behind the Rat Lord and hit him for 20 with his psionic blade, with Thimble Jr. II's help.
- **The Rat Lord pulled a BoomRat out of his hat**, a swollen rat marked with glowing red sigils, and threw it at the pipe. Thimble and Aalyn saved for 14 fire (Aalyn used his heroic inspiration). Alaric failed and was knocked back into the tunnel.
- The Rat Lord bit Wayne on a natural 1 and broke a tooth on his stone. Two wererats bit Wayne for 7 and 10; Wayne passed both Constitution saves.
- A wererat with a hand crossbow shot Thimble for 8.
- Aalyn cast *Bane* (one wererat failed) and Vexing Verse on the Rat Lord (disadvantage on Intelligence).
- **Round two.**
  - Bog killed two wererats, then **ate one of the fried rats in front of the Rat Lord** ("That's Jeffrey!"). The Rat Lord had disadvantage on his next attack.
  - Alaric's *Scorching Ray* killed a wererat and the last bandit. His *Minor Image* of a dying rat cried "Run, my child, run" in the Rat Lord's voice.
  - Thimble drank a potion and crit with *Chaos Bolt* for 32 thunder.
  - Wayne hit the Rat Lord for 25 more.
- **The Rat Lord disengaged and dove down the drain at the bottom of the pool.**
- Aalyn swan-dived after him (Acrobatics 21) and found three rat swarms packed over the drain.
- Thimble finished the last wererat by **ramming his staff into its groin**, launching it into the pool on top of Aalyn. Aalyn hid under the sinking body to look dead.
- Bog felt *Haste* wear off.

**Thimble's chaos die** stepped d10, d8, d6, then d4 over the two sessions without a surge.

**Loot**
- A natural 20 search of the top platform: **113 gp, 420 sp, three bloodstones, and a potion of healing**, plus food and ale.
- Aalyn, underwater, on the crossbow wererat: 3 cp and a malachite.
- From the bedrolls (Investigation 18): a disguise kit, thieves' tools, 7 sp and 13 cp.
- On the bandit: 3 gp and 2 sp.
- Wererats are known in the world, though uncommon.

**The valves**
- Wayne turned one wheel: the water dropped 5 ft, but fans in the north and south walls started pouring out poison clouds.
- He turned another: the light vents slammed shut and water flooded in. He reversed it, and the water drained.
- Aalyn's Melodic Surge and Alaric's Fire Bolt cleared the swarms. **Under them is a hinged grate.** Bog opened it.

**The maintenance room**
- A drainage pipe led down to a closed valve and a maintenance door. Bog went first with darkvision, and Alaric lit the room with *Control Flames*.
- Aalyn's *Healing Word* healed Thimble for 10 and Alaric for 11.
- **The Rat Lord** sat with his back to them. "You killed my children. You will pay." He threw a FlashRat, which lit the room. **Rats were taped to every wall and the ceiling like explosives**, and one was strapped to his chest.
- "The only one who can take my rats to the afterlife is me." The room exploded, and water began pouring in.
- No level up.
