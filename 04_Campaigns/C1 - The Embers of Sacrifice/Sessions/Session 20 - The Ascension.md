# Session 20: The Ascension

**Party:** Level 5 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 19 - The Fight Begins]], [[Session 3 - The Bridge King]], [[Malrik Greene]], [[Bragthar]], [[Oakhollow]]

> [!important] Played: "The Ascension" (Episode 20)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=X4IWM_p0_uU). The party finished off Malrik's guardians, with Bog's Bridge King's Scepter doing the heavy lifting. Talula broke free. Aalyn begged the others to give her the gemstone, and Alaric tossed it to her. She fled deeper, and Bog knocked Malrik out. At the bottom of a blood trail the party found the former Bridge King flayed and bound. Talula had placed the gemstone in his chest, and he rose as a demon lord.

## What Happened at the Table

**Opening**
- **DM retcon:** Aalyn's boots don't switch off when he's knocked out. He drifts down at their speed, so falling no longer costs him an automatic failed death save.
- The DM is being generous with action economy here because of the wild magic in the ley line chamber.
- Aalyn is up and prone. Of the heroic inspirations, only Thimble still has his.

**Round four**
- Aalyn let Thimble keep the mask, smirked at him, and cast *Cure Wounds* on himself (16, nearly full). He gave Thimble Bardic Inspiration.
- Malrik's lair action drew the ley line energy toward himself.
- The last stone golem squared up with Large Wayne and hit him for 15 (halved with Uncanny Dodge). Wayne hit back with the jade daggers. The psychic damage doesn't bite the golem much.
- Malrik cast *Fly* on the bone naga. It flew forward and hit Wayne and Bragthar with *Lightning Bolt* (17 each on saves).
- **The Bridge King's Scepter.** Bog pulled it out for the first time since he got it. Its three rubbery tentacles all hit the naga and restrained it. He found where its weak spot would be (Perception 16) and laid into it.
- Alaric hit the naga with a water bullet.
- **Malrik to Alaric:** "Son, give me the artifact. It doesn't have to come to blows." Alaric: "You're not my dad." Malrik: "Well, don't say I didn't try." He hit Alaric with frost and misty stepped into his face.
- Bragthar shredded more of the naga. Aalyn, holding Sheila's remote, ordered her to kill it, and she combined her arms into a flamethrower.

**Round five**
- Thimble saw Talula is about to break free (Perception 14 with Bardic Inspiration). The woman inside is trying to speak, but the binds on her neck stop her.
- Thimble's *Ray of Frost* missed. His wild magic animated everyone's rope (all saved). He turned his sorcery points into a 3rd-level slot.
- Aalyn misty stepped where he could see Malrik and hit him with *Eldritch Blast*, cracking the ward further. "Don't forget about me, buddy." Malrik: "You need to be alive if you want to be remembered."
- Malrik's chains caught Aalyn and Thimble.
- Wayne drank a greater healing potion and destroyed the last golem.
- The naga took the scepter's psychic damage, then cast *Command*. Bog and Bragthar fell prone.
- **Bog destroyed the bone naga** with a punch and drank the ancient fluid that burst out of it (Con save). The DM noted a mystery affliction to be revealed later.
- Bog found Alaric and Malrik in a side corridor. Alaric and his double both pointed at Malrik. Bog: "Seriously, dude. Can't you see we're doing something here?"
- Malrik's arcane pulse hit Bog and Alaric (both saved).
- **Talula slipped her chains.** Wayne noticed she and an armor were gone (Perception 9).
- Alaric disengaged, cast *Haste* on Bog, and left a *Minor Illusion* middle finger behind.
- **Talula.** Thimble, with truesight, saw her tear an armor in half, leap off a pillar so hard it collapsed, and land on Wayne (knocked prone). Saying Rosalie's name has stirred up the woman inside and unlocked strength the monster didn't have before.
- Malrik misty stepped clear and cast *Magic Missile* at Wayne, Bragthar, Alaric and Thimble. Alaric used *Shield*; Thimble is immune.
- Bragthar hit Talula with a natural 20. Thimble saw each wound also appear on the woman inside.
- **Sheila went down.** An armor stabbed her arm off, and she reverted to her wagon form with a wheel missing. She's inert, not dying.

**Round six**
- Thimble, still chained, shouted to Wayne asking whether they were going to kill her. Wayne: she's kicking his ass.
- Thimble pleaded with Talula again (Persuasion 13 with heroic inspiration). Her words came out muffled, but she mentioned **the artifact.**
- Thimble fed the chamber's energy into a *Fireball* at Malrik (extra die). It destroyed an armor and **shattered Malrik's ward** for 14.
- Wild magic again: Thimble's magic items started talking to him, then the rocks and chains did too (4 psychic, an hour-long effect). His Ring of Mind Shielding shut it all out.
- Aalyn broke the chains in a rage (Strength 19). Through the link he begged Alaric: **give Talula the gemstone.** "She's not working for him. Something will happen if we give it to her. I just can't prove it to you." He also asked them not to kill her, used his orchestral roar to blast himself back into view, and gave Alaric Bardic Inspiration.
- Wayne cloud jaunted to Malrik and tried to grapple him. Malrik used *Shield*.
- **Hasted Bog** caught Malrik with all three tentacles (restrained) and punched him repeatedly.
- **Alaric took the gemstone out of his bag of holding.** Talula's focus snapped from Wayne and Thimble straight to it (Perception). Not wanting to get any closer, Alaric tossed it to her from 5 ft away, drank a healing potion, and hit Malrik with *Fire Bolt*. He didn't want to kill him.

**Talula takes the gemstone**
- Talula caught it and looked at Alaric, then at Thimble. The woman inside couldn't speak, but her face said no.
- She leapt away toward the passage Malrik had come from.
- Malrik: "No, no, no. You don't know what you've done." He misty stepped out of the tentacles (14 psychic) and ran after her.
- Bragthar threw Alaric 20 ft up onto the platform and charged. When Malrik left, his last armor fell lifeless.
- Thimble tried to stretch *Suggestion* to 120 ft with all his sorcery points (Arcana DC 25) and failed. He stayed chained.
- Aalyn hit the running Malrik with *Eldritch Blast* from 111 ft. Malrik, bleeding: "You guys need to help me here. This is not good." Aalyn: "You have a lot of answers to give."
- Aalyn smirked. Then, privately to his patron: "I hope it was worth it." He dropped his rapier.
- Wayne cloud jaunted after Talula and tried to snatch the gemstone back (Sleight of Hand 17 at disadvantage, +2 from a psionic die). His fingertips touched it, but it slipped away, as if the laws of causality wanted it that way.
- **Bog knocked Malrik unconscious** without killing him, and told the others to make sure he doesn't go anywhere. He said hello to Talula. She ignored him.
- Alaric caught Talula in *Web* and left an illusory copy of the gemstone beside her. She failed the save, tore free and leapt out of sight.

**The lower chamber**
- Out of initiative. Aalyn stayed behind and tied Malrik up with the chains. A rushed search of Malrik turned up nothing tied to Talula.
- The others followed Talula's blood trail down a passage where the rock gives way to plant life, into a final chamber.
- Talula sat to one side, no longer holding the gemstone and no longer hostile.
- On a circular pedestal, bound like a crucifixion, was a tortured figure: tendons in the wrists and ankles cut, skin flayed from arms, chest and cheeks, prepared for something but still alive.
- Thimble, the last of them to deal with him, recognized him: **the former Bridge King,** without his crown.
- A glow grew in his chest. **Talula had placed the gemstone inside him,** the same stone the party once clawed out of the Baron's monstrous form.
- Wayne tried to cut it out and was thrown back by a wall of force.
- Black ink spread over the goblin's body and he grew from 3 or 4 ft to about 12 ft tall: **a demon lord reborn.** He looked at his hands, taking in his new strength. Session ends.

**After the session**
- The DM said he accomplished his goal for Aalyn's player. The pact was only to let the stone be lost, not to help Talula.
- **The party levels up to 6.** It takes effect after a short intro scene next session. Alaric gains illusionary beasts.
- Bog's player plans a rat cannon. The DM asked whether they'll survive the ascension.
