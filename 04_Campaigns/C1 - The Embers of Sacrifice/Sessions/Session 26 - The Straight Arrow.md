# Session 26: The Straight Arrow

**Party:** Level 6 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 25 - Boobs and Bodies]], [[Lolth]], [[Hollowmere]]

> [!important] Played: "The Straight Arrow" (Episode 26)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w4jDSKxJyG0). The party finished off the bandits at the guard post and looted it: gloves of thievery, a Pass Without Trace scroll, and a patch with a candle on it. In the border town of Westbury they stayed at the Broken Arrow, talked the young steward Kalisar ("Elmo") into robbing his master during a gong-and-bagpipes show, and watched it go wrong. In the morning Aalyn couldn't heal Kalisar's sick mother, called on Lolth by name, and was told the sickness is the one that took his love. Ends with Thimble planting another bean on the road out of town.

## What Happened at the Table

**Recap**
- Session 25 recapped from the players' notes. The recap mentioned that **Alaric has a brother** (see [[Fenril Fenwyn]]).

**The guard post fight**
- **Wayne** killed two bandits with his jade daggers.
- **Aalyn** landed three critical hits and killed a bandit captain.
- The Deceiver's **wolves** arrived. **Alaric**, still upside down in the snare, fireballed them.
- **Thimble**'s *Lightning Bolt* killed two more bandits.
- **Bog**, blinded, felt for the dead captain's face. He punched the captain's chest open and **took his eyeballs** (Sleight of Hand). He now has three eyeballs.
- **Aalyn** intimidated the fleeing **Deceiver** into stripping, then killed him with *Eldritch Blast* as he ran. Before dying he gave up where the bandits kept their loot.

**Looting the post**
- One bandit (natural 20 Investigation, after a heroic inspiration) carried a **spell scroll of *Pass Without Trace***, 10 gp Bog missed, and **a patch with a candle on it**, sewn inside his armor. It marks some organization or allegiance. Nobody recognized it.
- Thimble, downstairs: three rations and about 25 sp. Thimble's bird picked up another 10 sp.
- Aalyn, in the hidden third-floor keep: a chest with **150 gp** from the bandits' old scams.
- **Wayne** (natural 20) found the bandit leader's sandwich kitchen. The gloves he wore to cut roast beef were **gloves of thievery** (+5 to Sleight of Hand checks to pick locks).
- Bog ate the roast beef sandwich and gained **poison resistance**.
- Wayne scratched a triangle with an X in the dirt: his master's sign for "already looted." Thimble wants to leave marks like it.
- Thimble said he's low on gold because his savings are locked up at home. Wayne gave him 43 gp.
- **Bean count.** The DM's record: nine beans, two planted in the forest, one given to Commander Seth, six left.
- **The bean.** Thimble planted one in a nook of shrubbery by the post and watered it. As the party walked away, **three shrieking mushrooms** came up, screaming. Bog, still grabbing eyeballs, turned around to find them.
- Bog threaded his eyeballs on twine as they walked: **an eyeball necklace.**
- Alaric looked at the *Pass Without Trace* scroll. Aalyn could only use it if he could put it into a song.

**Westbury**
- **Westbury** is a small farming, shepherd and craft town at the far edge of Oakhollow's lands, a valley town of one- and two-story buildings with a three-story town hall and bell. Nobody agreed whether it is actually west.
- Pebbles loved her treats.
- They took rooms at **the Broken Arrow**, run by **Thorgo** and worked by his young steward **Kalisar**. Rooms cost six silver.
- Kalisar does nearly all the work. He needs money for **his mother's medical bills**. He said Thorgo keeps the cash in his office behind the front desk.
- Wayne taught Kalisar something and gave him three gold by accident. "You're a good teacher, Mr. Wayne."
- **The heist.** Wayne (bagpipes) and Thimble (war gong) played a show in the common room (18 and 25). Thorgo leaned on a table, entranced. Aalyn went for a walk; he could tell something was going on. Alaric was upstairs in the bath.
- Kalisar came out from behind the desk carrying **a whole locked desk drawer**, Wayne's lockpicks still stuck in it. Bog called him out in front of the room. The music stopped. Thorgo turned around: "Kalisar. My office." Kalisar was punished.
- **Thimble's alley.** With Bog as wingman and Wayne slipping exactly **10 gold** to a woman, Thimble got lucky in the back alley. Wayne counted it out correctly.
- Long rest at the inn.

**Thimble's dream**
- On a 13, a good dream: going back to **Glimmerstone** and how **his two brothers** will treat him. He got on much better with one of them and doesn't like the older one. His trust fund. Reveling in his new power. The return weighs on him more than on the others.

**Kalisar's mother**
- At breakfast, Aalyn healed Kalisar's injuries from the night before. Kalisar took him home to see if he could heal his mother.
- She has a **fever** (Medicine 8). An herbalist down the road gave her a concoction, but she's gotten worse. She has been sick **two weeks**.
- Aalyn renamed Kalisar **"Elmo."**
- Aalyn started to play the song he used to hum with his mother, broke off, and played something new. He cast *Cure Wounds* through Papa Glabb's lute (heroic inspiration used; a 2 on the reroll). She got 15 hit points back and her color returned, but the magic met resistance, as if **something has already taken hold of her.** It may not hold. Aalyn has seen something like this before.
- **Lolth.** Aalyn closed his eyes, went to the snowy clearing, sat at the tree, and called out: "I know your name now. Lolth, are you here?" She came, in the form he knows.
  - She would not say what's wrong with the woman. "Surely you don't intend to go against my plans."
  - Aalyn asked why she needs him, and what happens if he dies.
  - Lolth: he is important to her **as a needle is important for sewing.** A needle doesn't need to know the goal of whoever uses it. It drags the thread.
  - Aalyn: **his cooperation can be bought.** He will stop resisting, but he needs a breadcrumb trail. She grinned.
  - Her hint: "Were you able to save your love?" Look for **similar symptoms.** This is a sickness he has met before.
- Aalyn told Kalisar he has bought her more time, has seen this before, and isn't sure he remembers how to fix it. Kalisar knows of no one else who is sick.
- From memory: his love was dead within days, and others lasted no more than a week. That she has lasted two weeks gives him some hope and a lot of dread.
- He gave Kalisar **20 gold**. "Feel your emotions, kid. Don't hide them."
- **The note**, left in his room for Kalisar to find while cleaning: *"Elmo, I'm sorry I couldn't help. What is inflicting your mother isn't something that medicine can heal. It's barely something that magic can heal. Don't let the old man beat you too hard. But adventurers always have pretty tragic beginnings. If you're ever on the road looking for a friend, look for the bard with the bright orange hair."* Signed Aalyn Ravaceran.
- Meanwhile Bog ate, Wayne and Thimble took long showers, and Wayne washed Pebbles.

**The road out**
- About an hour out of Westbury, in open grassy plains, Thimble got bored and planted another bean. He's down to **four beans.**
- The DM will now remove bean results once rolled.
- The ground cracked and **a sandstone pyramid** rose out of the plain. Session ends.

**After the session**
- Wayne's gloves give +15 to Sleight of Hand.
- Alaric's player still has to send the DM his brother's info. His name is **Fenril**.
- Thimble has **two brothers.** His player puts him at about 270 years old (425 came up as a joke).
