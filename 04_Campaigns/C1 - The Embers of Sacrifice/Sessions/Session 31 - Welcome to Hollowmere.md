# Session 31: Welcome to Hollowmere

**Party:** Level 6 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 30 - Journey Through the Fog]], [[Hollowmere]], [[Durnhollow]], [[Luna au Vermillion]], [[The Holy Chain Knights]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "Welcome to Hollowmere" (Episode 31)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_9Ke3CXAF_0). *(Played after the holiday one-shot.)* The caravan reached Hollowmere. Luna offered the party rooms at the Silver Keep and Alaric a pass to the Celestial Archives. On the Commons the party was watched, then ambushed in an emptied street by Vargrax the Executioner, the brother of Kagra the Butcher, with bandits and two wererats after Wayne's hat. Thimble ran for the guards and caught up with Luna. The rest won the fight, and Vargrax died swinging at Aalyn. Then they found out who he was. Ends as Talia Shay, one of Aalyn's exes, runs out of a house and grabs him.

## What Happened at the Table

**Recap**
- Alaric caught Bog up on Sessions 29 and 30 from his notes.

**Out of the fog**
- The knights tended their wounded and counted **silver chain links** taken from their fallen.
- Luna is less shaken than the rest, as if numb to this (Thimble's Insight). She thanked Thimble for getting them through.
- Wayne lifted a wagon so the knights could fit a spare wheel. He overheard knights whispering about **a stone warrior who triumphed in the mist** and carried three men on his back.
- **Craters** in the valley before the city: concussive blasts of air pressure (Aalyn). Wayne knows the work: **Nerevath** came through, and smashed a wagon.

**Hollowmere**
- Wedged in a crevasse between mountains, with a river cascading through its plateaus and fog rolling down from the peaks. Slate and pale colors, silver filigree, bridges, steam. A modest front gate for a city slightly larger than Oakhollow. Nothing towers; it all stays low.
- It sits at the edge of the Free Kingdoms. Beyond the mountains, **the Cinder Peaks**, lie **the Runelands**, a place of wild magic; cinder sparks drift over the peaks. Thimble and Wayne have both been to the Runelands and dealt with the Obsidian Enclave there. The wild magic feels stronger here than in Oakhollow.
- **Thimble** (History 21) has been here. The city is built on **Durnhollow**, an old dwarven settlement beneath it, abandoned for hundreds of years. Dwarven work is woven into the city.
- **Aalyn** (History 5) has been here too. **It's a city he has tried to forget.**
- The knights skip the front gate and load the wagons onto **lifts by the river**: gondolas and magnetic grav lifts of old dwarven make, poorly kept up, that climb plateau to plateau.
- The city's guards wear slate armor. The Holy Silver Chain Knights aren't sworn to the city; they're more of a guild or mercenary order based here.
- **Aalyn (natural 20 Perception):** two very tall guards, about seven feet, in full face helms. **One recognized Wayne** and kept his composure. Wayne couldn't tell which (they might be Goliaths). Most people here are short: dwarves, gnomes, halflings.

**Luna's offer**
- The party can stay at **the Silver Keep** on **the Aristocrat Heights**, the top plateau. Dinner in the mess hall around 8.
- Alaric can visit **the Celestial Archives**, normally closed to outsiders. Luna will put in the paperwork.
- She raised her request for help again. Aalyn asked to talk tomorrow.
- She gave each of them **a silver chain link**, a pass showing they're affiliated with the order.
- **Her tips:**
  - **The Cloud Room** on the Heights, very refined.
  - **The Cog and Compass** on the Commons, the middle slab of **the Terrace**: an adventurers' pub, her favorite.
  - **Cinderward**, Hollowmere's arcane institute, just off **Inkwell Street**. A **professor from Glimmerstone** is there on sabbatical.
  - Avoid **the Fog Slab**, the bottom layer in the mist: hole-in-the-wall places, magic shops, not many good characters.
- The city's ruler is a **baroness** in **Hollow Hall** at the top. The Silver Keep stands beside it.
- The party got off at the Commons. Bog plans to get hammered at the first place, then the second. Thimble wants the magic school, to find "his prick from my hometown."

**Watched**
- Aalyn and Alaric noticed people actively watching and following them. Alaric sent Rec flat and sideways to look (Stealth 9), and the watchers vanished.
- Alaric loudly announced that the Off-Branded Misfits had saved the day once again. Aalyn suggested keeping a low profile.
- The next street was empty: shutters closed, fresh meals left on tables. Bog drank an abandoned beer. Aalyn's *Detect Magic* found nothing. Thimble spotted figures peeling out of an alley.

**Vargrax the Executioner**
- A huge purple tiefling with a warhammer. "You met my brother." **Kagra the Butcher**, killed by Aalyn in the Oakhollow fight pit. "I want that guy's head."
- Two of his men **turned into wererats**: "He has it. We need to take it from him." They want **Wayne's Rat Lord's Hat**, to avenge their god from "the impostor." Bandits closed in behind.
- Vargrax has a sister ("none of your concern"). His men came for honor, he said, not pay.
- Bog finished his beer, failed to talk the bandits out of it, and breathed lightning. Initiative.

**The fight**
- **Bog** breathed lightning on two bandits and killed one with his beer mug, leaving half of it in his head.
- The wererats fired four crossbow bolts at Wayne and all missed.
- **Alaric** cast ***Moat of Hell***: a hemisphere of hellish darkness with fire and screaming, difficult terrain, blinding.
- **Thimble** had flashbacks to destroyed cities, and **ran for the guards** before anyone got arrested.
- **Aalyn** flew 20 feet up and killed one bandit with *Eldritch Blast*.
- **Vargrax** burned (14), burst out of the moat, and missed Aalyn with a heavy crossbow.
- **Wayne** killed two bandits with thrown psionic daggers and tried to hide behind a table. His sprout stuck up.
- One bandit died in the moat. A burned wererat ran up next to Alaric to shoot at Wayne. Bog breathed on Vargrax again.
- **Thimble** found three guards in the next street and sent them, then ran to find Luna.
- **Aalyn** dropped to five feet: "Do you want to see something I learned the day I killed your brother?" His eyes went black, and **Orchestral Roar** blew Vargrax across the street into a building (24). "Bog, he's mine."
- Vargrax smashed Aalyn's big toe with his warhammer (11).
- **Wayne** cut off a wererat's hand with two thrown daggers and **hid properly this time**: a bottle rolled in front of his sprout.
- **Bog** gutted that wererat with punches and headbutted its head off. It rolled down the street like a bowling ball.
- Alaric dropped the moat and fired the **Chaos Bolt stored in his ring** at the other wererat on a wall (14 thunder). The wall caved and it fell. Then he sat down to sketch Aalyn fighting Vargrax.
- **Aalyn** hit Vargrax with his vengeful blade and a 2nd-level *Branding Smite*. Turning to watch the guards arrive, he let Vargrax swing. **The blade's necrotic backlash killed him mid-swing.** Aalyn faked being hit (Performance 24) and threw himself 10 feet down the street.
- The guards gave chase to the last wererat, which got away down an alley.
- Bog took one of Vargrax's eyes for his necklace. A purple iris.

**Thimble and Luna**
- Thimble ran the grav lifts with his Oakhollow boots (Acrobatics 17) and caught Luna's gondola one plateau short of the top.
- Luna knows the Executioner. "He's harmless. **He runs the orphanage for tiefling children.**" Aalyn, over the link: he swung at me and dropped dead.
- Luna sent Dame down to handle it, and rode up to the keep with Thimble.

**The aftermath**
- Aalyn told the guards **Jamas and Guile** it was self-defense, and Vargrax hit him so hard he killed himself (Deception 23). They let him search the body before they came back with more men.
- **Loot:**
  - Aalyn: Vargrax's warhammer, crossbow bolts, cheap jewelry from his horns, 6 gp.
  - Bog: 10 gp, and wererat teeth for his Skullcup (+1d12 to his next Skullcup attack).
  - Wayne: 12 gp and two rations.
  - Alaric: 25 gp, and **a worn postcard: Kagra, Vargrax and a tiefling woman with about thirteen tiefling children.** On the back, from Kagra to "Vargrax and Tiffany": the fighting pits should work out; hopefully this keeps the orphanage open another month; more money after a few more fights.
- **A candle is inscribed inside Vargrax's coat.**
- A door burst open, and **one of Aalyn's ex-girlfriends, [[Talia|Talia Shay]],** ran out and grabbed him. "Ally, what are you doing here? You almost died." Session ends.

**After the session**
