# Session 5: Bluelock and Bloodlancers

**Party:** [[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]]
**Ref:** [[Session 4 - Mushrooms & Trees]], [[The Tall Oak Forest]], [[Bragthar]], [[Lithoweaves]]

> [!important] Played: "Bluelock and Bloodlancers" (Episode 5)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=chsiG9CE7JM). The tree's core went into Wayne, Aalyn had a vision on watch, and the party took the Bloodlancer camp the hard way. The session ended before the camp was looted.

> [!info] DM Note
> Much of the DM's narration is missing from this recording (long music-only stretches). The vision scenes below are reconstructed from the players' side of the table.

## What Happened at the Table

**Aftermath of the Tree Blight**
- Aalyn, eyes glowing with rage, flew at Thimble, grabbed him by the throat and threw him to the ground for putting the necklace in the tree. He stared Thimble down for the rest of the scene.
- Wayne felt a strong urge to take the core and grabbed it.
- **Wayne's vision.** The black goo went into Wayne through his brand, and he blacked out. He saw what played as a flashback: a ball game across floating rock islands, Blue Lock style. He teleported between islands, passed, vaulted off the last defender's shoulders, and scored with a scissor kick (19). He seemed to know the people there.
- He woke on the ground holding a gemstone with a symbol floating in it. The tree now looks exactly like Wayne's own stone. Aalyn: "Whatever you do, don't give that back to Thimblewick."
- Wayne pulled on the tree where his stone had gone in, testing how durable it is.
- Wayne's goose came out of the bag. Thimble fed it one of his magic mushrooms, and they checked whether it was branded.
- They took a chip of the tree as a "receipt." Alaric flew up and saw no other stone trees nearby.

**The night**
- Camp by the pond. Bog slept on a rock. Aalyn took the last watch.
- **Aalyn's vision.** At dawn, writing a song on the rock with his lute, a figure appeared. He asked whether it was the figure from his nightmare or the princess. He wept: "Why must you give me impossible choices?" He reached out and plucked a string of light from it, which became a **golden string** on his lute. As the figure faded he begged it not to leave again, and fell off the rock.
- In the morning he played a new song about the mushroom grove and gave heroic inspiration to Bog and Alaric (Encouraging Song, from his Entertainer feat). Wayne felt no different. Aalyn snapped at Thimble again.

**Morning**
- Alaric was back to normal size.
- Thimble considered planting a bean. He didn't, yet.
- Alaric's owl kept pulling toward the ruins, about a day's travel away, and Alaric wanted to go. The party chose to clear the Bloodlancer camp first. Aalyn promised him they'd make it to the ruins.
- They kept to the road and out of the woods (where the goblins were).

**The Bloodlancer camp**
- The owl scouted (16): the camp was getting breakfast ready, with a goblin cooking.
- Plan: Wayne walks in the front as a distraction with an invisible Bragthar behind him, linked by his psychic whispers. The others flank. Thimble rode on Wayne's shoulder.
- Thimble pointed out a lookout too loudly. A horn sounded. Wayne walked in anyway ("Good morning, it seems I'm a bit turned around"), was shot in the back of the head, and peed a little.
- The fight, in highlights:
  - Thimble threw his dagger at a scout. Alaric's Fire Bolt set a wooden lookout tower on fire with the scout inside.
  - Invisible Bragthar mauled the commander early, about 27 damage in one turn, then spun like a Beyblade.
  - Aalyn's card trick: "Pick a card," then Spray of Cards, blinding a Bloodlancer. He shot 30 feet into the air on his boots and gave Alaric bardic inspiration ("You did start the fire").
  - Bog cleared the wall with Step of the Wind and spent the fight taunting ("store brand," "spaghetti"). He says he took no damage tanking the commander.
  - Alaric's Phantasmal Force failed. He used Minor Illusion for a fake fire, a cheering crowd for Aalyn, and later the dead scout's head crying "Help me." He cast Fog Cloud. The owl kept helping Bog and Bragthar.
  - Thimble ate another mushroom (wild surge), slipped into a tent mid-fight and looted it (a potion of healing and 10 sp), drank the potion, then came out with a 2nd-level Chaos Bolt for heavy lightning damage.
  - A sniper scout hammered Aalyn and Wayne. Aalyn dropped to the ground to get out of its sight and healed himself.
  - The commander heals with blood when he hits. He dropped Wayne, who crumbled into a pile of rocks, and Aalyn's Healing Word brought him back. The commander also cut down one of his own scouts.
  - Bragthar killed a Bloodlancer and ate his head. Bog finished the last one by twisting his fingers in its ears until its neck gave out.
- Rough fight: the party missed nearly everything for three rounds.

**After the fight (session ended here)**
- Aalyn flew to the commander's tent to loot it. Bog ate the goblin's breakfast.
- The goblin servant is alive, in a crate.
- The man in black robes hasn't been found yet.
- Three Bloodlancer patrol pairs are still out.
- Prank idea: wear the commander's armor, or Aalyn uses his disguise kit and the commander's face.

**Rules notes**
- Heroic inspiration (Encouraging Song): after a short or long rest, Aalyn can give it to up to two allies who hear him play, and he can count himself. It lasts until used.
- Aalyn is flavored as a half-elf. He ignores Trance and needs a full night's sleep.
- Thimble has two luck points a day.
- Aalyn's golden string adds +2 to his healing.
