# Session 12: The Establishment is Corrupt

**Party:** Level 5 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 11 - The Plot Thickens]], [[The Thieves Guild]], [[The Silver Lyre]], [[Tiberius Blackfang]]

> [!important] Played: "The Establishment is Corrupt" (Episode 12)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=lCaH3Wb-9js). The party talked and bribed Bog out of the Western Gate guardhouse, then went down into the sewers under the Orchid. Ends with Aalyn captured in the wererats' hideout as the Rat Lord rises from the water.

## What Happened at the Table

**Before play**
- The four who were there recapped Session 11 for Bog.
- The DM had Bog roll a Sleight of Hand check retroactively for the night at the Orchid: **21.** What it covered was not said.
- Bog's attractiveness roll: 18 (+1 Charisma).
- The group logo was settled: a full patch and a short "OBM" mark, with the L drawn as a lute.

**The Western Gate guardhouse**
- The party (not under arrest, only held for questioning) was brought to the guardhouse just outside the west gate. Bog was put in the holding cell with three others: a perfume thief, a twitchy man petting a rat he treats as his lawyer, and a huge bald man. Bog and the bald man hit it off.
- Bog told the party, through Aalyn's *Message*, what happened: the woman insulted his skin condition, so **he choked her. "She hurt me first."** Wayne's Insight caught his lies.
- **Commander Seth of the Western Gate** came out: slender, very verbose, polo shirt pulled tight. Aalyn cast *Disguise Self* into a lawyer's suit and introduced himself as "Aalyn Ravaceran, bard, lawyer." Seth knew his file.
- Wayne formed a psionic link with Bog and relayed the interview to the others.

**Bog's interview**
- The dead woman was **Angelique Firestorm**, a large woman. Bog doesn't know names.
- His story: "Lute Man" (Ino Silvertone) sang badly, wanted her gone, and told Bog to deal with her. They spent time together, then Bog left her in the room with the key and spent most of the night in the baths.
- Seth produced **rope from Bog's pack** that matched ligature marks at the scene. Bog said it was climbing rope.
- Bog asked for food and drink and got salted nuts and a carton of confiscated **fermented milk.**

**The party's interview**
- Seth questioned the four together, then split them and had each pair trace Bog's night on a map of the Orchid.
- From Ino's report: Bog left the stage room with Angelique, Aalyn stabbed Ino's hand, and Wayne pantsed and robbed him. Wayne denied it.
- The top-hatted, monocled man (the one who tried to bag Thimble when he was a potted plant) claimed Thimble and Alaric tried to push drugs on him.
- Alaric said the Fog Master got handsy in the steam room and Bog pushed him off.
- **Wayne told Seth about the bar and fight club downstairs.** Seth plans to send guards to look.
- Thimble's Deception (11) that Bog "climbs sometimes" was average. Alaric's history check (14) only covered elven law.
- Seth: "I know Mr. Bogtrotter murdered Miss Angelique." **Ino Silvertone's mother paid Seth 200 gold** to keep Ino's name out of it, and Seth hinted he could be persuaded too.
- **Aalyn's natural 20 Persuasion (30).** He promised Seth a good word with Sir Romulus and Princess Rosalie: a raise, early retirement, land, and a path to lordship. Seth wanted it confirmed first, plus something now.
- The party paid **100 gp (25 each from Aalyn, Alaric, Thimble and Wayne) and one of Thimble's magic beans.** Seth wants confirmation that Ino will take the fall.
- Aalyn's Ring of Mind Shielding: magic can't tell whether he is lying.

**Bog goes free**
- Bog was released. The bald man turned out to be named **Dragon.**
- While the others were interviewed, Bog wrapped the fermented milk in watertight cloth and fed it salt from the nuts until it built pressure. He left the milk bomb with Dragon, winked, and tipped the big guard 1 gp.
- Outside, three of Sir Romulus's guards in black were arguing with three Fern guards in green over a release order (Thimble Jr. II's message got through). **The milk bomb went off inside** and all six ran in.
- For the next day or two, the party rolls guard interactions at disadvantage.

**Back to business**
- Alaric sold two chain shirts to the dwarven smith at the Iron Branch Armory for 60 gp each (120 gp).
- At the Orchid, a young ginger guard named **Pud** (six days on the job, wants to be a comedian) let them in.
- Downstairs, Aalyn warned the masked doorman that the guards had heard about the bar. **Silas was out, and Tiberius had waited for hours and gone back to the keep.**
- The barkeep gave Wayne the job ticket and route map. Bog drank two Dirty Stumps (1 temporary hit point each).
- Thimble beat Alaric in an apple juice chugging race; Alaric spilled his over himself.
- Aalyn, eyes flaring orange, told Bog to stop killing innocent people, at least where they sleep. Bog: "We kill people that deserve it." Bog gave Aalyn 20 gp.
- The two windows off the hallway downstairs are a fence's counters, closed for now.

**The sewers**
- Thimble walked the ceiling in his spider slippers and Aalyn floated over the water in his boots. Alaric cast *Mage Armor*.
- A locked door. Bog couldn't bash it. Wayne's natural 20 Perception through the keyhole, Investigation 23 (no trap), then picked the lock (22). Alaric caught a rat with *Mage Hand*.
- **The runner's den.** An old thieves' rest stop overrun by rat swarms. Bog's lightning breath did 21 to the swarm, Thimble's *Shatter* finished another, and a giant rat woke up. Wayne killed it with a thrown dagger. Bog kept five fried rats for dinner.
- A crumpled note on the table marks blocked tunnels that match Wayne's map.
- Alaric's "criminals are rats too" joke fell flat, and he sulked at the back.

**The hideout**
- Thimble Jr. II found a large water pipe and heard voices. Wayne scouted it (Stealth 17): a tiered underground hideout with ragged bandits.
- Aalyn's plan: *Disguise Self* as a filthy hermit and talk his way in. **The code word for help is "Lazy Susan."** He dropped from the pipe into the water.
- A bandit asked, "Did the tunnels treat you kindly?" Aalyn didn't know the answer. "Not one of ours." Two bandits dragged him to the back.
- **Wererats** woke on their bedrolls, and the **Rat Lord** rose out of the central whirlpool. "Dinner time."
