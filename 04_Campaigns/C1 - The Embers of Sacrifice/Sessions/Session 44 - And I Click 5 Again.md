# Session 44: "and I click 5 again..."

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 43 - The Tempest Warden]], [[Durnhollow]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "and I click 5 again..." (Episode 44)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dlIuy2qyics). In the misty canyon below the steam layer, Alaric froze ice bridges and then an ice luge, which he and Wayne rode through a loop and straight through a second detention drone. Thimble and Bog rode an air current; Bog swatted the drone's orb away from Thimble. Aalyn's chest was a mimic, which he lured off a ledge. Through zero-gravity pockets and a tunnel the party reached Durnhollow's water layer. They solved a pipe puzzle to start the first pump, rode the pipes into the central turbine, kicked down doors, and solved three flywheel button puzzles (Wayne cracked one by pressing 5 over and over). Alaric mended a frozen sprinkler system, Wayne and Thimble solved an ice-block crane puzzle, and the party killed a wave of ice mephits. Aalyn found a silver key under a glyph-trapped false drawer, four silver ingots, a necklace of adaptation behind a pipe, and a store of aged dwarven ale.

## What Happened at the Table

**The canyon**
- The party put the soulvite shard in Alaric's bag for safekeeping.
- **Alaric built ice bridges** five feet at a time (*Shape Water*, *Ray of Frost*), with Aalyn chilling the air with *Prestidigitation*. "Teamwork, four sessions in."
- Bog flew with his wings (which tear through his back). Thimble rode on him.
- Aalyn scouted on his boots (Perception 19) and saw **another detention drone** patrolling in the mist.
- **An air stream** from a fan crosses the cavern. Thimble rode it like a Mario Galaxy launch star (Acrobatics 20 with feather fall); Bog boosted him the rest of the way.
- **The ice luge:** Alaric sat in Wayne's lap and remade the ice ahead of them as they slid; Aalyn helped Wayne steer and gave Bardic Inspiration. A natural 20 Arcana and a 29 Acrobatics turned it into a loop-de-loop. Aalyn, in red lifeguard shorts, dapped them through the loop.
- **At the drone:** Thimble showed it his ass (Deception, not enough). Bog offered it a drink (Persuasion 11). It fired an orb at Thimble; **Bog swatted it away (CON 15 exactly)** and threw it back, missing.
- **The luge rammed the drone and shattered it.** Alaric peeled off into the wall (1 damage). Wayne plowed into Thimble at full speed: 7 bludgeoning each. "Break catch, buddy."
- **The mimic:** Aalyn's chest (not on his map) bit his arm (5) and grappled him. He *Misty Step*ped out and lured it off the ledge into the mist (Deception 24).
- Wayne made a snow angel in the wall and vanished into it (Stealth 20).
- **Zero-gravity pockets** hang in the steam; things that enter keep their momentum in a straight line. Alaric and Wayne luged through them off a ramp to the far side.
- Thimble's backflip landed on his head (natural 1, 3 damage). Aalyn's backflip down a hole was clean. A rocky tunnel led down to **a dwarven stone floor.**

**The water layer**
- Pipes, a sluice gate that only opens while its lever is held, and a supply closet. Thimble smashed boxes for 3 cp.
- **The pump room:** an industrial pump in a standing basin, worker constructs going about their business, and a door blocked by fallen rock.
- **Pipe puzzle:** rotate pipe pieces to route water from the frowny-face source into every exclamation-point outlet. The table solved it. **The pump started** and the pipes filled.
- Wayne walked into a pipe and was swept along into **the central turbine**: a huge tank spinning clockwise, with what look like flowers on the water (not people under sprouts). Alaric made an ice bubble and then an ice raft.
- Bog ripped the closed gate off its hinges (Athletics 18) to follow.
- **Aalyn picked the lock** (thieves' tools, 18) into the turbine's control room: two levers that aren't armed yet.
- Wayne kicked an unlocked door (1 damage to himself). **Thimble kicked a door off its hinges.** Bog kicked a bathroom door into the sink (natural 20), got a bidet, and took the second dump of the campaign.
- Thimble's map shows **treasure behind a caved-in part of an ice room.**

**Aalyn's wing**
- A room of boxes (Investigation 19): **four silver ingots and 42 sp**, and papers showing a maintenance room to the northwest with a lever.
- He kicked open **the administrative office** (17): water-damaged logistics, and notes on **refining silver with water to enhance items, part of a larger project.** He kept it to himself for now.
- A desk drawer had an arcane glyph. **Aalyn's *Unseen Servant* picked it with his thieves' tools (26); a fire glyph destroyed the servant and damaged the tools** (unusable until fixed). Under a false bottom: **an ornate silver key**, like the bronze key from the steam layer.
- Aalyn pulled a lever at the end of a hallway: **it opened all the water gates in the central turbine.**

**The flywheels**
- Three flywheels, each with a button panel (three, four and five buttons). Each press flips that button and its neighbor.
- Alaric solved the first. Thimble pressed all the buttons at once on the second and got zapped (3). Wayne solved the second; Thimble got the third going and handed it to Wayne, who clicked 5 again, and again, and solved it.
- **Power on:** the turbine levers armed, and a door near Bog opened.

**The ice rooms**
- A freezing hall with a broken sprinkler system. **Alaric mended the three burst pipes** and the next door opened.
- **An ice-block puzzle**, like a Pokémon gym: blocks slide until they hit something, toward three cranes. Thimble jammed the first block in a corner. **Wayne telekinetically pulled a block into his own body to stop it** under a crane. Thimble had the plan for the second. Wayne pulled the third.
- **Ice mephits** dropped from the ceiling when Aalyn's lever rumbled. Bog crit one (21, one-shot); Wayne got advantage from it. Thimble's *Fire Bolt* killed one, then he slipped on the ice and went prone. Wayne killed one; Alaric hit a step-back Fire Bolt three: "Kobe."
- The cranes lifted all three blocks. A pipe opened and **a second pump** was revealed (its puzzle saved for next time).

**Aalyn in the pipes**
- Riding the pipes, Aalyn blasted a cracked section (13) and climbed into **a narrow storage room: a chest with a necklace of adaptation** (the treasure on Thimble's map), and **barrels of aged dwarven ale.** He started rolling barrels into his bag of holding.

**After the session**
- Alaric is out of spell slots and running on cantrips.
- The DM modified the ice-block puzzle to have three blocks.
