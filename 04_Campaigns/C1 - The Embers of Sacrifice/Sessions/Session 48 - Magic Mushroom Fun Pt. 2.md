# Session 48: Magic Mushroom Fun Pt. 2

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]]); [[Bogtrotter]] absent (asleep)
**Ref:** [[Session 47 - The Tide Warden]], [[Session 4 - Mushrooms & Trees]], [[Durnhollow]], [[Myra]], [[Rec]], [[Hollowmere]], [[Oughan Gor'Drak]]

> [!important] Played: "Magic Mushroom Fun pt.2" (Episode 48)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=uofbkmTFD5E). Mushroom gauntlet roulette, part two. Thimble drained the mushroom instead of it draining him. Wayne reached the Omnishroom and saw a vault with bronze, silver and gold doors and a fourth; the gold key is "above the lava pits, take the third track." Aalyn became a walking flashbang. Alaric's roll opened a portal to the Feywild, where nymphs bathed Thimble and a scarred black bear, Rai Zeph, knelt to Wayne as his Kyn'Ruthar. Aalyn scouted ahead through ballista-guarded mine shafts. The party freed Myra the myconid from her rock, found a dwarven blasting charge, and reached a quarry where Wayne's headbutt woke the stone.

## What Happened at the Table

**Recap**
- Alaric's player gave the recap from Alaric's point of view, as he had missed most of the fight.
- Bog's player is away on his honeymoon. **Bog fell asleep on a mushroom** and slept through the session.
- Thimble's Tides of Chaos is spent: until a long rest, his next spell surges. **One of his eyes now has a faint shimmer**, and Alaric can sense the surge built up in him.
- Thimble is still pantsless.

**Mushroom gauntlet roulette, part two**
- The pool and ring look like Bragthar's cove ([[Session 4 - Mushrooms & Trees|Session 4]]). Alaric's Arcana (11): magical, but he can't tell which way it leans. Thimble walked in while Alaric was still checking.
- **Thimble** (d8: 6, d20: 11, Con save 18): a fat green mushroom tried to pull his life force into the water, and **he pulled it back out of the mushroom**: 10 temporary hit points and his highest missing spell slot refunded.
- **Wayne** lay down in it (d8: 7, the same as last time; Intelligence save). This water is enhanced with silver dust from above, so instead of a psi-shroom he reached **the Omnishroom**: fungal tendrils pulled his mind into the network for a minute, able to see and hear through any fungus within a mile.
  - It showed him **a vault at the very bottom, surrounded by heat**: dark stone, **four vault doors, copper-bronze, silver, gold, and one more.** They need keys, and **he has two of the four already.**
  - **The gold key: "above the lava pits. Take the third track."** He'll subdue the earth monster eventually, "but you have a ways to go."
  - His sprout: **"You consumed a seed and it became part of you. You have the power to absorb..."** The minute ran out there. His sprout now has **three leaves**, slightly longer.
  - He has **advantage on Perception and Insight for 24 hours.**
- Wayne told the others. Alaric worked it out: four keys for four elementals, the bronze key for the Tempest Warden's lightning; fire and earth wardens are likely still below.
- **Aalyn** belly-flopped in (d8: 5). A shimmering gold mushroom shot a column of light: **for the next hour he is made of light**, bright light 30 feet and dim 60. Attackers have advantage on him, and an enemy starting within 15 feet of him must make a Constitution save or be blinded. He claimed the mushroom gave him positive affirmations, then admitted it didn't, and went to scout ahead with Rec.
- **Alaric** dipped in carefully, perfect diamond ripples of arcane symmetry (d8: 7, then a d20). The Omnishroom, hit twice in a row, **opened a portal at his feet: a wild grove in the Feywild.** "Anybody can go. You have five minutes."

**The Feywild**
- Alaric, Thimble and Wayne went through: pink and blue trees, rapids, pixies, oxen, deer, dogs, and around a central rock and pool, **nymphs and dryads**.
- Alaric (History, with advantage; the Fenwyns descend from the fey) knows the Feywild from his lineage's lessons: glorious, but deceptive and dangerous. **"Do not agree to anything."**
- The nymphs asked their names and invited them into the water, which "strips everything bare and lays everything out in the open."
- **Time runs slower here.** The DM gave Thimble a pocket watch (conjured in the material plane): five minutes outside is closer to an hour inside. Thimble considered casting *Planar Binding* on a nymph and decided against it. He cast *Detect Magic* on the water: glittering steam, pure magic.
- Wayne and Thimble failed Wisdom saves and felt pulled into the water. **Thimble got in.** Wayne sat on the edge.

**Rai Zeph**
- **A black bear with scars down over its eyes** waded over and nudged Wayne. Wayne linked to it psionically (and dropped the party's link to do it) and cast *Speak with Animals*.
- **It knelt and bowed: "My Kyn'Ruthar. It pleases me to know that you are safe and well."**
- History 17: the accent is **Goliath**, from the stormy south of Wayne's home continent. Insight (advantage): **the scars are self-inflicted, the penance of a storm Goliath who failed his trial of adulthood** and was exiled.
- **He is Rai Zeph**, a storm Goliath. Wayne recognizes his face: **a guard on Hollowmere's outer gate**, who broke a bead of sweat when the party first arrived.
- He fled the continent and heard of healing in Hollowmere. In a temple **they healed his eyes but also changed his body**: he showed his werebear hybrid form, then went back to the bear. He can change at will. **"They have their way with your body. It's not good."**
- That temple is **the Gilded Conservatory**, the botanical garden on the summit by the Silver Keep. (Thimble: that's where Jud came out after the fey grove.) **Rai stays in the city to warn people away from its healing.**
- He travels to and from the Feywild with **a satchel of diamonds**: the shaky connections between the planes (the raw-ether wildfires seen in Midland) can be brute-forced open, at a heavy toll. Persuasion: **he gave Wayne one diamond.**
- **He asked the Kyn'Ruthar to go home and speak with the elders: the Gor'Drak Dominion is ravaging the Goliath homelands and they need their protector.** Wayne: "Possibly soon in the future at some point."
- **"Should you need an ally in the city, find me at Terry's"**, an ale house, where he'll be in his guard uniform.
- His warning: pull your friend out before he can no longer leave.

**Leaving**
- Wayne yanked Thimble out. **Thimble felt his life had ended; nothing mattered but getting back to the nymphs.** He cast a 2nd-level *Chaos Bolt* at Wayne and missed; it burned a line through the trees and the foliage rotted along it.
- **Alaric cast *Dispel Magic* in Thimble's face** and the siren pull lifted. Thimble's surge (8): **a small flesh-and-metal modron construct** appeared, friendly to him, banging a gear against him in Modron. It lasted about a minute.
- Thimble bluffed the nymphs (he'd go back in for their treasure); they saw through it and offered to force him into the water. Rai: "Yes, please" leave.
- **Wayne: "You have my thanks, Rai."** "I am forever in your service as you are to our people, my kin."
- They came back through at 4:58. Alaric knows enough to understand the diamond is fuel for a planar gate to the Feywild.
- **They threw sleeping Bog in the pool** (d8: 4). **Bog is supercharged with chaos energy and can wild magic surge for a while.** His scales flicker between bronze and silver and his eyes twinkle. They put him back on his mushroom, soaking wet.

**Aalyn scouts ahead**
- With Rec, Aalyn followed mined-out shafts. A broken stationary dwarven drone sat on one platform.
- **Three doors, a game show.** Right door: a **mining ballista** (9 force; difficult terrain around him). Middle door: another, dodged. Left door: clear.
- More ballistas watch the crossings. Stealth (with disadvantage from his light; Rec helping) got him past one. He threw **a copper coin** to draw a shot, then ran for it; another caught him round the corner (11 force).
- Behind a rock past a big door, he **used his wand of resurrection to cast *Heal* on himself. It came out as a 3rd-level *Cure Wounds* instead.** He tapped the wand: "Oh, ****."
- Alaric, through Rec, wrote **"Need help?"** in *Illusory Script*. Aalyn sent directions back: left door, then look right first.

**Myra**
- A mushroom on a boulder whispered to Wayne: **Myra, a myconid.** She has lived on that rock for 60 years and disconnected from the network when she felt Wayne rummaging in it.
- She wants a new host. Alaric drew a Fire Bolt; Thimble cast *Blight* and **her pacifying spores stunned him.** Talked down.
- She asked to be cut off her rock to come along. Wayne's Sun Blade pained her (sunlight sickness); his daggers weren't enough; **he cut her free with the Sun Blade** and she grew new legs.
- She made **a fungal transport pod for sleeping Bog**, which floats behind the party.
- At the ballista crossing she **ballooned wide to block line of sight.** Wayne (Bend Luck) shoved two others clear of a bolt.
- Thimble ran toward a chest at "chest" and **a tether bolt pinned him through the shoulder** (14 piercing, speed 0). Myra covered him and healed him with spores (3) and broke the tether.
- The chest: **a dwarven blasting charge**, runic powder in a carved stone capsule. **Thimble has it.**

**The quarry**
- Aalyn drew the last ballista with another coin, and they came out into **a quarry**: chiseled granite, platforms, a 60-foot ceiling, and **three huge chain-shutter factory doors** on the far side. Nothing moving.
- Aalyn and Myra met. Aalyn checked his treasure map; nothing matched yet.
- **Wayne tried to Rob Deer the door** (knee and forehead at once) and missed by one. The clang echoed, and **stone woke up**: gargoyles on the high perches, **stone berserkers** stepping out of the rock, and a **stone berserker commander** at the center. Session ends before initiative.

**After the session**
