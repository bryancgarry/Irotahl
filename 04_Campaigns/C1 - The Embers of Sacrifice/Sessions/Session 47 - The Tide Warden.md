# Session 47: The Tide Warden

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 46 - A Quick One]], [[Durnhollow]], [[Soulvite]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "The Tide Warden" (Episode 47)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BL_pwDiGnXI). Alaric's capture bubble flooded in the pipes; Thimble blasted the ceiling and Alaric fell on him. The two gray oozes merged into a spectator, and later the other two made a second one. Riding water spirits into the conduit's glowing intakes put the party inside the Tide Warden for free hits. Thimble took two spirits, rail-gunned through the machine and gambled on a wild surge that blew up on him. Aalyn killed the first spectator, and Bog finished the Warden with a wet, lightning-charged middle finger. The room drained down a water slide: coin, the water soulvite shard, and a glowing fungal cavern with another mushroom circle.

## What Happened at the Table

**Alaric in the walls**
- Alaric, still in the detention drone's arcane bubble, was riding the pipes when they filled with rushing water. The spider drone washed away and the bubble cracked against the pipe wall.
- Through Wayne's psionic link he called for help. Wayne: the Tide Warden is "beckoning us." (Wayne named it on the spot. Later Wayne claimed it wore a name tag; it does not.)
- Alaric used *Control Water* to push the water out and freeze the cracks shut, without mending the bubble, so he could break it later.

**Round 3**
- The stunned Warden recovered at the end of its turn; the conduit sheened it again.
- Wayne *Cloud Jaunt*ed and used his weightless movement to float clear, 10 feet over the water.
- Aalyn jumped up onto a platform, *Misty Step*ped, and Eldritch Blasted two mephits. One died; the other dropped its *Fog Cloud*, and everyone saw **the two gray oozes merging**. He also saw the conduit's green intake light.
- **Thimble found Alaric.** A Perception roll and an Insight "vibe check" narrowed the arcane signatures to two spots; he cast *Shatter* at the ceiling right above him. Water poured down and **Alaric's bubble fell on Thimble's head** (3 bludgeoning) and shattered. "Nobody ever sees me there."
- Thimble then belly-flopped onto a water spirit and took its water form.
- A mephit's frost breath caught Alaric (4). He killed it with *Scorching Ray*, and found the ooze shrugs off fire. **Rec**, captured with him, popped back out.
- Bog breathed lightning down the Warden's line (16) and hit the green intake through the fog.
- **The merged oozes became a spectator**, eye stalks and teeth made from the bones in the ooze. It shot confusion and fear rays at Wayne, who saved both.
- The Warden slammed Bog twice; Deflect Attacks took the hit to zero.

**Round 4**
- Wayne *Cloud Jaunt*ed and dashed onto a water spirit. The Warden spent legendary actions on **crushing depths** against him (11 after a save).
- **Aalyn slid across the frozen water on his belly like a penguin** (Acrobatics) at double swim speed, entered the glowing intake in water form, and was **sucked up inside the Tide Warden**: a free, sure-hit Eldritch Blast (8) from the inside shattered the sheen. He came out with no spirit left. His tip to the others: one water spirit per glowing intake.
- With a DM ruling for this fight, he **Twinned his Eldritch Blast**: 23 to the spectator and 11 to a mephit.
- Alaric swam to Bog, cast *Summon Beast* (a water beast) and sent Rec to help Aalyn.
- **Bog killed a mephit and punched the Warden with force**; with no sheen, the blows landed like concrete.
- The spectator hit Aalyn with a wounding ray (5 after a save).
- **The Warden left the conduit** and engulfed Alaric, Bog and the beast (Alaric 24, the beast 24; Bog deflected nearly all of it). It threw them back and returned to the center. Two more intake lights came on, blue and pink.

**Round 5**
- **Wayne** swam under, *Misty Step*ped and dashed to a lit intake: inside the Warden with a luck point for advantage and sneak attack, **27 psychic**. He came out in the fog.
- **Aalyn Hexblade's Cursed the spectator and killed it** with two Eldritch Blasts through its eye, healing 7 from its life.
- **Thimble took a second water spirit** (Con save 19): his swim speed quadrupled. With a Dexterity save he aimed it into the machine like a rail gun, blending the Warden. He held himself in place with *Mage Hand* and cast a 3rd-level *Thunderwave* from inside (19).
- **His surge (45).** The DM offered a coin flip: 11 to 20 would raise his blender damage from 3d10 to 9d10, 1 to 10 would be a dud. He took it and lost. **The energy pressed back in on him underwater: 5d10 to himself**, and he was launched out through the fog into the far wall. He stayed up. He had also used Tides of Chaos.
- Alaric drank a greater healing potion (13) and Fire Bolted the Warden (13).
- Bog breathed lightning (18) through the malfunctioning conduit and punched it twice more (24). He peed on it and lost a Constitution contest; the room smells of asparagus.
- **Overloaded by Thimble, the conduit failed**: no more sheen.

**Round 6**
- The Warden slammed Bog (deflected) and Wayne (5 after Uncanny Dodge). **The blue quadrant flooded floor to ceiling.**
- Wayne switchbladed its forearm (21).
- Thimble drank a superior healing potion and fired a Fire Bolt shaped like a middle finger. **The Warden caught it and flipped him off.** Thimble took 1 point of "pride damage," and so did Aalyn when his blasts missed the same hand.
- **The other two oozes merged into a second spectator.** Its confusion ray caught Bog (7 psychic; he had to attack whatever was in reach); Wayne shrugged off its paralyzing ray.
- Alaric hit the Warden with *Psychic Lance* by name (8), and his beast clawed the spectator.
- **Bog finished it.** Two force punches (19), then a Flurry of Blows: he licked his middle finger, charged it with a little lightning, and jammed it into the Warden's raised middle finger. The lightning chained into the silver core. **The Tide Warden shrieked and fell apart.**

**The water slide**
- The other three pumps kicked on, the room flooded, then drained through the conduit's holes into **a water slide** down.
- Investigation on the way down: **Wayne 75 gp, Thimble 51 gp**, two others 25 gp each, and the natural 1 got **67 sp**.
- **The water soulvite shard**, a glowing quarter sphere like the first, floated down with them. It is in the party's possession.
- They landed on moss in **a dark, bioluminescent fungal cavern**, deeper than before, and walked out to find **another ring of mushrooms around a still pool.** Session ends.

**After the session**
- The second mini-boss is down ("our second gym badge"). The party went into Durnhollow at the end of Session 40; this is the seventh session inside, and it is still the same day.
- Thimble's wild magic is "loaded": after Tides of Chaos, his next spell before a long rest surges.
