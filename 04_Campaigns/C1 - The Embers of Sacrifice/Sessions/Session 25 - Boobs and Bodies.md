# Session 25: Boobs and Bodies

**Party:** Level 6 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 24 - Nerevath, The Feathered Scourge]], [[Rec]], [[Hollowmere]], [[The Free Kingdoms]]

> [!important] Played: "Boobs and Bodies" (Episode 25)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=1mQGKs6DGy0). A short session on the road. Thimble skated on Rec, the party swapped stories of their school days, and Thimble's Oak Greens turned out to be magic. At a guard post on the road the "guards" were bandits with an illusion trap. Alaric ended up dangling upside down and Bog ended up naked and grappled. The party fought back with an upside-down fireball, a boomrat, and a shatter that brought down the overpass. Ends mid-combat after Bog punched a bandit captain to death.

## What Happened at the Table

**After Nerevath**
- Wayne fed Pebbles another ration and told her to stay in the bag. She dove in.
- Alaric's ears are ringing from the honk blast. He took a short rest on his phantom steed.

**On the road**
- **Rec as a skateboard.** For overland travel, Thimble can ride Rec like a skateboard (no flying). His first ollie was scrappy but counts (Acrobatics 13).
- **School days** (a DM travel question, d100).
  - **Alaric's greatest achievement:** he graduated college at 13. The **Fenwyns helped develop the world's version of Tylenol**, testing it on him. Canon. The DM says it may prove crucial.
  - Alaric is about 22, young for an elf. For Thimble, school was about 250 years ago, and he remembers "a lot more candles back then."
  - Wayne remembers being 25. He doesn't know how long he was stone, or whether he ages. He told the others the woman in the demon realm turned him to stone. Wayne and Thimble have traveled together two or three years.
  - Thimble conjured a small fireball display without spending a slot (Arcana 16 at disadvantage). Alaric shaped it into a little flame Thimble.
- **Oak Greens.** Thimble lit a joint. Alaric (Arcana 15) found magical essence in it, nothing harmful. Thimble exhales glittering sparkles. Bog snorted some through his nose.
- Aalyn led (Survival 15) back onto the main road. About 6 p.m. they reached **a road guard post**: two stone towers joined by a covered overpass over the road.

**The guard post**
- Two "guards" playing dice called it a routine inspection because of bandit activity and had them stand between two boxes. Aalyn doesn't remember this from his last trip (History).
- Aalyn went through first.
- Wayne (Perception 17) heard the second guard tell the captain in **thieves' cant** that the skinny one (Alaric) has gold. Wayne raided the potato cart as a distraction and warned the link.
- Alaric passed his bag to Aalyn under a *Minor Illusion* high five. Aalyn's *Detect Magic* found **illusion magic** in the area.
- Bog stripped naked for his pat-down. It went badly, but it made the guards look away.
- Aalyn's Deception 13 didn't hide what he'd spotted: illusions on the boxes and on the ground under Alaric. "Alaric, move now!"
- A **snare** hoisted Alaric 10 ft upside down. The captain grappled the naked Bog.
- The guards' disguises dropped: **bandits**, led by a **Bandit Deceiver** with two **bandit captains.** Caltrops shot out around the boxes. Initiative.

**The fight**
- The **Deceiver** cast *Hold Person* on Aalyn (saved, 15). "Maybe you're stronger than I thought. Let me go get my wolves." He ran back.
- **Alaric**, upside down and laughing, cast *Fireball* for **39** (the Deceiver and both captains failed).
- Two more bandits threw open the overpass windows. "Let's see how you are as a piñata." Both crossbow bolts missed Alaric. One went through his illusory double.
- A bandit captain hit Bog with two scimitar strikes (deflected down to 2).
- **Wayne** filled his bag with potatoes (one for Pebbles to hide behind) and slipped through an open window into one tower. Two more bandits are inside, unaware. He threw a **boomrat** at the overpass bandits (one failed its save).
- The other captain slashed Aalyn (6). "Got to do everything myself."
- **Thimble** cast *Shatter* up into the overpass and **blew it out from between the towers.** Both bandits on it were thrown dead to the road.
  - The **Oak Greens effect**: targets of his magic glow, giving advantage on the next attack against them.
- **Aalyn** cast *Toll the Dead* on the fleeing Deceiver (24 necrotic, still up). He used *Unsettling Words* on the captain in front of him: "If you hit me again, that's going to be you next time."
- **Bog** turned in the grapple and hit both captains with his lightning breath (8; both failed, one only because of the Unsettling Words penalty). His breath sparkled from the Oak Greens. He uppercut the captain grappling him for 11, **punching his chest open and killing him.** Session ends mid-combat.
