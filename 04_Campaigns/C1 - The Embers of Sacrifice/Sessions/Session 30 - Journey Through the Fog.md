# Session 30: Journey Through the Fog

**Party:** Level 6 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 29 - The Holy Silver Chain Knights]], [[Luna au Vermillion]], [[Corvin Hale]], [[The Holy Chain Knights]], [[Lolth]], [[Fenril Fenwyn]], [[Rec]], [[Hollowmere]]

> [!important] Played: "Journey Through the Fog" (Episode 30)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2X_KY96B39w). *(Bog's player absent; Bog spent the session passed out.)* The high wore off badly: Wayne nearly cut the apple seeds out of himself, and woke up with a sprout on his head. Luna told Thimble she wants to use the brand as bait for the monster that killed her brother. The next day the caravan entered the magical fog around Hollowmere. Thimble led it through wearing the mask, until Wendigos tore the caravan apart. Aalyn, Alaric and Thimble fell unconscious in the fog and each had a vision; Wayne carried them out. Lolth set Aalyn a challenge: find her Spool before the others. Ends with Hollowmere in sight.

## What Happened at the Table

**The apple seeds**
- Aalyn cast *Cure Wounds* on Wayne's belly to heal the seeds. With his ear to it, he heard howling wind (Perception 7). The seeds, he said, told him they won't grow.
- Wayne tried to pass them behind a dumpster and couldn't. Aalyn looked: Wayne's insides are stony, like a cave, and empty.
- Two knights watched from a fence. Aalyn's "You got a problem with it, pal?" came out high-pitched, in his underwear: "Keep it moving, lady."
- He shifted his armor back on, ran in, and asked the bartender for prune juice. She smashed two apples (two copper). He stirred in a torn-up flower, roots and dirt included. Wayne was sure there were seeds in it.
- Aalyn's boots went off. He floated up 100 to 300 feet and couldn't get down. He threw his joint somewhere along the way.
- Alaric sobered up all at once and went for a walk.
- Wayne drew the Sun Blade behind the dumpster to cut the seeds out.

**Thimble and Luna**
- Luna stood at the edge of the woods under a full moon. She thanked Thimble for helping Barnaby.
- Thimble pressed her (Persuasion 15, with advantage). **"It was a night like this when I lost my brother."**
- The mark Aalyn showed her: in her research, that symbol **draws powerful monsters and misfortune.** She wants to use Aalyn **as bait to lure out the monster that killed her brother**, to get justice. She offered them the archives at the Vermillion chalet, with books and research on monsters.
- **Thimble told her he is marked too, and so are others in the group.** She stepped back three paces, then composed herself. She is sorry for their misfortune.
- **She was friends with Rosalie** and often visited Oakhollow.
- Thimble agreed to help on one condition: why is she so curious about Pebbles? (Persuasion 19.) Only that she's never seen a creature like it.
- She has never smoked; her father said it was bad for the lungs. Afterward, "when all is said and done," she will. "Whatever makes you comfortable, my love." She flushed and went to restack firewood.
- Walking back, Thimble winked at Corvin, who was sharpening his sword.
- He caught Wayne at the dumpster with the Sun Blade. Apple seeds need dirt to grow, and Wayne is stone. A bean wouldn't grow in him either.
- **The lotion.** Wayne rubbed his moisturizer on his backside and began to glow. Thimble tried it and glowed a little. It seems made for Wayne's condition. The knights coming back with their Oinkers takeout saw it.
- Alaric: "I remember why I stopped smoking." Aalyn felt watched (Perception 5), went back into Oinkers for food, heard the bartender say "Back for some of mama?", and retreated to his tent.
- Alaric cast the hut around Aalyn's tent. Corvin watched, looking for components to mess with.
- In the night Alaric saw Wayne tossing and turning with an upset stomach. At dawn Corvin was poking around their camp again.
- Bog was loaded into the caravan passed out, in an Oinkers T-shirt covered in lipstick. Corvin: "Whatever he did, he is very effective at."

**Wayne's sprout**
- Pebbles woke Wayne by tugging at the top of his head. **A sprout with two leaves is growing from his skull.**
- Thimble (Arcana 19): it's magic, and nothing nefarious. Alaric checked his mouth (clean) and pinched a leaf; Wayne felt it.
- Aalyn gave him a black hooded cloak from his costume to hide it.
- When the sun breaks through the clouds, **Wayne draws sustenance from it, like a solar panel.**
- As a kid, Wayne was told that if you eat the seeds they grow inside you and you become the plant.

**On the road**
- Thimble told the others what Luna wants. Aalyn: be careful, and use her liking for Thimble. Alaric: she said "use"; they need to know the danger first. Wayne: when was her brother killed?
- Thimble rode **Rec** like a skateboard beside Luna (the DM allowed it for the trip).
- **Wayne's first spark.** Trying to copy a card trick he knew needed magic (Intelligence save), Wayne thought hard and **his sprout spun like a propeller, raising a gust that blew the cards away.** He didn't notice. Alaric told him to rest his magic until tonight.
- **Dame.** Aalyn floated alongside his horse.
  - Dame was an orphan in Hollowmere and a street kid until an old knight in silver armor found him.
  - Under Luna, the order serves **the Pale Goddess**, a goddess of the moon. There's a chapel to her in the Silver Keep, and Luna spends hours in service at her family's chalet outside the walls. The creed: the Pale Goddess illuminates all and reveals what is true and right.
  - **The moon went dark** one night recently; the order was shaken. "They say those gods are still fighting up there." The timing matches the Bridge King's ascension.
  - Luna joined at about 18 and has been with the order five years. Dame is 28. She has been dealt an unfortunate hand and carries it.
  - Corvin is usually the life of the party. And one wagon carries their secret: **the Silver Berserker.** "We're coming up on the fog."
- **Corvin** to Thimble: "You travel with vermin. They do not deserve a place in our caravan. Nor do you."
- **Amelia**, a motherly knight, to Aalyn: the fog acts like a wall, and silver chimes on the carts ward it off. Never touch it. The Silver Berserker is an old dwarf who punches the mist, though they've never seen him do it.

**The fog**
- A pale wall of rolling fog along the tree line. Luna rang a silver hand chime and it parted into a bubble about 15 feet across. Riders ring chimes along the caravan to keep the bubble whole.
- Thimble (Perception 21) heard that Luna steers by the chime's tone. Then she couldn't read it, and Thimble heard they were drifting off course. Corvin: "Silence."
- Luna's chime started ringing on its own. The caravan halted.
- Thimble's *Detect Magic*: the chimes glow with a pale divine aura. The fog holds **enormously potent magic**: divination, illusion, evocation, maybe enchantment. Its origin can't be told.
- Aalyn flew up to the front. Corvin and Thimble nearly measured, and Aalyn stopped it. Luna grounded the chime. Her answer: faith in the bells.
- **Thimble put on the mask.** Everyone's brand flared. With truesight he could see through the fog: they had taken a fork downhill off the high road.
- Thimble asked the knights to trust him (Persuasion 26, with Tides of Chaos), and Aalyn vouched for him (23). Luna put her faith in them.
- Thimble led them through, past a midway haven with chimes hung in the trees.
- Near the end, a chime out of sync rang in the back. It was **Wayne's bagpipes**, warning of danger.
- **The attack.** Fog cut the caravan in two. Through the mask Thimble saw figures with horns, hooves and animal skulls tearing through the middle wagon and ripping off the chimes. Everyone's brand bled for 1.
- One pounced on Luna and dragged her into the fog, then let her go.
- Alaric's *Scorching Ray* hit one at their wagon, and its form showed before it skidded away: **a Wendigo.**
- The Wendigos ripped away chimes, the knights' and Luna's. Aalyn failed to grab one (Acrobatics 13).
- **Thimble ran into the fog to Luna** and fell unconscious beside her.
- Alaric made Wayne invisible and sent him after a chime with *Mage Hand* scouting. Wayne threw screaming rocks to draw them off, kicked the chime, grabbed it and rang it. A Wendigo clawed him (11, halved) but he kept his feet (luck point), and his opportunity attack **cut off one of its hooves.**
- At the front, Aalyn swished the **wand of conducting** to mimic the chimes' pitch. The Wendigos froze for a moment. Then the fog took him too.
- Alaric put *Rageful Nimbus* on Wayne. Wayne, Large, picked up the unconscious Bog. The chime jammed with a twig and the fog closed. **Alaric fell unconscious.**
- Wayne rang it clear (Performance 20, luck point). Beside a wagon, **a dwarf stood up (the Silver Berserker) and a Wendigo gobbled him up at once.**
- Wayne carried Alaric and wore Bog around his neck like a scarf. One Wendigo's charge left its horns stuck in his chest. He reached the front and slapped Aalyn awake.

**Aalyn's vision**
- A ruined amphitheater, snow falling, Aalyn at center stage. In the seats: **Rosalie**, battered and dead, crackling with dark energy; and at the center **a frail, plague-ridden girl, her face hidden by fog, shivering and alive.**
- Above them, countless sets of eight eyes blinked down. "You could not save." Silk strands wrapped the two. Over his shoulder: "I wonder how many more you will lose." Silken figures filled the empty seats, and blood from his brand spread out across the stage.
- The girl in the center reached a hand out to him for help.
- **Lolth:** "You are not available for offering, yet you bear the brand. Well, I suppose you can still be useful to me." Her challenge: **"There is a spool of my thread, the same thread woven into your bowstring. It is hidden away in the city you approach. You need to find it before the others. Good luck."**
- He woke, and the fog cleared 30 feet around him.
- Aalyn grabbed the other chime. A Wendigo knocked him prone (13); he *Misty Step*ped to Luna and Thimble and woke them with *Cure Wounds*.
- **Wayne (natural 20, crit table 13):** maximum double damage, **48**. His jade dagger went through the Wendigo's skull and into a tree beyond.

**Alaric's vision**
- A gilded marble hall in the elven capital of **Syfaeril**, a mountain rising into the heavens beyond the skylight. He is a small child, kept out of **the meeting of the Heptarion**, the seven houses. Youth isn't respected there. He is unseen, as he was by the Godhand.
- His storybook: his empty, dusty seat at the Fenwyn table; a battle with rats and golems without him; a campfire of silver knights watched from the trees.
- He cast sparkling lights that became playing cards. *(His player: his father played cards with him; it was their time together.)*
- His older brother stopped him, gently. "Not now, baby brother." He read Alaric his favorite story, **"The Fawn and the Moon,"** about a fawn who believed he was invisible until the moon said: "You were never invisible. You were only unconvinced."
- "Little brother, I see you even when you think I don't."
- He woke to Wayne slapping him: "Fen, stop that." The clear radius grew to about 120 feet.

**Thimble's vision**
- He stood outside **Glimmerstone**: spires rising from a mountain-sized geode. The city was colorless, its people a fog-like mass.
- Each blink carried him further up, to his family's manor. Portraits lined the hall, folded hands, judging eyes.
- **The banquet hall where his wild magic once erupted** has been kept as it was, a shrine to his shame, with his younger self at its center. "Oh, you're home at last." "We kept it so you can see what you did to the family."
- The room rotted. He ran down an endless hallway, the portraits turned away, decay following him.
- He remembered the raw magic he felt buried under rock. Lightning of every color broke the walls and floor, and he fell through rock beneath the city's mines into a dome of purple ether and stars, like a universe being made or unmade.
- **"Little spark, come find me, and I will show you what you are."**
- His waking surge pushed the fog back about 600 feet. Sunlight came through.

**Out of the fog**
- Luna woke the same way. Many of the knights were only unconscious; the chime-bearers and some in the wagons were killed. Dame survived with gashes on his chime arm; Corvin survived.
- They woke the survivors and fit them into the two wagons still working.
- Luna to Thimble: "Take my horse. Lead the way." Aalyn knelt with him: "What you did back there was really brave. Do not pin that on yourself."
- Thimble led (natural 20 Survival). Now that they're awake, the clear bubble moves with them; no chimes needed.
- **Rec** had swallowed fog and was leaking it. He blew most of it out (he may keep a little).
- Over the last hill: snowcapped mountains, a river, and a city climbing them in platforms. **Hollowmere.** Session ends.

**After the session**
- The DM used Bog's absence to go into the others' minds. Bog's player sent a two-sentence backstory: he's from the Dragonspine.
