# Session 14: A Little Bit of Everything

**Party:** Level 5 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 13 - The Rat Lord]], [[The Thieves Guild]], [[Tiberius Blackfang]], [[Malrik Greene]], [[The Obsidian Enclave]], [[House Fenwyn]]

> [!important] Played: "A Little Bit of Everything" (Episode 14)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ykSo9cSodAg). The party escaped the flooding sewers, Wayne got paid and got a name from Silas, Aalyn reported to Sir Romulus, and Bog started a riot of children. The party searched Malrik's Solarium, fought its guardian drones, and Alaric found a hidden chest from his parents.

## What Happened at the Table

**Before play**
- Bog got heroic inspiration for his player's real-life thousand-pound club.

**Out of the sewers**
- The blast: Aalyn and Thimble failed their Dexterity saves and took 5 bludgeoning from falling rock.
- **The Rat Lord crawled toward them, only a torso and one arm left.** "Leave my rats alone." Then he died.
- Bog and Wayne pried the hat off his skull (it had melded into his flesh). **Wayne took the Rat Lord's Hat.** Bog took the torso.
- The way in was crushed. The only other door was a sealed one-way slab.
- **The valve puzzle.** Five valves each opened or closed several locks on the main pipe. The party solved it, and the water stopped rising.
- Bog ate the Rat Lord's brain (Sleight of Hand 18). Nothing happened yet.
- Aalyn disguised himself as the Rat Lord and banged on the door crying for his rats. Something shifted in the walls.
- Thimble spotted a manhole grate 15 ft up. Alaric made an ice ladder with *Shape Water*.
- Wayne put on the hat: "True kings command armies. Rat kings command consequences." **The hat did not answer.** He needs to do something first.
- Thimble's *Thunderwave* blew the rocks off the grate. **His chaos die (d4) surged:** spatial magic warps around him, and he blinked down beside Wayne.
- Climbing out, the Rat Lord's blood dripped onto Wayne, and the hat felt more connected to him. Once the fountain's mist washed the blood off, the connection weakened.
- **They came up through a grate by the city-center fountain**, which is now a geyser from the valves they turned. Around 7 or 8 in the evening.

**Wayne and Silas**
- At the Orchid the guards were gone. Downstairs, the barkeep gave Wayne a completion slip to hand in with proof.
- **Silas Holloway** took the Rat Lord's Hat as proof, let Wayne keep it, and paid **50 gp.**
- **The runner who did the Obsidian Enclave job in the ruins is named Calder.** He doesn't talk about his jobs, but **he has a gambling problem.** He frequents the fighting pits as a spectator and "will be the skinniest one there."
- Silas has been out taking precautions because the safe house is compromised, and he looked hard at Wayne.
- **Tiberius works closely with a member of the Obsidian Enclave.** Silas hasn't seen him.
- Wayne didn't spot Calder (Perception 5). It was early in the night.
- **More wooden tokens cost blood.** The barkeep cut the back of his own hand once to show him, and Wayne cut his own stone hand four times. He has four scars and fresh tokens.

**At the fountain**
- Bog played with the children in the fountain, the Rat Lord's torso on his back. Alaric's *Minor Illusion* made it look like a backpack, Thimble kept cleaning the blood with *Prestidigitation*, and Alaric froze ice over the bottom to hold the blood in.
- Alaric made an ice slide for Thimble's spider slippers. Thimble fell on his backside (1 damage).
- **Alaric made dragons out of water and fire, and Bog stormed over.** "What is that?" He offered the kids 2 gp to pin Alaric. Alaric Misty Stepped away and ran, kids chasing him (Stealth 6).
- A seven-year-old said he'd catch Alaric for "Mr. Dragon." Bog told him dragons aren't real. **The boy said his mommy saw a dragon fly by outside the city the other day.**
- **Bog gave daggers to the two biggest kids** and offered gold for the little one. One older boy pinned the seven-year-old at knifepoint, his sister tackled him, and Bog tossed one more gold into the pile. **A quiet boy who went for the coin got slashed across the face and eye.** Bog and Thimble walked away.

**Aalyn and Sir Romulus**
- At the keep, Aalyn reported the wererats and the Rat Lord's death. Romulus will tell Tiberius.
- He told Romulus about Commander Seth and his promises (a raise, early retirement, land). Romulus asked whether he bribed one of his commanders. Aalyn's natural 20 Deception: no money changed hands.
- **Romulus will see what he can do.** Seth is Oakhollow through and through, and there are already rumblings between Romulus's guards and the Oakhollow guard. Aalyn suggested mixing them. Romulus thinks the looming threat is the only thing keeping the city from erupting.
- Romulus: "You have my protection, but that only goes so far in the city. Use it sparingly." He wants a report on Malrik's quarters, and asked whether to send maintenance into the tunnels (Aalyn said guards first).
- Aalyn muttered "Prince Cuckold" on the way out. Two maids heard, and he tipped them each 1 gp to keep it quiet.

**The slashed boy**
- Back at the fountain, Aalyn healed the boy's eye with the golden string on Papa Glabb's lute, without a scar.
- The boy recognized the Off-Branded Misfits patch. Aalyn gave him Birdolomew's trick coin. The boy wants to be just like him, and asked Aalyn to tell Bog he's cool.
- Aalyn picked up the two daggers.

**Finding Alaric**
- Aalyn shouted for Alaric (Performance, natural 20). Alaric heard (26) and came back.
- **Three young Alaric lookalikes** stepped out at the same time. Thimble and Bog fell for the wrong one. Aalyn's test, "What's our wagon's name?", got "Shaniqua," "Wagon" and "Bragthar." Alaric walked off disgusted.
- At the south gate, the night guard had orders to let the Off-Branded Misfits through. The day guard is his brother.

**The Mages Guild and the Solarium**
- The receptionist, **Ammona** (an old human woman), said Malrik's quarters are closed off.
- **Magister Pelen** said Orthus had told him not to allow access. Alaric's natural 20 Persuasion got him "a few minutes" and a silver key with glowing enchantments.
- Alaric's Investigation 26: **the Solarium door handle had been tampered with from the inside.** The room looked untouched otherwise.
- Alaric's Arcana 23 found glyphs around the solar refractory. When he reached into the canister port, a **glyph of warding** went off (Alaric saved and took 15). The door locked and **construct drones** rolled off the bookshelves.
- **Thimble's *Aganazzar's Scorcher*** killed one drone, and his chaos die surged again: flames spiral around him, and he set the floor under a drone alight.
- Alaric's *Scorching Ray* destroyed two more. Bog redirected a spear thrust and broke a drone with it.
- A four-legged drone's gear launcher shot Aalyn out of the air (16 force, unconscious). Alaric caught him with *Feather Fall*.
- **Wayne used his bagpipes in battle for the first time:** a +1 dart for 24 killed it. Thimble's *Fire Bolt* finished the last one. The drones crumbled to dust.
- Thimble chewed up a mushroom and fed it to Aalyn, who came back up.
- Alaric's Investigation 29: three parts of the refractory are broken. **A secret storeroom hides behind the bookshelves.**
- Inside is a chest whose craftsmanship Alaric recognizes. In it are **a letter and a cloak addressed to Alaric, from his parents.**
- No level up.

**After the session**
- Bog flayed the Rat Lord's skull and fitted it as a codpiece on his belt: a spring-loaded jaw, and a penis that pops out of the mouth. The DM will make it a magic item (the "Rat Lord's G-string"). Bog also has painting supplies.
