# Session 46: A Quick One

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]]); [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]] absent
**Ref:** [[Session 45 - Why Is This Here]], [[Durnhollow]], [[Soulvite]]

> [!important] Played: "A Quick One" (Episode 46)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=srX8dLQWMJ8). A short session. Bog breathed lightning into the central conduit to jump-start it, and the Tide Warden formed out of the water with ice mephits and gray oozes. Bog deflected its slam, Thimble stood his ground against its tidal surge on a natural 20, and Wayne stunned it with his psionic dagger. Aalyn touched a water spirit and gained a water form. Ends mid-fight with the oozes merging.

## What Happened at the Table

**The conduit**
- **Alaric was captured by the big robots** ("Pokéballed," as Aalyn put it) and is gone for this session, to cover his player being away.
- In the conduit room: water on the floor, **four pipes pouring water in the colors of the pumps**, and **a central conduit** running floor to ceiling with arcane sigils and channeling rings, **four intakes**, missing some rusted pieces but still functional. It needs a kick.
- **Bog breathed lightning on it (9).** The lights died for five seconds, then came back on. "I think that's my stomach, guys."
- The pumps rushed water in; a swell of water rolled across the floor and coalesced on the conduit into **the Tide Warden**, a water version of the Tempest Warden, furious. **Ice mephits** dripped from the ceiling and **gray oozes** rose from the floor.

**Round 1**
- **The conduit sheens the Warden in a glassy barrier: temporary hit points**, renewed each round.
- It slammed Aalyn (5) and Bog; **Bog's Deflect Attacks** stopped it and sent it back (it saved on a natural 20).
- **Wayne's psionic dagger (23) shattered the sheen.** The conduit charged blue; **a shard of metal with blue energy** sits at the Warden's core, and **small balls of blue light floated out of the pumps.**
- A mephit cast *Fog Cloud* on Wayne and Thimble.
- **Aalyn (Perception) saw one of the conduit's intakes glowing green** and a calm water spirit floating where a pipe pours into the arena. He killed a mephit with Eldritch Blast; **its death burst froze the water around it.** He *Misty Step*ped out.
- **Thimble's 3rd-level *Lightning Bolt*** (Innate Sorcery, a reroll) ran through the Warden at full damage and half to a mephit.
- Two mephits breathed frost on Aalyn (5) and Thimble (8).
- **Bog switched his breath to cold** (silver scales) and punched a mephit apart; its burst barely touched him. He flashed another mephit, which tried to grab it.

**Round 2**
- **The Warden's tidal surge** (Strength DC 16) hit Thimble: **natural 20, he didn't move**, like parting waves at the Glimmerstone shore. A mephit behind him was slammed into the wall.
- **Wayne charged his daggers and hit: failed INT save, stunned until the end of its next turn**, 33 damage. The sheen shattered again.
- **Aalyn touched the water spirit: the raging torrent buff.** He can take a water form (like *Gaseous Form*, in water), swims at double speed, is invisible as a puddle in the water, and adds 1d4 force to his attacks. He killed another mephit (17) and dropped the fog it held, then **sank into the water as a puddle.** He told Wayne by link: the blue orbs have to go into the conduit's intakes.
- Thimble tried to read the medallion like his ring of mind shielding: **it hummed back at him**, nothing more. He swam for another water spirit. The Warden tried to grab him; Wayne: it's stunned.
- A mephit breathed on Wayne (2 after Uncanny Dodge).
- Bog killed another mephit and showed the Warden his penis; the Warden answered with a jet of water and will come for him when it wakes.
- **Two gray oozes, out of sight, began melding together.** Session ends.

**After the session**
- Bog's player is getting married.
- Thimble wants to force-feed a rainbow egg to the Warden, and to use *Magic Jar* on Bog.
- Thimble wants a camera to photograph every boss.
