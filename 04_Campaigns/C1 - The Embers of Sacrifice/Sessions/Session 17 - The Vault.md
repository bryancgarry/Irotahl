# Session 17: The Vault

**Party:** Level 5 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 16 - Action and Apologies]], [[Tiberius Blackfang]], [[Malrik Greene]], [[The Obsidian Enclave]], [[The Volkarr]], [[Baron Fern]]

> [!important] Played: "The Vault" (Episode 17)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=w0yPkWqOXbU). The party collected Bog and reported to Sir Romulus, who sent them after Edith Veilheart's vault. Aalyn played a distraction while Wayne and Bog went in invisible. Wayne cracked the vault and found the wooden mask, a Fern pendant, and Malrik's logs. Ends with the butler Buro sprinting down the stairs toward them.

## What Happened at the Table

**Bog wakes**
- On the walk back, Thimble lied that he checked Bog was alive (Deception 24), but Aalyn saw through it. Thimble had shoved food down an unconscious man's throat after "gently" throwing him off a roof.
- **Bog's dream.** He was a hungry little gecko in a big tree. Mean people kept shaking his trees and cutting them down. He grew up into something like the humans (with a skin condition), knocked down the tree-cutters, and planted trees until no gecko went hungry.
- He punched up out of the haystack by the Orchid with a headache, a long rest, and **4 temporary hit points.** The Orchid's damage is roped off.
- Thimble told him he'd tried to feed him a mushroom on the roof. "Little dude gave me a mushroom." They're friends now.
- The party told Bog only that he'd been at the bar, and then things blew up.

**The keep**
- **Tiberius** stood guard outside Rosalie's room. Wayne showed him the Enclave note.
  - Tiberius doesn't work for the Enclave. "My allegiance is to the Darken family."
  - He says Darkstone has its own Enclave adviser, the Enclave keeps tabs on everyone, and they're no threat.
- **Rosalie's dark energy is about five times stronger** than when the party last saw her.
- **Sir Romulus.**
  - The party reported Malrik's traps and the destroyed solar refractory. Alaric is keeping the battery.
  - Romulus will send raw materials to the Solarium and have people look for documentation, so Alaric can try a repair. He knows who Alaric Fenwyn is, and winked.
  - Tiberius admired Alaric's cloak.
- **Edith Veilheart.** Romulus knows the name. She's among the nobles pushing back on his temporary rule and thinks him too young, though she hasn't been abrasive.
- Romulus's historians found no plans of her estate, only its location in Noble's Rise. The nobility won't cooperate, so the job calls for subtlety and stealth. He can't risk another falling out with them.
- "Try not to take all day. We're running out of time."

**Noble's Rise**
- **Bog has wings.** He flies around town every morning on his "draconic flight," and nobody had ever noticed. He insists dragons are still make-believe.
- The estate is a modest two-story house with a walled courtyard, guards loitering, and no ground-floor windows.
- **The plan.**
  - Alaric cast *Invisibility* on Wayne and Bog (concentration, one hour) and sent his invisible space guppy along. Wayne linked minds with Thimble and Bog.
  - Aalyn loaned Wayne his boots, but took them back since they'd need attuning.
- A crowd had noticed them arguing in an alley (Perception 18).
- **Aalyn's distraction.** He stood on a bench in the square, set down his tip pouch, and sang a country song about being terrible at stealth (natural 20 Performance). It made 80 gp.
  - It also held **the butler** for several minutes, until he checked his pocket watch, swore, and ran back inside.

**Inside the estate**
- Bog flew up and anchored his rope on the chimney (Wayne needed a minute to find the invisible rope). Bog then flew down to the courtyard.
- Wayne dropped onto a ledge (Acrobatics 25) and picked the lock of a round stained-glass window with a heart in it (26).
- Inside is a grand entrance with an interior balcony and a staircase going up and down. Voices came from a room on the first floor.
- Wayne checked the four upstairs doors. Most keyholes are covered from inside.
- **The master bedroom.** He picked the lock on the second try (16).
  - In the nightstand: **the other half of the heart key.** It fits with the half from Malrik's storeroom into one whole key.
- **The basement.**
  - A storage room with a desk and ledger leads half a level down to an open tiled room with an ornate rose-gold and steel vault door at the far end.
  - The tiles are pressure plates. Wayne rolled a torch one tile ahead at a time, stepping where it pressed down, and reached the vault in about 30 seconds. *(He later realized this set off a silent alarm.)*
- **The vault.**
  - The full key opened the outer panel. Behind it are four dials, each 0 to 9.
  - Wayne's natural 20 Perception on the room:
    - The ledger lists many names, including Malrik's but not Edith's.
    - There are files on people, and the vault holds many people's belongings.
    - Each person has their own PIN, and Edith's key half is the master that unlocks the panel. The code is nowhere in the house.
  - Bog flew down to Wayne. Alaric failed to see through the guppy's three eyes.
  - **Buro came down.** He walked the correct path through the tiles, checked the vault, and left without spotting them.
- **The code.** Thimble suggested the member ID from the Enclave note, and Alaric remembered it: **1497**. It worked. Gears swapped a vault room into place, and Wayne hauled the door open (Athletics 15).
- **Inside.**
  - Several chests. **Wayne took 400 gp.**
  - In a wooden box on cushions: **a wooden mask** of dark, twisting grain, smooth to the touch and radiating magic. Wayne's History 14: **it's like the masks worn by the Volkarr** back in Drak'mor.
  - In the same box: **a Fern family heirloom**, an octagonal silver pendant with an emerald center.
  - Also: **Malrik's redacted logs.**
- Bog saw **Buro sprinting down the stairs.**

**After the session**
- The DM warned that the arc is nearing its end and things are about to get dangerous. Players should think about a backup character.
- The DM confirmed that Aalyn's new pact is to let **Wayne** lose the artifact he's carrying. Aalyn doesn't know his original pact; that's intentional.
- Buro's attractiveness roll: 20, the hottest character so far.
