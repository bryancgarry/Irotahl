# Session 38: Digging For Information

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 37 - Back From The Hunt]], [[Hollowmere]], [[Talia|Talia Shay]], [[Vashara]], [[Luna au Vermillion|Luna]], [[The Holy Chain Knights]], [[Jackmerius Tacktheratrix|Jack]], [[Soulvite]], [[The Demonomicon]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "Digging For Information" (Episode 38)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CyE35SIYC54). At V's, Bog chafed a patron bloody against the pole, snapped a janitor's neck and hid the body, and was taken away under *Dominate Person* by the owner. Aalyn healed the patron, met Miss V (Vashara), signed her contract without reading it, and got four names of missing patrons to look into. In the Celestial Archives, Alaric studied dragon hatchlings, Wayne found a myth of a dwarf named Jack with soulvite daggers, and Thimble and Wayne slipped into the restricted section. Thimble's *Blight* destroyed a warded chest and freed a Tome of the Demonomicon; grabbing a book by hand set off the alarm. Luna covered for them. Ends with Luna asking, "What's a devil?"

## What Happened at the Table

**Recap**
- The players gave the recap. Alaric's player added Salvatore's name, the pear scheme, Archivist Willamir and the Chain Lords above him.

**Aalyn and Talia**
- In the back room at V's, Talia: "You look good." Aalyn dropped *Disguise Self*; she likes him "as you are, true to yourself."
- He asked for clients who might know about what he's hunting. Talia doesn't hear much business, but **the masked newcomer has caused a stir**: he's trying to fly under the radar and people are interested.
- **Miss V asked Talia to bring Aalyn.** Talia didn't want to.
- The old patron Aalyn hexed "couldn't get to that point." Most patrons pay to watch, not touch.

**Bog on the floor**
- Bored of Gorgonzola, Bog sat by a well-dressed patron (around 50 to 55, black hair going gray, no mask), dipped himself in the man's wine and cheese, and asked where the owner was. Miss V rarely comes out "unless something specifically calls for it," such as a disturbance.
- Bog **grappled him onto the center pole (27), stripped him, tied his hands to the pole with his own pants** and ground against him until his scales drew blood. Bog spat the blood at a worker: "Either you get her or you get on this pole with this guy." Three workers went for the owner.
- The music stopped. Gorgonzola whispered, "Come on, do that to me in the back." Bog dropped the man, naked and bloody on stage, and spat a gold piece at him.
- In a back room, Bog (Perception) saw **Gorgonzola hide something under the table**. "I shouldn't kill you."
- He followed her to a hidden backroom bar, then wandered into another room where **a janitor was cleaning a couch alone. Bog snapped his neck (13 damage), rolled the body under the couch and walked back** (Stealth 11). Aalyn didn't hear it.
- About three minutes later **a woman came through the curtain**: regal, revealing leather with metal adornments, black hair, **plum skin, red eyes**. "Hello, love. I'm going to need you to come with me." Bog resisted, so she **cast *Dominate Person*. He failed (6) and was charmed**, duration unknown. He went with her still naked and without his drink.

**Miss V**
- Talia went cold mid-kiss: a commotion. In the main room the patron sat tied to the pole. **Aalyn cut him free with his boot dagger and healed him with *Healing Word*** ("I'm gonna heal your little ass cheeks").
- **Miss V** watched from the curtain and knelt to check on him. Aalyn (attraction roll 7) kept his cool for Talia. She took his hand and led him downstairs to a private office with its own pole.
- He sniffed the drink (Investigation 12: liquor), drank it, and passed a CON save (19). She ticked something on a piece of paper.
- Talia talked about him; when she said he was in the city, Miss V had to meet him. **She gave her name: Vashara.**
- She'll share what her halls hear for a donation. Aalyn offered free performances instead. **She slid over a contract and he signed without reading it.** She folded it in thirds. "It's just the fine print, honey. Workers' rights and all that."
- **Four patrons have gone missing** lately. She's half glad (low-lifes, tips were down), but people whisper that something nefarious happens at V's. **She wrote four names on a slip; Aalyn doesn't know them.** Her associates will dig up where they were last seen, and **Talia will deliver the details.**
- About the man on stage: "Your guess is as good as mine." "Bring some friends."
- Aalyn warned her, for the place's sake, to keep a scaly guy out.
- Afterward he told Talia he signed a contract to perform. Her look worried him. Alone, he curled up and tried to call his patron ("Please God, scary..."): no answer. He found **5 gp in the couch**.

**The Celestial Archives**
- **Alaric (bestiary):** found *A Natural History of Draconic Lineage* and, by an author named **Quellin**, ***On the Raising of Dragon Hatchlings***.
  - Silver dragon eggs favor cold, damp, low light. **Bonding begins before hatching.**
  - **"There have been no recorded cases of adult dragons abandoning their search for their egg should it be lost."**
  - **Dragon eggs carry an arcane signature** that dragons sense by instinct and that the attuned can feel up close. The book says nothing about hiding it. Alaric: "Let's hope we don't find mommy again."
- **Wayne (arcana, Investigation 28):** a book on magical materials, and in one book a picture of **a dwarven warrior named Jack, hooded and bearded, wielding legendary daggers of *soulvite*** with a telekinetic link. Wayne and Thimble read it together.
- Wayne tried to pull a book from Thimble's hand: it slipped, his nose bled, his sprout drooped.
- **The restricted section** is an iron-barred circle in the middle with a keyhole, a spiral staircase up a spire, and moonlight from above. Willamir writes at a desk around the corner.
- Alaric cast *Detect Magic*: abjuration on the gate, and magic emanating from the spire. Wayne found **a *Glyph of Warding*** on the door. Neither knew if teleporting through would set it off.
- **Thimble and Wayne teleported through the bars** (Thimble first, then Wayne after him). Alaric looked away, sent Rec to watch Willamir, and signed to them to check the spire.
- **Alaric and Willamir:** Alaric asked about the Gordra Dominion. Willamir noticed his Fenwyn cloak. Syfaeril draws magic down from the Feywild through **the Heavenspear**; Drak'mor has the opposite, **"the world abyss," [[The Rikk Borgar]]**, a sinkhole that funnels this plane's energy down and gives the ley lines their flow. **The Chain Lords have locked those books in the restricted section.** Then he said he'd spoken too freely.
- **Top of the spire:** a glass dome, moonlight bounced off mirrors (like the Mages Guild's sunlight room in Oakhollow), fold-out scribe desks. Thimble felt strange in the moonlight. Wayne found ***Soul Marks and Abyssal Bindings*** by **Darius Lightbane** (unknown to them). Thimble pulled it to his hand by magic (natural 20).
- **In a glass case on a table sat a wooden chest with six silver locks.** Wayne's dagger, tossed through the barrier to Alaric, slowed as if through jelly. Willamir came over; Alaric covered with a book on the floor and a big apology (Deception), and got 10 more minutes.
- **Thimble's surge (85)** heightened his *Detect Magic*: the case is wrapped in **holy, divine wards**. His necrotic surge corroded three of the six locks; no alarm.
- **Thimble cast *Blight* (his first 4th-level spell, rerolled twice).** The table withered, the glass fell and shattered (gravity, not magic, so no trigger), the box and locks turned to ash. Out of the soot floated **a Tome of the Demonomicon**: not the introductory volume zero from Malrik's vault but one of the five tomes. **A golden hand on the cover, three fingers raised.**
- Thimble then grabbed **the latest volume of the charter of the Holy Silver Chain Knights** (about its officers, for Luna's brother) **by hand. The lights went red: the alarm.**

**Caught**
- Willamir, sleeves rolled up: "You all have violated the trust of the Chain Lords." Wayne teleported out to his dagger in Alaric's hand. Thimble walked down whistling: "I think they forgot to pay the light bills."
- **Luna came down.** Willamir: "The Chain Lords will be summoning you." She asked if they took anything. Through the bars, **Thimble told her it was for "you know what and you know who," and he was doing it for them** (Persuasion with advantage). She covered for them.
- **Alaric cast *Invisibility* on his bag of holding** and handed it to Thimble while scolding him. Thimble stashed the tome and the Lightbane book, then cleaned up: *Mending* on the glass, a similar box from the room tucked in the corner, duct tape.
- Luna and Willamir spoke quietly; he opened the gate. Alaric bowed both their heads in apology.
- **The whole Silver Keep was on lockdown.** At the front door they met Aalyn coming back from V's. Alaric walked past him, disappointed.
- Luna: **"We might have to accelerate our plans, as I will be summoned to the Chain Lords quite soon."**
- Aalyn asked Luna about Miss V. She doesn't frequent gentleman's clubs. Aalyn admitted he **signed a contract without reading it, and thinks Miss V might be a devil**: glowing red eyes.
- In the room, **Bog and his belongings were gone.**
- Luna: **"What's a devil?"**

**After the session**
- The players joke Bog is the Dark Urge, and that Thimble has a god complex and a growing lust for power.
- The DM confirmed *Soul Marks and Abyssal Bindings* is just a book. Alaric plans to keep chilling the egg at night.
