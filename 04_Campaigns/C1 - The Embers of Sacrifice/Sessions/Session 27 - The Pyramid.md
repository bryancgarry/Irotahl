# Session 27: The Pyramid

**Party:** Level 6 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 26 - The Straight Arrow]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "The Pyramid" (Episode 27)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4L5M3zpdQ7k). A puzzle dungeon inside Thimble's bean pyramid. The party bent a beam of magical sunlight through four colored crystals to open the inner hall, then raised four pillars: a sand-trap lever pulled by Aalyn's unseen servant, a balance-the-water scale with two explosive seeds Wayne pulled out, a wind-chime room solved by acrostic, and a serpent-path floor that dropped giant snakes on them. Wayne rolled natural 20 after natural 20. Ends as the beam opens the burial chamber and a mummy rises with four flaming skeletons.

## What Happened at the Table

**The pyramid**
- The bean grew into **a large sandstone pyramid**, pushing the party back 60 to 100 feet. Birds fled. Everyone felt a wash of malice or dark energy from it.
- Thimble cast *Mage Armor* (wild magic die now a d6).

**The sundial room**
- A beam of magical sunlight comes from **a great sundial** in the center of the room. It can rotate 360 degrees on a runed track.
- Around the room are **mirrors** (reflect 90 degrees) and **beam splitters** on movable stone pedestals, and **four crystals**: blue, purple, green and red. A gemstone sits above the far door. The beam picks up each crystal's color as it passes through.
- Alaric (History 17) has never read of anything like it. As a youth among the high elves he played a board game like it: a king that fires a laser, and mirror pieces to bounce it.
- Thimble's "dark vibes check" (Arcana 19): nothing malicious in this room, but magic he can't pinpoint. The runes are decorative.
- Alaric and Aalyn moved the pieces. All four crystals lit, then the gemstone lit three-quarters and powered down. Thimble noticed the beam had purple, green and red in it but no blue.
- Bog sat on the sundial drinking beer. Aalyn and Alaric turned it to fire into the blue crystal first. The beam took blue, purple, green and red in turn and **the door opened.**
- Behind them, the misty entrance became a stone wall.

**The inner hall**
- A long corridor with **four pillars** down its center and **four side doors.** The beam runs down the hall and is blocked by the first pillar. Another blue gemstone sits over the door at the end.
- Wayne checked for traps with Rec's help. Thimble lit a torch with *Control Flame* for him.

**First door: the sand room**
- A long room with sand piled in the corners and a lever at the end. Wayne threw his torch in (Perception 15).
- **Aalyn cast *Unseen Servant*** and sent it to pull the lever, grabbing Wayne and Thimble by their shirts to keep them back. (Athletics 18.) Sand poured from the ceiling, the doors slammed, and the servant waved goodbye as it was buried.
- **The first pillar rose.**

**Second door: the scale**
- The door was trapped "like a ring of C4." Wayne (natural 20 Investigation, 35 Sleight of Hand with his gloves) reached in with psionic energy and pulled **an explosive seed** out of it.
- Inside: a pool fed from the ceiling, **a 3-gallon bucket and a 5-gallon bucket**, and **a scale** with sand worth four gallons of water on one side.
- An Investigation check showed that a wrong answer sets off **another explosive seed** in the scale, and that if it blows the pillar won't rise.
- **Rec** slipped into the scale's gears and guided Wayne's fingers. Wayne (DC 25, 27) pulled the second seed. **Wayne has two explosive seeds.**
- Thimble half-filled the big bucket. Alaric took over with *Shape Water*, filled it "80 percent," and it was too much: the scale clunked down. With the trap disarmed, he siphoned water off until the scale leveled. **The second pillar rose.**

**Third door: the wind chimes**
- Wayne picked the drafty lock. A large room with **seven brass wind chimes**, floor to ceiling, six in a circle and one in the center, each with a picture carved beneath it: a **wagon, skull, golem, chicken, barrel, dragon**, and a fan in the center. Wind circles the room.
- Wayne (Perception 17) found the line carved spiraling down the chimes: ***"Golden cinders guide brave souls west."***
- Thimble's war gong set every chime ringing and the wind got stronger. Wrong chimes raise the wind; right ones calm it. Wayne and Thimble held their footing (Strength saves).
- Alaric worked it out from the first letters and called the order: **golem, chicken, golem, barrel, skull, wagon.** Wayne and Thimble struck the chimes, and Alaric's *Mage Hand* rang the wagon last.
- The wind turned harmonious and lifted Wayne and Thimble off their feet. **Both have advantage on their next Performance check.**
- A hidden room opened with a lever. Pulled. **The third pillar rose** under Bog, who had been leaning on it.
- Thimble thinks they should make a mixtape. He knows a guy back in Glimmerstone.

**Fourth door: the serpent paths**
- Bog opened the door himself. A mist of gas hit his face and did nothing (poison resistance). He tripped a wire on the way in. **Buzz saws** slid out of the walls and Wayne corkscrewed between them (Dexterity save).
- A long room of **colored serpentine paths** painted on pressure tiles. Over the arch: ***"Follow the serpent that travels when the sun kisses the horizon."***
- Wayne (natural 20 Perception) saw **hatches** in the ceiling and walls and **shed snakeskin** in the corners. The walls and ceiling are pressure tiles too.
- Alaric cast *Fly* on Aalyn, who held the hatches shut from above. Alaric waited in the hall.
- Thimble walked the **pink** path, jumping where the blue path crossed over it, and reached a dead end.
- Wayne sprinted the **blue** path on instinct. A shock of energy ran under the tiles and a false wall opened on **a lever.** He pulled it.
- The fourth pillar rose but **stuck two feet short.** Hatches opened and **two giant constrictor snakes** dropped from the ceiling, with **venomous snakes** from the side vents.
- *(After the session the DM explained: the colors match the crystals, the sundial is a clock, and sunset or sunrise points to the blue crystal. Following the wrong path armed the trap for when the lever was pulled.)*

**The snake fight**
- **Aalyn** (natural 20 initiative) floated up and killed a venomous snake with *Eldritch Blast*. He gave Thimble Bardic Inspiration and floated back toward the hall: "Wayne, big snakes."
- A venomous snake bit Thimble (8). A constrictor **grappled Thimble** (9). The other constrictor missed Bog and **bit itself** on a natural 1.
- **Thimble** used Innate Sorcery and cast a 3rd-level *Thunderwave* (DC 17): one venomous snake melted into the stone, and the constrictor was pushed 10 feet but **still grappled him.**
- **Bog** drank a potion of healing (13). He gave two venomous snakes "Indian burns," **twisting them in half**, and tucked the pieces into his waistband for later.
- **Wayne**, still at the lever, sneaked a thrown dagger into a constrictor for 19 and hid again.
- The constrictor crushed Thimble (10) and bit Aalyn (7). The other grappled Bog (12). **Bog deflected it** and sent 11 back.
- **Thimble's feat.** Thimble's player had swapped his 4th-level feat from an Intelligence increase to **Fey Touched**. The DM allowed it, and the swap is permanent.
- **Thimble** cast *Dissonant Whispers*. The snake fled and let him go. He asked Wayne if he'd pulled the lever. "I pulled it twice."
- **Bog** dove into the other constrictor's mouth and **punched his way out through it**, killing it.
- **Wayne** killed the fleeing constrictor with a limp dagger throw (doubled sneak attack). He doesn't like snakes.
- Wayne's dice: natural 20s and 19s all session after a long drought.

**The burial chamber**
- Inside the dead snake Thimble found **a very old, rusted iron dagger.**
- The last pillar rose. The beam hit the gemstone over the final door and it opened on **a burial chamber**: four coffins in the corners and, in the center, **a huge upright sarcophagus** of stone and gold with a gemstone on its forehead.
- The beam hit that gemstone. A **mummy** stepped out and raised its arms. Motes of light went to each corner, and **four skeletons** rose wreathed in **red, blue, purple and green flame.** Behind iron bars in side rooms, **more skeletons** began to rise. Session ends.

**After the session**
- Bog wants to make snake nunchucks. His eyeball collection is growing, and he keeps it on his belt.
