# Session 7: The Goose is Loose

**Party:** Level 4 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 6 - The Path to Godspire Ruins]], [[Lithoweaves]], [[The Tall Oak Forest]], [[Bragthar]]

> [!important] Played: "The Goose is Loose" (Episode 7)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=a9YHIu-KxEQ). A puzzle-boss against the Godspire lithoweave, played in theater of the mind. The party tuned and held its five rings, lost Bragthar and the goose, and Wayne and Alaric gave up part of their life force to collapse the core. The calcification stopped. Everyone leveled to 5.

## What Happened at the Table

**The lithoweave**
- With the illusion gone, the hilltop was a crater of stone and petrified roots in heavy, silent air. Above it floated a machine bigger than Bragthar: **five rings** of intertwined stone and metal, carved with glowing runes, each spinning its own way around a floating **black core** that pulsed like a heartbeat and bent the light like a black hole. Its rays scanned and rewrote the land.
  1. Outermost: emerald, earthy glyphs of bone and dirt.
  2. Sapphire blue with golden markings, flickering lights that never look the same twice.
  3. Silver, burning with golden fire that purifies rather than heats.
  4. Deep purple, blinking in and out of existence (out of time).
  5. Innermost: blinding white, unstable, primal, feeding the core's energy to the other rings.
- The core is an amalgam of the same **black ichor** seen on the forest floor, dripping into the ground. It seemed to watch them and tug at their minds and bodies.
- Alaric's Arcana 23 alerted it. It erased what he had learned, tore the hilltop up into a floating island and began to rise.

**How it worked**
- Alaric's History 24: this is like the solar refractory, perhaps the ancient original that inspired it. To reach the core you **tune** each ring's frequency, **synchronize** them, then **hold** them in place.
- Each round the party acted, then the machine answered with a pulse. Pulses forced Con saves (restrained or halved movement; Wayne immune, Bog with advantage), or a d20 per magic item, with a 10 or lower rendering it mundane for an hour.

**The rings**
- **Emerald ring.** Aalyn's Melodic Surge hit it and threw him off the island (he hovered on his boots). Thimble's war gong (23) calmed it. Bog's singing (12) and Wayne's squeaky bagpipes failed and drew energy blasts. A lava moat appeared. Aalyn's natural 20 Performance on the lute found its resonant frequency and stilled it completely; the lava cooled to rock.
- **Sapphire ring.** Thimble's gong (19) sent back a psionic wave: disadvantage on skill checks for everyone. Alaric's Minor Illusion (Arcana 10) slipped. Wayne had Bragthar press a psionic dagger into it (Dex 17), which tuned it, and Wayne and Bog held it down together (16).
- **Silver ring.** Aalyn's Prestidigitation lullaby (21) fed its fire, and he took 9 radiant. Thimble's Fog Cloud did nothing. Bog beaned it with a rock (natural 20) and took 11 force. Alaric's illusion of calm, laminar flame (19) settled it, and Wayne held it (Strength 16), taking 10 fire.
- **Purple ring.** Wayne's natural 20 Perception saw it blinking out of existence. He told Bragthar telepathically to charge when it blinked. Bragthar (Int 2) mistimed it and **vanished**, severing Wayne's psionic link. The ring threw the party back 20 feet and **reset the two rings before it**, undoing two rounds.
- **The goose.** The next pulse warped reality around Wayne's bag. The goose (Salvatore Pearcetti) grew to the size of an adult dragon, stepped on Wayne (7 force) and flew off toward Oakhollow, not happy. Bog yelled "the goose is loose" and maintains that dragons are just big geese.
- **Redoing the rings.** Wayne's psionic dagger retuned the sapphire ring (22) and Alaric's Mage Hand held it. Aalyn heard that the silver ring was divine and played a hymn, but his Religion 7 didn't land. Alaric's illusion of water **became real water** (24) and put out the fire, and Wayne held it. For the purple ring, Aalyn (21) heard that two actions had to land at the same moment. Aalyn and Thimble played together (24, and 15 or better), finally in rhythm after all their beef, and Bog held it.
- **Item pulses:** Alaric's deck of cards, Wayne's bagpipes, Aalyn's boots and Thimble's Ring of Mind Shielding each went mundane for an hour.
- **White ring.** Two guardian constructs stepped out of the fog. Aalyn's Melodic Surge did nothing. Thimble's Shatter blew one apart (it was an illusion), and Alaric's Minor Illusion deleted the other's core. Bog (Perception 19) found the ring primal, like Thimble's wild magic. Its white lightning parted around Bog, so he reached in (Con 16) and tickled it still, immune while he kept tickling. The lightning was drawn to Thimble, so he stayed back. Alaric (illusion cape, Deception 17) and Wayne (psionic teleport, Con 14) slipped through.

**The core**
- A black blob of ichor, metal and black-hole energy around a crystalline prism. Wayne's necklace was harmonizing with it.
- Wayne (Perception 15): to stop it, something must be given up, like emotion, willpower, a magic item, hit points or memories.
- Alaric offered the pile of Wayne's pebbles he had been collecting in his Bag of Holding. Wayne had no idea. They settled on their own life force instead and joined hands, Titanic style (Charisma 13 and 18).
- Their life force poured in. The core imploded and swallowed the rings, and as it collapsed it burst into a field of stars. **Watchful eyes peered down at Wayne, flanked by hair made of snakes.** Then it was gone.
- The floating island fell about 70 feet. Bog waved the aarakocra feather (Arcana natural 20) and floated down. Thimble tried to wild-surge his way out, didn't, and ate a mushroom (5 HP) on the way. The rest took 22 bludgeoning; Wayne and Alaric teleported at the last second and took half.
- **The calcification of the Tall Oak Forest has stopped.** What was already petrified stays stone: "stone trees," plural.

**After**
- Everyone leveled to **5**.
- The DM had Kevin (Wayne) roll a d12 before leaving: 11. Its purpose wasn't said.
