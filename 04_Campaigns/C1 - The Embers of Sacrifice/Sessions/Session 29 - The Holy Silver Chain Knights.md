# Session 29: The Holy Silver Chain Knights

**Party:** Level 6 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 28 - Emperor Hawk Tuah and The Goon Generals]], [[Luna au Vermillion]], [[Corvin Hale]], [[The Holy Chain Knights]], [[Hollowmere]]

> [!important] Played: "The Holy Silver Chain Knights" (Episode 29)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=oIyAlj-Cfbw). The riders were Imperator Luna of the Holy Silver Chain Knights, on her way to Oakhollow to deal with the rats the party had already killed. Aalyn's brand, Wayne's Rat Lord's Hat and a live rat nearly started a fight. Thimble pulled rank as a Danglesworth, and the knights took the party with them toward Hollowmere. At camp Luna sat with Thimble, and Aalyn rapped the tale of the Rat Lord. The next day a stray magic missile broke the wheel of Barnaby, a wandering wizard trader, and the party went shopping. The caravan stopped at Oinkers, a roadside bar in the village of Old Ham Tug. Ends with a wild magic smoke circle out back, and Thimble chasing after Luna.

## What Happened at the Table

**Recap**
- The DM recapped from memory. Alaric's notes: the flaming skeleton gooned with his left hand; the rat cannon; Bog's 61 damage in one turn on the mummy, which they think is the campaign's single-target record.
- It's midday on the fourth day of travel, about halfway to Hollowmere.

**The riders**
- **Luna** rides a white mare: white cloak, silver gauntlets, blonde hair in buns, emerald eyes. Two knights flank her in silver half-plate and chain, **Dame** and **Corvin**. A caravan of three wagons follows.
- "Hello, travelers. My name is Luna. I am with the Holy Silver Chain Knights." She asked whether they had seen the mass of burning trees.
- Aalyn bowed and introduced the Off-Branded Misfits. He told her a burning forest has been following them. She has heard tales of it but never seen it.
- Aalyn and Alaric asked to talk off the road, behind some trees (Persuasion 29). Luna told Dame they were no trouble. Corvin stayed on the road.

**On the road with Corvin**
- Corvin took Thimble for a child until Wayne lifted him to eye level.
- **Thimble has heterochromia: a purple right eye and a caramel-brown left eye.** His pocket watch carries the family mark. Corvin recognized him as a Danglesworth: "Usually you travel in much more sophisticated ways."
- Thimble called Wayne and Bog "my boys." Corvin gave Bog beer from his canteen. "Don't tell the Imperator."
- **Corvin Hale**, proud knight of the Holy Silver Chain Knights. Luna is the **Imperator**. She stays at the **Vermillion Chateau** on the outskirts of Hollowmere, her family's summer home. She hails from **Vermillion Bay** and turned 23 this cycle.
- Glimmerstone has the **world tournament** coming up.
- Thimble's Persuasion got it out of him: they've been contracted to deal with **a rat infestation in Oakhollow.** Wayne took off his Rat Lord's Hat.

**Behind the trees**
- Luna asked how Oakhollow fares, and how the princess is: "Is she not handling the marriage well?" Aalyn told her Rosalie is dead. Her face dropped.
- Aalyn told her about the mile-wide hole in the city and the Gorak Dominion at its doorstep. Then he told her the story up to the cathedral: the stone trees, the wedding, Malrik's betrayal, Rosalie's mother turned to a demon beneath the city.
- He asked whether she wields divine magic. She does. He tore his shirt open and showed her **his brand**: "Does this symbol look familiar to you?"

**The scan**
- Luna froze. Dame shook her and they ran back to the road. Corvin, hand on his hilt: "Imperator, scan them."
- She crushed a component and cast gold and silver magic over them. **Her eyes locked on Wayne's hat, then on Bog's Skullcup.** Bog offered to show her.
- **Wayne (natural 20 Religion):** Luna is like a cleric. The Holy Silver Chain Knights deal with monsters and **lycanthropes**. She was judging how far the hat had corrupted him, and whether he wields it or it wields him.
- Luna: "You are not right. You wield dirty magic." Dame: "We seek truth before silver." Luna: "Stand ready. We strike only if provoked."
- Thimble's Persuasion (22, with advantage and heroic inspiration) earned them the chance to explain.
- **Alaric's "sleight of sniff."** Squeezing past Luna, he tried to smell her and choked on the air (Sleight of Hand 10 against her passive Perception). Dame grabbed him, and Luna drew a dagger from under her armor skirt. "I'm being generous with letting you explain yourselves. Do not test me again."
- They told her they had killed the Rat Lord and his wererats, and that the hat and the cup came from him. Aalyn vouched that Wayne's only ailment is being stone, and that he doesn't turn into a rat.
- **Wayne pulled a rat out of his bag.** The knights drew their swords. "You've been changed by it. We cannot allow you to live."
- **Thimble pulled rank.** "Do you know who you're talking to? You're going to look down at me, respect my name, and then force your beliefs on me about who I hang out with?" (Intimidation 24, with Aalyn's Bardic Inspiration and Tides of Chaos). His voice cracked, and his wild magic erased the crack.
- Luna: "Corvin, please. You are Danglesworth? I am Vermillion. We might not know each other, but perhaps we come from the same cloth." Thimble and Alaric's Persuasion (11 and 26) settled it without violence.
- Luna: if they speak the truth, the mission is done, "plus we have other things to worry about." Thimble and Alaric heard her whisper that **he is branded.**
- Bog (natural 1 Sleight of Hand) nearly set off his Skullcup while fiddling with it, then caught the shot with Deflect Attacks and jammed it back in.
- Luna invited the party to ride with them to Hollowmere. Of the burning trees: those flames appear to those who need them, **thanks to the Wild Mother's grace.**
- She asked Wayne and Bog not to use their magic items around the wagons. Riding off, she gave Aalyn and Thimble a quick side glance (Thimble used Bend Luck).
- Corvin threw his now-dirtied canteen at them as he rode off. Thimble: "That's why I never mesh well with those religious types."
- Aalyn: be on our best behavior; they could get a carriage to Glimmerstone out of this.

**With the caravan**
- They ride in the back supply wagon, with rations and gear. Dame rides alongside. They're free to leave, but supervised (Insight 9).
- Aalyn sent Luna a *Message*: sorry for stepping on toes, happy to answer any questions. "Noted."
- A short rest in the wagon. Aalyn gave Alaric and Thimble heroic inspiration.
- The knights move twice as fast. Hollowmere is two or three days away by caravan, four on foot.

**Camp**
- Alaric ritual-cast *Leomund's Tiny Hut*. The knights served stew around the fire.
- **Luna sat with Thimble.** He saved them a trip, and she thanked him.
  - She's **Luna au Vermillion of Vermillion Bay**, born, raised and trained there. She came to Hollowmere **traveling with her brother** one summer and stayed, and found family in the knights.
  - She asked about Wayne ("He likes to call me his father"), and about **Pebbles**: "She's not normal." Thimble (Insight 18) saw that Pebbles had caught her interest in the scan too. For now it's curiosity, not malice. She wouldn't meet her yet.
  - Thimble: "A lot can be accomplished if you just follow your dreams." Natural 20. She flushed and dropped her soup.
  - Leaving to clean up: **"Would you sacrifice anything to be free?"** Thimble, by *Message*: "You haven't even learned a sliver of my story."
- **Aalyn's rap.** With the wand of conducting beatboxing and Papa Glabb's lute, he performed the song he wrote after the Rat Lord fight. The knights banged their pans.
- Alaric taught Wayne to count with cards, avoiding the face cards. He gave Wayne a regular deck to practice with.
- **Corvin and the hut.** Late that night Corvin inspected the hut and asked to come in. Alaric said no; the spell can't be changed once cast. Corvin said that after Aalyn's performance, someone named Yanick tried to talk the Imperator into stopping at **Oinkers** tomorrow. Alaric is from Syfaeril and doesn't know it. Corvin left, put out.
- Alaric and Wayne kept watch (18 and 19); nothing.

**Barnaby**
- About six hours into the next day, Wayne practiced a magic missile out the wagon window. The caravan screeched to a halt.
- The missile had blown the wheel off **Barnaby**'s cart: an elderly forest gnome, a wandering wizard trader with a little wizard hat and a two-handled hand cart.
- Alaric knows him by reputation: big in the wizard crowd, he always turns up at random times and always has what you need. Thimble knows him as a fellow gnome. Alaric mended the wheel with Aalyn's help.
- Barnaby knows the Fenwyns ("Please give your mother and father my best") and recognized Thimble's grin: was he studying with **Professor Bifflefapple at Hollowmere**? Thimble has just been traveling.
- **His wares:** potions of healing, greater healing and resistance; a bag of holding, a cloak of protection, sending stones, goggles of night; an amulet "for friends who drink a lot"; a ring of spell storing; a wand of fireball ("You have to earn that, Wayne"); and scrolls of *Sleep*, *Color Spray*, *Silent Image*, *Gust of Wind*, *Misty Step*, *Lightning Bolt*, *Speak with Dead* and *Blink*.
- **Alaric** bought the **ring of spell storing** (400 gp) and the ***Blink* scroll** (75 gp).
- **Aalyn** bought **glamoured studded leather (modified)** for 500 gp, borrowing 90 gp from Alaric. Barnaby had just picked it up from **"the professor" in Hollowmere**, who is revamping old relics: armor set with arcane crystals that cast illusions. Barnaby fitted and attuned it on the spot.
- **Wayne** bought the **goggles of night** and the **cloak of protection** (105 gp).
- **Thimble's loot compass.** Barnaby recognized it as the work of "Bird," his fellow wandering trader ([[Birdolomew Feathersworth]]). He adjusted it for free: it can now cast *Locate Object* once per day. *(The DM will write it up.)*
- Thimble traded his **potion of fire breath** and 50 gp for a **potion of superior healing** and **two potions of greater healing**.
- Bog passed on the drinker's amulet. Alaric was mortified.
- They say that where Barnaby goes, the wind follows.

**Oinkers**
- About two hours later, at dusk, the caravan pulled into **Old Ham Tug**, a village of two streets, behind **Oinkers**. The sign shows a silvery-ivory pig with a golden apple in its mouth.
- A cramped, run-down bar: four stools, a fireplace, two levels of old oak tables and a tiny stage. Servers skate around on magical roller skates in deep-cut vests.
- Alaric had Aalyn fill his new ring: **Disguise Self** (by accident, cast through his invocation) and a **2nd-level Healing Word.**
- Aalyn rolled on the three women in the room and walked straight back out. Thimble stepped outside to smoke.
- **Bog and Wayne at the bar.** The bartender has no apple juice. Bog got a sampler: ale, meat, wine, tea and a strong spirit. "Usually two gold, but for you, darling, one." He paid two. She smashed a raw apple into a glass for Wayne.
- Wayne drank it (Constitution 9), realized there were **seeds** in it, and ran outside. Bog stayed with the bartender.
- Wayne dry-heaved and nothing came up. He's sure the seeds will grow inside him. Thimble gave him his joint. Alaric sat down with them, very high.
- Thimble stored a 2nd-level *Chaos Bolt* in Alaric's ring. **Three of its five runes are now lit.**

**The wild magic smoke cloud**
- Everyone in the smoke circle rolled on the surge table.
  - **Aalyn** smelled overwhelming fresh bread, and his eyes became torches as he stared at the stars.
  - **Thimble**'s joint grew into a harmless **blunt snake** that coiled around him.
  - **Wayne went incorporeal.** He could see the apple seeds through himself.
  - Alaric just rocked back and forth.
- **Pebbles** climbed out with a mohawk of feathers and a beard, floating.
- Aalyn shifted his new armor and was in his underwear.
- **Luna** came around the corner, saw the scene, said "Oh, sorry, another time," and left. Thimble ran after her. She had walked past the caravan to the edge of the woods and stood staring into the forest. Session ends.

**After the session**
- Thimble has about 38 joints left.
- About a year of play: the campaign started in February.
- A holiday one-shot is planned: assassinate Santa Claus.
