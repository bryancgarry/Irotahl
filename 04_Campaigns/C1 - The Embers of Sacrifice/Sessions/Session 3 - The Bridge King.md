# Session 3: The Bridge King

**Party:** [[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]]
**Ref:** [[Oakhollow]], [[The Tall Oak Forest]], [[Graz'zt]] (the Bridge King), [[Bragthar]], [[Malrik Greene]]

> [!important] Played: "The Bridge King" (Episode 3)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=v6bN7t722Oo). The earliest recording. Sessions 1 and 2 were not recorded. The party talked their way out of Oakhollow, beat the Bridge King's riddles and his troll, and came away with the troll.

## What Happened at the Table

**Before play**
- Bog leveled up. The DM allowed Savage Attacker on his unarmed strikes.

**Leaving Oakhollow**
- Alaric's brand, on his elbow, tingled in Oakhollow, and he heard a faint echo of the Baron's roar. He kept it to himself.
- Darkstone guards in black and gray held the south gate, brought in by Sir Romulus.
- Wayne showed his wedding invitation, table number and all, and rolled a natural 1 on Persuasion.
- Aalyn invoked Princess Rosalie (18). The guards said Rosalie's orders and Romulus's orders conflicted.
- Under cover of Alaric's Minor Illusion, Thimble forged a royal decree from Romulus in Aalyn's name (natural 20 on Sleight of Hand, 25 total). The guards let them through.
- On the way out Aalyn muddied the guards' armor with Prestidigitation. The party may have made an enemy at the gate.

**The road into the forest**
- The Tall Oak Forest lies between Oakhollow and Bluelock Lake. The lumber trade floats logs downriver to Azure Port.
- [[Malrik Greene]] had told Alaric the Godspire ruins are in this forest.
- Thimble built a clockwork pocket watch that carries a Prestidigitation magic mark.
- Alaric's celestial owl familiar is named **Thimble Jr. II**.

**The Bridge King**
- A goblin in a junk crown held the bridge with his troll, Bragthar, and demanded five riddles.
  - Aalyn tried a mirror ploy. The mirror broke.
  - Wayne answered "my mind," correctly, in the middle of the fight.
  - Thimble guessed "time." Wrong.
  - Bog guessed "hands," then "fire." Wrong.
  - Alaric got his riddle later.
- The fight:
  - Grease went down. Bog's lightning breath caught the Bridge King on a natural 1.
  - Thimble's Suggestion failed on Bragthar, who speaks only Giant.
  - Aalyn hit the Bridge King with a rap-battle Vexing Verse.
  - Bog's Flurry of Blows ("purple nurples"). He found a copper piece in his belly button.
  - Wayne stole the crown (Strength 16 against a natural 2) and ordered Bragthar to sit. **Whoever wears the crown commands Bragthar.**
- Aalyn cast Comprehend Languages and, on a 25 Deception, recruited Bragthar. He speaks Giant, his ex lives in Oakhollow, and he asked about dental. Wayne kept the crown.
- The Bridge King jumped off the bridge and floated away downriver.

**Loot**
- Bog: the **Bridge King's Scepter** (homebrew).
- Wayne: one walloping blowgun needle.
- Alaric: a **Card Sharp's Deck**.
- Aalyn: a potion of healing, and a coin purse of 203 cp, 84 sp and 47 gp.
- Thimble: a **Bag of Beans** (9 beans).

**Other changes**
- Thimble's wild magic die went up to a d8.
