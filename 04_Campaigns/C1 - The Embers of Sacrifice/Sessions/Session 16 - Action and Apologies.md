# Session 16: Action and Apologies

**Party:** Level 5 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 15 - Here We Go Again]], [[The Obsidian Enclave]], [[The Thieves Guild]], [[Bragthar]]

> [!important] Played: "Action and Apologies" (Episode 16)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=glpPLO-KMLY). *(Gabe absent. Bog spent the session unconscious.)* Aalyn made a new deal with the figure who has been haunting him and blew a hole through the Orchid. The party escaped through the sewers with Calder, who named Edith Veilheart. After a long rest at the Dirty Stump, apologies were made, the hidden message was found, and the party set out to retrieve Bog.

## What Happened at the Table

**The raid**
- With seconds to spare, Thimble cast *Message* down through the floor (it's under a foot thick) to Aalyn, and to Wayne on a lucky d10 roll of 10: "The feds are coming. Get out."
- Wayne told Calder they could save him from something bad if he helped them. Aalyn took 9 more bludgeoning.

**Aalyn's vision**
- Through the crowd, the figure who has been flickering in and out came for him again. Its gold is gone now, and it is shrouded in dark red. It reached out a hand. **Aalyn took it.**
- **The grave.** Snow over a grave, the specific day he keeps reliving. The figure took form:
  - A breathtakingly beautiful woman, coalescing like an ink blot, dressed in tight cloth that looks like black ink.
  - Fully black eyes with vertical ripples, skin as pale as the snow, and hair as black as a starless sky. Cold rolls off her.
  - "Shall we?"
- **The office.** A faceless figure behind a desk. Mumbled words, but the same rage. Aalyn drew the dagger from his boot and held it white-knuckled in his lap. She pulled him back: "You're weak. Come on."
- **The street home.** His hometown street blended with the walk away from the Orchid under Fern guard. Whispers from both: "Are they criminals?" "I thought he was helping our town." "He's no good for our town." His anger turned to regret. **"Maybe I'm the cancer that needs to be removed."** She said, "Good. But we're not there yet."
- **The instrument room.** Splintered old instruments on the floor. Behind him: "Please calm down, my love," and humming, until the humming cut out. His body walked the same path as before: out the window, across his father's fields, into the forest.
- **The clearing.** An endless snowy forest with a single tree in the middle. She sat on a rock.
  - "How do you plan on making the world a better place if you're dead?"
  - Aalyn: maybe the world would be better without him.
  - She: "Our agreement had its terms. Regardless of how you feel, I do intend to receive my end of the bargain."
- **The new deal.**
  - One of his companions holds an artifact not meant for them, the source of an ancient rite that was rudely interrupted. It will find its way to the proper hands.
  - **When he fights to keep it, Aalyn must not aid him. "Let it be lost."** He doesn't have to work against him, and he's free to try to get it back afterward.
  - She wouldn't say why. Aalyn accepted, for the sake of saving his friends. *(After the session, the DM confirmed the companion is Wayne.)*
- She had him hold up his pact weapon and **poured inky red energy into the rapier** through sigils he'd never seen. His throat tightened as if he were being choked, and he blacked out.
- Aalyn's inventory has a new placeholder entry for the rapier. It replaces the old one, and its rules are still to be worked out.

**The blast**
- Aalyn stood. Energy flared from his eyes, bigger than ever, and curled into flame horns, like the figure's. **Burning runes circled his neck, with new words being written into them.**
- One swing of his rapier threw the crowd against the walls, blew all three basement doors open, shattered the lights, and **blew a hole up through the first floor and out the roof.** Debris was launched hundreds of feet into the air.
- **Bog, at the bar above, was blasted onto the roof and knocked unconscious.**
- Afterward, the roots of Aalyn's hair are black, fading into white with gold and orange highlights.
- Alaric's take: shock, intrigue ("you have to tell me how you did that"), and his first curse word. Wayne: stone-faced outside, gong-struck inside.

**The escape**
- Calder: "You two, get me the hell out of here and I'll tell you whatever you want."
- Alaric's *Major Image* hid them. Wayne linked minds with Aalyn and Thimble. Aalyn grabbed his jacket and instruments, and the four went down into the sewers.
- **Thimble and Bog.**
  - Thimble climbed to the roof and found Bog out cold. He fed Bog his last mushroom, which didn't wake him.
  - He slipped climbing down but stuck the landing on the bar (natural 20 Acrobatics). The cowering bartender told him to take what he needed, so he grabbed two dinners. He dropped both plates walking up the front wall (natural 1), then got back up with more.
  - He dragged Bog out of sight of the guards in the hole and half-fed him. Bog stirred but didn't wake. There was no soil anywhere to plant a bean.
- **Jud is alive.** He wandered up, having seen the debris fly.
  - The night of the reception he got swept up in the wave of black ink and floated in a barrel. He never brought the drinks because a "baddie" was talking to him.
  - Thimble's Insight 15: mostly true, but he's leaving out a detail or two.
  - Jud hauled Bog off the roof and **rolled him into a haystack beside the Orchid.**
- Thimble dropped *Suggestion*, so Ellie will wake.
- Jud talked Thimble past two guards as a pair of drunks, and they left through the south gate.
- **Everyone met at Bragthar's stable yard** outside the south gate around midnight, the others coming up through a sewer grate. Bragthar was asleep curled up like a cat, and Wayne left him rations.

**Calder talks**
- The job wasn't from the Enclave on paper. Enclave members get things through shadier means, and it came to Calder through Silas.
- **The client was a noblewoman, Edith Veilheart.** She's a benefactor of the Enclave and has taken an interest in "this guy's work" for some years.
- **Her butler, Buro, made the request.** The item was **a wooden mask**, likely locked in the vault at her estate.
- Then Calder ran.

**The Dirty Stump**
- They took rooms at the Dirty Stump, just past the Mages Guild, where it all started. Alaric paid 3 sp for three rooms.
- **Aalyn got the same room he shared with Rosalie** the night before her wedding. The blanket still smells faintly of her perfume.
- Wayne's egg (Animal Handling 16): he can feel a sense of adventure and gratitude from it.
- **A long rest at last**, the first in about five sessions.

**The morning** (gray and cloudy, about 9 a.m.)
- Wayne's egg (Survival 17): its life force is stronger and it's getting close to hatching. Names under consideration: **Keith**, or Thimblewick Jr. III.
- Alaric recast *Find Familiar* but couldn't hold the image of an owl, and summoned **a three-eyed space guppy** the size of Thimble. He hid it with *Minor Illusion*.
- **The hidden message.** Wayne's natural 20 Perception on the Enclave note: certain letters are bolded, and they spell **"WATCH THEM."** It took Thimble a while to see it.
- **Aalyn and Alaric.** Aalyn apologized for the night before. He's learned he doesn't need to help in every situation, and will try not to put them in danger. Alaric's History 15: Aalyn's magic comes from an otherworldly source. "It seems you've recently made another promise as well." Aalyn looked confused.
- **The group, minus Bog.** Alaric revealed his space guppy, and they moved to Wayne and Thimble's room.
  - Alaric says he never worked *for* the Enclave, only with them briefly.
  - Alaric: if they don't want the party at the Veilheart estate, that's where they should go. The main quest, after a few things here.
  - They agreed Bog needs to be eased into hearing he's a dragonborn, and needs to stop committing violence.
- **Aalyn and Wayne.** Aalyn apologized for ruining Wayne's standing with the thieves. Wayne: there are other thieves guilds, and "maybe we just don't stab people right off the bat." They agreed on pickpocketing, but no killing.
- They headed out to find Bog.
