## What Happened at the Table
*(From the Session 24 recap.)*

**The mask and the grimoire**
- The party pried the mask off Thimble's face with a crowbar and a hammer. Good checks, and no scarring.
- Alaric put the mask on to read Malrik's Abyssal grimoire but couldn't make sense of it. He gave it back to Thimble, who resonated with it and unlocked some of the book on a very good roll.
- The book is a general glossary of the world's supernatural side: lore on the demons and the Abyss. It's one volume of a larger encyclopedia, and **other volumes are out there.**

**Tiberius leaves**
- The party talked with Tiberius outside as he was getting ready to leave the city, loading Prince Darken on a stretcher into a carriage.
- The talk turned to his runic armor. **Bog knows of a dwarven smith** in a remote, little-traveled part of the world who crafts such things.

**Around the city**
- **Aalyn** went to the music shop and talked the old shopkeeper into giving him his lute back for free. He name-dropped a genie he made up on the spot.
- **The Solarium.** The others went to the Mages Guild, talked with Ammona and Magister Pelen, and repaired the solar refractory using Malrik's blueprints, with Pebbles helping at the very end.
  - It showed **the illusionary prophecy Malrik held dear**, which may have set him on his path.
  - The plans suggest it could be rebuilt and improved with aetherstone.
  - They returned everything to Pelen and told him to get out of the city.
- **The temple.** Aalyn visited a non-denominational temple on the outskirts and met two priests, one rounder than the other. One, Marv, found religion after his divorce, and they share a bed. They had no idea what the brand means. All they talked about was **the Wild Mother.**
- **The forged note.** Aalyn, with Alaric's help, forged a note from Prince Darken ordering the city evacuated before the Bloodlancers arrive. He went to drop it off with a guard and ran into something magical (Session 24 opens there).
