# Session 34: The Hunt Begins

**Party:** Level 6 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 33 - Friends and Lovers]], [[Hollowmere]], [[Selara Du'Cant]], [[Corvin Hale]], [[The Holy Chain Knights]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "The Hunt Begins" (Episode 34)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=iGpGmSfckkU). The party joined the hunt for Nerevath. Bog punched Father Celery of the Fortunate Five before they left the city. Aalyn charmed the baroness into two tips: stick to the two pamphlet routes, and find her ancestors' relic in a chest in a mountain lake for a bigger prize. Up the river path they crossed rapids and a rigged rope bridge, mercy-killed the crushed knight Sir Tobias Strong Legs, and pressed on into snow and cold past a frozen lake and an old mine. At a vast frozen lake with a chest on an island, the ice cracked under Wayne, and a huge centipede-like water monster burst out with its young. Ends at initiative.

## What Happened at the Table

**Before leaving**
- Alaric wanted the library and the ruins, but the ruins aren't going anywhere. The plan: **make sure Wayne's goose isn't killed.**
- **The Fortunate Five**, introduced by **Lady Priscilla**, a cheerful 20-year-old "old in spirit":
  - **Brimsley Pocket Pickle**, the leader, regal, a rapier, a seasoned blade.
  - **Groank**, a hulking mass of a man of even fewer words.
  - **Whisper**, tiny and masked, two daggers, whispers too quietly to hear. Talks your ear off around the campfire.
  - **Father Celery**, a priest whose holy book is bookmarked with a stick of celery.
  - They've never lost a hunt. Aalyn has never heard of them (History 6).
- **How the hunt works:** whoever brings back the beast's head first wins. Everyone got a **starter pamphlet with a map of two routes**. A day or two, weather permitting.
- **Bog punched Father Celery** right through his offered celery (7). His wrist folded and his nose caved. Brimsley was too slow to stop it. Groank turned, Whisper drew. Thimble's Persuasion (11) kept it from a fight. Celery healed himself with divine magic from his book: "You punched like a girl."
- **Whisper sliced Thimble's blunt in half** and smoked the other half through his mask. A nod of respect.
- **The baroness:** "Please, no fighting until you leave the city. If you wish to kill each other, please just do it out there."
- **The other groups:**
  - The robed group (Perception 19 from Bog): **elves, with darker skin than Aalyn's or Alaric's**, mostly women. They left first at a ridiculous pace and gave Bog a glare that said both kinds of "I'm going to..."
  - The cookie-cutter party has **a small rabid chihuahua-looking dog**. They took the woods.
  - Corvin Hale leads lowly knights and priestly, magic-wielding members of the order.

**Aalyn and the baroness**
- The baroness is **Elixni Vexthorne**.
- No spare pamphlets. Aalyn *Message*d Wayne to snatch one from Lady Priscilla; Wayne asked out loud "Who's Lady Priscilla?" and she gave him hers.
- **This is the third hunt** the city has sponsored. The beast has plagued their lands for a few days.
- She says the region enjoys an unprecedented era of peace thanks to **the marriage of House Darken and House Fern**. She would have died to be at the wedding. She loved **Rosalie's mother**, who she says is **"just on holiday, for some time."** Aalyn thought Rosalie's mother died when Rosalie was young.
- She'll **grant Aalyn time after the hunt, if they succeed**, to hear about the wedding.
- **Natural 20 Persuasion:**
  - The previous groups took **two different paths** up the mountain to where Nerevath was believed to be. Some are more dangerous than others. **Stick to one of those two** and it should be smooth sailing.
  - **A secret treasure chest in a lake up in the mountains** holds **a relic of her ancestors, who liked to fish there.** Bring it back and she may **increase the prize**.
- Aalyn bowed lower than her. He offered Selara a handshake; she kissed his hand instead. "Mr. Ravaceran."

**The road**
- Wayne opened **a psionic link** (two hours) for the party, and renewed it later on the climb. Aalyn told them about the lake chest; it isn't on the pamphlet.
- Alaric: if this is the most competent group on the third try, get to Nerevath first and worry about treasure on the way back. Aalyn: stay close enough to the other groups to clean up their messes. Watch for groups turning on each other.
- Alaric navigated (Survival, Rec scouting). An hour along the trade route between **two great dwarf statues** out of the city.

**The crossroads (10 a.m.)**
- **The river path** (left), following the river into the mountains: rocky, open, probably less wildlife and fewer fights but more treacherous ground. **The forest path** (right).
- Thimble's gut (natural 1 Insight): both feel bad. Why would she send them where the others died?
- They took the **river path**, for the lake and to save their fighting strength. **The Fortunate Five** came the same way. **Corvin's knights** took the forest.

**The rapids (45 minutes on)**
- 60 to 70 feet of fast, cold water; **honk-blast marks** in the rock show Nerevath came this way.
- Aalyn flew Alaric and Thimble across with his boots (Athletics). Alaric froze the water under his feet. Wayne hopped the boulders.

**The rope bridge (2:45 in)**
- A shoddy 75-foot rope bridge across a gorge. Aalyn and Bog flew. Alaric walked it casting *Mending* on every foot, and (Perception 18) saw **the far end had been rigged**. The rope snapped; he cast *Feather Fall* and teleported across.
- Wayne threw Thimble (Athletics, no modifier, DC 25). He made about 26 feet. Aalyn swan-dived and carried him the rest.
- **Groank was floating down the river**, laughing.
- Aalyn tied Thimble's and Wayne's ropes together with a square knot (Survival 22) and strung a line. **Wayne (about 400 lb) crossed hanging under it.** Bog doesn't share his good rope.
- The clear day clouded over fast; the weather shifts here on the Runelands border. Big scratch marks in the cliffs where something huge landed badly.

**Sir Tobias Strong Legs (3:30 in)**
- A man in knightly armor lay under a huge honk-blasted tree, only his torso showing. **Sir Tobias Strong Legs**, of a minor noble house of the Midlands (Aalyn, History 14). He met the beast last night and the tree fell on him in the duel.
- His legs were a crushed ruin. **The tree was acting as a tourniquet.** He insisted his legs were just asleep and he could not live as anything but a Strong Legs.
- Thimble told him they'd lift the tree and he'd be fine, and to close his eyes. **Bog lifted the tree** and **Thimble cast a 2nd-level *Chaos Bolt* that leapt three times** (force, then fire that cauterized the wounds, then lightning), killing him. Alaric walked away.
- **Loot (Thimble):** a **Strong Legs signet ring** (some hundreds of gp), a **+1 longsword** (on Thimble's back; he can barely hold it), a **potion of superior healing**, and **a pamphlet from an earlier expedition**.
- **His notes** describe a creature at the top of the mountains, in the same area, that doesn't match Nerevath: **a gray beast, not malicious, not the flying type.**
- Thimble rolled his wild magic surge (a d10, checking whether he's turned into a tree) and came up safe; it's a d8 next time.

**The cold (5:30 in, about 3:30 p.m.)**
- Light snow and dropping temperature. From here, **a Constitution save every hour** against the cold (DC 5, rising by 1 each hour).
- **Wayne rolled a natural 1**: the wind chilled his sprout. **One level of exhaustion** (−2 to d20 tests, −5 feet of speed).
- Aalyn's armor shifted to **a puffy black coat with gold accents**.
- Aalyn, scouting by air, found two side spots and they **marked both on the map**:
  - **A frozen lake**, past a rocky rise where trees grow again.
  - **An old mine** in the rocks to the right.
- **Thimble's loot compass** (Investigation 20) pointed toward the lake, but not quite honed in, as if **something in the mine was disturbing it**. *(At the table the DM said the compass upgrade from the trader hadn't been done yet. DM, after the fact: it should have happened, and is still to be applied.)*
- They skipped both to save time and strength.

**The great frozen lake (4:30 p.m.)**
- An hour on: an unavoidable, much larger frozen lake in a valley, feeding the lower one. **The peak they need is the great snowcapped one behind it.** Everyone made the hourly save; Thimble barely.
- Thimble's compass swung here: **this was the lake all along.** In the middle, **a chest on a small rock island.**
- The robed elves had just crossed to the far end. **The Fortunate Five were sledding down a mountainside** in pairs; Groank body-surfed.
- Alaric: why hasn't any earlier group taken it? **Aalyn: the baroness said her ancestors fished there. The real chest may be under the water, and the island one a trick.**

**The ice**
- Wayne (stone, heavy) charged straight across. **The ice cracked behind every step**, and weak points split open between holes.
- Alaric walked behind refreezing the cracks with magic, but the fresh ice hadn't bonded and caved again. Then he started building **an ice ladder, slide and sled**.
- Thimble's ice gave way (DEX save 20, he didn't fall in). Bog just hung out, safest of all. Wayne reached the island.
- Under the ice: **a very large dark figure.**
- It burst out behind Wayne: **a huge centipede-like water monster** with fangs and claws. **Its young burst from the ice on the sides.** Initiative next session.

**After the session**
- Bog plans to eat bug for dinner. Thimble is very pleased his one spell went off three times.
