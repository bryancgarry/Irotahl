# Session 40: The Elevator Down

**Party:** Level 7 ([[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]], [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]], [[Bogtrotter]], [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]], [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]])
**Ref:** [[Session 39 - Escape From the Keep]], [[Hollowmere]], [[Durnhollow]], [[Luna au Vermillion|Luna]], [[Rec]]

> [!important] Played: "The Elevator Down" (Episode 40)
> [Watch on YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MPkNx7-TAms). Professor Bifflefapple walked the party to a side entrance to the dwarven ruins: an abandoned mine on the middle plateau with an old cargo lift. He mentioned students in new black clothes and dwarven research into an enhancement for the mountains' silver. After a stop at a deli (a greater healing potion and 30 dirty water dogs), the party blasted open the caved-in mine and rode the lift down. They dodged an arcane scanner and hid from watching eyes, but Thimble was spotted: spider constructs, then, after Wayne kicked a crippled one down the shaft, a large centurion construct and two sphere guards. Aalyn's twinned *Eldritch Blast* took the centurion apart. The lift carried on to a vast lit dwarven city beneath the mountains, and stopped on a highway leading to a giant vault door.

## What Happened at the Table

**Recap**
- The DM recapped Sessions 38 and 39: Bog's night under Vashara, Luna's arrest on what may have been Corvin Hale's push, the escape, the Pale Codex, Papa Glabb's harp and songbook, and the regroup at the Drunken Star.

**Morning at the Drunken Star**
- 10:00 a.m., drizzling. Thimble finished his reading of the tome. Alaric opened **the Pale Codex: blank pages, it needs moonlight.** He read his tome of understanding instead for half an hour.
- Thimble: "I think they took my girl too. We should go find her at some point." Alaric: anyone they'd ask would arrest them.
- **The knights hunt the party only on the Silver Keep's grounds. The city guard is separate and doesn't care.**

**Professor Bifflefapple**
- Outside (Perception 18), **the professor** was at a market stall buying avocados. Alaric asked for the ruins' entrance.
  - He'd sent them to the baroness before, but **she has kept to her quarter lately.** There's **a side entrance like his workshop's, on the middle plateau**: an old abandoned mine with an elevator. "The footings are a little shoddy."
  - About Thimble: "Hopefully not like his father either. I'm getting ahead of myself."
  - **Aalyn asked about the magical thread.** Not his field: try a tailor. But **some students on campus have been wearing new threads lately**: very black, emo goth, fully covered.
  - **His study of Nerevath last night has him near a breakthrough.** He wants to see them after.
  - Tip: **"the dwarves here were researching a special material, an enhancement for the silver of these mountains."** He knows nothing about a thread.
  - He sleeps 13 hours a night. What he dreams about is "a conversation for next time."
- **Wayne's lessons:** he pulled an avocado with his mind (Arcana) but too hard: it flew past and beaned a man in the back of the head. With two hands he aimed at Thimble and **triggered a wild magic surge: reversed gravity for six seconds.** Thimble floated 20 feet up and glided down on his feather fall. "I think this is the breakthrough."

**The deli**
- **A greater healing potion for 100 gp** (regular 20 gp; superior "a lot more"). The party's money is mostly the baroness's heirloom payment; the hunt reward is still unpaid.
- **Hollowmere dirty water dogs, 1 gp each:** roll a d20 when you eat one, 1 to 10 damage and 11 to 20 healing, scaled by distance from the line. **Someone took 30.** The first one eaten rolled 8: 2 poison damage.
- No infernal puzzle box (the DM: "infernal" sounds like devil stuff). An amulet of the drunkard might be found in the dwarven place.

**The mine**
- Past the city edge, along the cliffs where the river runs: **an old mineshaft with a rail cart, caved in by boulders.**
- Bog's heart wasn't in it (disadvantage). Aalyn's *Eldritch Blast* (7) cracked the top rocks; the pebbles fell on Wayne, who blended in so well a blast clipped his eyebrow. **He collected 13 Wayne pebbles.** Bog punched a hole (13). **Thimble's *Thunderwave* blew it open** (audible 300 feet, like quarry blasting).
- Inside it's pitch black. **Alaric cast *Light* on his clothes. Wayne wore his goggles of night**: huge eyes.
- Five minutes down a clean passage: **a huge cargo platform** with gear wheels on rails, dwarven architecture on the walls.

**The lift**
- **Wayne pulled the lever with his mind** (Alaric's teaching moment, Arcana with advantage). Old machinery woke, dust fell, dwarven lights lit the platform, and it descended.
- About 10 minutes down, steam and gears. **An arcane ethereal scanner**, a 10-foot wall of light, swept the platform front to back. Alaric *Misty Step*ped around it; Aalyn hopped over from the barrels; Wayne ran up the wall and flipped over; Bog vaulted a barrel (27). **No one was scanned.**
- Then **eyes in the pipes** on both walls watched for anomalies. Alaric hid in a dwarven chest of tools behind an illusion; **Bog folded himself into a working cannon**; Aalyn climbed into an empty barrel; **Wayne curled up against the wall as cover with Thimble under him** (Stealth 19).
- The eyes locked on Wayne's sprout (life where none belongs) and found **"a bio essence that's not meant to be here": Thimble**, peeking out of Wayne's armpits. He walked out: "What are you people?"

**Spider constructs**
- **Five dwarven spider constructs** shot out of the walls as spheres and unfolded. They're constructs, not undead or elementals.
- Wayne ("Ew, I don't like bugs") killed one with daggers. **Thimble's upcast *Chaos Bolt*** (thunder) shattered another. Aalyn peeked out (Stealth 6), then *Eldritch Blast* destroyed one and de-armed another. Thimble's *Shatter* blew one apart; Aalyn dodged the shrapnel. One fell over a box, broke its last leg and curled into a ball. Aalyn blasted the last off Thimble's knees.
- **Wayne and Aalyn played skyball with the crippled sphere: Aalyn long-snapped it and Wayne kicked it off the platform** into the shaft. Thimble was looking at **a stone casket with remains** on the platform, too heavy to move. The crates are dwarven shipping stock.
- The red light fell to the bottom. **The platform stopped and the lights went red: an emergency security protocol.**

**The centurion**
- From a large tube above, **a big centurion construct** unfolded, flanked by **two sphere guards on wheels** with blade and shield arms.
- **Bog came out of the cannon** and opened up the first sphere. **Wayne's sneak attack** through the gap finished it.
- **Wayne's psychic dagger phased right through the centurion: immune to psychic.** "I think this guy's an illusion."
- Thimble's *Shatter* (half). The second sphere slashed Thimble twice (19); no *Mage Armor* this morning.
- **Aalyn cursed the centurion with Hexblade's Curse and crit** ("You look like a kitchen appliance that discovered violence").
- **The centurion's steam breath** (CON DC 16) burned Wayne and Thimble (18 fire, halved for one) and set the crates and barrels alight.
- Alaric slipped out of his box under *Minor Illusion*. He cast ***Summon Fey***, a fuming fey spirit that bowed to Wayne, teleported behind the centurion and **hit with its "Fayblade" on back-to-back natural 20s** (13 force), then winked at Wayne. Rec helped Thimble.
- Bog hammered the centurion (32) and **opened a beer on its joints.** Wayne added 33 with advantage. **Thimble's *Vampiric Touch*** drained the sphere (13, healed 6).
- **Aalyn twinned *Eldritch Blast*: two natural 20s on the centurion.** It spun off, crashed and **its steam core exploded.**
- **Thimble's wild magic surge (16) had no visible effect yet.** He hit the sphere with *Fire Bolt*; the fey spirit flanked it. **Bog finished the last sphere with his empty beer bottle**: "It's a chore, not a hobby."

**Durnhollow**
- The lift churned on for about half an hour, out of the tunnel into **a vast underground vista: a whole lit dwarven city carved into the stone**, halls hundreds of feet high, columns everywhere, highways of stone and silver. **One giant silver deposit.**
- **Lava glows in the depths; river water drips through the cracks and steams where it meets the lava.**
- The platform locked into a gantry at the end of a highway. Alaric (Perception 19): **a grand highway to an enormous vault door, 100 to 200 feet across**, with building façades along it and **large figures standing in front.** "Those things totally aren't going to come to life and try to kill us, right?" Session ends.
