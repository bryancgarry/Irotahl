# The Story So Far

*The Embers of Sacrifice, also known as the Off-Branded Misfits.* A summary of the whole campaign as it happened at the table, from Oakhollow to the summit of the Hollow Reliquary. It only covers what the party has seen or learned, with no DM secrets, so it's safe to share with players. For details, follow the session links. For hidden lore, see [[DM Secrets]] and [[Open Threads]].

**The party:** [[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]] (Spano), [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]] (Trevor), [[Bogtrotter|Bog]] (Gabe), [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]] (Leszek) and [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]] (Kevin). All level 8 as of Session 54.

---

## Part One: The Wedding and the Brand (Sessions 1 and 2, unrecorded)

The party came together in [[Oakhollow]], a city of the Free Kingdoms, for the wedding of Princess Rosalie Fern to Sir Romulus Darken, Prince of Darkstone. Aalyn was there for Rosalie, who had once given him her family's winged boots. Wayne and Thimble arrived as a pair. Alaric was a runaway noble from [[Syfaeril]], and Bog was a bronze dragonborn monk.

At the reception, glass shattered, smoke filled the hall, and assassins struck. Rosalie was stabbed. Her father, [[Baron Fern]], let out a monstrous scream, and the party found themselves in a dark mirror dimension where the Baron had become a monster. As they fought him, a great symbol in the distance exploded and marked them. When they killed him, the world cracked and they were back in the great hall. **Everyone but Bog carried a brand from that night.** Later they learned it came from a pale stone the Baron had used. Rosalie didn't die. She was held suspended in red magic.

## Part Two: The Stone Trees (Sessions 3 to 8)

Something in [[The Tall Oak Forest]] was turning the world to stone, and the party set out to stop it.

- **[[Session 3 - The Bridge King|Session 3]].** They talked their way out of Oakhollow and beat the riddles of the Bridge King, a petty, cruel goblin who held a bridge with a troll. Wayne took the Bridge King's crown, and the troll, [[Bragthar]], who obeys whoever wears it, joined the party. The Bridge King floated off downriver. Thimble skipped a stone off his skull, and he swore he'd remember it.
- **[[Session 4 - Mushrooms & Trees|Session 4]] and [[Session 5 - Bluelock and Bloodlancers|Session 5]].** Bragthar's hidden mushroom cove, two Bloodlancer orc scouts on the road, and the first stone tree, which woke into a Tree Blight when Wayne's necklace touched it. Its core went into Wayne. The party took a Bloodlancer camp the hard way.
- **[[Session 6 - The Path to Godspire Ruins|Session 6]].** In the camp they freed [[Malrik Greene]], the Obsidian Enclave's adviser to Oakhollow, from a blood-rune cage. His confiscated note read "The daughter is key." They met the aarakocra merchant [[Birdolomew Feathersworth]], solved the race shrine at the Godspire ruins, and pierced the illusion at the summit to find the machine itself: the Godspire, a **lithoweave**.
- **[[Session 7 - The Goose is Loose|Session 7]].** A puzzle-boss against the lithoweave's five rings. Bragthar charged a ring and vanished, the goose (a pear merchant named Salvatore Pearcetti, turned into a goose by Thimble's wild magic) was lost to it, and Wayne and Alaric gave up part of their life force to collapse its core. It fell in on itself like a black hole and vanished. The calcification stopped, but what was stone stayed stone.
- **[[Session 8 - Gnomes and Fireballs|Session 8]].** Bragthar fell back out of the sky. Wayne found a goose egg. The party found a hidden shrine to "the Architect," and on the road home met a wagon of Glimmerstone gnomes. Aalyn's rap battle tied, and a gnome named Muzzle swung a bat at Thimble.

## Part Three: The Mystery Beneath Oakhollow (Sessions 9 to 23)

- **[[Session 9 - The Off-Branded Misfits|Session 9]].** The party beat the gnomes and won their trap wagon, **Sheila**. Bragthar was turned away at the gate. The party named itself **the Off-Branded Misfits**.
- **[[Session 10 - The Orchid|Session 10]].** A night at the Orchid. Wayne found [[The Thieves Guild]] under the inn and took a rat job from Silas Holloway. Aalyn humiliated his rival Ino Silvertone. In the morning, the party found Rosalie floating in red magic at the keep.
- **[[Session 11 - The Plot Thickens|Session 11]] to [[Session 13 - The Rat Lord|Session 13]].** Sir Romulus and his bodyguard [[Tiberius Blackfang]] briefed the party on Rosalie and the court records missing from around her birth. Tiberius showed them his own brand. Bog was arrested for murder and bribed free. In the sewers the party wiped out the wererats, and the Rat Lord fled.
- **[[Session 14 - A Little Bit of Everything|Session 14]] to [[Session 16 - Action and Apologies|Session 16]].** They searched Malrik's Solarium in the Mages Guild. Alaric found a hidden chest from his parents holding his grandfather's cloak. Wayne woke the Rat Lord's Hat. A pit fight at the Orchid ended with Aalyn attacking the runner Calder and blowing a hole through the inn. Calder named the noblewoman Edith Valeheart. A hidden note from the Enclave read "WATCH THEM."
- **[[Session 17 - The Vault|Session 17]] and [[Session 18 - The Stage is Set|Session 18]].** The party robbed Edith Valeheart's vault: a wooden mask from the forest ruins, a Fern pendant, and Malrik's logs. The mask split open around Thimble's wild magic. Malrik was seen at the Grand Cathedral. Beneath it, in the Fern catacombs, the party found a chained demon, and through the mask Aalyn saw who she was: **Talula Fern, Rosalie's mother**.
- **[[Session 19 - The Fight Begins|Session 19]] and [[Session 20 - The Ascension|Session 20]].** Malrik demanded his mask and the pale gemstone on Wayne's neck, said he'd seen the end of the world and had "the vessel" ready, and woke the chamber's guardians. Talula broke free, and Alaric tossed her the gemstone. She fled deeper. At the bottom of a blood trail the party found the former Bridge King, flayed and bound, with the gemstone in his chest. He rose as a demon lord.
- **[[Session 21 - The Godhand|Session 21]].** The Bridge King finished his ascension and pulled the party into an abyssal domain. He disintegrated Sheila and killed Bragthar ("You abandoned me for them"). Four more demon lords appeared beside him: **the Godhand**. He named himself **Graz'zt** and made Malrik his apostle. A spider-bodied figure told Aalyn "It's crazy how effective compassion can be as a tool." A winged woman called Wayne "my hollow vessel" and Thimble "a chaos born." A horned one was fascinated that Bog is a dragonborn. Tiberius cut his way in and held all five off. Thimble shattered the domain with ley-line power and tore a chasm through the center of Oakhollow. As the party fled, Graz'zt told Thimble: "I told you I would remember that."
- **[[Session 22 - A Long Night Ends|Session 22]].** Wayne's egg hatched into **Pebbles**, a stone goose-dragon. **Rosalie died.** Prince Darken survived, crippled. Aalyn sang the city through the night. Malrik's bag held an aetherstone order to Glimmerstone and charts marking another gemstone in the sea between Midland and Syfaeril. The Bloodlancers were at the gates, and Alaric's augury warned against staying to fight.
- **[[Session 23 - Magic and Religion|Session 23]].** The mask came off Thimble, and he partly read Malrik's Abyssal grimoire: an introductory volume to a larger encyclopedia of demons. The repaired solar refractory showed Malrik's prophecy of rifts in the sky and demons from above and below. Tiberius left with Prince Darken. Bog mentioned Doran, a dwarven smith who makes armor like Tiberius's.

## Part Four: The Road West (Sessions 24 to 30)

- **[[Session 24 - Nerevath, The Feathered Scourge|Session 24]].** The party stole Commander Seth's beer-geyser bean and left Oakhollow. Their brands burned in the night on the road. The giant goose from the lithoweave, **Nerevath**, swallowed Pebbles. Wayne traded rations to get her back, and Nerevath flew off toward Hollowmere.
- **[[Session 25 - Boobs and Bodies|Session 25]] and [[Session 26 - The Straight Arrow|Session 26]].** Bandits at a fake guard post, one wearing a patch with a candle on it. In the border town of Westbury, a heist with the steward Kalisar went wrong. Aalyn couldn't heal Kalisar's sick mother, and called on **Lolth** by name for the first time. She told him it was the same sickness that took his love.
- **[[Session 27 - The Pyramid|Session 27]] and [[Session 28 - Emperor Hawk Tuah and The Goon Generals|Session 28]].** Thimble's bean grew a pyramid. Inside, a puzzle dungeon and the mummy Pharaoh Sokatoa, who took Thimble for "the bean god." Bog tore him apart and took his bandages as wraps. Wayne looted a Sun Blade and an air elemental gem. A burning grove kept turning up beside the party.
- **[[Session 29 - The Holy Silver Chain Knights|Session 29]].** Three riders blocked the trail: **[[Luna au Vermillion|Luna]]**, Imperator of [[The Holy Chain Knights|the Holy Silver Chain Knights]], with [[Corvin Hale]]. Thimble pulled rank as a Danglesworth, and the knights took the party with them toward Hollowmere.
- **[[Session 30 - Journey Through the Fog|Session 30]].** Luna admitted she wanted to use the brand as bait for the monster that killed her brother. In the fog around Hollowmere, wendigos tore the caravan apart. Aalyn, Alaric and Thimble had visions. Lolth set Aalyn a challenge: find her Spool, hidden in Hollowmere, before "the others."

## Part Five: Hollowmere (Sessions 31 to 39)

- **[[Session 31 - Welcome to Hollowmere|Session 31]].** The party reached [[Hollowmere]], the city of mists. Vargrax the Executioner ambushed them on the Commons and died. [[Talia]], Aalyn's ex, grabbed him in the street.
- **[[Session 32 - Exploring the City|Session 32]] and [[Session 33 - Friends and Lovers|Session 33]].** Talia pointed Aalyn to V's, a lounge in the Fog Slab. At Cinderward, Professor Bifflefapple coaxed out Wayne's first telekinetic pull and showed the party an airship found under the mountain ice. Luna told Thimble how her brother vanished. [[Selara Du'Cant]] of the Obsidian Enclave appeared asking about Malrik. Jud turned up posing as a knight. Bifflefapple wants Nerevath's heart to fly the airship. The baroness sent five groups to hunt Nerevath.
- **[[Session 34 - The Hunt Begins|Session 34]] to [[Session 36 - The Summit|Session 36]].** The hunt. A frozen lake, remorhazes, and the baroness's heirloom chest. At the summit, Nerevath was fighting an adult silver dragon over her nest, and hooded drow closed in. Wayne learned through his link that Nerevath is the pear merchant. The dragon turned on the rival hunters, called Bog kin and woke a silver heritage in him. **Alaric secretly took one of her eggs.** A flashback showed young Alaric brought before the gold dragon Aurixia by his brother Fenril. The party leveled to 7.
- **[[Session 37 - Back From The Hunt|Session 37]].** Salvatore turned out to be Pebbles' mother. The party was paid for the baroness's heirlooms. Selara shook on a truce: "Do not push the baroness."
- **[[Session 38 - Digging For Information|Session 38]] and [[Session 39 - Escape From the Keep|Session 39]].** At V's, Bog killed a janitor and was taken under *Dominate Person*. Aalyn met **Miss V, Vashara**, signed her contract without reading it, and got four names of missing patrons. In the Celestial Archives, Wayne found a myth of a dwarf named Jack with soulvite daggers, and Thimble freed **the Tome of Malcanthet**, one volume of the Demonomicon. Bog woke knowing he'd told a voice everything about the party, and that "the lounge misses you" will call him back. Nobody in the world knew what a devil was. Luna was taken away by the Chain Lords, and Corvin Hale sent the knights after the party. Alaric and Aalyn walked out with the Pale Codex and Papa Glabb's harp. The party chose the dwarven ruins below the city.

## Part Six: Durnhollow (Sessions 40 to 50.5)

Beneath Hollowmere lies [[Durnhollow]], a ruined dwarven city of silver and Soulvite.

- **[[Session 40 - The Elevator Down|Session 40]].** Bifflefapple showed the party an old cargo lift. Constructs attacked, and the lift carried them down to a vast, lit dwarven city.
- **[[Session 41 - A Man and His Goose|Session 41]] to [[Session 43 - The Tempest Warden|Session 43]]. The steam layer.** Wayne dove after Pebbles into a chasm, heard her first word, "Dad," and launched himself back up: the Feat of the Kyn'Ruthar. Clausius Clapeyron's journal led to an enhancement chamber that made Aalyn's sword Storm Song, and nearly killed Thimble. The party defeated **the Tempest Warden** and took the air soulvite shard.
- **[[Session 44 - And I Click 5 Again|Session 44]] to [[Session 47 - The Tide Warden|Session 47]]. The water layer.** Pump puzzles and turbines. Plaques named **King Atul Stonemarch** and his marriage pact to a drow princess, with four elementals as a gift. In a hidden study (a busy teleportation circle, a black statue of Malcanthet, a record of an eclipse days earlier), Thimble spoke Malcanthet's true name and received a medallion with a face. The party defeated **the Tide Warden** and took the water soulvite shard.
- **[[Session 48 - Magic Mushroom Fun Pt. 2|Session 48]].** Mushroom roulette. The Omnishroom showed Wayne a vault of four doors and where to find the gold key. A portal to the Feywild, where a scarred bear, [[Rai Zeph]], knelt to Wayne as his Kyn'Ruthar and asked him to go home to the Goliath elders. The party freed **[[Myra]]** the myconid.
- **[[Session 49 - The Rock God|Session 49]].** The quarry's stone warriors knelt to Wayne, "the Rock God." The party restarted a silver refinery with Myra as manager. Wayne pushed Aalyn down a minecart track and over a lake of lava.
- **[[Session 50 - Into the Inferno|Session 50]].** A lava roller coaster to an old dwarven market. The fire enhancement chamber made Aalyn's stone greataxe magic. Wayne and Thimble sailed for an obsidian castle and met **[[Vistro IV]]**, king of the fire salamanders, who turned on them when Wayne called himself the Kyn'Ruthar and Thimble showed the Tome of Malcanthet.
- **[[Session 50.5 - The Wayne and Thimble Episode|Session 50.5]].** Captured, Wayne and Thimble met **[[Old Ash Tongue]]** in the salamanders' prison, who sang them the song of the Kyn'Ruthar, a destroyer who turned the green to grey "and then turned again." They broke out, won a trial by combat, and made peace. Vistro knew Thimble's medallion as King Atul's, the Mind Medallion. He asked them to rid the world of the temple in the lava lake, and gave them summoning tattoos. Old Ash Tongue joined the party.

## Part Seven: The Hollow Reliquary (Sessions 52 to 54)

- **[[Session 52 - The Hollow Reliquary|Session 52]].** The fire sages parted the barrier around **[[The Hollow Reliquary]]**, an Abyssal temple honoring the Hollow Vessel. Outside stood King Atul's army, all stone, frozen mid-charge. The Mind Medallion woke **King Atul**, and he went in with them. The party passed through the nave, fell through the Turning, and broke the Chamber of Body.
- **[[Session 53 - What Is Left of You|Session 53]].** The Mind trial: needles of void, false paths, and guiding each other blind. Wayne let a needle in willingly. The murals spoke of the Goliath chant Tyrn'Haka, the Endless Climb. In the Soul trial they rode a blade into the Vessel's memories. **Wayne and Thimble became the blade**, and their brands darkened. **Aalyn, Alaric and Bog held on.** Aalyn spent his free natural 20, his golden bowstring blazed, and **Sumina's soul appeared**, until Lolth erupted from a nest of spiders ("Insolent child") and dragged her back. King Atul, a mind with no soul, couldn't pass and stayed on the Soul floor. At the summit, the Hollow Vessel Remnant rose.
- **[[Session 54 - The Heart of the Void|Session 54]].** Thimble remembered the crystal gallery in Glimmerstone and his father's slap, and something in him wouldn't let the void take it. The Remnant pulled everyone into its domain, fed by three stone faces. Wayne broke Body, Aalyn broke Soul under a cloak of gold, and Alaric broke Mind. The Remnant's last stand began turning Wayne to stone again, and Jack's memory broke him free ("One of these days, you will fly"). His stone came back darker, an ebony. **Wayne tore the Shell of the Void Heart out of the Remnant's chest.** The Reliquary began to come apart, and a voice spoke at his shoulder: *"Oh, how perfect you are."*

## Where Things Stand

- The party is at the summit of a collapsing Reliquary, with **Malcanthet** at Wayne's shoulder. **Wayne holds the Shell.**
- **King Atul** is still on the Soul floor below.
- Wayne and Thimble's brands are dark. Aalyn still wears the gold cloak. Lolth knows he reached for Sumina.
- Still waiting above and below: Vistro's reaction, the vault of four doors and the Soulvite Colossus, the airship part for Bifflefapple, Vashara and the missing patrons, the Temple of 1000 Candles and Lolth's Spool, Luna's fate, Alaric's silver egg, and the Goliath elders' call home.
- Ahead, eventually: **Glimmerstone**, Thimble's home city, and the World Games.
