# Story Planning

> [!info] How to use this note
> A bank of places the party could spend real time in, and the threads that tie them together. **Nothing here is an arc yet.** Pull beats from any area and combine them into arcs as the campaign takes shape. Each area stands on its own, so none of them depends on another happening first.
> - **Target:** Campaign 1 ends around level 17, give or take. The party is level 8.
> - **The Tomes** of [[The Demonomicon]] stay in the background. Each area notes where one sits in case the party stumbles onto it, but nothing here depends on them.
> - **Established** facts link to their vault notes. Anything marked **Idea** is a proposal to keep, change, or cut.
> - Player-facing quest tracking lives in [[Quest Log]].

---

## The Throughline
Whatever order the areas come in, each one should move at least one of these forward.

- **The Second Sundering.** All five of [[The Godhand]] are reborn, and the Abyss is uniting to finish the invasion. Every area should show a piece of that: a front organization, an apostle, a lithoweave waking, a place where the Abyss bleeds through. See [[The Second Sundering]].
- **The brands.** Every branded PC finds a personal way to break their [[Abyssal Brands|Abyssal Brand]] and its soul contract. Bogtrotter dodged his with three natural 20s. Each area should give at least one PC a real step on that road.
- **The lithoweaves.** Every [[Lithoweaves|lithoweave]] that runs feeds [[The Heart of Entropy|Tharizdun]]. Each one the party finds or stops is a quiet piece of the finale, where the [[The Lattice of Heaven|Lattice]]'s strands get reconnected.
- **The hidden top of the hierarchy.** The Godhand believe they serve an impersonal Mandate. They serve Tharizdun. Hold the reveal for near the end. Until then, use only what Abyssal scripture already says (the "pale seed," "the one who caught it became the Deep").
- **Fixed endpoint.** Some or all of the Godhand fall, the Lattice is reconnected, and the party confronts Tharizdun, opening the way for the lawful planes in Campaign 2.

### Scale guide
*A loose sense of stakes by level, not a schedule.*
- **Levels 8 to 10:** regional. Cities, dungeons, one Godhand front at a time. The Second Sundering is rumor and omen.
- **Levels 11 to 14:** continental and planar. Apostles in person, other planes reachable, the Second Sundering out in the open.
- **Levels 15 to 17:** the war itself, the Godhand directly, and the finale.

> [!tip] Idea: the Pale Goddess as an ally of the brand-breakers
> Branded souls never reach her at death, so every branded elf is a soul taken from her. She has a reason to want brands broken. Her favor (through a shrine, a dream, or the Chain Knights) could be one route for a PC's brand, without her ever being a solved answer.

> [!tip] Idea: Jack came from beyond the severed planes
> Jack is "from another world" in a world cut off from the lawful planes. If he crossed from one of them, he's a living hint at Campaign 2 walking around in Campaign 1. Keep it buried, but it explains why he's hunting the Godhand.

## Areas of Exploration
Each area has: **Premise**, **Getting There**, **Leads Onward**, **Story Ties**, **Foundation Ideas**, and **Open Questions**.

### Hollowmere and Durnhollow *(current)*
**Premise.** The party's unfinished business in the city of mists and the ruins beneath it.
- Escape [[The Hollow Reliquary]] and answer to [[Vistro IV]].
- Finish [[Durnhollow]]: the lava floor, the power and the elevator, the Vault Room, the Soulvite Confluence and the Colossus, [[Caelum au Vermillion|Caelum]], and the lithoweave behind the final vault.
- Confront [[Vashara]] and her wendigos.
- The Temple of 1000 Candles, its lycanthropy ritual, and the Spool of Lolth's Thread.
- [[Luna au Vermillion|Luna]]'s detainment by the Chain Lords of [[The Holy Chain Knights]].
- Restore the airship with Professor Bifflefapple.

**Getting There.** Already here.

**Leads Onward** *(all established)*
- **The airship.** Once restored, it's the party's ticket to every other area here.
- **Atul's Dark Stone vault.** Aetherstone beneath [[Glimmerstone]]. The Hellhammer letters (Doran, Litho-armor) pointing to [[Drak'mor]]. [[Tiberius Blackfang]] as their courier, carrying Prince Romulus Darken's seal and a brand drawn in the margin.
- **The Reliquary's stone book** names [[The Soul Sanctum]] and shows its sigil.
- **The Temple's true seat** may be Sowengrad, in [[The Golden Republic]].
- **Luna and the Chain Lords** point at the order's leadership and Vermillion Bay.
- **[[Rai Zeph]]'s plea.** The Gor'Drak Dominion is ravaging the Goliath homelands, and the elders want their Kyn'Ruthar home.
- **Malrik's bag.** An aetherstone order to D. Bronzebottom in Glimmerstone, and charts marking another Ember in the sea between Midland and Syfaeril.
- **The cold over Hollowmere.** [[Vaelindra]] is coming.

**Story Ties.** Three Godhand in one city: Malcanthet (Vashara), Baphomet (the Temple), and Lolth (the Spool and the Cult). The Pale Goddess through the Chain Knights. The Plane of Fire through the salamanders. A dormant lithoweave in Durnhollow's last vault.

**Foundation Ideas**
- **Vashara is a tragedy, not a villain.** She learned to still a body but not a mind or a soul, so what she makes comes out as wendigos: she's a dark mirror of what Malcanthet wants from Wayne. The faceless helm ("For A. If I can't wake him...") says what she's really after: Atul back.
- **Idea: her last stand is at the lithoweave.** She tries to run Durnhollow's lithoweave with Soulvite to reverse Atul's stone. It's the first time the party sees a lithoweave wake, and (unknown to everyone) it feeds Tharizdun. Stopping her means choosing Atul's last chance or the world's safety.
- **Idea: the missing patrons are among the wendigos.** The four missing patrons were the raw material. Bastian Dray, in the cold room, was next in line. That makes the wendigo fights personal and quiet.
- **The Temple ritual shows the Abyss uniting.** A Baphomet temple running its ritual on Lolth's Spool is the first sign that the Godhand are cooperating. The Spool's thread shows only in moonlight, so the five anchors fire on a bright night.
- **Idea: the Temple's healing is real.** People really are cured there, and the city loves it. Shutting it down costs Hollowmere something, which keeps the tone tragic.
- **Why the Chain Lords took Luna** *(decided)*. Three reasons together: her obsession with what happened to her brother [[Caelum au Vermillion|Caelum]], the Tome of Malcanthet going missing from the Silver Keep on her watch, and a turn in support among her own knights led by [[Corvin Hale]]. Caelum is Vashara's pseudo-vessel in Durnhollow's deepest room: in practice a buffed-up wendigo with a huge greatsword in one hand.
- **The airship.** If the compression mechanism comes out cracked, Bifflefapple needs one more rare part. That part can point at whichever area you want next (Aetherstone, a Hellhammer smith, a Runestone component).

**Open Questions.** How Luna's detainment looks now. Whether Atul survives. Whether Vashara dies, flees, or finds some kind of peace.

### Glimmerstone and the Glimmerlands
**Premise.** Thimble's home city, carved into a shining geode mountain on Midland's western shore, during the World Games. A revolution brews between arcane wielders and those without magic. The Luminarch, the city's leading figure, has a special event planned. Thimble's father plays a villainous role.

**Getting There**
- Thimble going home.
- Atul's notes: the only large reserve of Aetherstone lies beneath Glimmerstone.
- Alaric's childhood promise from Fenril: the stone in the far west that held every color.
- The world tournament, first mentioned on the road to Hollowmere (Session 29). **Idea:** Bogtrotter is the obvious competitor, which gives him a spotlight.
- **"Little spark, come find me."** The voice beneath Glimmerstone that called to Thimble in the fog (Session 30).
- Malrik's aetherstone order to D. Bronzebottom. The Bronzebottoms run the mines and sit on the council.
- Thimble's two brothers, and Bifflefapple's grudge with the Danglesworths.
- The loose ends of Thimble's past: Zenina and the Luminox Convergence gala, and the black-robed man with a hobble who sent Muzzle's gnomes after him.

**Leads Onward**
- [[The Second Sundering]] begins here, so this city can be where the war stops being a rumor.
- Thimble learns what he is: kin to the obyriths, and the one who can relink the planes.
- The eladrin's oldest blades were cut from [[Aetherstone]], which points at [[Arborea]] and the old fey.
- **Idea:** the World Games bring delegations from every continent. Use them to introduce NPCs for any future area: Goliaths of Drak'mor, Vyrmor dragonborn, Calenthian elves, even Gor'Drak orcs.

**Story Ties.** Built into [[The Chaos Primordial]]'s body, on the first Sundering's last battlefield (the Shining Mountain of Abyssal scripture). Malcanthet loathes Thimble for carrying her Tome.
- **Idea: Graz'zt works here.** A revolution is perfect ground for the Godhand of ambition and deception, and it gives the party's oldest enemy a front of his own.

**Foundation Ideas**
- **The revolution is real, and someone is feeding both sides.** The grievances are honest. The Second Sundering hides behind them.
- **Thimble's father.** Why shape a son out of raw chaos? Options:
  - **(a) His own vessel.** A mirror of Malcanthet and Wayne: both members of the duo turn out to be *made* things.
  - **(b) A cure for the divide.** He believes the chaos-born can carry the mountain's power and end the gap between the arcane and everyone else.
  - **(c) He serves the Abyss**, as an apostle or something less.
- **The gallery memory.** Thimble holds on to his anger at his father (Session 54). Coming home should test that.
- **Idea: the Luminarch's event opens the mountain's heart.** A ceremony meant to share the Aetherstone core is the act that cracks the gateway.

**Open Questions.** Who the Luminarch is. What Thimble's father wants. What the Second Sundering looks like when it physically starts.

### Drak'mor
*Sites: [[The Soul Sanctum]], Vol'undar and [[The Rikk Borgar]], Hellhammer and the Ashpeaks, the Gor'Drak Dominion.*

**Premise.** Wayne's homeland: a proud, violent continent east of Midland, half under the orc empire of [[Oughan Gor'Drak]].

**Getting There**
- Malcanthet sends Wayne toward the Soul Sanctum, and the Reliquary names it.
- Session 55's memory of the bottom of the Rikk Borgar and the first half of [[The Soul Armor]].
- The Hellhammer letters from Atul's vault: Doran and Litho-armor.
- General Veraxous's letter: the Bloodlancers' invasion of Midland comes from here.
- [[Rai Zeph]] asked Wayne to go home: the Dominion is ravaging the Goliath homelands, and the elders need their protector.
- Bog knows Doran, the smith who makes armor like Tiberius's.
- Continuing Jack's journey.

**Leads Onward**
- The **Tyrn'Haka**, the Endless Climb, toward the second half of the Soul Armor. Where it ends is open.
- Litho-armor points at lithoweave technology.
- The floating mountain pieces around the Rikk Borgar may be fragments of [[Ysgard]].
- [[Vyrmor]] lies just south. The two were once joined.

**Story Ties.** Malcanthet's harvest at the Rikk Borgar and her house of making in the Sanctum. Orcus through the Dominion. Annam, Memnor, and Naki-Uthai through the Goliaths' god of the Endless Climb. [[The Volkarr]] serve Malcanthet without knowing it.

**Foundation Ideas**
- **The Soul Sanctum keeps its vigil.** Abyssal scripture calls it the house "where her hollowers keep their vigil." **Idea:** they still do. Hollowed things go on finishing work for a mistress who never comes back, and the second step of Wayne's hollowing (the needles) is still waiting there for him.
- **Body, Mind, Soul continues.** The Reliquary covered the Vessel's story. The Sanctum is where his mind was taken, and the Rikk Borgar is where his soul fought back.
- **A sacred rite that's poisoned.** At some point the party learns the Void Trial has been a harvest for ages. Do they tell the Volkarr? Goliath faith is built on a hollowed god.
- **Idea: Hellhammer has a lithoweave.** The smiths of the World Forge have been studying a dormant one, which is where Litho-armor comes from. It's a crafting hub, a second known lithoweave, and a link to Atul's research.
- **Idea: the Dominion is raising the Sundering's dead.** Orcus's apostle drafts the old battlefields into the Bloodlancers. It's the Second Sundering's military face.
- **Tiberius.** He carried the Hellhammer letters and bears a brand. Drak'mor is a natural place to find out what he's doing.

**Open Questions.** Where the Tyrn'Haka ends. Where Jack is. How the Volkarr react to the truth.

### Syfaeril
*Sites: Calenthia and [[House Fenwyn]], [[Silath Shal]], the Cradle of Ash, the Wood Elf Wilds.*

**Premise.** The elven continent west of Midland, ruled through the Septarion of Calenthia, where House Fenwyn is a Lesser House tolerated but never trusted.

**Getting There**
- Alaric's family and [[Fenril Fenwyn|Fenril]].
- Silath Shal, the Spear of Heaven, where legend says would-be gods climb.
- The Cradle of Ash points east toward the Rikk Borgar.
- Aalyn chasing anything fey that could lead him to Sumina.

**Leads Onward**
- The Fenwyn vault opens onto a pocket of [[Limbo]].
- Arborea and Titania's court.
- **Idea:** Silath Shal as the end of the Tyrn'Haka. Wayne's Endless Climb would literally end on the tallest mountain in the world.

**Story Ties.** Lolth's origin as Araushnee, Titania's twin. Fenmarel, her old lover, is the Fenwyn ancestor. The archmage who wrote the Demonomicon was an Arborean elf.

**Foundation Ideas**
- **The Fenwyn are outcasts in silk.** They're a Lesser House because of where they came from, even if only the Head of House knows it.
- **Idea: Lolth wants Alaric.** She holds Aalyn's love captive, and she might court Alaric as an echo of the lover she lost. One spider, two party members, two very different kinds of hold.
- **Fenril.** The Sasuke and Itachi dynamic. Options for what stands between them:
  - **(a)** Fenril learned the family's secret and left to hunt Lolth, and did something that made Alaric think it was his fault.
  - **(b)** Alaric's runaway night set something in motion that cost Fenril his place in the house.
- **The Cradle of Ash.** **Idea:** the Ash Elves are descendants of something burned in the Sundering, and their valley "pointing east" is an old pilgrim road toward the Rikk Borgar.

**Open Questions.** The current Head of House. Fenril's story. What's in the vault.

### Vyrmor
**Premise.** The wild jungle continent south of Drak'mor: ruins of a vanished civilization, ley-rich land, a sanctuary for dragons, and seven Dragonlords who each hold a Dragonsword.

**Getting There**
- Bogtrotter, a bronze dragonborn, in a land where dragonborn revere dragons. Vaelindra called him kin and woke a silver heritage in him, and Baphomet wanted to know who made a dragonborn.
- Vaelindra and the stolen egg: dragon matters get settled among dragons.
- Tiberius: a tabaxi with a dragon-scale glaive.
- Alaric's line to Aurixia: "Our family saved your kind when the corruption came."

**Leads Onward**
- Drak'mor, to the north.
- The Dragonswords, as weapons fit for a finale.

**Story Ties.** Baphomet, Godhand of the labyrinth and the beast, fits jungle ruins. Fenmarel's outcasts may have saved the dragons in the Sundering.

**Foundation Ideas**
- **Why Bog is unbranded** *(decided)*. No hidden cause: he rolled three natural 20s in a row to resist the brand at the ritual. His silver heritage and Baphomet's interest are still open hooks.
- **Idea: the vanished civilization tended the Lattice.** Its ruins hold the best surviving records of what lithoweaves really are.
- **Tiberius as the road not taken.** A branded survivor already hunting the Godhand alone, showing the party what they could become.

**Open Questions.** Bog's backstory (still blank). Who the Dragonlords are. Whether Vaelindra's thread resolves here or in Hollowmere.

### Oakhollow and the Free Kingdoms *(return)*
**Premise.** Where the campaign began. The aftermath of the Baron's bargain, the ascension beneath the Grand Cathedral, and Rosalie's death, in a patchwork of feuding city-states.

**Getting There**
- Aalyn's unfinished business: Rosalie's death, [[Romulus Darken|Prince Romulus Darken]], Ino Silvertone.
- Prince Romulus Darken's seal on the Hellhammer letter.
- Wayne's sending stone to Tiberius.
- The Godspire site in [[The Tall Oak Forest]].
- The Bloodlancers pushing in from the Eastern Shores.

**Leads Onward**
- Darkstone, the Darken family's mercenary companies, and [[Orthus]], the Enclave's adviser there.
- [[Talula Fern]], still loose somewhere after the ascension.
- [[The Silver Lyre]], for Aalyn.
- Aalyn's mother, if her home is nearby.

**Story Ties.** Graz'zt's origin. [[Malrik Greene]] and the Obsidian Enclave. The Godspire, the first lithoweave. The lilacs beneath the cathedral.

**Foundation Ideas**
- **Coming home changed.** The party watched Graz'zt rise and the Godhand appear here, and Thimble tore a chasm through the city getting out. Oakhollow should still carry those scars, and the Bloodlancers may have taken it.
- **Idea: Graz'zt is sentimental about his bridge.** The goblin is still inside him. A small, petty, personal touch from a god makes him unsettling.
- **Who woke the Godspire.** Options: **(a)** Orthus, who reported the activity there, sent Malrik to the ruins, and seemed to know what Wayne's gemstone was. **(b)** The Darkens, chasing the "weapon" the Bloodlancers wanted. **(c)** No one meant to. It woke because the Mandate pressed on it.
- **The Architect.** The hidden shrine near the Godspire ("Trust in the design," the inverted ruins, four missing figures) is a ready-made thread toward whoever built the lithoweaves.
- **Idea: Aalyn's mother's songs are fey.** "Her songs weren't really hers." They could be Verenestra's, or songs the Silver Lyre has kept for longer than it knows.

**Open Questions.** Oakhollow's current state. What happened with the Fern and Darken alliance.

### The Golden Republic
**Premise.** A prosperous western empire of wheat fields and trade. Its capital, Sowengrad, may be the true seat of the Temple of 1000 Candles.

**Getting There.** Following the Temple's trail out of Hollowmere.

**Leads Onward.** Whitehaven, which guards against the dangerous magic of the north, points at [[Nythkael]].

**Story Ties.** Baphomet.

**Foundation Ideas**
- **Idea: the Temple is the Republic's healthcare.** Its charity runs the empire's hospitals, and lycanthropy spreads through real cures. Taking it down could break the Republic.
- **Idea: Baphomet's labyrinth under the capital.** Sowengrad's catacombs as the Temple's true heart.

### Runestone and the Obsidian Enclave
**Premise.** The arcane heart of Midland, built in a colossal crater, home of the secretive mages who advise every court.

**Getting There**
- [[Selara Du'Cant]], the Enclave's advisor in Hollowmere.
- Malrik's disappearance.
- The Enclave helped seal Durnhollow, and several of its mages died doing it.
- Durnhollow's Soulvite drew on Runeland ley energy.

**Story Ties.** Graz'zt through Malrik.

**Foundation Ideas**
- **Idea: the Enclave maps lithoweaves.** They know more about them than anyone and may be waking them as power sources, feeding Tharizdun as blindly as the Godhand do.
- **Selara's own agenda** can cut either way: ally, rival, or someone who'd sell the party out.

**Open Questions.** The Enclave's origin.

### The Holy Chain Knights and Vermillion Bay
**Premise.** Luna's order and her family's home. A militant faith of the Pale Goddess, sworn to hunt and bind the corruption of the deep, led by the Chain Lords.

**Getting There**
- Luna's arrest, and Corvin Hale's hunt for the party.
- Luna's brother, Caelum, Vashara's pseudo-vessel.
- [[Dame]], the one friendly knight left.
- How the Silver branch came to hold the Tome of Malcanthet.

**Story Ties.** The Pale Goddess. Berserk's Holy Iron Chain Knights are the model.

**Foundation Ideas**
- **The brands make the party prey.** The Chain Knights hunt the corruption of the deep, and five of the party carry it on their skin. Allies today, inquisitors the moment someone sees a brand.
- **Idea: the three branches.** Gold as the leadership and inquisition, Silver as city garrisons, Bronze as hunters on the frontiers.
- **Idea: the Chain Lords have been infiltrated.** A faith that hunts the Abyss is the best place for the Abyss to hide.

**Open Questions.** Where Vermillion Bay is. Who the Chain Lords are.

### Beyond the World
*Planar excursions. The chaotic outer planes and the inner planes are still bound to the world.*

- **[[Limbo]].** Through the Fenwyn vault or a card from [[The Deck of Many Things]]. Fenmarel, if he lives, and his wolves. Alaric's long-term goal of a wolf companion.
- **[[Arborea]].** Titania's court and the eladrin. Petitioning the fey about Verenestra. Aalyn would be walking into a family feud far older than him.
- **[[Ysgard]].** Possibly reached through the floating fragments around the Rikk Borgar. The Shield-Halls where Naki-Uthai drank his kin. Annam, and maybe Memnor.

- **[[The Abyss]].** Late campaign. The Godhand hold no physical form in the world outside Moon Darkenings, and the deeper layers can't be reached by ordinary means. **Idea:** the Second Sundering opens the ways in.
