# The Pale Goddess

## What the World Believes
Goddess of the moon, dreams, and the passage of souls. The most widely worshipped faith in Midland, and patron of the [[The Holy Chain Knights|Holy Chain Knights]].

> [!info] DM Note
> The Pale Goddess is Sehanine Moonbow, a fey deity of [[Arborea]]. Her worship and lore follow the official Sehanine Moonbow.

> [!secret] Not the moon itself
> revealed:: false
> She represents [[The Moon]] but has no connection to [[The Order Primordial]], whose body it truly is.

> [!secret] Branded souls bypass her
> revealed:: false
> A soul bearing an [[Abyssal Brands|Abyssal Brand]] never reaches her at death. It is delivered intact and aware to [[The Abyss]].
