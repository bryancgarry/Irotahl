# The Pale Goddess

## What the World Believes
Goddess of the moon, dreams, and the passage of souls. The most widely worshipped faith in Midland, and patron of the [[The Holy Chain Knights|Holy Chain Knights]].

> [!info] DM Note
> The Pale Goddess is Sehanine Moonbow, a fey deity of [[Arborea]]. Her worship and lore follow the official Sehanine Moonbow.

> [!info] One god among many
> She is a member of the elven pantheon, one of several deities across Irotahl's peoples who represent the moon. The world is polytheistic, and different gods can stand for similar or the same things. The primordials outrank all of them.

> [!secret] Not the moon itself
> revealed:: false
> She represents [[The Moon]] but has no connection to what it truly is: the calcified soul of [[The Chaos Primordial]].

> [!secret] Branded souls bypass her
> revealed:: false
> A soul bearing an [[Abyssal Brands|Abyssal Brand]] never reaches her, or any god, at death. It is delivered intact and aware to [[The Abyss]].
