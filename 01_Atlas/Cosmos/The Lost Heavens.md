# The Lost Heavens

## What the World Believes
Only scraps of scripture suggest anything ever lay beyond the planes the world knows. No one in Irotahl knows what a celestial or a devil is.

> [!info] DM Note
> Mount Celestia, the Nine Hells, and every other lawful outer plane (Mechanus, Arcadia, Acheron, and the rest) are severed. Their details and the Shattering itself are Campaign 2 material. Players slipping into out-of-character terminology about devils or celestials is an intentional in-world reveal, not a lore error to correct.

> [!secret] They wove the Lattice and chained Tharizdun
> revealed:: false
> Led by Mount Celestia and the Nine Hells, the lawful planes wove [[The Lattice of Heaven]]. When [[The Heart of Entropy|Tharizdun]] shattered it, they chained him at the bottom of [[The Abyss]], then cut their own strands to save themselves and withdrew. Within a generation they passed into myth, and within several more into silence.

## Connections
- Campaign 1 ends with the Lattice's strands reconnected, opening the way for the lawful planes to return in Campaign 2. See [[The Second Sundering]].
