# The Time Primordial

## What the World Believes
Almost nothing. Some dragons and dragon-revering peoples keep fragments of a myth about a great dragon older than the world. Most scholars treat it as a tale dragons tell to flatter themselves.

> [!info] DM Note
> Keep this almost entirely out of play in Campaign 1. Inspired by Akatosh in *The Elder Scrolls*. Dragons in Irotahl are **independent** of it: they are not its children or its priests.

> [!secret] The third primordial, alive
> revealed:: false
> Time is the third primordial, a dragon god, and the only primordial still alive. When Order and Chaos clashed, Time turned their collision from a frozen instant into a sequence of events. That sequence is causality. Every cause and effect in the world runs on Time.

> [!secret] Where it is
> revealed:: false
> Undetermined and hidden for now.

> [!secret] Time and the Mandate
> revealed:: false
> [[The Heart of Entropy|Tharizdun]] presses on causality from his chains to steer events toward his release. If causality is Time's, then the Mandate the Godhand serve is Tharizdun leaning on a primordial's power. How Time regards that is open.

> [!secret] The end of Time
> revealed:: false
> Every age ends. At the end of Time waits [[The Eternal Void|the Devourer]], the thing that eats the world when its time runs out. Campaign 3.
