# The Moon

## What the World Believes
A pale light that keeps the dark at bay. Many faiths claim it: the elves know it through [[The Pale Goddess]], and other peoples through gods of their own. When it fails during a [[Moon Darkenings|Moon Darkening]], disaster follows.

> [!secret] The moon is the Chaos Primordial's soul
> revealed:: false
> When order struck [[The Chaos Primordial]] at [[Creation]], its body calcified, and its soul calcified with it. That stilled soul hangs in the sky as the moon. The broken-off piece of its body lies on the land as [[Glimmerstone]]. They are one being in two places.

> [!secret] The moon and the Embers
> revealed:: false
> [[Embers of Sacrifice]] are the calcified souls of chaos-beings, the moon's children. That is why they look like small pale moons. When an Ember is invoked, black void erupts from it and pulls those in the ritual into a fragment of [[The Abyss]].
> A **Moon Darkening** is the same thing at the scale of the sky. When one of the five great Embers lets an obyrith soul take flesh again, the parent soul answers: darkness floods its face, its light fails, and the barrier between the Abyss and the world thins.

> [!info] DM Note
> The Pale Goddess is one deity among many who represent the moon. She has no special claim on it. Primordials outrank gods.
