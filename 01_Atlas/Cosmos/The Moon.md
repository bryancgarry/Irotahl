# The Moon

## What the World Believes
The domain of [[The Pale Goddess]], a light that keeps the dark at bay. When it fails during a [[Moon Darkenings|Moon Darkening]], disaster follows.

> [!secret] The moon is the Order Primordial's body
> revealed:: false
> The moon is the body of [[The Order Primordial]]. Its light is order, and it holds the barrier between [[The Abyss]] and the material plane a little firmer every night it shines. The Pale Goddess is not connected to it. Mortals simply know the moon through her.
