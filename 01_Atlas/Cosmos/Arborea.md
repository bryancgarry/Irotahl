# Arborea

## What the World Believes
The Bright Courts. Close, wild, and wounded. Home of the eladrin and the Archfey, ruled by Queen Titania.

## Role in History
A chaotic outer plane that stayed bound to the world after [[The Shattering of Spheres]]. During [[The Sundering of Planes]], Arborea and its eladrin fought beside the giants of [[Ysgard]] and the mortal peoples against the obyriths.

**The eladrin blades.** The eladrin fought with blades colored by their seasonal courts: green for spring, gold for summer, blue for autumn, and silver for winter. The silver blades were poured, and while the metal was still molten, the warrior sang part of themselves into it, so the blade remembered who held it. This is the forerunner of [[Soulvite]]. The oldest blades were cut from [[Aetherstone]].

> [!secret] Lolth was Titania's twin
> revealed:: false
> [[Lolth]] was once an Archfey of Arborea and Titania's twin sister. Her fall draws on Araushnee: betrayal of her own court and a forbidden affair with [[Fenmarel]], who was cast out into [[Limbo]] for it.

> [!secret] Verenestra is Lolth's captive
> revealed:: false
> Lolth holds the Archfey Verenestra captive. [[Aalyn Ravaceran|Aalyn]] knows her as Sumina, his believed-dead childhood love.

> [!secret] The outcasts' flank
> revealed:: false
> During the Sundering, [[Fenmarel]] returned from Limbo with a host of outcast elves and the wolves of Limbo. The courts refused them their banners, and afterward refused to take them back. They settled in Syfaeril as [[House Fenwyn]].
