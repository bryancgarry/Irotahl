# The Chaos Primordial

## What the World Believes
In myth, one of two great powers that made the world and died making it.

## What Actually Happened
Raw chaos, shaped by causality, clashed with [[The Order Primordial]] and ignited [[Creation|Irotahl]] into being. Struck by the power of order, Chaos calcified.

> [!secret] Its soul is the moon, its body is Glimmerstone
> revealed:: false
> Its stilled soul hangs in the sky as [[The Moon]]. A fragment of its body broke off in the clash and fell onto the western shores of Midland. The gnomes of [[Glimmerstone]] carved their city into it without ever knowing what it was. Its mines yield pure [[Aetherstone]]. The moon and Glimmerstone are one being in two places.

> [!secret] Its children
> revealed:: false
> The raw chaos spilled in the clash gave rise to beings of chaos. The greatest were the five **obyriths**, who became [[The Godhand]]. When chaos-beings die, their souls calcify the way their parent's did, into pale stones: the [[Embers of Sacrifice]].

> [!secret] Thimble is chaos-born
> revealed:: partial
> [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]] was shaped out of raw chaos by his father, using the Glimmerstone fragment of the Chaos Primordial's body. That makes him kin to the obyriths.
> **Revealed so far:** Malcanthet called him "a chaos born" in front of the party (Session 21). **Still hidden:** what it means, and that his father made him.

## Connections
- The final battle of [[The Sundering of Planes]] was fought on the site that is now Glimmerstone, and [[The Second Sundering]] begins there.
