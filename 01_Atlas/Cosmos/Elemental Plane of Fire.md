# Elemental Plane of Fire

An inner plane that stayed bound to the world after [[The Shattering of Spheres]]. The world knows it mainly through the fire salamanders and elemental magic.

## Role in History
During [[The Sundering of Planes]], the Abyss drove a wedge into the Plane of Fire and raised [[The Hollow Reliquary]] on a stolen shore there. Because [[The First Hollow Vessel]] had stilled the Ashen Reach and whole salamander nations, the children of fire laid siege to the Reliquary, led by the war-sage Vistro, who bound it in a ring of living fire.

When the Sundering broke the world, the Reliquary was flung into the stone beneath [[Durnhollow]], dragging the ring and the siege's vanguard with it. Their descendants, the [[The Fire Sages of Vistro|Fire Sages]] under Vistro IV, have held the fire ever since.
