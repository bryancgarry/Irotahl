# Ysgard

The plane of giants and endless battle, where the halls of the Ordning stand. The Abyss called it the Shield-Halls.

## Role in History
Ysgard and its giants were the heart of [[The Sundering of Planes]]. Its giants held the line against the Five, beside [[Arborea]], the Beastlands, and the mortal peoples of the world.

> [!secret] The Vessel was sent against his own kin
> revealed:: false
> [[Malcanthet]] built [[The First Hollow Vessel]] to break the giants' line. He was a prince of Ysgard. On the Shield-Halls, giants knelt to the prince they remembered, and he drank them as they knelt. Their souls inside him are what eventually turned him.

> [!info] DM Note
> Optional: the floating mountain pieces around [[The Rikk Borgar]] could be fragments of Ysgard that fell when the world broke.
