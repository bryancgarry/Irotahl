# Limbo

## What the World Believes
Almost nothing. To scholars, Limbo is a word for the formless chaos past the edge of the world, if they know the word at all.

## Role in History
A chaotic outer plane, still bound to the world after [[The Shattering of Spheres]]. Pure, churning chaos: matter and shape that won't hold still unless a will holds them.

> [!secret] Fenmarel's exile
> revealed:: false
> When [[Fenmarel]]'s affair with Araushnee (the Archfey who became [[Lolth]]) came to light, he was cast out of [[Arborea]] into Limbo. He came back out of it during [[The Sundering of Planes]] with a host of outcasts and the wolves of Limbo.

> [!secret] The wolves of Limbo
> revealed:: false
> Fenmarel's hunting pack still runs in Limbo, led by a great wolf in the vein of Fenrir. Winning one as a companion is [[Alaric Fenwyn|Alaric]]'s super long-term goal.
> **Open:** the great wolf's name, and what it takes to bring one into the world.

> [!secret] Chaos made into chance
> revealed:: false
> [[The Deck of Many Things]] was made here. Fenmarel turned Limbo's raw chaos into fate dealt from a deck.

> [!secret] The Fenwyn vault
> revealed:: false
> [[House Fenwyn]] keeps a vault in their home city that opens onto a pocket of Limbo, a storage space with no fixed size or shape.
