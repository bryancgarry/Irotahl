# The Soul Sanctum

> [!summary] At a Glance
> Old ruins half buried in the Drak'mor mountains, known to the Goliaths only as ruins. Over a doorway is carved a plain triangle, point down, with a pupil-less eye at its center. [[Wayne Kerr]] was turned to stone here. Inspired by Hollow Knight's Soul Sanctum.

## What the World Believes
Abyssal scripture speaks of it only in religious terms, as "the Mother's house of making, where her hollowers keep their vigil," never as a place on a map.

> [!secret] Where the Vessel was made
> revealed:: false
> The Sanctum is [[Malcanthet]]'s house of making in the Abyss, where her hollowers perfected the art of [[Hollowing and Petrification|hollowing]] and where Naki-Uthai was hollowed into [[The First Hollow Vessel]]. The Reliquary honored the Vessel. The Sanctum made him. When the Sundering broke the world, it was flung through the planes like [[The Hollow Reliquary]] and came to rest in Drak'mor.

> [!secret] What happened to Wayne here
> revealed:: false
> Malcanthet reached through Wayne's Void Trial mark with a projection or vestige and began hollowing him where the first Vessel was made. His body was stilled into stone. The piercing of his mind never finished.

## Seeds
- The Hollow Reliquary's stone book names the Sanctum and shows its sigil (guaranteed).
- Malcanthet sends Wayne here from the Reliquary's Seat.

> [!info] DM Note
> Wayne's next destination after the Reliquary. Open: its interior, what remains inside, and whether its hollowers still labor there.
