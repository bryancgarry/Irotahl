# Drak'mor

> [!summary] At a Glance
> The continent east of Midland and north of [[Vyrmor]]. Violent, proud, and half under the Gor'Drak Dominion. Home of [[The Rikk Borgar]] and [[Wayne Kerr]]'s people.

## The Gor'Drak Dominion
The orc empire of Oughan Gor'Drak, where strength is the only merit. Its Bloodlancers drive the invasion of Midland's Eastern Shores.

> [!secret] Oughan serves Orcus
> revealed:: false
> Oughan is an apostle of [[Orcus]], whose influence is strongest in Drak'mor. The Tome of Orcus is somewhere in Drak'mor.

## The Goliath Tribes and Vol'undar
The Goliaths gather at the monastery of **Vol'undar**, home of the **Volkarr**, the ancient shamans who choose warriors for the Void Trial. There are five tribes:

| Tribe | Aspect |
| --- | --- |
| Kerr'Athrai | Cloud. Roam the floating mountain pieces around the Rikk Borgar. |
| Igna'Thar | Fire |
| Vaar'Thain | Frost |
| Gor'Thrall | Stone and hill |
| Zeph'Thale | Storm |

The Goliaths honor Naki-Uthai, the Brave Climber, as a god of bravery and the Endless Climb.

> [!secret] Their god was real
> revealed:: false
> Naki-Uthai was a real giant prince and [[The First Hollow Vessel]].

## The Ashpeaks
The dwarves of the Ashstone Kingdom and the city of **Hellhammer**, home of the World Forge.
