# The Rikk Borgar

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A great abyss at the center of [[Drak'mor]], sacred to the Goliath tribes, ringed by floating pieces of mountain where the Kerr'Athrai roam. Believed bottomless. Depending on who you ask, it is the primeval void from which the world was made, a gateway to hell, or the source of all life.

## The Void Trial
The Goliaths' most sacred rite. Only warriors chosen by the Volkarr attempt it: a brutal descent through fire and the void's temptation, survived by very few. Those who return with a rare stone from the bottom earn the title **Kyn'Ruthar**.

[[Wayne Kerr]] completed the trial. He has no memory of what happened below.

> [!secret] Malcanthet's harvest
> revealed:: false
> Long ago, [[Malcanthet]] corrupted the bottom of the Rikk Borgar. For ages, the trial has sorted Goliath warriors for her without the Goliaths knowing. The stone a trial-taker brings back is her mark, and her tether: through it she can reach those she has selected, even while the Abyss remains hard to cross. She selected Wayne this way.

> [!secret] The Kyn'Ruthar's last stand
> revealed:: false
> A remnant of the turned Vessel's last stand still fights back down there. It judges the trial-takers too, and can leave a spark of itself in them. Wayne carries one, which is why he can go either way.
