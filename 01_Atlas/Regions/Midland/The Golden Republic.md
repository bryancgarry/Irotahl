# The Golden Republic

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A prosperous western empire stretching from golden wheat fields to northern tundra, ruled by diplomacy and trade.

## Notable Places
- **Sowengrad.** The capital in the golden plains.
- **Windhaven.** Powerful trade city to the north.
- **Whitehaven.** Northern tundra city guarding against the dangerous magic of the north.
- **Sundos.** Controls the northern edge of [[The Golden Sands]].

> [!secret] The Temple's true seat
> revealed:: false
> Sowengrad may be the secret headquarters of [[The Temple of 1000 Candles]].
