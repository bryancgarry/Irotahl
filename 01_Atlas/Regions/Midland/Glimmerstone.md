# Glimmerstone

> [!summary] At a Glance
> The gnome city carved into a giant shining geode mountain in [[The Glimmerlands]], on Midland's western shore. Its mines yield [[Aetherstone]]. Home of [[Thimblewick Danglesworth|Thimble]].

## Overview
The next major arc of the campaign takes place here. When the party arrives, the World Games are being held in the city.

## Key NPCs
- **The Luminarch.** The city's leading figure.
- **Thimble's father.** Plays a villainous role in the arc.

## The City Today
- A small revolution is brewing between arcane wielders and those without magic.
- The World Games draw visitors from across Irotahl.
- A special event involves the Luminarch.

> [!secret] The mountain is the Chaos Primordial
> revealed:: false
> The mountain is the calcified body of [[The Chaos Primordial]]. The gnomes carved their city into it without ever knowing what it was.

> [!secret] The Sundering's last battlefield
> revealed:: false
> The final battle of [[The Sundering of Planes]] was fought on this site. Abyssal scripture calls it the Shining Mountain.

> [!secret] Where the Second Sundering begins
> revealed:: false
> [[The Second Sundering]] starts here. The city's politics are its cover.

> [!secret] Thimble is chaos-born
> revealed:: false
> Thimble's father shaped him out of raw chaos using the Primordial's body (or a part of it). Thimble does not know.

> [!info] DM Note
> Open: how Thimble's father, the revolution, the World Games, and the Luminarch's event connect.

## Played
- **The Bronzebottoms** run much of the mining and crystal exports as chief arcane etherists, and sit on the council. Thimble's father sits on the city leader's advisory council, and his family owns many of the high-rises. Malrik ordered aetherstone from a D. Bronzebottom. See [[Session 22 - A Long Night Ends]].
