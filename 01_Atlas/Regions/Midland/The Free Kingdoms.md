# The Free Kingdoms

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A patchwork of feuding city-states.

## Notable Places
- **Darkstone.** Seat of the Darken family and their mercenary companies.
- **[[Oakhollow]].** Once ruled by Baron Fern. [[The Tall Oak Forest]] lies outside it.
- **Silverwind.** Merchant city of trade and diplomacy.
- **Brackenholt.** Forest land governed by a council of druids.
