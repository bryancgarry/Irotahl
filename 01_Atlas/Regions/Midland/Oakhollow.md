# Oakhollow

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A city of [[The Free Kingdoms]], once ruled by Baron Fern, where the campaign began. [[The Tall Oak Forest]] lies just outside it.

## Notable Places
- **The Grand Cathedral.** Beneath it, the Bridge King's ascension took place. Purple lilacs grow in the ascension room.
- **The Bridge King's bridge.** Home of a petty, cruel goblin the party met in their earliest days.

- Baron Fern, Malrik Greene (former Obsidian Enclave advisor), the Bridge King.

## What Happened Here
- **The branding** (around sessions 2 and 3). Baron Fern invoked an [[Embers of Sacrifice|Ember of Sacrifice]] to save his injured daughter and offered up the party. See [[Abyssal Brands]].
- **The stone trees arc.** The party stopped the Godspire lithoweave in the Tall Oak Forest.
- **The ascension.** A few days after the branding, [[Malrik Greene]] used the same Ember on the Bridge King beneath the Grand Cathedral. The party was present. Most did not understand what they witnessed.

> [!secret] The Bridge King became Graz'zt
> revealed:: false
> The Ember found its match in the Bridge King, who became [[Graz'zt]] at the Fifth [[Moon Darkenings|Moon Darkening]]. Graz'zt made Malrik his apostle and pulled him into the Abyss. The players do not know this.

> [!secret] The Baron's daughter
> revealed:: false
> The Baron was not the Ember's match, so his bargain only half held: his daughter was held in a temporary stasis rather than healed. She has since died.
