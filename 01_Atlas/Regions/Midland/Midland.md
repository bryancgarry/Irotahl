# Midland

> [!summary] At a Glance
> The largest continent, at the center of the world. Mostly human, home to every people. Often the prize in other lands' wars.

## Regions
- **[[The Golden Republic]].** Prosperous western empire, wheat fields to tundra, ruled by diplomacy and trade.
- **[[The Golden Sands]].** The sun elves and their city of Solrio.
- **[[The Northern Peaks]].** The moon elves and their city of Lunstar.
- **[[The Runelands]].** The arcane heart of Midland. Home of Runestone and the Obsidian Enclave.
- **[[The Free Kingdoms]].** A patchwork of feuding city-states, including [[Oakhollow]].
- **[[The Obsidian Fields]].** The dwarven Blackrock Kingdom.
- **[[Hollowmere]]** and the ruins of **[[Durnhollow]]** beneath it.
- **[[The Glimmerlands]].** The western shore and [[Glimmerstone]].
- **Vermillion Bay.** Home of the au Vermillion family. Location and character still open.
- **The Eastern Shores.** The Bloodlancers' invasion front.
