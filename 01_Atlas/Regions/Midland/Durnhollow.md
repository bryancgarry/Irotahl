# Durnhollow

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A ruined dwarven city and steam-powered facility beneath [[Hollowmere]], built on silver mining and [[Soulvite]] refinement. A three-layer megadungeon calibrated for five players at levels 7 to 8. Inspired by Dwemer ruins, Destiny raids, Zelda dungeons, Dwarf Fortress, and Deep Rock Galactic.

## History
Durnhollow was ruled by King Atul Stonemarch and his queen Vashara, a drow princess. Four elementals (air, water, fire, earth), drow diplomatic gifts to aid Soulvite research, were later amplified beyond control by Runeland energy. The city fell at the Fourth [[Moon Darkenings|Moon Darkening]], about 1,000 years ago. Obsidian Enclave mages helped seal it, and several died doing it.

Lore is delivered through the journals of chief metallurgist Clausius Clapeyron, with figures such as Foreman Bram Stonevein, floor worker Hera Dunwick, and the drow attaché Ambassador Ilyss. Players discover all lore cold.

> [!secret] Vashara's fall
> revealed:: false
> Durnhollow's miners broke into the cavern holding [[The Hollow Reliquary]]. Vashara opened its doors with her own blood, learned hollowing from its stone book, and found an [[Embers of Sacrifice|Ember of Sacrifice]] inside, which corrupted her. When Atul led his army across the lava lake to stop her, she petrified them all. She now makes wendigos with a crude imitation of [[Hollowing and Petrification|hollowing]].

> [!secret] Ambassador Ilyss
> revealed:: false
> Ilyss is implied to be complicit in the city's downfall.

> [!secret] Vashara is still active
> revealed:: false
> Fresh ink, a live experimental rat, and a recently used teleportation circle show Vashara still works here.

## Layout
Entry reveals the **Soulvite Confluence**: an altar with four empty silver cradles before a sealed vault housing the **Soulvite Colossus** (CR 14 final boss, with four swappable modular augments). All four elemental mini-bosses must be defeated. Each drops a Soulvite crystal fragment, and all four are deposited at the Confluence at once to open the vault. Each mini-boss uses Destiny-style mechanics that echo into the Colossus fight. A central magnetic elevator spine connects the layers and stays offline until reactivated on the lava layer.

### Steam Layer (Logistics and Dispatch)
20 rooms on a linear spine with two hubs and an East Wing for air arcane experimentation.
- **Boss:** Tempest Warden (CR 9 air elemental). A vertical cylinder arena with 7 pressure plates that trigger vent sequences to ground it for damage phases.
- **Puzzles:** pipe control room, terminal, cooling chamber, Steam Nexus.
- **Flavor:** the Commissary, a social encounter with the Old Lunch Lady Construct.
- **Loot:** Rope of Climbing, Sending Stones, Walloping Ammunition ×10, Boots of the Winterlands, Pipes of Haunting, Silverwind.

### Water Layer (The Sluice Works)
20 rooms, hub-and-spoke, inspired by the Lakebed Temple.
- **Core mechanic:** current reversal. Act 1 has a fixed clockwise current. Act 2 gives players control after the Central Current Engine is activated.
- Four colored pump rooms (yellow, green, red, purple) gate the **Tide Warden** arena at the bottom center.
- **Puzzles:** the quenching chamber (three-rune flood puzzle for the green pump key) and a tidal lock corridor.

### Lava Layer (Forging and Mining)
Four color-coded pump stations around a central lava channel, with a foreman's observation platform. Periodic lava surges. Fire and earth mini-bosses. A mines transition level sits between layers.

### The Vault
The Soulvite Colossus fight on the top floor. A [[Lithoweaves|lithoweave]] rests in the chamber after it.

> [!secret] The vault's lithoweave
> revealed:: false
> It is not running. Vashara uses it to experiment on the hollowing process.

## Enemies
Dwemer-inspired constructs: Dwarven Spiders, Spheres, Centurions, Ballista, and Surge Elementals. Wendigos.
