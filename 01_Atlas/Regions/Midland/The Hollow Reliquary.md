# The Hollow Reliquary

> [!summary] At a Glance
> An Abyssal pilgrimage site of lightless black stone with one soaring spire, leaning a few degrees off true, in the lava lake beyond [[Durnhollow]]. Its faith: **to rise, you must fall.** Inside, down is holy, and pilgrims climb the spire by descending into it. Run in Session 52.

> [!info] DM Note
> Everything inside is Abyssal scripture. It honors [[The First Hollow Vessel]] as he was made and never hints that he turned. Session 52 holds the full read-aloud text, dialog, and statblocks.

## History
The Seat of the Vessel, where he was enthroned between battles and worshipped by pilgrims who wished to become as he was. The Abyss raised it on a stolen shore of the [[Elemental Plane of Fire]]. The Vessel had stilled the Ashen Reach and whole salamander nations, so the children of fire laid siege, led by the war-sage Vistro, who bound the Seat in a ring of living fire. When the Sundering broke the world, the Reliquary was flung into the stone beneath Durnhollow, dragging the ring, the sages, and the siege's vanguard with it. Their descendants under Vistro IV have held the fire ever since.

Vistro tasked the party with ridding the material plane of it.

> [!secret] What the fire holds in
> revealed:: false
> A fragment of [[The Void Heart]] rests at the Seat. Its leaking void is the source of the site's corruption, and it is what the ring of fire truly holds in.

> [!secret] It can't be unmade
> revealed:: false
> The Reliquary can't be unmade from this side. Only something like a Sundering could tear it loose.

## The Danger
**It hollows the living.** For every hour on the island or inside, each creature makes a DC 12 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, it gains 1 Stone, which fades after a long rest outside the barrier.

| Stone | Effect |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Speed reduced by 10 feet |
| 2 | Disadvantage on Dexterity checks and saves |
| 3 | Can't take reactions, speed halved |
| 4 | Petrified (stone from within) |

**The Abyss leaks through it.** It is stitched into the Abyss, not resting in the material plane. At the Apex, the seam is thin.

**The brands** warm on the island, go cold when a faithful path is taken, and burn at the Apex (real pain, no damage).

## Layout
Each trial has a **faithful path** (the Abyss's answer, which works but costs something lasting) and other ways through. The trials work like BG3's Gauntlet of Shar.

### 1. Outside
- **The Field of Stone.** King Atul's petrified army. Atul, woken by the Mind Medallion, tells his brief, tragic account. He can't enter.
- **The Lilac Hollow.** Dead trees and purple lilacs feeding on pooled Abyssal energy, the same flowers as beneath Oakhollow's Grand Cathedral.
- **The Doors.** The script asks pilgrims to *give as he gave*. A piece of yourself (blood costs 1d4 necrotic, unhealable until a long rest) placed in the split-heart sigil opens them. Three wrong offerings or forcing the doors wakes 3 Gargoyles and 1 Basilisk.

### 2. The Nave
- **The Offering Hall.** Basins of offerings with Abyssal inscriptions.
- **The Prayer Alcoves.** One bears *Forgive me, Atul* in Vashara's hand.
- **The War Murals.** The Sundering from the Abyss's side. A hidden second hand in the fourth mural is Memnor.
- **The Stone Book.** Speaking its prayer wakes the shaft. Its page *The Making of the Vessel* names [[The Soul Sanctum]] and shows its sigil. **Guaranteed beat:** Wayne recognizes the sigil from the Drak'mor ruins.

### 3. The Turning
Pilgrims step off the edge and fall. Halfway down, gravity flips, and they land inside the spire. Optional: the Inverted Stair (Athletics or Acrobatics DC 13).

### 4. The Inverted Spire
- **Chamber of Body: The Stilling.** Stone shadows of each character, each hunting its own original. Swap partners to win. Wayne's shadow wears the pendant.
- **Chamber of Mind: The Silencing.** Needles of void cut out words. Five pillars with dials, each player knowing someone else's setting. Faithful path: let a needle in and hear the answer from the Mother, at the cost of a memory.
- **Chamber of Soul: The Descent of the Blade.** A black greatsword in a well of the Sea of Void. Three depths (Hate, Grief, Hesitation), then *"What is left of you?"* Always passes. Wayne's light never goes out. A Tome carried in alerts Malcanthet.

### 5. The Apex
- **The Sealed Point.** The three sigil pieces open onto a red-black sky.
- **The Field of a Thousand Wars.** Floating slabs of Ysgard full of stilled giants, and the Ashen Reach with a war-sage whose shard can go back to Vistro.
- **The Seat of the Vessel.** The throne holds the Void Heart fragment. Naki-Uthai's name is carved in Giant at its base, readable only on Wayne's natural 20.
- **The Hollow Vestige.** The pre-turn Vessel, a re-flavored Stone Golem at CR 8. It attacks Wayne first.
- **Malcanthet** speaks through the fragment: tender toward Wayne, sending him to the Soul Sanctum, furious at Thimble over the Tome.

## What Actually Happened (Sessions 53 and 54)
- The Mind and Soul trials ran as described in [[Session 53 - What Is Left of You]]. [[King Atul Stonemarch|Atul]] couldn't pass the Soul trial and stayed on the Soul floor.
- The Apex was cut down to a single summit chamber: a crystallized heart in a mass of void, with calcified branches.
- The **Hollow Vessel Remnant** pulled the party into its domain, an obsidian sphere fed by three stone faces (Body, Mind, Soul). It's vulnerable to radiant damage. Its helm is a calcified copy, and the real one is somewhere in the world.
- [[Wayne Kerr|Wayne]] tore the [[Shell of the Void Heart]] out of the Remnant's chest. The anchor is gone, and the Reliquary is coming apart. See [[Session 54 - The Heart of the Void]].

- **The Vessel's Votive:** re-flavored Periapt of Wound Closure.
- **Lilac from the Hollow:** nonmagical, never wilts.
- **Shard of the Ashen Reach:** nonmagical, for Vistro.
- **[[The Void Heart|Fragment of the Void Heart]]:** cursed, very rare.
