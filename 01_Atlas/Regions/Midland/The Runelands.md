# The Runelands

> [!summary] At a Glance
> The arcane heart of Midland, rich with ley line energy.

## Notable Places
- **Runestone.** Built in a colossal crater, the world's nexus of magical energy, and home of [[The Obsidian Enclave]].
- **Emberhearth.** A city within the glowing Forest of Embers.

## Connections
- [[Durnhollow]]'s Soulvite process drew on Runeland chaos energy. See [[Soulvite]].
