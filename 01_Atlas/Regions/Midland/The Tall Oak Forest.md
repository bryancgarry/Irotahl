# The Tall Oak Forest

> [!summary] At a Glance
> A great forest outside [[Oakhollow]]. Site of the campaign's first arc, the stone trees.

## What Happened Here
Someone woke the Godspire, a [[Lithoweaves|lithoweave]] in the forest, and it began turning the forest to stone. The party went in to stop it from calcifying the world. When they did, it collapsed like a black hole and vanished. They learned very little about it.

## Played
- Lies between [[Oakhollow]] and Bluelock Lake. Loggers float timber downriver to Azure Port.
- [[Bragthar]]'s hidden mushroom cove, with its roulette pool. See [[Session 4 - Mushrooms & Trees]].
- A Bloodlancer camp by a dry riverbed, up the right fork. See [[Session 5 - Bluelock and Bloodlancers]].
- A stone tree in a logging clearing, the same stone as Wayne, woke as a Tree Blight when Wayne's necklace touched it. Its core went into Wayne.
- The Godspire ruins at the peak: a race shrine (elves, dwarves, giants, gnomes) whose mask was already looted, a dragon temple with an obsidian obelisk (Bog shattered it), and older inverted ruins with a shrine to "the Architect." See [[Session 6 - The Path to Godspire Ruins]] and [[Session 8 - Gnomes and Fireballs]].
- The lithoweave hid behind an illusion of an ancient tree at the summit. The party collapsed it in [[Session 7 - The Goose is Loose]]. The calcification stopped, but what was stone stays stone.

> [!secret] Who woke it
> revealed:: false
> Every lithoweave the Godhand or their agents wake feeds [[The Heart of Entropy|Tharizdun]]. Who woke the Godspire, and where it went, is open.
