# Silath Shal

> [!summary] At a Glance
> The Spear of Heaven. The tallest mountain on Syfaeril, piercing the heavens. Legends say those who would become gods must climb it. No one who has tried has returned.

> An earlier version put [[The Soul Armor]] at its peak. That is no longer settled.
