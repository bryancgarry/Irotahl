# Vyrmor

> [!summary] At a Glance
> The wild jungle continent south of [[Drak'mor]], once joined to it. Ruins of a vanished civilization, ley-rich land, and a sanctuary for dragons.

## The Dragonlords
Seven rulers, each holding a Dragonsword.

## Peoples of the Jungle
Dragonborn, tabaxi, tortles, lizardfolk, and others. Dragonborn hold dragons in reverence.

> [!secret] The Tome of Baphomet
> revealed:: false
> The Tome of [[Baphomet]] is somewhere in Vyrmor. See [[The Demonomicon]].
